# Draw Steel Downtime Projects

> What downtime projects are (the structured between-session activities PCs use to pursue long-term goals) and short descriptive entries for canonical project types — research, crafting, relationship-building, training, etc.
>
> If full mechanical resolution rules become needed by a skill, they should split into a skill-local reference (paralleling Negotiation/Tests).

## What downtime projects are

Downtime projects are between-respite activities heroes pursue toward long-term individual goals — uncovering forbidden lore, forging weapons of great power, building ships, training in new skills, and so on. Each project covers the kind of work a movie or novel would summarize in a montage rather than play out scene by scene.

Downtime projects have prerequisites — required items, a *project source* (lore detailing how to do the work), or both. Heroes can sometimes find *guides* (books, schematics, NPCs, or recordings) that accelerate specific projects, awarded at Director discretion. Full mechanical resolution lives in skill-local references when needed.

## Crafting Projects

Crafting projects enable heroes to create vehicles, supernatural treasures, and more.

### Build Airship

Hire a crew of carpenters, mages, and shipwrights who construct the vessel over an extended period.

### Build or Repair Road

Hire masons, engineers, and guards who build or repair a road outward from a starting location.

### Craft Teleportation Platform

Build a permanent teleportation platform activated by a supernatural password. Anyone who knows the password can use it to teleport themselves, other creatures, or objects to a location they know — or to a paired platform whose password they also know.

### Craft Treasure

Craft items from the Treasures catalog. Each treasure has its own prerequisites and specs noted in its entry.

### Find a Cure

Research a cure for a disease, curse, or supernatural affliction. If the affliction was caused by a creature's innate feature, the remains of any creature of that kind serve as the prerequisite — e.g., curing a werewolf's Accursed Bite requires the corpse of any werewolf, not just the one who bit you.

### Imbue Treasure

Create leveled treasures by imbuing magic or psionic power into a mundane base item (sword, shield, etc.).

## Research Projects

Heroes can undertake many different types of research downtime projects, which can involve seeking out new lore, improving existing knowledge with study, uncovering rumors or secrets, and more.

### Discover Lore

Research lore that can't be discovered through a single test — locating a lost treasure, deciphering a ritual, tracing a royal lineage, or recovering knowledge after a failed recall test.

### Go Undercover

Infiltrate and spy on a target organization. The project yields insider knowledge — maps, operational details, common-or-obscure secrets.

### Hone Career Skills

Revisit your pre-adventuring career to refresh and sharpen the skills it taught you.

### Learn From a Master

Study under a master — or their surviving teachings — to acquire a specific skill or insight the master is qualified to convey.

### Learn New Language

Learn a new language taught by the project source.

### Learn New Skill

Learn a new skill taught by the project source.

### Perfect New Recipe

Master a new recipe. Ingredients depend on the dish and may be hard to find. Recipes from your own culture are easier to perfect than foreign ones.

## Other Projects

Not all heroes want to craft or research during their time between adventures. Some might wish to build organizations, reconnect with family or friends, or just go fishing.

### Community Service

Help people in a settlement — odd jobs, tutoring life skills, cleaning streets and public spaces, finding lost valuables, and the like. Must be undertaken in one fixed location; you can't community-serve while traveling.

### Fishing

Fish at a body of water — for sustenance, relaxation, or bragging rights.

### Spend Time With Loved Ones

Reconnect with people you love who you haven't seen in a long while; requires being able to communicate with them. Yields a temporary boost to morale and resilience.
