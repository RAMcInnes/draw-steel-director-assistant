# Draw Steel Cosmology

> The metaphysical structure of the Draw Steel setting — planes, manifolds, and the timescape that contains them. For the active actors who *rule* these planes, see [factions.md](factions.md). For Orden's geography specifically, see [regions.md](regions.md).

## The timescape

The **timescape** is the multiverse — a collection of worlds spanning high fantasy, dark fantasy, and space fantasy. Each star in Orden's night sky is another world (a fact most people on Orden don't know).

Old-fashioned people call these worlds **planes**. Sages, using geometric formulae, call them **manifolds**. The terms mean the same thing.

## Energy and the upper-vs-lower divide

Manifolds sit on an energy gradient.

- **Higher worlds** are more energetic, affording access to alien technologies. Great starfreighters ply the space-lanes; knights wield psionically-powered hard-light blades against star pirates with hard-light blasters.
- **Lower worlds** lack the energy for such tech to function, and so rely on **magic** to break the rules.

Technology from the upper worlds does not function on the lower worlds — unless powered by a strong psionic mind, or by the miracle mineral **iridoss** (also called **prismacore**). Conversely, magic works best on the lower worlds and dwindles as one ascends.

## The planes (highest to lowest)

### Axiom — the Plane of Uttermost Law

Native home of the **memonek**. A world teeming with complex, inorganic life — lakes of mercury, glass-winged birds, iridescent metal flowers. The minds of memonek are highly ordered; their reason is their great pride. **UNISOL** (the Universal Solar League) is headquartered here.

### Proteus — the Sea of Eternal Change

The Plane of Uttermost Chaos. Home to the formless **proteans**, who rebelled against the **Synlirii** (here called *voiceless talkers*) and exiled them downward to the World Below. Proteans now master their own world and take to the stars in their living **changeships**, hurling their small fleet against UNISOL.

### Quintessence — the City at the Center of the Timescape

The lowest of the upper worlds. Capital city is **Alloy**, also called **the City of Brass** — proteans, memonek, devils, fire dwarves, and even humans rub shoulders here. Alloy is the gateway to the timescape, where travelers between upper and lower worlds meet to trade goods and information, free from UNISOL's inflexible law.

### Orden — the Plane of Gods and Sorcery

The highest of the lower worlds, where magic rules. The gods are forbidden from interfering directly in a world with such a low energy state, and rely on **saints** to enact their will. The default game world; see [regions.md](regions.md) for its geography and [overview.md](overview.md) for the genre register.

### The World Below — the Dark Under All

Sister-manifold to Orden, almost coterminal with it. A plane of exiles. No stars, no sky — only endless caverns and warrens, vast caves and sunless seas. Some caverns hold entire cities, like **Or-Mazaar, City of the Black Star**. Ruled by **A Lie Cloaked In Star's Silver, the Queen of Night** (see [factions.md](factions.md)). Power waning.

### Equinox — Dusk

A smaller, parasitic manifold home to the twilight celestials and their servants the **shadow elves**. Permanent twilight, fierce fairy-tale magic, weird jungle world. Ruled by **Every Strike of Lightning a Lover Betrayed, the Queen of Shadows**, who plots to bridge the sea between worlds and colonize Orden — making a new home for her people before their old world dies. Power waxing.

### The Seven Cities of Hell

The last plane of law, among the lowest of the lower planes. A land of devils proud of their bureaucratic civilization. Each of the seven cities is ruled by an **archduke** scheming to ascend to the Throne of Hell. Devils have little interest in the other planes — life in Hell is far more interesting. The seven archdukes did, however, conspire once to create the **Order of Desolation / Illriggers** to extend their power outward and defend Hell from the demon hordes below.

### The Abyssal Waste

The lowest plane. A heat-blasted desert under a baleful, giant orange sun. Demons claw and scramble over each other, competing for souls. Mindless collections of organs, claws, and teeth, demons collect souls until they reach sentience and gain identity and the blessing of memory. They will do anything to escape upward, lest they lose their collected souls and fall into the mindless state called **lethe**.

At the center of the waste lies the **Necropolitan Ruin** — *the Last City*, a city of the dead, ruled by **Khorsekef the Ultralich** (see [factions.md](factions.md)).

## The Law of Time

Cause and effect work on Orden because **Ord** placed the dwarves in the world and imposed the **Law of Time**. Before that, time misbehaved across all of Orden — and within the **wodes**, it still does. Within a wode, cause and effect are only distant cousins; villagers may wander in and emerge unchanged a century later. When pressed on how this works, the elves look baffled. *"How does what work?"*
