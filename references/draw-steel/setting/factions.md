# Draw Steel Factions

> Short descriptive entries per faction in the published setting — what they are, who leads them, what they want, who opposes them. Geographic context lives in [regions.md](regions.md); cosmological context in [cosmology.md](cosmology.md); dated events in [timeline.md](timeline.md).

## Capital — The Great Houses

The four ancient noble families of Capital, each ruling a district and playing the Great Game (espionage) against the others. With the prince dead three years ago and no heir, all four are jockeying for the throne.

### House Alvaro

Led by **Duke Prospero**. Sponsors the **Imperial University** — the greatest center of learning in the world, drawing nobles from across Orden (including Vanigar) to study diplomacy and statecraft. The university's **Actian School** has historically doubled as the prince's intelligence agency; its graduates are the world's greatest spies. House Alvaro are the best players of the Great Game.

### House Vorona

Led by **Duke Marco**. Runs the **Imperial Navy**, the largest military organization in Orden. Vorona's engineers perfected the secret of blackpowder and guard it jealously. The navy's **Far Mariners** (the marines) are the closest thing Capital has to city-wide law enforcement; each great house otherwise polices its own district. Vorona also sponsors the **Imperial War College** (the Academy), whose graduates form an informal alumni network across noble families called the **Old Class Ring** — feeding Vorona intelligence other factions can only dream of.

### House Navarr

Oldest of the great houses. Led by **His Grace Orsino, Duke Navarr**, archbishop of the **Church of Saint Ysabella the Pitiless** — the most powerful church in Capital. House Navarr enforces the church's law (which they call justice), consolidating a vast patronage network of subordinate churches and orders of knights across the region.

### House Valetta

Arguably the most powerful great house, controlling the **Arbitros Fiat** (tax collectors). Led by **Duchess Lenore**. After her husband Maximo was assassinated, Lenore opened the *Codex Mortis* and spoke a ritual that should have returned him to life. Instead, she brought about the **Lilac Night** — every mortal in her district, herself included, was transformed into a deathless revenant. Lenore is now an immortal vampire queen ruling a dead district. After the Lilac Night, House Valetta could no longer reliably deliver collected taxes to the prince.

## Capital — The Ascendant Guilds

After the Lilac Night, three guilds used their wealth to buy great-house status, joining the Great Game.

### The Fulcrum

Capital's assayers' guild, led by **Lady Shirome**. Controls the **Trade Integrity Board** (lending rates, trade negotiations between Capital and other governments). The Fulcrum convinced the prince to switch the city to paper money — making Capital the first and only city in Orden with a robust monetary policy.

### The Broadsheets / The Font

Formal name **the Font**; popularly called the Broadsheets. Led by **Guildmaster Inān al-Adwiyya**. Publishes the thrice-daily news sheets every Capital citizen reads, distributed by a vast network of young people called the **Paperfeathers**. Lady al-Adwiyya knows almost everything happening anywhere in the city.

### The Rasp / Farrier's Guild

Formal name **the Farrier's Guild**; popularly called the Rasp. Led by **Lord Kashimir**, a heliox from Alloy. Holds a monopoly on transport throughout Capital — most importantly, on importing flying tapestries from Alloy, which let the rich and powerful cross the 13-mile-wide city in minutes. Kashimir also runs the **Kites**, couriers famous for delivering a message anywhere in Capital in only a few hours. He is not shy about seeking more power.

## The Iron Saint's Regime

The post-Omund order ruling Vasloria. See [Omund's Land](regions.md) for the geographic and historical context.

### Ajax the Invincible / The Iron Saint

Overlord of Omund's Land, ruling from the **Chrysopolis** (sky-fortress raised from the secrets of fallen sky elf cities). Killed Good King Omund, abolished all faiths and temples, executed the dukes who organized the barons, placed a high bounty on every Dragon Phalanx warrior's head.

### Mortum

Ajax's wizard, who unlocked the secret of the ancient sky elf flying cities to raise the Chrysopolis. Mortum also unlocked the **synliroi body banks** — the source of two of Ajax's most chilling tools: immortality granted to nobles who voluntarily submit to Ajax, and the **war dogs**, brutal patchwork soldiers who owe their new lives to the Iron Saint and fight for him fanatically.

### The Hawklords

Once a remote, proud, almost mythical order of mountaintop riders. After Ajax's takeover they made a pact with him to avoid extermination, and now serve as his **elite counterinsurgent force** — mounted on giant hawks, projecting his power, enforcing his law, and crushing revolts before they form. Their mastery of the air means any rebellion is quickly seen and answered.

### Stone Dwarves of Kal Kalavar

The dwarves of the **Hanging City** (Kal Kalavar) pay Ajax tribute in prisoners abducted from travelers foolish enough to use the roads unescorted. The prisoners serve as forced labor or are fed into the body banks. The stone dwarves brood under the mountains and dislike the deal — but lack the power, or the will, to rebel.

### High Elves of Irranys

The high elves of the fallen celestial city of **Irranys** pay Ajax tribute with ancient artifacts they plunder from the ruins.

## Resistance and Independents in Vasloria

### The Dragon Phalanx

Good King Omund's draconian knights — once several thousand strong, organized into eight dragonflights, with members from virtually every heroic vocation (shadows, censors, tacticians, elementalists). For 30+ years they were symbols of justice across Vasloria. Now broken, hunted by bounty. Some folk still see them as heroes of a lost age. In every town and village, though, there are always desperate people willing to summon the Hawklords against any dragon knight foolish enough to travel without disguise.

*Many people still call the Phalanx "incorruptible." Concrete evidence to the contrary exists. The reputation persists anyway — see [overview.md](overview.md) on in-world point of view.*

### The Orchid Court

Loose, decentralized government of the **wode elves**, who refuse to bow to Ajax. Lacking centralized cities, the Orchid Court makes any blanket conquest difficult.

## Cross-Plane and Cosmic Powers

For the planes themselves, see [cosmology.md](cosmology.md).

### UNISOL — The Universal Solar League

Upper-worlds polity headquartered on Axiom. Ensures and protects trade across the upper worlds via the great starfreighters; defends them from time raiders and pirates. Rigid law.

### The Starslayers

Infamous pirate band on the legendary ship the **K.R.A.D.1 Fearless**. Hurl themselves against UNISOL's tyrannical might.

### The Order of Desolation / The Illriggers

Devil order created by the seven archdukes of Hell, in a rare moment of cooperation, to extend their power into the timescape and defend Hell from the demon hordes below.

### The Three Sisters Below

Three queens ruling lower planes. The current source names two:

- **A Lie Cloaked In Star's Silver, the Queen of Night** — *first* of the Three Sisters Below; rules the World Below from **Or-Mazaar, City of the Black Star**. Her power wanes.
- **Every Strike of Lightning a Lover Betrayed, the Queen of Shadows** — *third* of the Three Sisters Below; rules Equinox; plots to bridge the sea between worlds and colonize Orden, making a new home for her shadow elves before their parasitic world dies. Her power waxes.

The second sister is implied by the count but unnamed in the source material populated so far.

### Khorsekef the Ultralich

Once the Infinite Pharaoh of Khemhara, ruling from the Heliopolis. Now ruler of the **Necropolitan Ruin** (the Last City) at the center of the Abyssal Waste. Intends to return to Orden and sit once again on his throne.
