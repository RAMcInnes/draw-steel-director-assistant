# Draw Steel Timeline

> Major historical events in the Draw Steel setting. Used by skills to anchor "when in history" the campaign sits and to surface relevant past events when planning arcs. For the actors involved, see [factions.md](factions.md); for places, see [regions.md](regions.md); for cosmological context, see [cosmology.md](cosmology.md).
>
> Dates are presented relative to "the present" of the Age of Chaos. The setting is presented from an in-world point of view — no objective canonical timeline exists.

## Current era — The Age of Chaos

The default time-frame of the game. Order is collapsing across Vasloria; succession is unsettled in Capital; the lower planes wax and wane uncertainly.

## The recent past

### ~3 years ago — Death of the Prince of Capital

The prince of Capital died at age 41, leaving no heir or likely successor. Was he murdered like a commoner, or assassinated by a political rival? Evidence is scant; rumor substitutes for fact. The four great houses and three ascendant guilds (see [factions.md](factions.md)) have been jockeying for the empty throne ever since. Everyone knows a war of succession is coming — but each player would prefer someone else make the first move.

### The Lilac Night (date not specified, post-Ajax)

After her husband Maximo was assassinated, **Duchess Lenore** of House Valetta opened the *Codex Mortis* and spoke a ritual she believed would return him to life. Instead, she brought about the Lilac Night: every mortal in House Valetta's district, herself included, was transformed into a deathless revenant. Lenore is now an immortal vampire queen ruling a dead district.

The Lilac Night cost House Valetta its ability to reliably deliver collected taxes to the prince. In the aftermath, three guilds — the Fulcrum, the Broadsheets/Font, and the Rasp/Farrier's Guild — used their wealth to buy great-house status, joining the Great Game.

### ~15 years ago — The Fall of Omund's Land

Good King Omund, who had ruled western Vasloria (Omund's Land) for 35 years, was killed and his castle taken by **Ajax the Invincible**, now called the Iron Saint. In a cascade of related events:

- **Mortum**, Ajax's wizard, unlocked the secret of the ancient sky elf flying cities and raised the **Chrysopolis** as Ajax's sky-fortress.
- Mortum also unlocked the **synliroi body banks**, granting immortality to nobles who voluntarily submit to Ajax — and producing the war dogs, Ajax's brutal fanatic soldiers.
- The Dragon Phalanx was broken; bounties were placed on every surviving member.
- Ajax abolished all faiths and temples, and executed the dukes who organized the barons.
- The Hawklords made a pact with Ajax to avoid extermination, and now serve as his elite counterinsurgent force.
- The stone dwarves of Kal Kalavar agreed to pay tribute in abducted travelers.
- The high elves of fallen Irranys agreed to pay tribute in plundered celestial artifacts.

### ~50 years ago — Omund's reign begins

**Good King Omund** comes to rule western Vasloria. For 35 years his draconian knights — the Dragon Phalanx — protect the weak from the strong and dispense justice. Roads are safe; people walk into the woods unafraid of meeting anything more threatening than a nymph or conversational manticore.

## Ancient history

### The Synlirii exiled

In the deep past, the **Synlirii** (also called the *voiceless talkers*) ruled the Plane of Uttermost Chaos. The formless **proteans** rebelled and exiled them downward to the World Below, where they remain.

### Khorsekef and Khemhara

In the deep past, **Khorsekef** was the **Infinite Pharaoh** of Khemhara, ruling from the **Heliopolis**. He is now the Ultralich, ruling the Necropolitan Ruin in the Abyssal Waste, and intends to return to Orden and reclaim his throne.

### Ord and the Law of Time

Before **Ord** placed the dwarves in the world, time misbehaved across all of Orden. Ord's act imposed the **Law of Time**, establishing cause and effect — except within the wodes, where the old conditions still hold.
