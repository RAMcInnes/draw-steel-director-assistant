# Draw Steel — Group Tests

Ruleset reference for `exploration-encounter`. Source: *Draw Steel Heroes* v1, Chapter 9, book p. 260.

## When to use

Two or more heroes attempt **the same task together** that calls for **the same test**. All three conditions hold:

- **Shared task** — the same action, at the same moment (all climbing one tower; all sneaking past one ogre).
- **Shared test** — natural framing is same characteristic, usually same skill pool.
- **Shared consequence** — success/failure lands on the whole group.

**Not every collaborative scene is a group test.** If heroes perform *different* sub-tasks toward one goal (one digs a trench, one drills farmers, one scouts), that's a **[[montages|Montage]]**, not a group test.

## Making a group test

1. Director sets the difficulty — **easy, medium, or hard** — per the normal [[tests|test]] framework.
2. **Each participating hero makes the test individually.** Skills and edges/banes apply per-hero normally. Each hero may use **different** skills (one Climb, one Lift, one Jump) — only the characteristic is shared.
3. **Participants can't use Assist a Test on each other.** If a hero wants to help rather than roll, they simply don't participate (and aren't counted toward the threshold). Outside-the-group help (setting up ropes/pitons beforehand) should translate into the Director *lowering the difficulty*, not an in-test assist.
4. Director waits until all rolls are in, then interprets.

## Resolution

**Pass 1 — succeeded or failed:**

- **Half or more individual successes → group success.**
- **Otherwise → group failure.**

"Success" covers straight success, success-with-consequence, and success-with-reward. "Failure" covers straight failure and failure-with-consequence.

**Pass 2 — collective reward or consequence:**

- On **group success**, if **half or more of the succeeding heroes earned rewards** → **collective reward** (worth roughly two individual rewards); **individual consequences are ignored.** Concrete shapes: two consumables, two juicy info drops, two hero tokens, or something task-tailored (sneaking through a camp → sabotage war engines + steal horses on the way out).
- On **group failure**, if **more than half of the failing heroes incurred consequences** → **collective consequence**; **individual rewards are ignored.** Defaults: each hero takes a bane on their next power roll, *or* Director gains **2 Malice per hero** at the start of the next combat. Task-tailored hits harder (the whole camp goes on alert, waves of enemies attack).

**Pass 3 — default:** Fewer than half trigger reward/consequence → group simply succeeds or fails with no collective modifier.

## Partial success

Group tests are **binary on pass/fail**, with partial-ness expressed in pass 2. The four resulting shapes:

| Result | Individual pattern |
|---|---|
| Success with collective reward | Half+ succeed, half+ of those earned rewards |
| Plain success | Half+ succeed, reward threshold not hit |
| Plain failure | Half+ failed, consequence threshold not hit |
| Failure with collective consequence | Half+ failed, more-than-half incurred consequences |

Design so the **binary pass/fail is the core story beat**; the reward/consequence layer adds flavor. Don't hinge the scene on which individual rolls were which.
