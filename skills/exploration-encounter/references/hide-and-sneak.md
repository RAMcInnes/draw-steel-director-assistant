# Draw Steel — Hiding & Sneaking

Ruleset reference for `exploration-encounter`. Source: *Draw Steel Heroes* v1, Chapter 9, book pp. 258–259.

## Hiding

### Preconditions to hide from a creature

1. You have **cover or concealment** from that creature (see Chapter 10), and
2. That creature is **not observing you** — they can't currently pinpoint your specific location.

If a foe is watching you duck behind a barrel, the hide fails while they continue to observe. If a dragon is chasing you, you can only hide *after* breaking line of sight (e.g., turn a corner into stalagmites), *then* attempt the hide.

**"Observed" is situational.** Line of effect usually = observing, but not always — a guard in a crowded market can't track every creature in line of effect, so fading into a crowd is easier than hiding in an empty street. Director has final say.

### Resolution

- **In combat** — use the **Hide maneuver** (Chapter 10). With cover/concealment from a non-observing creature, you are **automatically hidden** unless the Director rules otherwise.
- **Out of combat** — Director may call for a **Hide-skill test** to determine how well hidden you are.

### Benefits while hidden from a creature

- The creature **can't target you with non-Area abilities** (Area-keyword abilities still affect you).
- You gain an **edge on ability rolls against that creature.** The edge **persists until the end of the turn you stopped being hidden** — so you can start your turn hidden, emerge, attack, and still have the edge on that attack.

### Losing hidden status

Hidden ends when you:

- **Lose cover/concealment** from the creature, or
- **Use an ability, interact with an enemy, move without sneaking, or otherwise make noise or reveal yourself.**

Triggering activities **resolve first, then** hidden ends. Make a strike while hidden → resolve the strike with the edge, *then* become unhidden.

## Searching for hidden creatures

You can search for hidden creatures **within 10 squares** of you, with **line of effect** to them.

**Resolution** — a **Search maneuver**:

- You make an **Intuition test with the Search skill.**
- Each hidden creature within 10 squares makes an **opposed Agility test with the Hide skill** (see Opposed Power Rolls in [[tests]]).
- Compare totals per pair. Higher total → that creature is no longer hidden from you. Otherwise they remain hidden.

Director can swap characteristics/skills when fiction warrants (e.g., **Presence + Handle Animals** to hide among sheep; **Reason + Eavesdrop** to find the hidden by breathing).

**Calling out what you find.**
- As part of the search maneuver, **point out any creatures you notice to allies within 10 squares** — making those creatures no longer hidden from those allies.
- If a creature is hidden from your allies but *not* from you, use a maneuver (no test) to point them out.

## Sneaking

**Preconditions** — you are **hidden** from a creature AND **not in combat**.

**Resolution** — **Agility test with the Sneak skill** at Director-set difficulty. While sneaking, **speed is halved.** Success = you remain hidden during movement.

Characteristic can swap at Director discretion (e.g., **Presence + Sneak** to blend into a packed street).

## Surprise

Draw Steel has no distinct "surprise round." Ambushes resolve via **hiding**, **sneaking**, and the **NPCs-Roll-for-Deceptive-Tasks** option in [[tests]]:

- Assassin creeps on a camp, no hero on watch → Agility to sneak up. Fail = twig snaps, heroes notice immediately. Success = unnoticed until the assailant is on top of them.
- Hero declares watch → Intuition to notice the ambush.

Combat then begins per Chapter 10; any lingering hidden benefits carry into the first exchanges.
