# Wyverns

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 324–325. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

A wyvern perched on a rotting tree or a jagged mountain peak marks the edge of the civilized world — beyond is forest primeval, raw and ancient sorcery older than roads. Despite superficial resemblance, wyverns aren't dragons or dragon-kin; they're distant relatives of the **terror lizards of Ix**. Leathery hides run brown to red to black depending on native terrain. They're solitary hunters who track prey by following the smell of fear. Relentless stingers, acid-tipped. No front limbs — they keep distance and strike from the sky with their long tails, disinterested in drawn-out struggle.

- **Perch hunters.** Wyverns pick high ground — stone outcroppings, natural spires, dead trees — to overlook their territory. Sting from above first, drive the victim into hiding, then tear them out with hooked claws and fearsome teeth. Encounter maps with verticality and broken line-of-sight let the wyvern cycle its preferred loop.
- **Fear-scent tracking.** Flavor trait, not mechanical — wyverns find frightened or fleeing prey by scent. GM lever for pursuit scenes: a hero who breaks and runs brings the wyvern to the party wherever they go.
- **Pair encounters during hatching season.** Solitary most of the year, but mated pairs aggressively hunt *anything* during hatching season to feed their young — and nothing provokes a wyvern faster than a threat to their eggs. Professional monster slayers refuse wyvern contracts during this window. This is when the party is needed most, and it's when two wyverns plus a nest defends the encounter.
- **Can't speak, can be reasoned with.** A clever hero carrying fresh meat might be able to negotiate — social-scene potential that isn't combat. Useful hook for parties that can't win a straight fight.
- **Acid immunity + fly.** Both non-minion wyverns have Acid Immunity 5 and Fly. Acid-damage parties do reduced work; parties without ranged answers spend the whole fight chasing a flying enemy.

## Shared traits (both stat blocks)

- **Tenacious Hunter.** Any creature affected by a condition imposed by a wyvern can't be hidden from the wyvern. Once a sting lands a save-ends debuff, stealth is off the table for the whole duration.
- **Rage traits.** Both non-minion wyverns have a "near another wyvern" rider: Lurkers deal +3 damage while within 10 of another wyvern; Predators can't be dazed or frightened while winded *or* within 10 of another wyvern. Pair encounters are mechanically stronger, not just narratively.
- **Acid Immunity 5, Fly.** Shared across both. Expect acid damage as the signature damage type.

## Wyvern Malice features

Spent at the start of any wyvern's turn.

- **Simmering Anger (3 Malice).** One wyvern makes a free strike against each adjacent enemy. A wyvern that has closed into melee with multiple heroes converts Malice into map-wide melee damage.
- **Boiling Fury (5 Malice).** Until end of round, each wyvern has double edge on strikes *and* can use their signature ability instead of a free strike on opportunity attacks. The whole point: Sedating Stinger or Agonizing Stinger becomes the opportunity-attack punishment, turning any melee disengage into a save-ends rider.
- **Overflowing Rage (7 Malice).** Each enemy makes an Intuition test: tier 1 taunted by nearest creature-or-object save-ends *with double edge on power rolls against them*, tier 2 taunted save-ends, tier 3 no effect. The "everyone attacks the nearest thing" button — drops party tactics into chaos; paired wyverns with a nearby nest of hatchlings make the taunt target ambiguous.

## Wyvern Lurker — elite

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 4, elite, EV 24, beast, wyvern
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 325
**Movement:** Fly.

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 120, Speed 9, Stability 2, Free Strike 6, Acid Immunity 5. The stealthy cousin — faster, less armored, strikes from concealment.

**Tactics:** **Agonizing Stinger** (Signature, Melee 2, two targets, Magic) — damage with bleeding save-ends on M<2/M<3 at tier 2/3. **1 Malice rider:** +6 acid damage against a target hidden from the lurker. The lurker *wants* targets to hide — Tenacious Hunter + the bonus damage means hidden targets are lit up and hit harder. **Acidic Anguish** (3 Malice, Melee 2, Magic) — heavy acid damage + weakened save-ends; weakened targets take 1d4 acid/turn. The soften-then-finish pipeline: Swooping Torment to strike-and-hide, Agonizing Stinger next turn, Acidic Anguish to weaken, let the acid DoT run. **Swooping Torment** (Maneuver) — fly up to speed, then attempt to hide. *Each enemy the lurker passes adjacent to chooses: 3 sonic damage OR fall prone.* Forced choice on the whole flight path — heroes without stability take the prone, damage builds take the sonic. **Retaliatory Dive** (Triggered, Ranged 5): when damaged by a ranged ability, flies adjacent + free strike. Ranged attackers within 5 eat a dive every time they fire. **Ruthless Rage:** +3 damage within 10 of another wyvern.

Counterplay: no ranged from within 5 squares; apply bleeding/weakened to deny Tenacious Hunter hide-bonus targeting; break concealment of the party's own stealth characters before the lurker cashes the 1 Malice rider; stay in groups to dilute Swooping Torment's forced-choice damage.

## Wyvern Predator — elite

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 4, elite, EV 24, beast, wyvern
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 325
**Movement:** Fly.

**Flavor:** Size 3, Stamina 140, Speed 7, Stability 3, Free Strike 6, Acid Immunity 5. The larger, more aggressive bruiser — reach 3, slows and restrains.

**Tactics:** **Sedating Stinger** (Signature, Melee 3, two targets, Magic) — damage + slowed save-ends on M<2/M<3. **Effect:** *if a slowed target is already slowed, they are restrained save-ends instead.* Two-hit pipeline on the same hero: first sting slows, second restrains. **Tail Sweep** (6×3 line Area) — damage with +3 acid on A-save. **5 Malice:** use again, same or different line. Map-clearing AoE with Malice repeat. **Grasping Jaws** (2 Malice, Maneuver, Melee 2) — damage + grabbed on A-save, tier 3 adds bane on Escape Grab. **Deterring Sting** (1 Malice, Triggered, Melee 3): when melee-damaged, Sedating Stinger back + shift up to 3. Melee attackers eat a stinger rider *and* the wyvern repositions. **Stubborn Rage:** while winded or within 10 of another wyvern, can't be dazed or frightened. Pair encounters or late-round winded states shut off the party's control tools.

Counterplay: ranged from 4+ to sidestep Sedating Stinger's reach; bring a second movement-control tool since Tail Sweep's Area bypasses single-target forced movement; split the two wyverns so Stubborn Rage's 10-square rider doesn't apply; burst the predator above winded before Stubborn Rage's "can't be dazed or frightened" clause unlocks.

## Encounter notes

- **No minions, horde, leader, or solo.** Both stat blocks are elites. A canonical wyvern encounter is one Lurker or one Predator solo, or both together as a mated pair (multiplying Ruthless Rage and Stubborn Rage), or a pair plus environmental elements (eggs, hatchlings-as-objects, nest terrain).
- **Verticality matters.** Both wyverns have Fly. An encounter map without meaningful altitude — no cliffs, towers, trees, open sky — forces the wyvern to play against its kit. Maps *with* altitude let them perch-and-dive.
- **Negotiation window.** The "fresh meat to reason with a wyvern" hook is a real pre-fight option for clever parties. If the GM wants the wyvern to be more than a combat, seed this in narration so the party recognizes the opportunity.
- **Hatching season tension.** Any encounter involving a nest, eggs, or hatchlings ramps up wyvern aggression and adds non-combatant stakes. A party that threatens the nest fights an enraged wyvern that won't flee; a party that leaves the nest alone might walk past the wyvern entirely.
