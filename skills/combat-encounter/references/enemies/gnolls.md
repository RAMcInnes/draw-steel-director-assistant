# Gnolls

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 153–158. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Hyena-faced fiends born when a pack of abyssal hyenas lapped at the ichor of a dying demon prince, As'sylrak the Warper. His last act was to give them cunning to match their hunger, bind his will to theirs, and turn their paws into hands — then they devoured him. Gnolls spread out of the Abyssal Wasteland across the timescape and now thrive in deserts, plains, and sparsely populated places. A pack's wild cackle in the night is a warning: a hunt is on, and whoever heard it is the quarry.

- **Ceaseless Hunger.** Gnolls live for the hunt and the taste of flesh — they aren't choosy with their targets. One quiet mercy for humanoid communities is that gnoll war bands constantly fight each other over territory, which keeps their total numbers in check.
- **Abyssal Hyenas.** Stronger and smarter than their beastly cousins, abyssal hyenas follow gnoll packs as scavengers and as reserves — gnoll spellcasters can transform a hyena into a gnoll with the right ritual mid-combat (see Bloodpool Malice and the Summoner's Cackletongue).
- **Tusker Demons.** Elephantine tusker demons march with gnoll war bands as beasts of burden and war engines. Their faces are nothing but a twisted crown of tusks around a ravening maw. They follow gnoll commands — until they fall into *lethe*, at which point they charge anything that moves.
- **Languages.** Most gnolls speak Tholl. Carnages and older gnolls usually also know Variac.

## Shared traits (all non-minion gnolls)

- **Cackletongue (the core combo).** Every non-minion gnoll has a personal Cackletongue maneuver at their own Malice cost. Every Cackletongue includes a clause that reads, essentially, *"if any ally target hasn't used their own Cackletongue this turn, they can use it immediately at no cost."* One gnoll firing a Cackletongue can cascade into multiple free Cackletongues across the pack in a single turn — buff stacking, free strikes, mass shifts, mass save-ends frightens. This is the gnoll trick. Build around it.
- **Death Frenzy / Death Circle.** When a non-minion gnoll ally drops to 0 Stamina nearby:
  - *Death Circle (minions):* the gnoll minion can shift up to their speed.
  - *Death Frenzy (horde):* the gnoll shifts up to their speed *and* makes a free strike.
  - The moment a horde gnoll dies, the surrounding minions relocate for free; the moment a horde gnoll dies near another horde gnoll, the survivor gets a free hit. Killing one gnoll triggers a ripple across the pack.
- **Heroes Beware (encounter-building).** A 1st-echelon encounter that contains *more than two non-minion gnolls* can be considered one difficulty category harder at the Director's discretion. Cackletongue + Death Frenzy stacking is the reason — the combo scales non-linearly with how many non-minion gnolls are present.

## Gnoll Malice features

Spent at the start of any gnoll's turn.

- **Iron Jaws (3 Malice, Maneuver, 1-cube within 3).** A gnoll acting this turn drops an iron-jawed snare into the area. The first enemy to enter the area makes an Agility test (with a bane if they were unaware); tier 1 takes damage + bleeding save-ends, tier 2 takes damage + bleeding EoT, tier 3 no effect. **While any enemy is bleeding from the snare, every gnoll in the encounter gains an edge on strikes against them.** This is the map-wide buff for 3 Malice — drop early, it pays for itself all fight.
- **Bloodpool (5 Malice).** One gnoll minion explodes into a pool of blood, painting a 3-square area. Any gnoll starting their turn in the pool deals +5 damage on their next strike that turn. Once per round, an abyssal hyena starting their turn in the pool transforms into a gnoll marauder at their current Stamina. Sacrifice a minion to convert a hyena into a harrier + buff the pack's damage through a zone — a tempo recovery button when the pack has been whittled down.
- **Echoes of Laughter (7 Malice).** Until end of encounter, the map is drowned in cackling and howling. Each enemy takes a bane on the first power roll they make each round. Whenever a gnoll is killed, this effect is suppressed until the start of the next round. Map-wide suppression on the heroes' first action each round — devastating in long fights, but killing any gnoll breathes the party a round of clean air.

## 1st Echelon

All statted gnolls in the book are 1st echelon (Level 2). Combine minions and horde freely; the Cackletongue chain works as long as at least one non-minion is on the map.

### Abyssal Hyena — minion

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 2, minion, abyssal, animal, gnoll
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 154
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to speed.

**Flavor:** The tagalong hyena pack. Size 1M, Stamina 7, Speed 8. Natural predators, not transformed — they're the raw material the gnoll casters upgrade mid-fight.

**Tactics:** **Snapjaw** (Melee 1, one target per minion) grabs at 17+. **Death Snap** — when the hyena drops to 0 Stamina, they make a melee free strike before dying. The fastest minion on the board (Speed 8, Captain'd to 10), so use hyenas as swarm chaff that arrives first, pins with grabs at 17+, and leaves a free strike behind when picked off. Bloodpool and Summoner's Cackletongue both convert a hyena into a gnoll marauder at current Stamina — keep a hyena or two in reserve to promote mid-fight.

### Gnoll Chainflail — minion

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 2, minion, abyssal, gnoll
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 154
**With Captain:** +1 damage bonus to strikes.

**Flavor:** Ranged minion swinging a weighted chain at distance. Size 1M, Stamina 4, Speed 5.

**Tactics:** **Chain Shotput** (Ranged 8, one target per minion) pushes 1/2/3 per tier. **Death Circle** lets them shift to speed when an ally drops. The cheap ranged-push minion — four chainflails can chip and reposition a single hero 4 squares backwards in one round, pulling them out of the front line into a Marauder or Bonesplitter. Counterplay: close the distance and kill them; they're fragile.

### Gnoll Mage Mauler — minion

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 2, minion, abyssal, gnoll
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 154
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to melee distance.

**Flavor:** Anti-caster skirmisher. Size 1M, Stamina 4, Speed 5.

**Tactics:** **Wizard Ripper** (Melee 1, one target per minion) is a multi-damage-type strike: tier 1 acid, tier 2 cold, tier 3 lightning *and the target can't use magic abilities EoT*. Effect clause always adds a bane on the target's next power roll. The dedicated caster-hate minion — four mage maulers who can all swing at a wizard on a 2-Malice Captain's captain-reach are a caster's worst opening turn. Pair with a Bonesplitter or Marauder to finish while the mage can't answer with spells. Counterplay: ready a teleport or reaction shift.

### Gnoll Wildling — minion

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 2, minion, abyssal, gnoll
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 154
**With Captain:** gain an edge on strikes.

**Flavor:** Feral flailer. Size 1M, Stamina 5, Speed 7.

**Tactics:** **Flail** (Melee 1, one target per minion) — and at tier 3 the wildling gets a *free strike against a creature adjacent to the target*. Built to bounce around a tight hero formation. **Death Circle** shift-to-speed on a non-minion ally's death (standard minion version). Good in groups of 4 next to a Bonesplitter or Marauder, because the ally's Cackletongue chain hands out free strikes the wildling can capitalize on.

### Gnoll Abyssal Archer

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 2, horde, abyssal, gnoll
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 155

**Flavor:** Corpse-tainted dark longbow archer. Size 1M, Stamina 15, Speed 5.

**Tactics:** **Dark Longbow** (Ranged 10) deals corruption damage; slows at 17+ on M<2; **gains an edge against any target not at full Stamina** — so the first hit sets up every subsequent one. **Archer's Cackletongue (2 Malice, 2-burst, Maneuver)** grants each ally in the area an edge on their next strike, plus the standard chain-free-Cackletongue clause. **Bloodscent** — doesn't need line of effect against any non-full-Stamina creature as long as a size-1 gap exists between archer and target. **Distant Death Frenzy** — when a non-minion ally falls within 5 squares, the archer makes a *ranged* free strike instead of melee. The back-line harassment centerpiece: first-round chip + permanent edge + no-LOS follow-up. Counterplay: stay at full Stamina and in full cover, or burst them down; a pair of archers setting up on round 1 is miserable.

### Gnoll Abyssal Summoner

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 2, horde, abyssal, gnoll
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 155

**Flavor:** Fire-throwing caster who pulls hyenas up through the ground. Size 1M, Stamina 20, Speed 5.

**Tactics:** **Flame Wad** (Ranged 5) fire damage; on I<2 at tier 3 the target is burning (save ends), taking 1d6 at the start of each turn. **Call Abyssal Hyenas (3 Malice, Ranged 5, Maneuver)** summons two hyenas into unoccupied squares within distance — the infinite-chaff tap. **Summoner's Cackletongue (4 Malice, 2-burst, Maneuver)** turns one abyssal hyena in the area into a gnoll marauder at current Stamina, plus the standard Cackletongue chain clause. **Death Frenzy** horde-version triggers. The summoner's job: turn one nearby hyena into a marauder through Cackletongue, call two more hyenas with Call Abyssal Hyenas next turn, repeat. The Bloodpool Malice feature is the third axis — three ways to convert hyenas into gnolls. Counterplay: kill the summoner fast; don't leave hyenas alive near them.

### Gnoll Bonesplitter

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 2, horde, abyssal, gnoll
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 156

**Flavor:** The grappling brute with a three-tail flail. Size 1L, Stamina 25, Speed 5.

**Tactics:** **Three-Tail Flail** (Melee 1) pushes 2 at every tier and at tier 3 also grabs and imposes a bane on the target's Escape Grab maneuver. While a target is grabbed, the bonesplitter can only use Three-Tail Flail against *that* target — commits to the lock. **Bonesplitter's Cackletongue (4 Malice, 2-burst, Maneuver)** — up to three allies in the area make free strikes, with the standard chain clause. This is the big pack-wide Cackletongue payoff: Bonesplitter grabs the hero, fires the Cackletongue, three nearby gnolls free-strike the pinned target. Counterplay: break the grab (bane on Escape is real — get help); prevent the Cackletongue by dropping the Bonesplitter under 1-shot range before their turn.

### Gnoll Cackler

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 2, horde, abyssal, gnoll
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 156

**Flavor:** Psionic hyena-laugher turning heroes against each other. Size 1S, Stamina 15, Speed 5.

**Tactics:** **Moment of Brutality** (Ranged 8) is the mind-control strike — tier 1 forces a free strike against a creature of the cackler's choice (on I<0), tier 2 ditto at I<1, tier 3 *forces the target to use a signature ability against a creature of the cackler's choice* (on I<2). Also: an ally targeted by Moment of Brutality ignores the damage and gets a free strike. **Cackler's Cackletongue (4 Malice, 2-burst, Maneuver)** makes each enemy in the area Intuition-test or be frightened save-ends/EoT, plus the standard chain. **Death Frenzy**. The Cackler is *the* reason this book has a "Heroes Beware" warning — hitting a fighter with tier 3 Moment of Brutality turns their signature ability on their own party. Counterplay: screen the cackler with a defender so they can't land the strike, or ready a counter-spell that targets ranged magic.

### Gnoll Marauder

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 2, horde, abyssal, gnoll
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 157

**Flavor:** The hunt-pack skirmisher. Size 1M, Stamina 20, Speed 7.

**Tactics:** **Fury Flail** (Melee 1) damages and at tier 3 knocks prone + bleeds save-ends on A<2. **2+ Malice rider: targets one additional target per 2 Malice spent** — cheap AoE upgrade. **Marauder's Cackletongue (3 Malice, 2-burst, Maneuver)** shifts each ally up to their speed, plus the chain clause. This is the repositioner of the horde — combine with a Bonesplitter's grab-and-free-strike or an Archer's edge to set up a full pack strike. Marauders are also what a Bloodpool / Summoner's Cackletongue upgrades a hyena into mid-fight; budget for the transformation when planning the encounter.

### Tusker Demon — elite

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 2, elite, abyssal, demon, gnoll
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 157

**Flavor:** Size 3 elephantine demon, Stamina 100, Speed 7, Stability 3. No face — just a crown of tusks around a ravening maw. Not technically a gnoll, but marches with the pack; carries the Demon keyword and the associated **Lethe** clause (edge on strikes in *and* out when winded).

**Tactics:** **Gore** (Charge, Melee 2, two targets) pushes 1/2/3 and at tier 3 proning. If used as part of the Charge main action, adds +4 damage. **Vengeful Tusker** (triggered action) — when an enemy within 7 deals damage, the tusker immediately uses the Charge + Gore combo against them. Extremely dangerous reaction — punishes ranged focus fire, which is otherwise the correct answer to every tusker. **Trample** — moves through enemy squares at normal speed, deals 5 damage to each creature whose space they enter for the first time that turn, and can end their turn in a prone size-1 creature's space to prevent standing. **Lethe** (winded = edge both directions). Counterplay: everything the tusker does is movement-based, so slow, immobilize, or restrain *before* they get to full charge length. Don't group up — two targets on a Gore charge is the design.

### Gnoll Carnage — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 2, leader, abyssal, gnoll
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 158

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 100, Speed 5, Stability 1, Free Strike 5. A gnoll who has eaten enough to evolve — the alpha. The only gnoll in the pack that dying doesn't actually stop (see Endless Hunger).

**Tactics:** **Shrapnel Whip** (Signature, Melee 2, two targets) damages with bleeding save-ends escalating across tiers (tier 3: 14 damage + bleeding & dazed save-ends). An *ally* targeted by this ignores the damage and makes a free strike — the carnage can sweep their own pack into free hits while damaging heroes. **Carnage's Cackletongue (4 Malice, 5-burst, Maneuver)** gives each ally +3 damage on their next strike until the carnage's next turn, plus the chain clause — the pack-wide damage pump that triggers every other Cackletongue on the map in one round. **Death Rampage** (when a non-minion ally within 5 drops) — carnage shifts to speed, then either free-strikes two creatures or fires a full Shrapnel Whip. **End Effect** — at end of each turn, can take 5 unresistable damage to end one save-ends effect. **Endless Hunger** — *if the carnage is reduced to 0 while gnolls remain on the map, one gnoll on the map transforms into a carnage at their current Stamina.* The leader respawns as long as any gnoll is alive.

Villain actions escalate through the fight:
- **Call Up From the Abyss (VA1).** Summons four abyssal hyenas into unoccupied squares within 10. Refills the board mid-fight; fresh hyenas are fuel for Bloodpool, Call Abyssal Hyenas chains, and Summoner's Cackletongue.
- **Edacity (VA2).** Self and three allies each move up to their speed and make a free strike; damaged targets with M<2 are knocked prone. Mass-reposition-and-strike — the pack's big move action.
- **Deepest Wounds (VA3).** Presence test from each winded enemy in a 5-burst: tier 1, *can't regain Stamina until end of encounter*; tier 2, can't regain Stamina save-ends. And **for the rest of the encounter, all gnolls have a double edge on power rolls that target a winded enemy.** The fight-ender. If heroes are winded when this lands, there is no recovery path — burst the carnage before VA3.

Counterplay: (1) kill the last gnoll on the board before killing the carnage, so Endless Hunger has no target. (2) Burst the carnage in one round — End Effect means they'll never stay debuffed. (3) Stay above winded until after VA3 would resolve.
