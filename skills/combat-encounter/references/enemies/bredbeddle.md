# Bredbeddle

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 50–51. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

In their true forms, bredbeddles are ogre-sized headless giants. Forever searching for their missing heads, they are drawn to populous lands, where they decapitate passers-by and magically adopt their victims' forms. Although they have no traditional sensory organs, they are keenly aware of their surroundings. Some lurk along dark country lanes in their monstrous true forms; others walk disguised among crowds, dropping the facade only long enough to waylay a stranger and lop off their head.

- **Immortal Wanderers.** Bredbeddles don't age, and if slain, their bodies reform a few days later. Long ago, they were giants who stood high among the nobility of giant kind, until they declared themselves rulers of all giants — their kin thwarted the coup and beheaded them. Now these fallen nobles roam the world searching for their missing heads, each wielding the axe that decapitated them. Tales tell of the heads being kept in a fire giant's cellar to be returned after an indeterminate period of good behavior, which no bredbeddle has yet exhibited.
- **Green with Frenzy.** The longer a bredbeddle goes without a head, the more twisted and desperate their power grows. Their desire to be whole stretches out of their body as shadowy dark-green ribbons, wrapping and clinging to every surface they spill onto. Travelers warn of roads haunted by "wild green knights," often avoiding those routes for months.
- **Languages.** A bredbeddle communicates in High Kuric or the languages of their current head's previous owner. Long-lived ones who have worn many heads likely know Caelian as well.

## Head mechanics

The bredbeddle's entire kit pivots on **heads** — theirs and yours.

- **Heady or Not (trait).** While headless, the bredbeddle can pick up a severed head as a main action and physically transform into that head's original owner — appearance, size, weight, stability all change. If the new form is size 1L or smaller, their melee and burst distances drop by 1. Effect lasts until killed, beheaded, or 24 hours pass (after which the head falls off and can never be reattached to this bredbeddle). Ripping a head off requires a **hard Might test** as a maneuver, and failure eats a free strike.
- **Resilient Form (trait).** Nothing except Heady or Not can physically transform the bredbeddle — immune to polymorph-style shenanigans.
- **Lop (3 Malice, Melee 2 signature).** The bleeding rider is the headline: on tier, targets with insufficient Might (M<1 / M<2 / M<3) are **beheaded outright**. Beheaded heroes stay alive but are bleeding, have line of effect only to adjacent squares, and die after 24 hours if they can't reattach. A beheaded target can reattach as a maneuver by entering their head's square. The severed head lands adjacent to the bredbeddle — and feeds into Heady or Not or Headway.
- **Headway (5 Malice, Ranged 20 maneuver).** Throws a head they possess (their own or someone else's) at a target for 9/13/16 damage plus dazed and (at ≥12–16) prone. If it was their own attached head, they're left headless afterward — which enables Scramble and the headless-only pressure but turns off most of their base kit.
- **Scramble (maneuver, headless only).** Shift up to speed; push any creature they become adjacent to 1 square; every square they exit becomes difficult terrain. This is how a headless bredbeddle reaches the next severed head.

## Bredbeddle Malice features

At the start of the bredbeddle's turn, Malice can be spent to activate one of the following.

- **Engreen (3 Malice).** Axe lengthens and turns ghastly green: +1 distance to melee strikes and burst dimensions until end of turn. Cheap range extension — pairs with Executioner's Swing (2 burst → 3 burst) to catch an extra hero.
- **Shadow Stockade (5 Malice).** Ghostly size-1L stockade appears in an unoccupied square within 10. Any size 1L or smaller enemy entering is **restrained (save ends)**; when they stop being restrained, the stockade fades. At the start of each of the bredbeddle's turns, they can slide the stockade and its occupant up to 5 squares — a mobile prison that can drag a hero into Lop range.
- **Solo Action (5 Malice).** Take an additional main action this turn, even while dazed. The dazed clause matters: the End Effect only clears save-ends conditions, so Solo Action is the escape hatch when a controller is stapling debuffs on.
- **Green Phantom (7 Malice).** A phantom bredbeddle phases in anywhere on the map, takes one turn, and vanishes at end of round. Same stats but 24 Stamina, corruption immunity 10, can fly; uses any non-villain ability. Only one phantom at a time. Best used to flank or reach an isolated backliner.

## Bredbeddle

**Role:** (solo — no additional role)
**Tags:** level 3, solo, EV 60, bredbeddle, giant
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 51

**Flavor:** An ogre-sized headless giant in bloody plate, wielding the same axe that once beheaded them, skulls hanging from their belt. Size 2, Speed 5, Stamina 300, Stability 4, Might +3 / Presence +2 / Reason –3. **Solo Turns** — two turns per round, not consecutive. **End Effect** — at the end of each turn, they can take 5 unresistable damage to end one save-ends effect on themselves.

**Tactics:** The encounter is a **decapitation puzzle**, not a damage race. Open with **Challenge** (Villain Action 2, Ranged 5): point at the party face and make them choose — refuse and turn green (double edge on all the bredbeddle's rolls against you for the encounter), or accept and make an unmodified Might test where a bottom-tier result beheads you outright and a top-tier lets you trade 40 damage for their head. Follow up with **Turn Green** (VA1, 3 burst) to stack green on the cluster, then **Executioner's Swing** (2-burst signature, 3 Malice lets it shift 2 and hit anyone it reaches during the shift) to spread bleed. **Lop** is the finisher on anyone the bredbeddle has dropped Might on — combo: Shadow Stockade to restrain, slide into Melee 2, Lop for guaranteed behead on M<2. **Headlam Rampage** (VA3, Melee 2, four creatures) is the cleanup — bleed on every target and beheadings on anyone the bredbeddle has pressured Agility down. **Envious Imitation** (2 Malice triggered) turns ranged artillery against itself by copying the triggering ranged ability back at the attacker with *their* bonus. Once headless, the bredbeddle pivots: **Scramble** toward the nearest severed head (including one the party beheaded!), reattach via Heady or Not, resume Lop pipeline. **Green Phantom** is the panic button when heroes are out of Lop range — phase one onto the backline for a free turn. Counterplay: keep Might-boosting buffs up on the front line, cleanse green quickly (save ends — feed it Reason/Intuition debuffs to compress the save window), *don't refuse Challenge*, stabilize anyone who gets beheaded (24-hour timer — pick the head up as a maneuver and reattach by entering its square), focus-fire between the solo's two turns to force End Effect trades, and disrupt Shadow Stockade positioning with forced movement before the bredbeddle can slide it.
