# Undead

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 259–274. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book. 1st Echelon entries are detailed; 2nd–4th Echelon entries are descriptions only.*

Some are mindless soldiers raised by a necromancer; others rise when bitter death meets cursed ground; a rare few make the *choice* to become something beyond mortal. All were living once. All now walk in defiance of the natural order. A rumor of a ghoul pack sends entire villages into panic; an open grave in the churchyard or a translucent spirit on a street is reason enough to hire adventurers immediately.

> **Content Warning: Body Horror.** The undead entries reference grisly body horror and viscera. Soften descriptions to spectral or clinical alternatives if it helps the table.

- **Corporeal vs. spectral.** The two main families. Corporeal undead are physical bodies animated by necromantic magic (zombies, skeletons, ghouls, vampires, mummies, liches). Spectral undead are malice-given-form, born from traumatic death or necromantic will (shades, specters, wraiths, haunts, ghosts). Spectral undead have **Phasing** — they can move through creatures and objects at their usual speed (but can't end a combat turn inside them) and deal corruption damage when they pass through a creature the first time per round.
- **Encountered together.** A necromancer raises zombies, skeletons, and ghouls for guard duty because each serves a different function; a lich keeps wraiths as messengers and soulwights as lab assistants; an ancient tomb has mummies and vampires. Undead don't eat/sleep/breathe and share an affinity for lightless places, so mixed-type encounters are the norm.
- **Dark places.** Sunlight is a nuisance — rarely harmful, but bright to dead eyes and revealing to fearful mortals. Most undead lair in tombs, ruins, and dungeons and only stalk the surface at night.
- **Arise (most 1st–2nd echelon undead).** The first time they drop to 0 Stamina from damage that isn't **fire** or **holy** (and their body isn't destroyed), they come back at 1 Stamina, prone. Zombies and giant zombies return with 10 / 50 Stamina. Burn bodies or bring holy damage — otherwise every horde-tier undead gets a second life.
- **Resistance profile.** All undead carry corruption and poison immunity scaling with level (1 at Echelon 1, 4 at Echelon 2, 7 at Echelon 3, 10 at Echelon 4). Most are unbothered by cold. Mummies have **Fire Weakness 5.** Vampires (in optional rules) take damage from sunlight.
- **Languages.** Most undead speak (or at least understand) the languages they knew in life.

## Shared traits

- **Corruption + poison immunity scaling by Echelon.** Plan around this — parties leaning on those damage types do reduced work.
- **Phasing on spectral undead.** All spectral undead ignore creatures and terrain during movement, and most deal corruption-on-pass-through.
- **Arise (1st & 2nd Echelon).** Most corporeal horde undead get one free revival at 1 Stamina (zombies: 10 / giant zombies: 50). Fire and holy damage deny it.

## Undead Malice features

Tiered by undead level — a higher-level undead spending Malice can use any prior tier's feature via the **Prior Malice Features** 2–7+ Malice entry.

### Level 1+

- **Ravenous Horde (2 Malice).** At the end of the round, each hero not already adjacent to an undead is beset by two rotting zombies who burst up from the ground into adjacent unoccupied spaces. Each zombie is winded. Can't be used two rounds in a row. The map-reset button that turns any "I stepped away" move into a fresh wave of chaff.
- **Paranormal Fling (3 Malice).** Up to three unattended objects on the map rise 1 square and are pulled 5 squares toward the nearest enemy within 3. Dungeon dressing (candlesticks, broken statues, loose flagstones) becomes ammunition. Lean on whatever the GM seeded on the map.
- **The Grasping, the Hungry (5 Malice).** Ravenous rotting arms burst from 9 connected squares of a surface (vertical or horizontal — walls work). Any creature ending their turn adjacent makes an Agility test: ≤11 5 damage + restrained save-ends, 12–16 5 damage + restrained EoT, 17+ 5 damage. Restrained targets take 1d6/turn. Zone denial tuned to the party's backline shape.
- **Dread March (7+ Malice).** Up to four undead move up to their speed and make a free strike. +1 undead per additional Malice. If an undead is reduced to 0 Stamina during the march, they don't die until the march resolves — so injured undead get one last swing guaranteed. The "close the distance with the party" button for any round the heroes hang back.

### Level 4+

- **Blood Hunger (5 Malice).** One undead uses a signature ability against a bleeding creature; as a free triggered action, each undead within 5 moves up to speed and can make a free strike against the same target. Focus-fire engine: one bleed-tag, then every nearby undead piles on.

### Level 7+

- **Necrotic Rupture (5 Malice).** Until end of round, any undead reduced to 0 Stamina deals 8 corruption to each enemy within 3. Converts the party's kills into area damage — spend when multiple undead are close to dying and clustered near heroes.

### Level 10+

- **Death Tax (7 Malice, non-minion).** Each enemy within 5 makes a Might test: ≤11 10 corruption + lose 2 Recoveries, 12–16 8 corruption + lose 1 Recovery, 17+ 5 corruption. A target with insufficient Recoveries is *also* weakened save-ends. A Recovery-economy attack — 4th Echelon undead don't just kill heroes, they erode their resources across the whole adventuring day.

## 1st Echelon

Low-level undead come in droves. Skeletons, ghouls, specters, and shades can overwhelm heroes through sheer numbers. A single zombie rarely appears alone — bank on 8–12 minions/horde plus a leader.

### Crawling Claw — minion

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, undead, soulless
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 261
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to speed.

**Flavor:** 1T severed hand with Climb movement, Stamina 4, Speed 6, A +2. The lingering fragment of a dead necromantic experiment.

**Tactics:** **Fingernails** (Melee 1) chips damage, and **the claw shifts a number of squares equal to the damage dealt**. A single tier-3 hit (3 damage) lets the claw relocate 3 squares for free — essentially strike-and-retreat built into every swing. **Disorganized:** allies can't flank with it. They're map-presence chaff; swarm in from walls (Climb) to threaten the backline without committing real melee resources.

### Decrepit Skeleton — minion

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, undead, soulless
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 261
**With Captain:** edge on strikes.

**Flavor:** Standard bow-skeleton minion, Stamina 3, Speed 5, A +2.

**Tactics:** **Bone Bow** (Ranged 10) deals low damage plus *a second target within distance takes 1 damage*. Four decrepit skeletons firing each round means eight targets hit for chip damage. **Bonetrops:** when destroyed, their space becomes difficult terrain, and the first enemy entering takes 1 damage. Leaves a trail of board-hazard squares behind the pack as heroes cut them down.

### Rotting Zombie — minion

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, undead, soulless
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 261
**With Captain:** +1 damage bonus to strikes.

**Flavor:** Slow-moving corpse. Size 1M, Stamina 5, Speed 4, M +2.

**Tactics:** **Rotting Fist** (Melee 1) — tier 3 prones small targets or slows larger ones save-ends on M<2. **Death Grasp:** when destroyed, their space is difficult terrain; the first enemy entering with M<2 is slowed save-ends. The "you cleared them, now walking through the bodies taxes you" minion. Pair with Ravenous Horde Malice to replenish these on demand.

### Shade — minion

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, undead
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 261
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to speed.
**Movement:** Fly, hover.

**Flavor:** Living darkness. Stamina 4, Speed 5, P +2.

**Tactics:** **Life Drain** (Melee 1) deals corruption; tier 3 forces the target to *move up to their speed and can't end closer to any shade*. This is crowd-control by running-away — shades push heroes across the map. **Shadow Phasing** lets them move through creatures (1 corruption pass-through). Use them to scatter the party formation, then let corporeal minions pick off the stragglers.

### Ghoul — horde

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, horde, EV 3, undead
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 262

**Flavor:** 1M corpse-eater. Stamina 15, Speed 7, A +2.

**Tactics:** **Razor Claws** (Charge, Melee 1) — tier 3 bleeding save-ends on M<2. **Leap** (Maneuver): jump 3 squares; if landing on a Size 1 enemy, that enemy is proned and the ghoul free-strikes. **Hunger:** +2 speed on Charge turns. A ghoul can Charge-leap-strike across 12+ squares to slam the party's backline, prone-stunning the target and opening Blood Hunger Malice. **Arise** means a free resurrection at 1 Stamina unless killed with fire/holy.

### Skeleton — horde

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, horde, EV 3, undead, soulless
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 262

**Flavor:** Stamina 10, Speed 5, A +2. The classic archer-skeleton upgraded to a horde unit.

**Tactics:** **Bone Shards** (Melee 1 or Ranged 10) — damage plus *target takes 2 damage the first time they willingly move*. Movement-tax on melee heroes. **Bone Spur** (2 Malice, 1-burst Maneuver) damages + M<0/1/2 bleeding save-ends *and* each target takes a bane on next strike. The "lock down a cluster" button. **Arise** applies.

### Specter — horde

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 1, horde, EV 3, undead
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 262
**Movement:** Fly, hover.

**Flavor:** Stamina 10, Speed 5, P +2. Translucent malice.

**Tactics:** **Decaying Touch** (Melee 1, magic, corruption) weakens P<0/1/2 save-ends. **2 Malice upgrade:** potency +1 *and any living creature killed by this damage rises next round as a specter under the Director's control.* The self-replicating weakener — kill heroes with specters and you get more specters. **Hidden Movement:** go invisible, move, become visible — perfect setup for first-strike weakened riders. **Corruptive Phasing** (2 corruption pass-through).

### Soulwight — horde

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 1, horde, EV 3, undead
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 263

**Flavor:** Stamina 10, Speed 5, M +2. Wields a cursed longsword that ages its victims.

**Tactics:** **Soulstealer Longsword** (Melee 1) — corruption damage; tier 2 slows save-ends, tier 3 slows *and* weakens save-ends, all on M-save. **Effect:** the target appears to rapidly age with each hit — cosmetic but horrific (reverses on soulwight death). **Stolen Vitality** (3 Malice, Maneuver, Ranged 5, one ally): ally regains 10 Stamina; soulwight can't use again until they strike a creature with Soulstealer. The minion healer — save for when an ally is about to die. **Arise** applies.

### Umbral Stalker — horde

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 1, horde, EV 3, undead
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 263
**Movement:** Climb.

**Flavor:** Stamina 15, Speed 7, A +2. A shadow-walker built to strike from concealment.

**Tactics:** **Chilling Grasp** (Melee 1) cold damage; tier 2 shift 1, tier 3 shift 2 after the hit. **Freezing Dark** (3 Malice, 3-cube within 1) cold AoE + the area grants concealment and blocks line of effect. Paint a concealment zone, then **Shadow Jump** (1 Malice, free Maneuver) teleports to any concealment within 10. The stalker becomes functionally untargetable by non-adjacent attacks while laying down their own cloud. **Corruptive Phasing**.

### Zombie — horde

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 1, horde, EV 3, undead, soulless
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 264

**Flavor:** Stamina 20, Speed 5, M +2. The classic grabby shambler.

**Tactics:** **Clobber and Clutch** (Melee 1) — tier 3 grabs. **Effect:** a grabbed target takes 2 corruption at start of their turn; **a creature taking 5+ corruption this way becomes insatiably hungry for flesh**, curable only via the *Find a Cure* downtime project in *Draw Steel: Heroes*. Narrative conversion hook — a grabbed-too-long hero becomes a downtime problem. **Zombie Dust** (3 Malice, 2-burst Maneuver) prones the zombie to spit out a rot cloud: corruption damage + weakened/dazed on M-save. **Endless Knight:** returns at 10 Stamina the first time destroyed without fire/holy.

### Ghost — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 1, leader, EV 12, undead
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 264
**Movement:** Fly, hover.

**Flavor:** Stamina 80, Speed 6, Stability 1, Corruption/Poison Immunity 3. The lynchpin of a low-level haunted-house encounter.

**Tactics:** **Heat Death** (Signature, Ranged 5, two targets, magic) cold damage + slow save-ends on P-save. **Effect:** next strike against the target gains edge — hands the pack a free edge on the ghost's mark every turn. **Haunt** (Maneuver, Ranged 8, self or phasing ally) shifts the target up to speed; 2 Malice = one extra target. The formation-mover that keeps the phasing allies in ideal pass-through positions. **Shriek** (1 Malice, Triggered) halves incoming strike damage + 2 sonic back. **Phantom Flow:** each phasing undead within 10 can't be slowed or weakened — the ghost bubble-protects the whole spectral line. **Corruptive Phasing** (2 corruption pass-through).

Villain actions:
- **Paranormal Activity (VA1).** 5-burst; each Size 3 or smaller object rises 1 square and vertically pulls up to 5 toward the nearest enemy within 3. Area-wide improvised attacks from the environment.
- **Spirited Away (VA2).** 5-burst; each enemy with P<1/P<2/P<3 is *levitated* EoT (tier 3 lasts whole encounter). Levitated heroes fly 1 square higher at end of each turn and, if they can't already fly, are slowed and weakened while floating. The "pull three heroes into the air useless" button.
- **Awful Wail (VA3).** 5-burst; sonic damage + *a target with P<2 who is winded after this is reduced to 1 Stamina.* The finisher for any hero already low.

Counterplay: holy and fire damage deny Arise on all the ghost's ally pack; ranged attacks bypass the Heat Death → edge chain; high Presence resists Spirited Away's levitation and Awful Wail's execute.

## 2nd Echelon (descriptions only)

More powerful and self-aware forms rise above the horde and claim isolated domains. The longer an undead thrives, the stronger it becomes. Includes **mummies** (great heroes voluntarily preserved to guard tombs, or great villains denied death), **vampire spawn** (newly cursed blood-drinkers still close to their mortal selves, driven by thirst and a master), and **mournlings** (sorrow-and-rage earth/flesh constructs built by long-dead druids to defend natural places, keening even while they beat enemies to pulp).

- **Fleshflayed Shambler Zombie** (Level 4 Minion Brute, EV 6 for four) — Bone Carvers (Melee 1) deals damage; edge/double-edge adds bleeding save-ends. **Fleshfused Spines:** adjacent enemies who grab or melee-attack take 2 damage back.
- **Ghoul Craver** (Level 4 Minion Harrier, EV 6 for four) — Taste (Charge, Melee 1) damage; **double edge against a bleeding target**. **Ever So Hungry:** enemy adjacent to 3+ cravers can't shift. The bleed-focus pack that locks grabbed/bleeding heroes in place.
- **Hollowbone Launcher** (Level 4 Minion Artillery, EV 6 for four) — Hollowbone Slug (Ranged 10) tier 3 bleeds; **Effect:** each creature adjacent to the target takes 2 damage (splash). **Brittle Revenge:** explodes for 2 damage to adjacent enemies when destroyed.
- **Flesh Mournling** (Level 4 Horde Defender, EV 6) — Multiarm Strike (Melee 2) damage + target can't shift EoT. **Horrid Wail** (5-burst) psychic + frightened save-ends; a target still frightened at encounter end can't take a respite activity next respite — narrative cost past the fight. **Immutable Form** (shape can't be altered), **Arise** at 10 Stamina.
- **Giant Zombie** (Level 4 Elite Brute, EV 24) — Size 3, Stamina 140. Rotten Smash (Melee 3, two targets) grabs on tier 2/3; **Knocking Heads** (1 Malice, Triggered when grabbing two targets or starting turn with two grabbed): smashes them together with a double-edge Rotten Smash. **Endless Knight** returns at 50 Stamina. **Negative Nerves:** halves tier-1 rolled damage.
- **Mummy** (Level 4 Horde Brute, EV 6) — Fire Weakness 5. Accursed Bindings (Melee 3) corruption + pull + tier-3 restrain save-ends; **Effect:** the mummy's next ability against the target has +1 potency. **Eldritch Curse** (3 Malice, Ranged 10) *cursed* save-ends — bleeding, weakened, and allies edge against them. **Blast of Mummy Dust** (1 Malice, Triggered) — 8 poison to a restrained creature the mummy moves near.
- **Vampire Spawn** (Level 4 Horde Harrier, EV 6) — Climb. Exsanguinating Bite (Melee 1) corruption + bleed + **regain Stamina equal to corruption dealt**. **Vampiric Celerity** (Maneuver) shift-1-then-move + edge on next ability. **Unslakable Bloodthirst:** speed 10 while any creature within 10 is bleeding, and *must* target bleeding creatures on their turn. Drafting-predator — once a hero is bleeding, the whole vampire pack converges.
- **Wraith** (Level 4 Horde Hexer, EV 6) — Fly, hover. Chilling Gravetouch (Melee 1, magic) cold + slow save-ends; **Effect:** *any living creature killed by this rises next round as a ghoul craver under the Director's control.* Kill-to-recruit conversion. **Hidden Movement** (invisible-move-visible), **Stolen Vitality** (1 Malice, Triggered when an enemy regains Stamina): the target regains *half* and the wraith takes the rest. **Agonizing Phasing** (5 corruption + bane on next strike).
- **Mummy Lord** (Level 4 Leader, EV 24) — Stamina 155, Fire Weakness 5. Accursed Slam (Melee 1, two targets) bleeding save-ends *and* all subsequent abilities have +1 potency against bleeding targets. **Binding Curse** (1 Malice, Ranged 20) frightened save-ends with psychic penalty on move actions. **Summon My Guard** (2 Malice, Triggered when first winded): one mummy or four ghoul cravers appear within 10. **Cursed Transference** end-effect clears a save-ends for 10 unresistable (5 Malice transfers it to another creature). Villain actions: Plague of Flies (5-burst poison), Land's Guardian (+2 speed + auto-burrow + surface-breach prones), Unbound Horrors (5-burst corruption + tier-3 frightened *and* restrained save-ends). The signature 2nd-Echelon boss for desert/tomb encounters.

## 3rd Echelon (descriptions only)

Older, taboo methods — accursed blood rituals, terrible operations, collective resistance to death — produce 3rd Echelon undead that wildly vary from one another. Key subgroups: **koptourok** (Variac "dead tourist" — suffocation victims risen desperate for breath), **haunts** (mass-death spirits stranded together in grief-turned-violence), **waxen** (corpse-wax enhanced minions often set ablaze as sacrificial accelerants), and **vampires / vampire lords** (true vampires grown past their spawn, and the lords who command territory).

- **Blood-Starved Vampire** (Level 7 Minion Harrier, EV 9 for four) — Climb. Feeding Frenzy (Melee 1) tier 3 bleeding EoT; *if target is already bleeding, prone + can't stand until end of next turn.* **Unslakable Bloodthirst** (speed 10 around bleeding creatures; forced to attack them).
- **Faded Echo Spirit** (Level 7 Minion Hexer, EV 9 for four) — Fly, hover. Hollow Grasp (Melee 1) corruption + weakened on P-save; **the weakened ends if the target ends their turn with no spirit within 5** — forces heroes to choose between retreating from the spirit pack (weakness cleared) or engaging and taking more hits. **Corruptive Phasing** (4 corruption pass-through).
- **Mummy Rotwrap** (Level 7 Minion Brute, EV 9 for four) — Fire Weakness 5. Fetid Wrappings (Melee 2) tier 2 pull 1, tier 3 pull 3; **Effect:** each ally has edge on strikes against the target until end of round. Pair with ghoul cravers or blood-starved vampires to turn the edge into bleeds.
- **Dirt Mournling** (Level 7 Horde Controller, EV 9) — Burrow, Climb. Mudslide (Melee 2) damage + grab; **Effect:** a 3-cube centered on the target becomes difficult terrain for enemies. **Mourning Cry** (3 Malice, 3-burst) corruption + frightened save-ends of *all* undead (ends early if this mournling dies). **Rupture:** Dig-maneuver breach makes free strikes against each adjacent enemy. **Arise at 15 Stamina.**
- **Haunt** (Level 7 Horde Controller, EV 9) — Fly, hover. Lash Out (Melee 3) damage + slide 2/3/5; **Effect:** force-moving into another creature deals +4 damage + slide 2 to that creature. **Crushing Despair** (3 Malice, 3-burst) damage + prone; **prone-from-this target can't Stand Up while any haunt is within 20.** **Invisible Horror:** phases *and* is invisible while using a move action. Chain lashes to smash heroes into each other across the map; keep them proned indefinitely at range.
- **Koptourok** (Level 7 Horde Hexer, EV 9) — Choking Grasp (Melee 5) damage + grab; **a grabbed creature is suffocating**; can hold two at once. **Inhale** (3 Malice, 5-burst) pulls 3/5/7; **Effect:** edge against grabbed targets, and *if any target pulled adjacent, the koptourok can fly until end of encounter.* The breath-stealer — heroes grabbed by this won't benefit from auras/resistances gated on breathing. **Exhale:** first time made winded, 8 corruption burst to enemies within 3 + weakened on M<3.
- **Waxen** (Level 7 Horde Artillery, EV 9) — Wax Fling (Ranged 15) damage + tier-3 slowed save-ends; *already-slowed target is restrained save-ends instead.* **Burn Bright:** fire damage *ignites* the waxen — +4 fire at start of each turn *and* +4 fire damage on strikes. **Erupt** (3 Malice, 2-burst): if ignited, shift up to speed then burst; destroys the waxen and leaves difficult terrain. The ally-you-burn sacrificial bomb.
- **Vampire** (Level 7 Horde Hexer, EV 9) — Climb. Exsanguinating Bite (Melee 1) bleed + tier-3 extra corruption; regains Stamina equal to corruption dealt. **Vicious Pursuit** (3 Malice, Melee 1) slows save-ends; if bleeding, shift-speed first. **Reactive Charm** (2 Malice, Triggered when attacked, Ranged 5): redirect the strike onto another enemy. **Unslakable Bloodthirst.** Core vampire kit — hunt bleeders, charm redirect onto party members. Optional sunlight rules: **Creature of the Night** regens 25% max Stamina in darkness concealment; **Curse of Day** gives damage weakness 5 in direct sun until they re-shadow.
- **Vampire Lord** (Level 7 Leader, EV 36) — Stamina 200, Speed 12, Climb/Hover/Teleport. Crimson Embrace (Melee 1) corruption + bleed save-ends; *regains Stamina = half damage dealt + ends one save-ends effect.* 2 Malice: shift 3 + additional target per 2 Malice. **Arise, My Children** (2 Malice, Ranged 10 Maneuver): two blood-starved vampires appear. **Redirected Charm** (3 Malice, Triggered, Ranged 5): redirect a strike onto an enemy. **Lord's Bloodthirst:** speed 15 + edge on power rolls while any creature within 20 is bleeding; bleeding creatures within 5 can't hide. Villain actions: Let Us Feast! (20-burst; P<4 is now bleeding save-ends), Red Mist Rising (5-burst + *vampire turns to mist and is untargetable for the round, returns from the area at round end*), Sacrifice (mark allies; at end of round each sacrificial ally takes 50 corruption, then Wave of Blood 20-burst Might test for +5 damage per killed sacrifice). Kingdom-level antagonist with the cleanest "burn sacrificial allies for a map-wide finisher" button in the book.

## 4th Echelon (descriptions only)

The deadliest fall into two categories — terrible machines that know only destruction, and powerful tyrants who aren't content with conquering just death. The **bonecage** is a giants-bone living cage that crams corpses inside and trawls battlefields for fallen bodies to repurpose; **lithgekh** ("lich corpse" in the First Language) are failed lichdom aspirants whose souls never found a proper vessel, left hungry for magic and often enslaved by the liches who tempted them into the ritual. Plus **Count Rhodar Von Glauer** and the **Lich** as the capstone solos.

- **Giant Shambler Zombie** (Level 10 Minion Brute, EV 12 for four) — Size 3. Rotten Kick (Melee 2) damage + push 2/4/6 per tier. **Meat Shield:** each ally adjacent has damage immunity 3. Four shamblers clustered around a vampire lord or lich give the whole protection layer.
- **Skeleton Knight** (Level 10 Minion Defender, EV 12 for four) — Size 1L. Four Swords Swing (Melee 1) tier 3 immobilizes EoT. **Bitter Bones:** when destroyed, their space becomes an impassable barrier; adjacent enemies take 5 damage. **More Swings:** any free strike becomes *two* free strikes. Drops a barrier in the formation and doubles every opportunity-attack trigger.
- **Wraith Skulker** (Level 10 Minion Harrier, EV 12 for four) — Fly, hover. Draining Rake (Melee 1) cold + tier 3 slowed save-ends + shift 1/2/3. **Effect:** wraith turns invisible until start of next turn. **Corruptive Phasing** (5 corruption pass-through). Strike, shift, vanish — repeat.
- **Bonecage** (Level 10 Horde Controller, EV 12) — Size 3, Climb. Ribcage Chomp (Melee 2, two targets) damage + grab; **can have up to four Size 1 targets grabbed at once**; grabbed creatures bane their Escape Grab, and the bonecage has damage immunity 5 against their abilities. Force-moving the bonecage moves everyone grabbed. 3 Malice: grabbed targets can't teleport. **Labyrinth of Bone** (5 Malice, four 10×1 lines within 3, up to two 90° bends each): Agility test for 9/7/4 damage; area difficult terrain until it's used again or encounter ends. Terrain-builder plus party-abduction bucket.
- **Lithgekh** (Level 10 Horde Hexer, EV 12) — Fly, hover. Heartstopper (Ranged 15, magic) corruption + frightened save-ends; *a target frightened this way takes a bane on any ability that targets undead.* **Mystic Battery** (1 Malice, Triggered when a creature uses a magic ability within 20): halves the damage/healing; lithgekh regains Stamina equal to the rest. **Devour Magic:** each ally within 10 has edge on magic abilities. Anti-caster + buff-ally engine; a lich with two lithgekh in tow dominates any caster-heavy party.
- **Count Rhodar Von Glauer** (Level 10 Solo, EV 144). The named vampire-count solo — descriptions only from the page range extracted, full stat block on pp. 275+.
- **Lich** (Level 10 Solo, EV 144). The classic phylactery-bound archmage who completed the ritual — the counterpart to the lithgekh's failure. Full stat block on pp. 275+.
