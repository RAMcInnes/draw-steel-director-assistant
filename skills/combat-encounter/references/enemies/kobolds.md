# Kobolds

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 190–195. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Size-1 draconic humanoids who organize in *legions* — disciplined paramilitary communities inspired by imperial Roman army structure, complete with centurions, signifers, and shield walls. Found in every biome: walled cities, secluded temples, cavern-mazes, and (most famously) dragon lairs, where kobolds settle into a wyrm's domain and gradually take on its color and affinity over generations. Most legions just want to be left alone, but one charismatic centurion — or the patronage of a malevolent dragon — flips the switch and a legion becomes a genuine threat: disciplined formations, trap-laced home turf, exploding javelins, and clever use of natural hazards. Kobolds who don't take themselves seriously die fast; the ones who survive are *innovators*.

- **The shield is the soul.** Nearly every kobold in the legion has **Shield? Shield!** — while adjacent to another kobold with the trait, they gain stability 1, have cover, and grant cover to allies. A solo kobold is free to chop; a kobold *pair* is a fortress. Breaking the formation is the whole game — isolate a single kobold before attacking.
- **Legion over individual.** Kobold tactics assume cooperation: shifting allies into position, free strikes handed down a chain, trap triggers synchronized with forced movement. A single kobold is a minor threat; eight of them arranged in a testudo is a puzzle.
- **Terrain mastery.** Kobolds know their ground. A legion fighting on home turf uses their traps *and* the natural ones — beehives, weak ice, rockslides, flammable brush. If kobolds retreat onto a specific tile, assume it's about to kill you.
- **Dragon symbiosis.** A legion living in a dragon's domain grants the dragon faster territorial hold and in return becomes **immune to the domain's hazards**. This is the in-world justification for meteor-kobolds, omen-kobolds, etc., each subtly different from the base kit. GM lever: swap a damage type on the stat blocks for dragon-specific flavor (lightning for thorn, cold for crucible, etc.).
- **Cute pets (terrible ones).** Trained Gummy Bricks (acidic oozes) and Shieldscale Drangolins (draconic pangolins) are the legion's combat companions. The drangolin tunnels into an enemy camp with minions riding along on **Burrow Bond**; the gummy brick hides in plain sight as terrain, then engulfs the first hero who walks past.
- **Languages.** Most kobolds speak Kethaic and understand Caelian.

## Shared traits (nearly every kobold)

- **Shield? Shield!** The core trait. Keep kobold minions and horde units paired whenever possible; design encounter openings so the first hero action must choose between breaking the formation and taking a bad trade.
- **Size 1S, Speed 5-ish.** Most kobolds are small and slow. The legion doesn't outrun the party — it pre-places itself on good terrain and waits.
- **Agility-heavy stat array.** Most kobolds have Agility +1 or +2 and Might 0. Forced movement and stability-based effects slice through them; Agility-save-ends conditions stick.

## Kobold Malice features

Spent at the start of any kobold's turn.

- **Maniple Tactics (3 Malice).** Up to 3 kobolds each make a free strike, swap positions with an adjacent kobold, and then that kobold makes a free strike. A chain of up to six strikes bouncing through the shield line, all while preserving (and rearranging) Shield? Shield! adjacencies. The staple Malice sink.
- **Set the Initiative (5 Malice).** Two kobolds take their turns in a row. Back-to-back Artifex trap-spawn + Sagittarion volley, or Centurion villain action + Venator ambush, or two Legionary taunt locks. Plan for a combo, not two single turns.
- **Shield Wall (7 Malice).** Until end of round, every kobold with Shield? Shield! imposes an additional bane on incoming strikes and abilities. The "party is about to alpha-strike" button — spend right before the brute's big hit lands.

## Minions

### Kobold Princeps — minion

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 1, minion, kobold, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 191
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to Stamina.

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 4, Speed 5. Spear-and-shield support minion.

**Tactics:** **Hasta** (Melee 2) does minor damage, and every swing *shifts one ally within 3 squares up to 2 squares*. Chain princeps strikes to reposition the pack for free — shuffle a sagittarion onto high ground, a legionary into a taunt range, a veles into flank. The princeps's damage is a rounding error; its *movement economy* is the whole point. Pair with Maniple Tactics for a round where the shield line walks into perfect position without spending action points.

### Kobold Tiro — minion

**Role:** defender
**Tags:** level 1, minion, kobold, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 191
**With Captain:** +1 bonus to speed.

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 5, Speed 5. Dagger-and-shield stickler.

**Tactics:** **Pugio** (Melee 1) chips damage; tier 2/3 lets the tiro shift 1–2 after. The rider is the work: **the target can't shift until start of the tiro's next turn**. Stops rogue disengages, bard-dash retreats, monk-flurry escapes — any "I hit and move" build. Four tiros paired with four other minions in Shield? Shield! formation pin the party's frontline in place while the sagittarions and adeptuses chip from range.

### Kobold Sagittarion — minion

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, minion, kobold, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 191
**With Captain:** +5 bonus to ranged distance.

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 3, Speed 5. Composite-bow sniper.

**Tactics:** **Composite Bow** (Ranged 10) — straight damage, and *gains an edge while adjacent to any ally*. The whole point of Shield? Shield! for the sagittarion is that an adjacent ally gives both cover *and* the edge. Park two sagittarions side-by-side with a tiro or princeps — each gives the other the Shield? Shield! cover, and both get edge from adjacency. With Captain +5 range, snipe from 15. Counterplay: close the distance or isolate the sagittarion; an isolated, exposed sagittarion has no edge and no cover.

### Kobold Veles — minion

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, minion, kobold, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 191
**With Captain:** +1 bonus to speed.

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 4, Speed 6. Javelin-throwing skirmisher.

**Tactics:** **Pilium** (Melee 1 or Ranged 5) chips damage with a rider: until start of veles's next turn, *the target can't make opportunity attacks against any kobold*. The disengage enabler for the whole pack — pilium-tag a defender, then the shield line walks around them freely. Throw from 5 squares out to stay safe. Counterplay: lean on area reactions or abilities that don't rely on opportunity attacks (sentinel-style defender stances, mark-on-hit effects).

## Horde

### Kobold Adeptus

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, horde, kobold, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 192

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 10, Speed 5. The legion's wand-caster.

**Tactics:** **Shocking Bolt** (Ranged 15, magic) — lightning damage and *gains edge if the target is adjacent to any enemy; each enemy adjacent to the target also takes 2 lightning*. So the adeptus punishes the kobold flanking game: when tiros/velites have the target boxed in, Shocking Bolt both gets edge *and* sprays 2 lightning onto every kobold in adjacency — which means *the kobolds are immune-or-resistant in a dragon-domain setting* and the heroes aren't. **Arcane Telum** (3 Malice, Ranged 15, three targets, Maneuver) *ignores banes, double banes, and damage immunity.* The answer to the "heavy resistance/shield/cover buff on the hero" meta. Counterplay: kill the adeptus behind the shield line; use abilities that don't care about edge (the Arcane Telum rider doesn't affect the hero's defenses much, but spreading out shuts off Shocking Bolt's edge).

### Kobold Legionary

**Role:** defender
**Tags:** level 1, horde, kobold, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 192

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 20, Speed 5. The centerpiece shield-and-gladius infantry.

**Tactics:** **Gladius** (Melee 5, reach thanks to spear-like extension) deals tier-scaling damage and **taunts EoT on every tier**. The legion's lock-on. **3 Malice, captain rider:** if the legionary is acting as a captain, they and every member of their squad shift up to 2 squares before using Gladius — the whole squad repositions into a new formation and *then* the taunt lands. **Shield Bash** (Maneuver, Melee 1) is push 1 + prone on M<0/1/2 by tier — the kobold answer to a stacked party frontline, shoving the defender back and flattening them. Counterplay: Presence saves against the taunt; break formation from range; high-Might heroes weather Shield Bash's prone rider.

### Kobold Artifex

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 1, horde, kobold, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 192

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 10, Speed 5. The trap engineer.

**Tactics:** **Chain Hook** (Ranged 5) deals damage and pulls 1/2/3. **Rider: if this forced movement triggers a trap that uses a power roll, that roll has a double edge.** The artifex wants one thing in the world: heroes yanked into traps. **Activate Trap** (Maneuver, Ranged 10) instantly triggers any existing trap or terrain object. **3 Malice rider:** the artifex *places* a new trap in the encounter (angry beehives, flammable oil, snare, spike pit per the Dynamic Terrain rules) and triggers it immediately. The GM who preps three traps in the map and three artifices in the pack is running a lethal encounter — traps become ammunition. Counterplay: scout and disarm traps before the fight; watch adjacency to hooks; knock out the artifex before they burn a 3-Malice trap summon.

### Kobold Signifer

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 1, horde, kobold, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 193

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 15, Speed 5. Standard-bearer. The legion's morale engine.

**Tactics:** **Signum** (Melee 1) damages and *shifts one ally within 10 up to their speed if that shift ends adjacent to another ally*. **2+ Malice rider:** one extra ally shifts per 2 Malice — spend 6 Malice and four kobolds rearrange the whole formation for free. **Glory to the Legion** (5 Malice, Maneuver, 5-burst) heals each ally 5 Stamina. **Upholding High Standards** (trait): allies within 5 squares gain +2 speed and +2 damage on strikes until EoT. The damage and speed buff is *always on* — the signifer is the passive-aura centerpiece of any kobold squad. **Critically: if the signifer dies, any kobold minion can pick up the standard and become a signifer themselves.** The legion's battle standard is a *persistent map effect*. Counterplay: kill the signifer, then immediately kill every nearby minion before one grabs the standard.

### Kobold Venator

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 1, horde, kobold, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 193

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 15, Speed 5. The scout/assassin in legion colors.

**Tactics:** **Dolabra and Net** (Melee 1) — solid damage; tier 2/3 restrained save-ends on M<1/M<2. **3 Malice upgrade:** the net is lit on fire — restrained targets take 2 fire per turn. **Lost in the Crowd:** the venator can attempt to hide while adjacent to any non-hiding ally, even while observed. **Not What I Seem:** the venator *starts the encounter disguised as a minion*, and has a **double edge on their first main action**, after which they reveal. The GM's classic trick — place the venator visually indistinguishable from three tiros, and in the first round the "harmless minion" lands a double-edged net restrain on the healer. Counterplay: spread out so the venator can't hide; true-sight / detection on suspect minions; prepare for one nasty opening surprise and mitigate it.

## Elites

### Trained Gummy Brick — elite

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 1, elite, kobold, ooze, soulless
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 194

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 40, Speed 5, Stability 2, Acid Immunity 3. An acidic ooze trained to sit still and eat adjacent creatures.

**Tactics:** **Engulf** (Melee 1) — acid damage with dazed save-ends on A<0/A<1, or **restrained save-ends on tier 3 (A<2)**. A restrained target is *pulled into the brick's space*, moves with the brick, and takes 4 acid at start of each of their turns. Brick can engulf as many creatures as will fit. **2 Malice upgrade:** Engulf targets one additional creature. **You Didn't Pay Attention!** (Free triggered action, when a creature moves or is force-moved adjacent): Engulf the triggering creature with a **double edge**. Kobold Chain Hook + Legionary Shield Bash + Signifer shift all feed movement into the brick's trigger. **Translucent Brick:** fully occupies its space, blocks line of effect, *is hidden until it acts*. Drop one in a narrow corridor — the party walks up, someone moves adjacent, surprise acid-engulf with double edge. Counterplay: destruction from range at the moment it reveals; AoE/splash damage on engulfed allies is a trade but extracts them; movement abilities that don't count as "moving" (teleport) sidestep the trigger.

### Shieldscale Drangolin — elite

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 1, elite, kobold, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 195

**Flavor:** Size 2 or 3, Stamina 80, Speed 7, Free Strike 5, **Burrow**. A draconic pangolin — fire-clawed, armored, tunnel-digging.

**Tactics:** **Fiery Claws** (Melee 1, two targets, Melee 2 if Size 3) — big fire damage across tiers. **Drangolin Plume** (5 Malice, Main action) — the drangolin shifts up to speed and Fiery Claws each creature that comes adjacent during the shift (single power roll vs all). The spin-attack button. **Erupt** (3 Malice, Main, 2-burst): digs up from underground into the target area, deals damage + push + A<0 prone, and +2 fire damage in the breach square. The set piece entrance — drangolin burrows under the party, surfaces in the backline, erupts, knocks everyone prone. **Ashen Cloud:** any ally adjacent has concealment. **Burrow Bond:** when the drangolin burrows, each adjacent Size 1S-or-smaller ally (read: every kobold minion) *moves with them*. This is the kobold transport truck — load four tiros onto the drangolin, burrow across the map, erupt into the party's artillery line with a full shield-line in tow. Counterplay: area damage catches the cloud of adjacent kobolds; fire resistance muffles the signature; target the drangolin specifically before it burrows.

## Leader

### Kobold Centurion — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 1, leader, kobold, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 194

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 80, Speed 5, Stability 2, Free Strike 2. The legion's commanding officer — arrogant, theatrical, weaponized.

**Tactics:** **Pilum** (Signature, Melee 1 or Ranged 10, two targets) — damage across tiers with weakened save-ends on M<1. **Effect:** each ally adjacent to a target makes a free strike against them. The Pilum + shield-line combo — every centurion Pilum activation is effectively "two heroes take damage + four nearby kobolds each free-strike." **3 Malice rider:** while weakened, the target is *also restrained*. Turns weakened save-ends into restrained save-ends, a massive upgrade. **Concentrate All Fire on That Hero!** (Maneuver, Ranged 10) — until start of centurion's next turn, the centurion and their allies have edge on power rolls against the marked target. **3+ Malice:** additional target per 3 Malice. The focus-fire button for the whole legion. **Testudo!** (Triggered action, 5-burst): when a creature uses an ability targeting the centurion or a nearby ally, each ally in the burst shifts up to 2 before damage resolves, *and* each kobold with Shield? Shield! gets damage immunity 2 against the triggering ability. Map-wide defensive reaction — the shield wall literally closes. **End Effect:** at end of each turn, take 5 unresistable damage to end one save-ends effect.

Villain actions:
- **Firetail Pilum (VA1).** The centurion moves up to speed ignoring difficult terrain, and Pilums every creature they pass through — single power roll against all, +5 damage, and weakened targets take 2 fire at start of each of their turns. A run-through parade-move that pilum-tags half the party on the way past.
- **Boom Pilum! (VA2).** 5-cube within 10. Pilum each target with a double edge; each is then pushed up to 3. The explosive single-round catastrophe for any clumped party.
- **Are You Not Entertained?! (VA3).** 10-burst. Targets with P<2 are taunted save-ends; each ally within distance makes a free strike; **until end of encounter, the centurion has damage immunity 2.** The "I am invincible" final-round button — the centurion becomes a durability rock while the legion finishes the job.

Counterplay: (1) burst the centurion before VA3's damage immunity bricks your damage output; (2) take out adjacent Shield? Shield! kobolds so Testudo doesn't cover the centurion; (3) Presence saves resist Are You Not Entertained?'s taunt; (4) End Effect nullifies save-ends controls — use damage and hard effects instead; (5) if the centurion moves into Firetail Pilum range through a bottleneck, ready abilities that punish movement.
