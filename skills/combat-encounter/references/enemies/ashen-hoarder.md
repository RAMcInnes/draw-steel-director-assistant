# Ashen Hoarder

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 46–47. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Grave robbing is a dangerous business, but necromancers need bodies for their experiments. To procure supplies and stay out of danger, these mages build ashen hoarders — hulking constructs made of magically reinforced humanoid bones. A single ashen hoarder is a full encounter on its own.

- **Appendages and Spikes.** Four bone-column legs let the hoarder move quickly and avoid anyone trying to hinder its grim work. Powerful clawed arms dig up corpses and fight enemies. When it claims a body, it impales the corpse on the sharp bones protruding from its back, where corruptive energy charges the cargo for the necromancer's use.
- **Weaponized Bodies.** When cornered, the hoarder reanimates its impaled corpses as **zombie mines** and detonates them with corruptive energy. Once the threat is dead, it recollects the pieces of its original load.
- **Soul Shackle.** The hoarder is powered by a soul split between its body and a **Soul Shackle** — typically a ring or amulet — used to command it. Destroying the shackle frees the trapped soul but sends the hoarder into an unshackled rage; it lashes out at every living creature it can before collapsing on itself. Freeing the soul still trapped inside the hoarder's remains requires the **Find a Cure** downtime project (Draw Steel: Heroes) and the aid of a powerful holy figure.
- **Burrower.** Movement is Burrow — it tunnels at full speed.
- **Solo economy.** Takes **two turns each round** (non-consecutive) via Solo Turns, plus villain actions and Malice spends — the action economy of a small warband in one body.
- **Bladed Body.** An adjacent enemy who grabs the hoarder or uses a melee ability against it takes 3 damage passively.
- **Soul Singularity.** At 0 Stamina, the hoarder explodes in a singularity of bone shards and soul energy: each creature within 5 squares with M<3 takes 11 corruption damage, and any creature killed by this damage has their soul sucked into the Abyssal Wasteland — they can't be brought back to life until the soul is recovered.
- **End Effect.** At the end of each of its turns, the hoarder can take 10 unresistable damage to end one save-ends effect on itself.
- **Weakness: Holy 5** — lean on radiant/holy damage if the party has it.

## Ashen Hoarder Malice features

At the start of an ashen hoarder's turn, Malice may be spent to activate one of the following features.

- **Relentless Strikes (3 Malice).** The hoarder moves up to their speed and makes a free strike against two targets.
- **Blade Wall (5 Malice).** Summons a 10-square wall of bones and blades into unoccupied squares within 5 squares of itself. Each square has 5 Stamina; any enemy who comes adjacent to the wall for the first time in a round or starts their turn there takes 3 damage.
- **Solo Action (5 Malice).** The hoarder takes an additional main action on their turn. Usable even while dazed.
- **Bone Storm (7 Malice).** Bone lances rain down on everyone within 20 squares — each enemy makes an Agility test. On ≤11, 14 damage + restrained and bleeding (save ends); on 12–16, 11 damage + bleeding (EoT); on 17+, 6 damage.

## Ashen Hoarder

**Role:** solo
**Tags:** level 4, solo, EV 72, construct, undead
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 47

**Flavor:** A three-square hulk of magically reinforced human bones walking on four spindly bone-columns, with clawed arms and a spiked back studded with impaled corpses that pulse with green corruption. A stolen soul glows in its chest; a ring or amulet somewhere in the world commands it.

**Tactics:** Primary line is **Claw and Blade** — melee-2, two-target signature ability that grabs and bleeds at the same time, and the hoarder can have up to two size-1 creatures grabbed at a time. Impaled targets are held via **Impale** (3 Malice, 4×1 line) — impaled creatures are restrained + bleeding AND move with the hoarder, up to three at a time; combined with **Armor of Corpses** (2 Malice triggered action), impaled creatures soak half of any damage the hoarder takes, and the cost drops by 1 Malice while impaling. **Corpse Bomb** is the ranged AoE (4-cube within 20, weakens save-ends); spend 2 Malice to slow + weaken instead, or 3 Malice to double the drop with a second cube. **Bone Dozer** (maneuver) is a 2×speed straight-line steamroll that shoves everyone in the path — creatures with M<3 get pushed forward into obstacles and dazed (save ends). Villain ladder: **Skeletal Eruption** launches an 8×3 line into the air, restraining whoever lands prone; **Mobile Mine Field** sprays six zombie mines that explode on adjacency (4 corruption + chain-detonate), and the hoarder can inch them forward 2 squares at the start of each turn; **Ossuary Assault** moves + Claws with double edge, with a free **Impale** on tier 3. Solo Turns + Solo Action (5 Malice) lets the hoarder act three times a round at peak. Counterplay: Holy 5 weakness (holy/radiant damage is the fast kill), break grabs to deny the Armor-of-Corpses discount, and watch the Soul Singularity endgame — a hero killed by the death explosion is *gone* until their soul is retrieved from the Abyssal Wasteland.
