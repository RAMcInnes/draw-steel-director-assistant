# Olothec

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 212–213. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Ancient alien intellects the synlirii call *olothec* — "ancient enemies," though some scholars note an uncanny resemblance to the synlirii word for "ancient ancestor." Older than almost anything in the timescape, olothec are protean beings of nearly pure psionic intellect and geometric reasoning. They hate the mundane world and everything in it: to an olothec, any creature who can't reshape its own flesh at will is an abomination to be corrected — devolved into oozing primordial thralls. They war eternally against the voiceless talkers, whom they see as meddlers who try to refine evolution rather than return it to chaos. Olothec lair in drowned sea-tunnels and beneath ancient ruins, surrounded by slime servants drawn from every ancestry.

> **Content Warning: Body Horror.** Olothec stat blocks lean hard into transformation-of-the-flesh themes (devolution, slime-heads, gelatinous torsos, leg-pillars). Reshape or soften these effects for table safety — the source text explicitly invites this.

- **A writhing shroud of mouths.** At a glance an olothec looks like a knotted mass of tentacles; up close, each tentacle ends in a tooth-filled mouth tipped with a three-eye cluster that focuses psionic attacks. Fetid psionic slime coats the whole body as both armor and conduit.
- **Psionic apex.** Psychic immunity, psychic-damage main actions, psychic villain actions. Psychic-damage parties feed the olothec and starve themselves; physical / elemental builds do the real work.
- **The four states.** Olothec combat tracks four target states — clean, *slimed*, *transformed*, and *devolved*. Effects stack these states over the fight, and some effects key off having at least one applied. Keep a status marker per hero.
- **Gelatinosis — permanent devolution.** A hero who is weakened by Devolving Tentacles for 1 continuous minute, or reduced to 0 Stamina by Devolving Tentacles' psychic damage, or suffers all three Oozing Transformations, **permanently becomes a slime servant**. This is not a save-ends condition — it's a narrative consequence. The olothec is a monster whose *default* win condition is to turn a PC into one of its thralls. Brief the table carefully.
- **Hates voiceless talkers.** Useful hook in campaigns where both factions operate — an olothec shrine intruded upon by a voiceless talker scout is an instant three-way fight, and olothec may accept the party's temporary assistance against their older enemy.
- **Languages.** Most communicate telepathically via Mindspeech and speak Urollialic. Many know Old Variac and Caelian but use them reluctantly.

## Target-state glossary

Track these per-hero markers during play — most olothec abilities key off at least one being applied.

- **Slimed** (save ends). Target takes 4 psychic whenever they make a power roll. Slimed creatures lose concealment against the olothec (Slime Sense).
- **Transformed** (save ends). The Director picks one of the three transformations per Oozing Transformation hit; when the transformed effect ends, all transformations end together.
  - *Head* → ball of slime: no communication, line of effect only within 3 squares.
  - *Legs* → ooze pillars: slowed on land; automatic full-speed swim.
  - *Torso* → gelatinous arms: no edges, no double edges, no surges.
- **Devolved** (save ends). Target has -1 to every characteristic except Reason. Stacks with transformed and slimed.

## Olothec Malice features

Spent at the start of the olothec's turn.

- **Solo Action (4 Malice).** One additional main action this turn, usable **even while dazed**. Central combo piece — Devolving Tentacles, then Solo Action for Slime Spew or Oozing Transformation in the same turn. Cheaper than most solos' version (4 instead of 5).
- **Writhing Envelopment (4 Malice, Main action, Melee 3).** Only targets slimed/transformed/devolved creatures. Psychic damage + grabbed on A<2/A<3/A<4. **Effect:** pull to adjacent, while grabbed target *can't save-ends anything*, and the olothec has double edge on strikes against them. The "finisher on a softened hero" — once a hero has any state applied, Writhing Envelopment locks them in, cuts off their save-ends escape, and stacks double-edge. Pair with Devolving Tentacles to cycle weakened-save-ends into dead.
- **Memory of Chaos (6 Malice, encounter-long).** Each creature starting their turn within 3 of the olothec with R<3 is dazed and slowed until end of their next turn. The aura-of-wrong — commits the fight to melee-under-duress for low-Reason heroes. Spend early and let it run the whole encounter.

## Olothec

**Role:** (solo — no additional role)
**Tags:** level 6, solo, EV 84, horror, olothec
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 213

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 450, Speed 7 **Fly + Swim**, Stability 0, Free Strike 7. Might +4 / Agility -1 / Reason +4 / Intuition +2 / Presence +3. **Psychic Immunity 6.** **Solo Turns** — two turns per round, not consecutive. **End Effect** — at end of each turn, take 10 unresistable damage to end one save-ends effect.

**Tactics:** A transformation-mill that wants to seed slimed onto the party, convert it to transformed, then devolved, then permanent. Flight + Swim on a 450-Stamina body means the olothec ignores most terrain constraints and can relocate between melee reach and the slime-servant rear line at will.

**Traits:**
- **Primordial Mind.** Cannot be frightened or taunted. Fear/taunt builds do nothing to this fight.
- **Slime Sense.** A slimed or transformed creature can't have concealment from or hide from the olothec. Rogues and stealth-casters lose their primary defense as soon as they get slimed — and getting slimed is the default outcome of the signature ability.

**Signature — Devolving Tentacles** (Melee 3, two targets, 2d10+4): 11/17/20 damage + on M-fail *weakened save-ends OR target is slimed (save ends)* at tier 1/2, and **weakened AND slimed** at tier 3. Effect: slimed targets take 4 psychic on each power roll. The seed move — spammed every round to keep slimed applied to as many heroes as possible. Remember Gelatinosis: a hero who stays weakened from this for one continuous minute (ten rounds) becomes a permanent slime servant; or who gets killed by this damage; same outcome.

**Slime Spew** (Area, 7×2 line within 1, 2d10+4, Main): acid damage 6/10/13 inverted by tier (13 on tier 1, 10 on tier 2, 6 on tier 3 — the lower outcomes hit harder); push-special on A-fail, tier 1 also prones. **Effect:** pushed targets go to an unoccupied space in the area as far from the olothec as possible. **1 Malice rider:** area becomes difficult terrain and A<3 targets entering or moving within it are proned. The crowd-clearing zone-creator. Land this across the party's formation to shove everyone outward, then follow with flight to reposition.

**Oozing Transformation** (2 Malice, Ranged 10, 2d10+4, Main): psychic damage 13/20/23 + *transformed save-ends* on I<2/I<3/I<4. **Effect:** each transformation hit lets the Director pick one body part (Head / Legs / Torso) per the glossary above. **Three transformations on one target permanently Gelatinosis them.** This is the primary permanence engine — each Oozing Transformation lands another organ change, and three of them on the same hero over the course of a fight is a campaign-ending body-horror beat. The party must cleanse transformed mid-fight or cheat death narratively.

**Jaunt** (Maneuver, Psionic): teleport to an unoccupied space within 10, or swap with a creature/object within 5. The repositioning maneuver. Swap-with-creature is the sinister use — pull a hero into the olothec's current melee bracket by yanking them out of the party formation into the olothec's previous space.

**Liquify** (Triggered, when a creature within 5 damages the olothec, Ranged 5): 8 psychic damage to each enemy within 5 and psychic weakness 3 until end of olothec's next turn. The "stop punching me" punish — but it hits *everyone* within 5, not just the attacker. Clustering adjacent to the olothec eats 8 psychic per trigger and stacks psychic weakness for the next turn, pre-amplifying everything else the olothec does.

**Villain actions:**
- **Horrifying Form (VA1, Ranged 20, 2d10+4, each enemy).** 10/14/17 psychic + frightened save-ends on P<2/P<3/P<4. **Effect:** while frightened this way, the target *can't make saves to end any other effects*. The lockout button. Combined with slimed save-ends from Devolving Tentacles, a frightened-locked target keeps their slime for the rest of the fight.
- **Psychic Pulse (VA2, 10-burst).** Slide each target up to 5 + 12 psychic + weakened and slimed save-ends on M<3. **Effect:** slimed targets take 4 psychic on power rolls; and until start of next turn, *olothec has damage immunity 4*. Mass status-app + defensive wrapper. Spend after VA1 has frightened-locked the party, to spread slimed without risk of being killed during the interim round.
- **Return to Perfection (VA3, 10-burst, 2d10+4).** 7/13/16 psychic + *devolved save-ends* on R<2/R<3/R<3. Effect: devolved target has -1 to all characteristics other than Reason. The mass-devolution finisher — late-round button that turns three or four heroes into -1-to-everything-but-Reason shells simultaneously.

**Counterplay:**
1. **Non-psychic damage.** Psychic Immunity 6 + most attacks dealing psychic means the olothec is extremely self-protective. Physical weapons, fire, radiant, elemental — bring anything but psychic.
2. **Stay outside 5 squares when possible.** Liquify's AoE retaliation makes adjacent melee clusters bleed. Attack from 6+; spread out so one Liquify can't catch two heroes.
3. **Save-ends cleansing.** The whole fight is save-ends stacks. A caster who can remove a condition per turn slows the transformation pipeline from inevitable to merely dangerous.
4. **Clock Gelatinosis carefully.** 1 continuous minute = 10 rounds of weakened. A fight lasting that long with the olothec spamming Devolving Tentacles on the same hero permanently devolves them. Either end the fight fast, or rotate aggro so no single hero stays weakened continuously.
5. **Kill before three Transformations stack.** One slime-head is recoverable. Three transformations = permanent Gelatinosis. Focus fire to end the fight before the third Oozing Transformation lands on any single hero, or cleanse transformed mid-fight.
6. **End Effect is expensive.** 10 unresistable per turn to shed one save-ends. Against a 450-Stamina solo with Solo Turns, the olothec can pay this indefinitely — but it means the damage ceiling is still finite. Track: two End Effects per round = 20 chip damage the party didn't have to deal.
7. **Save the anti-fear for VA1.** Horrifying Form's "can't make other saves while frightened" clause is the worst round of the fight. A cleanse or Presence-buff handed out pre-VA1 breaks the lockout.
