# Elementals

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 113–116. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

When the gods formed the mundane world, they took sparks of creation from the roiling plane of Quintessence and gave sentience to the most basic elements — air, earth, fire, water. Many elementals embody a single reagent, but older elementals fuse multiple into new shapes: lizards ablaze with fire, eagles woven from storm-cloud, manta rays gliding over dry land. They cultivate and reshape the world they live in; when a tree, stone, or river doesn't suit them, they break it down with elemental fury and rebuild it anew.

- **Duality of Form.** Elementals are mercurial — creators and destroyers at once. Across ages they remake themselves to reflect what the world has become. Any given encounter's elementals might be the same spirits the party's grandparents met centuries ago, wearing different skins.
- **Rebirth and Reformation.** When an elemental dies in the mundane world, their spirit returns to Quintessence and rests for *decades* before returning — and then in a form that reflects the plane they re-enter. Useful for a long-arc callback: "the same spirit, a new shape." Treat death as removal from the current campaign, not a permanent deletion.
- **Languages.** Most elementals speak Low Kuric. Older ones pick up Caelian when they need to parley.

## Shared elemental mechanics

- **Fickle and Free (trait).** Every elemental in this category has this: they can't be restrained, slowed, or knocked prone, and they ignore difficult terrain. Plan encounters around this — control builds that lean on those three conditions hit a wall. Force movement, damage types, and banes/edges still land.
- **Convocation X (maneuver, Ranged 5, self or one elemental).** Every elemental has a Convocation maneuver themed to their element. The *base* effect is a short damage-immunity buff on the targeted elemental (fire, lightning, cold, poison, or a defense bane for earth). The *3 Malice upgrade* is the interesting lever — each element gets a distinct encounter-long battlefield or buff effect (fire ground, lightning vortex, stone carapace, water stream, thorn patch). Convocations are the connective tissue that makes a mixed elemental warband feel coordinated rather than a pile of solo stat blocks.

## Elemental Malice features

At the start of any elemental's turn, Malice may be spent to activate one of the following.

- **Elemental Swap (3 Malice).** Two elementals on the map teleport to swap places; each has damage immunity 2 until end of round. Position-repair button — rescue a caught artillery, pull a brute into range, or get a wounded elemental behind cover.
- **Split (5 Malice).** An elemental acting this turn cleaves into two copies: same stat block, each gets half current Stamina, one size smaller, both shift up to speed. Doubles the action economy at the cost of individual durability. Best used on a crux or essence that's still near full Stamina and can trade halved HP for a second turn of artillery.
- **Convocation of Chaos (7 Malice, Ranged 8 maneuver).** Until end of encounter, target elemental gains +5 speed and +5 damage to strikes — *and* whenever **any** elemental within line of effect uses a Convocation, the Chaos target gains that Convocation's effect too. In a mixed warband, this turns the buffed elemental into a stacking sink that accumulates every Convocation (cold immunity + fire immunity + lightning immunity + stone carapace + water stream + thorn patch). Save it for an elite you want to make unkillable mid-fight.

## Crux of Fire

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 3, elite, EV 20, elemental
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 114

**Flavor:** Size 1T fiery lizard ("blazecaster"). Speed 6, Stamina 80, Stability 0, Fire immunity 5, Presence +2 / Agility +2, Might –1. Territorial — questions interlopers from afar and rains fire before they close. Lives in volcanic zones and planar convergences.

**Tactics:** Long-range fire artillery. **Spitfire** (Ranged 12 signature, two creatures/objects) deals 8/12/15 fire + **burning (save ends)** on A<1/A<2 — burning is 1d6 fire at start of each target's turn, so the crux front-loads a condition that ticks for free while it reloads. **Convocation of Flames** self-buff gives fire immunity 5 for a turn; **3 Malice upgrade** wreathes the ground within 3 squares of the target in fire for the encounter — anyone entering for the first time or starting their turn takes 3 fire, stacking nicely with burning. **Flame Jet** (1 Malice, triggered on the crux taking damage) — ignores the damage's effects and flies up to speed; if they don't end on solid ground, they fall. Reposition tool, not escape: fly up and over a wall, not off the map. Pair with essences of storms' aerial mobility or keep the crux behind a force of earth wall. Counterplay: cold/water damage, close the 12-square gap fast, and treat burning as a priority cleanse because Spitfire re-applies it every turn.

## Essence of Storms

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 3, elite, EV 20, elemental
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 114

**Flavor:** Size 1S falcon/eagle of cloud-stuff ("galeweaver"). Speed 8 **Fly**, Stamina 100, Stability 0, Lightning immunity 5, Agility +2 / Presence +2. Scouts for handlers and other elementals — insatiably curious, usually the first to spot and talk to travelers, but quick to bluster when threats appear.

**Tactics:** Aerial push-harrier. **Bluster** (1-burst signature) hits each enemy for 5 / 5+4 lightning push 1 / 5+7 lightning push 3 — **Effect** always lets the essence shift up to 3 squares before or after, so Bluster is a drive-by that rearranges the map and exits. Great for opening a lane for the crux or knocking a hero off a ledge. **Convocation of Squalls** — 3 Malice upgrade conjures a 3-aura vortex around the target: difficult terrain for enemies, and at end of each target's turn they can push one creature in the area up to 5 squares. Park this on the force of earth in the middle of the party for chain-displacement. **Thunderclap** (1 Malice triggered, Ranged 5) — when a creature within 5 deals damage to the essence, that creature takes 5 lightning. Cheap punish for melee attackers. Counterplay: reach/ranged answers that don't close distance, stability to resist the push train, and watch the shift-before-or-after clause on Bluster — opportunity attacks don't land against an opponent that starts and ends adjacent-ish via a mid-ability shift. Split is particularly strong on an essence at full Stamina — two speed-8 flyers are suddenly in everyone's backline.

## Essence of Tides

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 3, elite, EV 20, elemental
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 115

**Flavor:** Size 1M shimmering blue manta ray ("tidedrifter") that glides over land as easily as water. Speed 7 **Swim**, Stamina 80, Stability 1, Cold immunity 5, Might +2 / Presence +2. Coastal pranksters — easygoing and funny until family or friends are threatened.

**Tactics:** Forced-movement controller. **Water Wing** (Melee 1 signature, two targets) — 7/11/14 + **slide 1/2/3**; **Effect** on P<2 **reduces stability to 0 and +2 squares on force movement (save ends)** — the stability-zero rider is the hook: once it lands, every subsequent slide from *any* elemental (Bluster, Water Wing, the storm vortex, etc.) is effectively 2 squares longer. Target the Presence-light hero first. **Convocation of Waves** — 3 Malice upgrade creates a trailing water stream within 1 square of the target that lasts the encounter and **slows enemies with M<2 who end their turn in it (save ends)** — trails behind the essence as they move, drawing a slowing ribbon across the battlefield. **Sea-Salted Wounds** (1 Malice triggered) — when an ally deals rolled damage to a creature, the essence makes a free strike against it. Pairs with a crux/galeweaver/force landing a rolled hit to chain a Water Wing slide. **Water Glide** trait — whenever the essence starts their turn in a water space, they can fly (no opportunity attacks) until end of turn. Place a pond, fountain, or a Convocation-of-Waves stream on the map and the essence becomes a flyer for free. Counterplay: high-Presence hero to dodge the stability rider, no-cheese maps avoiding standing water, and disengage-ready melee so Water Wing doesn't trade slides for adjacency.

## Force of Earth

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 3, elite, EV 20, elemental
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 116

**Flavor:** Size 2, nine-foot great ape of dirt and rough stone ("earthen bulwark"). Speed 5 **Burrow**, Stamina 132, Stability 2, Might +2 / Presence +2. Guardian and historian among other elementals — works tirelessly to thwart ills from befalling the group.

**Tactics:** The anchor. **Slam Into Dirt** (Melee 2 signature, two targets) — 8/12/15 + **restrained (save ends)** on M<1/M<2; **Effect** turns target's space into difficult terrain. Restrained + adjacent means the crux has a parked target for Spitfire, and the essence of tides has a guaranteed Water Wing follow-up. **Convocation of Quartz** self-buff bane on incoming melee; **3 Malice upgrade** grows a stone carapace on the target for the encounter: +3 stability and 15 temp Stamina. Land this on the force itself or on the crux/essence of storms to turn a squishy flyer into a credible frontliner. **Break Armor** (1 Malice triggered, on taking damage) — halves the damage, but gives the force **damage weakness 3 and +3 speed until end of encounter**, and **weakness grows by 3 each time it's used**. High-risk escape valve; each use cuts the force in half faster. Use once when low-Stamina and the force still has work to do, not on the first big hit. **Primordial Strength** trait — +6 damage against objects, which matters on destructible-terrain maps (walls, pillars, chains holding a bridge). **Stone Swim** trait lets the force burrow through stone without dragging allies — ambush reveal, or escape through the wall behind them. Counterplay: ranged damage from outside Melee 2, cleanse restrained fast, and bait Break Armor early to stack weakness before the real focus-fire turn.

## Field of Growth

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 5, elite, EV 28, elemental
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 115

**Flavor:** Size 3 massive centipede of vegetation ("verdant primeval"). Speed 8 **Climb**, Stamina 120, Stability 2, Poison immunity 5, Might +2 / Intuition +2 / Presence +2. Caretakers of the life-and-death cycle — hunt undead and their creators, and sabotage divine servitors who peddle resurrection magic.

**Tactics:** Ranged control with no safe distance. **Hampering Roots** (Ranged 8 signature, two targets) — 8/13/16 + **prone and can't stand (save ends)** on R<1/R<2; **Effect** on already-prone targets *upgrades to restrained (save ends)* — Hampering Roots into a Hampering Roots repeat on the same target chains prone → restrained, and because the field is Fickle-and-Free, it ignores difficult terrain the party drops on it in response. **Convocation of Verdure** self-buff grants 15 temp Stamina for a turn; **3 Malice upgrade** overgrows the ground within 1 square of the target for the encounter — any enemy who strikes the target is **pulled 5 squares toward the area** after the strike, and entering for the first time or starting a turn inside **knocks prone**. Paired with the base Hampering Roots prone rider, this builds a gravity well: strike the field → get pulled in → fall prone → next Hampering Roots upgrades you to restrained. **Rose Thorn Lash** (1 Malice triggered on any damage dealt to the field, Melee 3) — 6 damage + **bleeding (save ends)** on A<2. **Roots Run Deep** trait — the field can target any creature touching the ground, even through walls and without line of effect. This is the big deal: heroes can't break line of sight to the field by hiding behind terrain. Positioning to "deny angles" doesn't work; only vertical escape (fly, climb) or killing the field does. Counterplay: flying heroes, R-boosts against the prone rider, and fast save-ends cleanse before the restrained upgrade lands. A level 5 controller in a mixed elemental warband is the logical Convocation-of-Chaos target — Split + Chaos + every Convocation feeding a single field is the encounter's nightmare scenario.
