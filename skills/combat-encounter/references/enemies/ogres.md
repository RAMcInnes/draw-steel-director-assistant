# Ogres

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 209–211. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Bull-necked, bulging, and oversized — ogres are small for giants but tower over humanoids, and they indulge every impulse because nothing around them can usually stop them. Most live wild, where they can make their own rules; the worst take whatever they want from anyone too weak to protest.

- **Extreme Emotions.** Ogres don't do mild. They leap with ecstatic joy over trivial victories and scream with murderous rage over everyday frustrations. Those heightened feelings push many ogres into selfishness — and make them easy prey for evil powers promising food, wealth, or companionship. Ogres who do embrace good throw the same emotional weight into protecting their chosen people or place.
- **Savory Stories.** Ogre legends tell of a lost age of great stone halls and sumptuous feasts — always ending tragically, always with a different villain (a scheming mage, a talking cat) swindling the ogres of what was rightfully theirs. How much truth is in them nobody knows, but cruel ogres use them as justification to take back "what's owed."
- **Aristocracy of Bullies.** Wicked ogres who hoard more than they can eat or spend use the excess to tempt other ogres into working for them — making their kin grovel for scraps in exchange for raids and theft. Most such petty rulers don't die of old age; their own ambitious, volatile subjects eventually replace them.
- **Cyclops: Ogre Progenitor.** Ogres descend from cyclopes, old-world single-eyed giants who still wander remote canyons and valleys. Cyclopes stand taller and hit harder than their descendants, but their eyesight is poor and usually irritated, so they ended up as scavengers — and as hired muscle for warlords or wealthy ogres who can guarantee food and shelter. Rumor says a cyclops can emit a mile-long energy ray from their eye; the cloudy, dirt-crusted reality is that they almost never manage to focus one.
- **Languages.** Most ogres speak Caelian and High Kuric.

### Director's note on ogre lairs

Ogres appreciate luxury but are the wrong size to use most of it. They can guzzle wine from stolen bottles and perch stolen crowns on their heads, but they can't fit into small folks' fine clothes or wield their tiny weapons, and any chair they sit in becomes splinters. Their lairs tend to be cluttered with expensive furniture and decorations in various states of damage. Sample lair treasures (an ogre might have one or two): Giant's-Blood Flame, Growth Potion, Icemaker Maul. Lore-related components: a lead slingstone that killed a giant, a slaughter demon's horn used as a drinking horn. Project sources: notes in Low Kuric for the Thundering I weapon enhancement, notes in High Kuric for a Bastion Belt or the Devastating weapon enhancement. All item details in *Draw Steel: Heroes*.

## Shared traits

- **Defiant Anger.** Most non-minion ogres (Goon, Juggernaut, Blue Blood) have damage immunity 2 *while winded*. The fight gets harder once the heroes bloody them, not easier. Plan to burst through ogres in one commit round rather than bring them to half and linger.
- **Size 2 or 3.** Ogres are big targets but also have the mass to deal forced movement back. Cyclopes are size 3 with extra reach implicit in their Wild Slam burst.

## Ogre Malice features

Spent at the start of any ogre's turn.

- **Shockwave (3 Malice, Maneuver).** One ogre this turn jumps and lands on their rear, causing a 3-burst shockwave. Each size 1 creature in the area makes a Might or Agility test — tier 1 takes 5 damage + vertical push 3, tier 2 takes 4 + vertical push 2, tier 3 is just push 1. Cheap AoE pop that turns stability-test failures into fall damage. Best paired with terrain that has a drop.
- **Bully (5 Malice).** Each ogre in the encounter shifts up to their speed and makes a free strike. **They gang up on the same target if they can.** The mid-fight focus-fire button — five ogres closing on the wizard simultaneously, each getting a free swing on the way.
- **Rampage (7 Malice).** Until end of round, each ogre has damage immunity 5 *and* deals +5 damage on abilities, *and* heroic abilities that target any ogre have their Heroic Resource cost reduced by 1 (minimum 1). A mutual escalation — the ogres hit harder and soak harder, but heroes get a break on their best toolbox too. Spend when the fight is nearing its crescendo and both sides want to trade haymakers.

## Elites

### Ogre Goon

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 2, elite, giant, ogre
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 210

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 100, Speed 5, Stability 4, Free Strike 5. The signature club-swinging ogre bully.

**Tactics:** **Club Swing** (Signature, Melee 2, two targets) — big damage with push 2/4/6 per tier and prone on tier 3. *Any target who takes damage from this forced movement takes an extra 4 damage.* Slam two heroes into a wall or each other for the terrain bonus. **Grabby Hand** (1 Malice, Maneuver, Melee 1) — grab on tier 2/3, with a bane on the Escape Grab maneuver. Sets up the next trick. **People Bowling** (3 Malice, Maneuver, 6×1 line) — the goon must have a size 1 creature or object grabbed; they hurl it across the line, ending the grab. *The hurled creature or object is targeted by the ability* — so one hero gets thrown and then takes the damage themselves, plus everyone in the line. Tier 3 prones. **Swat the Fly** (triggered action, Melee 1) — when a creature within reach moves or shifts away, slide them up to 5. The goon doesn't let you leave. **Defiant Anger** — winded damage immunity 2. Counterplay: don't end a turn adjacent (Swat the Fly triggers off departure), and kill the goon through half-life without leaving them winded-and-alive for multiple rounds.

### Ogre Juggernaut

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 2, elite, giant, ogre
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 210

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 80, Speed 6, Stability 2, Free Strike 5. The pitchfork-swinging charger — faster than the Goon, more mobile, hits lines instead of clusters.

**Tactics:** **Pitchfork Catapult** (Signature, Charge, Melee 2, two targets) — damage with vertical push/slide on failed Agility tests across tiers. **1 Malice upgrade:** each target with M<1 bleeds save-ends. Catapult heroes upward *and* bleed them — gravity and DoT in one action. **Earth-Breaking Jump** (Main action, 3-burst) — the juggernaut jumps up to 6 squares before using the ability, then damages everyone in the burst with push 2/4 and prone on failed Might tests. Map-skipping AoE opener. **Horrible Bellow** (2 Malice, Maneuver, 3-burst) — each enemy in the area makes an Intuition test; frightened save-ends at all tiers on I<0/1/2. *While a target is frightened this way, any ogre gains an edge on strikes against them.* So the Juggernaut Bellow buffs the whole ogre warband for the rest of the fight against anyone who fails. **Hrraaaaaagh!** (free triggered action) — when the juggernaut takes damage, they move up to their speed and make a free strike. Free mobility-and-hit every time they get hurt; stacks viciously with Defiant Anger. **Destructive Path** — destroys mundane size-1 objects in their path automatically, and can charge through mundane wood/stone walls 2 squares thick or less. Use walls as entrance cues — the juggernaut doesn't care about doors. Counterplay: don't stack heroes in line for Pitchfork Catapult + Earth-Breaking Jump; save Presence-good heroes to anchor the Intuition test against Horrible Bellow.

## Minions

### Cyclops — minion

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 10, minion, giant, ogre
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 211
**With Captain:** +4 bonus to speed.

**Flavor:** Size 3, Stamina 14, Speed 6, Stability 5. The ogre progenitor, downscaled to minion form. Massive, bad-eyed, dangerous in melee.

**Tactics:** **Wild Slam** (Signature, Main action, 1-burst area) — damage and secondary chip damage on failed Agility tests escalating per tier, with push 3 at tier 2 and prone + restrained save-ends at tier 3. *The cyclops automatically treats their initial power roll as a 17.* So Wild Slam essentially always lands tier 2+ with a chance of a critical. Four cyclopes slamming in overlapping bursts is terrifying. **Limited Vision** (passive) — the cyclops has line of effect only within 3 squares. *Whenever the cyclops takes damage from a ranged ability, they can move up to 3 squares toward the source of the damage.* Poor eyesight, but they track damage to its source — ranged parties can't safely kite a cyclops. Counterplay: fight at 4+ squares of distance where the cyclops has no line of effect; force them to move through terrain.

### Ogre Tantrum — minion

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 7, minion, giant, ogre
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 211
**With Captain:** +5 bonus to ranged distance.

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 10, Speed 5, Stability 2. The ogre version of a siege weapon — rip up a tree, throw it.

**Tactics:** **Throw Fit** (Signature, Ranged 8, one target per minion) — damage with push 2/4 per tier. *The tantrum can grab a size 1L/M/S creature or object adjacent to them (on a<3) and use it as the projectile.* The projectile creature is then vertically pushed 8 (or 13 with Captain), *ignoring stability.* So a Tantrum can grab a hero, throw them 8 squares up, and drop them for fall damage. **Excessive Anger** (passive) — while winded, damage immunity 3 *and* speed 8. Winded Tantrums are faster, tougher, angrier — typical ogre design. Counterplay: burst them (10 Stamina) before they go winded, or the fight gets worse.

### Ogre Blue Blood — minion

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 7, minion, giant, ogre
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 211
**With Captain:** gain an edge on strikes.

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 13, Speed 5, Stability 4. The ogre-aristocrat wannabe — one of the petty-lord's hired thugs, with pretensions.

**Tactics:** **Crush Underfoot** (Signature, Melee 2, one target per minion) — damage with prone at tier 2/3 on failed Might tests. *A prone target takes +4 damage.* The Blue Blood punishes its own prone-inflicting Signature — one Blue Blood primes, a second Blue Blood follows up. **In My Stead** (passive) — *whenever the Blue Blood would make a free strike, an ally within 5 squares can make the free strike instead.* The aristocratic "you, peasant, do the work" clause. Pair with an Ogre Goon or Juggernaut and route every Free Strike trigger through them. **Defiant Anger** — winded damage immunity 2.

## Encounter building

Ogres don't have a Leader in this section — the Goon and Juggernaut anchor fights as elites, while the higher-level minions (Cyclops, Tantrum, Blue Blood) can backfill older groups to scale encounters up. An Aristocracy of Bullies setup works well as a Goon + 4 Blue Bloods (with the Goon handing free-strike triggers to the Blue Bloods via In My Stead's inverse setup, and the Blue Bloods Crush-Underfoot proning for each other). For a higher-level band, a pair of Cyclopes + a Tantrum creates a punishing attrition fight — Cyclopes for melee and auto-17 Wild Slam, Tantrum ripping up the battlefield and hurling heroes skyward. Shockwave Malice fires well off a Juggernaut's Earth-Breaking Jump since both rely on vertical push; Rampage at 7 Malice is the finisher when the Goon is winded and Defiant Anger has kicked in.
