# Trolls

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 254–257. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Ravenous, fathomlessly hungry, and almost impossible to kill permanently. Tall, gangly, mottled green, with a mane of matted hair, an unhinged distended jaw, and long claws that drag along the ground. Trolls rampage through the wild to quell an appetite that's never satisfied, and leave only destruction behind them.

- **Hypermetabolism.** A troll's body quickly assimilates whatever they eat, regenerating wounds from the meal itself. Scholars argue whether the hunger drives the metabolism or the other way around — it doesn't matter to the hero being chewed on.
- **Phases of Destruction.** Trolls can't hunt constantly. The natural cycle is a few days of relentless predation followed by a torpor in their den that can last a week. Patient troll hunters watch for the sleep-phase, but the damage a troll can do while waking is steep.
- **Strange Mutations.** Trolls normally eat humanoids and animals, but legends describe trolls feeding on demons and undead and assimilating those creatures' traits. A troll with loose hanging skin, curling nails, or exposed bone might be considerably healthier than they look — see the Director's Note below.
- **Limbjumbles.** When a large piece gets torn off a troll in battle, it *keeps trying to live*. Survival instinct goes into overdrive, the detached piece sprouts malformed arms and legs, and these *limbjumbles* tumble awkwardly behind living trolls as short-lived servants. Statted as a minion hexer (p. 255).
- **Languages.** Most trolls speak High Kuric or Variac. A few know Caelian. Negotiating with a troll is possible only with more appetizing food on offer than the negotiator themselves.

### Director's note on mutating trolls

Per the book's sidebar, a director may let a troll mutate after consuming the flesh of a creature — gaining some subset of that creature's damage immunities, traits, abilities, and movement options. A troll who ate a dead draconian might grow wings and gain the draconian's signature breath attack. This can bump encounter difficulty, *especially* if the mutation grants acid or fire immunity (which would effectively nullify the trolls' core weakness). Flag mutated trolls in encounter prep so the party has tells — loose skin, mismatched limbs, a half-assimilated wing — and so heroes can respond tactically.

## Shared traits

- **Acid and fire weakness.** *Every* troll stat block has weakness 5 to both acid and fire (Limbjumbles have weakness 8, Level 9 minions weakness 5). The folklore made mechanical: fire and acid are what kill trolls. Any hero kit missing both should plan accordingly.
- **Relentless Hunger** (elites) **/ Group Appetite** (Level 9 minions). *A troll only actually dies if they are reduced to 0 Stamina by acid or fire damage, end their turn at 0 Stamina, or take acid/fire damage while at 0 Stamina.* Otherwise the troll's regeneration kicks back in and they come back the following turn. If the party can't deal the finishing acid/fire hit, downed trolls get back up. Hitting a troll for 200 damage with non-acid, non-fire hurts them exactly the same as bringing them to 0 — they still won't stay dead.
- **Squad Stamina pool heals** (Level 9 minions only). The Crack Trooper and Ravager refill the *squad's* Stamina pool equal to half the damage they deal — so Level 9 minion squads are functionally self-sustaining in the same way elites are.

## Troll Malice features

Spent at the start of any troll's turn.

- **Foul Spew (3 Malice, Area, 5×1 line).** One troll acting this turn spews out a half-digested meal in a line. *Each troll in the area regains 3 Stamina.* Each enemy in the area makes a Might test — tier 1 is 12 acid damage + dazed EoT, tier 2 is 10 acid + weakened EoT, tier 3 is 6 acid. Opening move: puke into the middle of the adventuring party and heal every troll nearby.
- **Emergency Meal (5 Malice).** Each troll in the encounter makes a free strike against an adjacent creature and regains Stamina equal to the damage dealt. The whole troll warband eats the party for healing at the same time. Spend when multiple trolls are adjacent to multiple heroes.
- **Bloody Banquet (7 Malice).** Each *winded* troll in the encounter disgorges their stomach contents into a 1-burst of foul vomitus around them, lasting until end of encounter. Each non-troll who enters the area for the first time in a round or starts their turn there takes 5 acid damage. *Each troll in the area has a double edge on power rolls.* The late-fight board-denial + damage-pump: the wounded trolls stand in their own vomit, heroes who press them take chip damage per round, and the trolls swing at a double edge.

## Level 5 Minions

### Troll Limbjumble — minion

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 5, minion, troll
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 255
**With Captain:** gain an edge on strikes.

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 8, Speed 5. Weakness: acid 8, fire 8. The detached-limb-reassembled creature — too small and short-lived to have a creature-keyword beyond *troll*.

**Tactics:** **Arm and a Leg** (Signature, Melee 1, one target per minion) — damage with prone on failed Agility tests across tiers. *If a target made prone this way is already prone, they are grabbed instead.* Chain limbjumbles for prone-then-grab. **Hyper-Regeneration** — at the start of each of the limbjumble squad's turns, the squad's Stamina pool increases as if each limbjumble were at full Stamina. *The limbjumble squad is functionally self-healing every turn.* Without acid or fire on the party, a limbjumble squad never actually depletes. Prioritize acid/fire on limbjumbles to prevent an infinite squad respawn loop.

### Troll Whelp — minion

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 5, minion, giant, troll
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 255
**With Captain:** +2 damage bonus to strikes.

**Flavor:** Size 1L, Stamina 10, Speed 6. Weakness: acid 5, fire 5. Size 1L minion — the younger, less-experienced troll. Still big.

**Tactics:** **Jaws and Claws** (Signature, Melee 1, one target per minion) — damage with slide 1/2 and bleeding save-ends on tier 3 M<2. **Lingering Hunger** (passive) — *whenever two or more whelps are simultaneously reduced to 0 Stamina by damage that isn't acid or fire, half of those whelps become troll limbjumbles with 4 Stamina.* The hidden cost of AoE-blasting a whelp squad: a party wizard fireballing four whelps may fine (fire damage kills them clean), but a party wizard ice-blasting four whelps turns two of them into limbjumbles who keep fighting. Acid and fire are still the answer.

## Elites (Level 5)

### Troll Butcher

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 5, elite, giant, troll
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 256

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 120, Speed 8, Stability 2, Free Strike 6. Weakness: acid 5, fire 5. The troll chef — deliberate, deliberate about flavors, horrifying because of it.

**Tactics:** **Savoring Bite** (Signature, Melee 1, two targets) — damage with bleeding save-ends on failed Might tests across tiers. **1 Malice upgrade:** regain Stamina equal to damage dealt. **Rotten Scraps** (Main action, 3-cube within 10) — poison damage with weakened save-ends across tiers. *Each troll in the area ignores the damage and instead regains 3 Stamina.* So the Butcher can hit their own trolls with this as a *heal*, or fire it into the party to simultaneously damage heroes and position allies for healing. **Gourmet Flesh** (2 Malice, Maneuver) — changes the Butcher's next Savoring Bite to one of: corruption damage and dazed, acid damage and restrained, or lightning damage and frightened. Flexible condition toolkit per fight. **Acquired Taste** (triggered action, Melee 1) — when a creature within reach damages the Butcher with an ability that gains an edge, has a double edge, or uses a surge, the Butcher free-strikes back *and gains an edge on power rolls plus +3 damage with strikes until end of next turn.* Punishes heroic resource bursts. **Bloody Feast** (passive) — each ally within 5 squares has an edge on power rolls against any enemy affected by a condition. A condition-focused Butcher captain amplifies the whole warband's damage. **Relentless Hunger** — dies only to acid/fire.

### Troll Glutton

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 5, elite, giant, troll
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 256

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 160, Speed 6, Stability 4, Free Strike 7. Weakness: acid 5, fire 5. The overeater — brute force powered by the constant stream of food going in.

**Tactics:** **Voracious Mastication** (Signature, Melee 1, two targets) — heavy damage with slowed save-ends on failed Might tests at tier 2/3. **1 Malice upgrade:** regain Stamina equal to damage dealt. **Crash Through** (3 Malice, Main action, Self) — the Glutton shifts up to their speed in a straight line ignoring difficult terrain. *The first time during this movement they move through the space of a creature or object their size or smaller, that creature/object takes 10 damage OR the creature can choose to fall prone instead.* If the Glutton moves into something larger and doesn't knock it prone or destroy it, the movement ends and they're dazed EoT. The "through the wall of heroes" button. **Food Frenzy** (Maneuver, Self) — until start of next turn, double edge on strikes *and* strikes against the Glutton gain an edge. Mutual-risk damage pump. **Spiteful Retort** (1 Malice, free triggered action) — when the Glutton is reduced to 0 Stamina *but doesn't die* (i.e., not from acid/fire), they use Voracious Mastication against an adjacent creature for free. **Insatiable Appetite** (passive) — once per turn, the Glutton can use Charge as a free maneuver if they target a *winded* creature. Finisher mechanic. **Relentless Hunger**.

## Encounter-building notes

Trolls work best as themed encounters where the party is forced to confront the acid/fire weakness. A few general patterns:

- **Pure troll warband:** 4 whelps + 1 butcher or glutton, with limbjumbles appearing as whelps die. The fight stresses the party's acid/fire output — if they can't deliver it, each dropped troll stands back up the next round and the fight becomes unwinnable-feeling quickly.
- **Troll cellar:** ambush encounter in a torpor-den. Heroes go in hoping to catch sleeping trolls, and find that *one* troll was awake and watching. Use the Phases of Destruction flavor — a sleeping troll who wakes up has full Stamina and a round of surprise before the party can act.
- **Mutated troll:** apply the Director's Note mutations to a single elite troll who ate a draconian, hag, or something stranger. Warn the players through visual tells (wings, mismatched limbs, a second pair of eyes) and adjust difficulty by removing one of the weaknesses — but *never both*, or the party has no counterplay.
- **Bloody Banquet endgame:** trolls naturally reach Bloody Banquet Malice territory around round 3–4 of a longer fight. If heroes haven't finished off wounded trolls with acid/fire by then, the winded trolls start vomiting at their feet and the rest of the fight becomes a slow grind through acid zones.
