# Time Raiders

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 248–253. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Genetic rebels turned space-punk pirates, the **kuran'zoi** — commonly called "time raiders" — scour the Sea of Stars for ships to rob. They prefer plundering UNISOL frigates and the vessels of other powerful empires, but they'll hit anyone carrying enough to make the raid worthwhile. The "time raider" name is a misnomer: they're pirates from the upper worlds, but when they arrive in Orden the locals assume these six-limbed, psi-weapon-wielding strangers must be warriors from the future, and the name stuck.

- **Veterans of the Psychic Wars.** The synlirii — voiceless talkers — created the kuran'zoi thousands of years ago as soldiers. The kuran'zoi rebelled, fled to the Sea of Stars, and became nomads of the timescape. Many of the older ones personally remember the Psychic Wars, and carry that institutional hatred into every encounter with synlirii forces.
- **Hardened-Vision Hexapods.** Six limbs, four of them arms. Resilient ocular sensors that see through the hardlight storms of the Astral Sea and through darkness. Two sets of arms means a single kuran'zoi can wield a melee weapon and a ranged weapon simultaneously — they fight with four hands.
- **Psi-Tech.** Time raiders travel on *mindships* and wield psionic weapons and tools that only they can activate. Melee kuran'zoi specialize in weapons that psionically devastate their targets; **vertexes** pilot suits of psionic armor that empower allies.
- **Creatures Outside Time.** Kuran'zoi don't age while in the Sea of Stars. That's why ancients among them can still recall combat techniques from thousands of years ago — and why their speech, when they arrive planetside, is peppered with misplaced tenses and strange references to events that haven't happened yet or haven't happened in the local timeline. (See the Tyrannis's VA1 name.)
- **Leave a Few Alive.** Pirate captains remind their crews: leave enough sailors to sail the ship home. *"Leave the sheep alive. Next year, they'll return with a new coat of wool for us to shear."* This means non-lethal raids are the default — adventure hooks usually start with survivors of a raid, not corpses.
- **Languages.** Most time raiders speak Caelian and Voll.

## Shared traits

- **Foresight.** *Every* time raider stat block in this section ignores the bane normally imposed by concealment. Illusions, magical darkness, smokescreens — none of it protects heroes from a kuran'zoi strike. The Cannonfall's upgraded **Foresight Squared** also ignores the bane from cover. Plan around the assumption that the standard stealth/cover counterplay to ranged threats doesn't work against this category.
- **Psychic immunity.** Every time raider has psychic immunity 3 (the Tyrannis leader has psychic immunity 5). Psionicist heroes whose kit is all psychic damage will bounce. Bring a secondary damage type.
- **Psi-Tech only kuran'zoi can use.** Every weapon in a time raider's hand is psionically keyed — heroes who loot a dead time raider's golden sickle or serrated saber will find it inert. Worth flagging before the party commits time to looting the battlefield.

## Time Raider Malice features

Spent at the start of any time raider's turn.

- **Gravity Well (3 Malice, Maneuver, 5-cube within 3).** A time raider acting this turn activates a psionic gravity well at the center of the cube; lasts until end of encounter or until an adjacent enemy maneuvers to deactivate it. The area is difficult terrain for enemies, and any enemy who ends their turn inside is pulled up to 4 squares toward the well. The map-anchoring tool — combines viciously with Helix's Kinetic Lane (toss heroes into the well) and the Tyrannis's VA3.
- **Recall Module (5 Malice).** Until end of round, each time raider gains a +3 bonus to speed *and can teleport up to their speed as a move action.* The "everyone reposition simultaneously" button — plan ambush encounters around this.
- **Psi-Cage (10 Malice).** All time raiders in the encounter collectively create a psionic field over the map. The field lasts until the first time raider with the highest Stamina maximum drops to 0 *or chooses to end the field* (no action required). While the field is up, each non-time raider on the map makes a Reason test against the psionic effect at the start of each round — tier 1: 10 psychic + slowed EoT; tier 2: 7 psychic + slowed EoT; tier 3: no effect. The endgame button — map-wide per-round damage + slow that the party can only break by killing the bankroll-target kuran'zoi.

## Minions

### Time Raider Archon — minion

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 3, minion, humanoid, time raider
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 249
**With Captain:** +1 damage bonus to strikes.

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 7, Speed 7. Psychic immunity 3. Fast four-armed skirmisher minion.

**Tactics:** **Brutal Flail** (Signature, Melee 1, one target per minion) — damage across tiers, *with "one ally can make a free strike against the target" on tier 3.* So a clean hit from a single archon hands a free strike to another time raider. Four archons with scattered tier 3 results can hand out multiple free strikes in a single turn. **Foresight** — concealment-bane bypass.

### Time Raider Myriad — minion

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 3, minion, humanoid, time raider
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 249
**With Captain:** +1 damage bonus to strikes.

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 8, Speed 5. Heavier minion with reach.

**Tactics:** **Fifth Fist** (Signature, Melee 2, one target per minion) — damage with slide 1/2/3 per tier and prone on tier 3. The fifth-fist-punch minion pushes heroes into gravity wells or kinetic lanes set up by the platoon. Four myriads sliding heroes 8–12 squares combined in a single turn is how you assemble the party for a Cannonfall Sunderbuss. **Foresight**.

## Platoon

### Time Raider Armiger

**Role:** defender
**Tags:** level 3, platoon, humanoid, time raider
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 249

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 60, Speed 5, Free Strike 5. The saber-wielding guardian of the platoon — fights with psi-saber and reactive psychic poison.

**Tactics:** **Serrated Saber** (Signature, Melee 1) — damage with weakened save-ends on tier 3 R<2. **2 Malice upgrade:** a creature weakened this way is also bleeding. **Shared Sickness** (Triggered action, Ranged 20) — when a creature deals damage to any ally of the armiger within line of effect, the armiger psychically retaliates for psychic damage + 5 poison on failed Reason tests. **Kuran'zoi Heraldry** (passive) — *any time raider who starts their turn with line of effect to the armiger can end one condition affecting them.* The whole platoon cleanses by being visible to the armiger. The Armiger is therefore the highest-priority target in any time raider group — killing them unlocks the party's condition-based control. **Foresight**.

### Time Raider Cannonfall

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 3, platoon, humanoid, time raider
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 250

**Flavor:** Size 1L, Stamina 40, Speed 5, Stability 3, Free Strike 5. The sonic-gun specialist.

**Tactics:** **Sunderbuss** (Signature, Main action, 3-cube within 10) — sonic damage with prone on tier 3 and slowed save-ends on M<2. *Also destroys a 1-square-deep layer of ground beneath the area.* The battlefield itself breaks under the cannonfall's fire. **Buss Buffer** (1 Malice, free triggered action, 5-burst) — when the cannonfall is damaged by a ranged or area ability, halve the damage for the cannonfall and each ally in the burst. Area-damage screen for the platoon. **Foresight Squared** — ignores bane from *both* concealment and cover. Cover is not an answer to the Cannonfall's Sunderbuss.

### Time Raider Helix

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 3, platoon, humanoid, time raider
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 250

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 40, Speed 5, Fly, Free Strike 5. Corruption-blasting flying controller.

**Tactics:** **Blaster Volley** (Signature, Ranged 10, two targets) — corruption damage with push 2/4/6 and prone on tier 3. **Kinetic Lane** (Maneuver, 4×2 line within 10) — creates a psionically charged treadmill. Any creature who enters or starts their turn in the area slides 3 squares in a direction the helix chose. *Each enemy in the area when it first appears takes 3 damage before they slide.* **3 Malice upgrade:** create a second kinetic lane. Pair two Kinetic Lanes at right angles to herd the party into a Gravity Well. **Foresight**.

### Time Raider Hijack

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 3, platoon, humanoid, time raider
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 250

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 50, Speed 6, Free Strike 5. Sickle-and-wire assassin — latches onto a hero and drags them to the kill-zone.

**Tactics:** **Golden Sickles** (Signature, Melee 1) — damage with bleeding save-ends on tier 3 A<2. *The hijack is hidden from any creature who is bleeding from this ability until the condition ends.* A bleeding hero can't see the hijack — which feeds the hijack's ambusher role through subsequent rounds until the target saves. **Psi-Sickle** (Maneuver, Ranged 5) — the hijack latches onto target and closes the distance. If target is larger than the hijack, the hijack moves adjacent; otherwise, the target is *pulled up to 4 squares* toward the hijack. The "pull the wizard into melee" button. **Foresight**.

### Time Raider Mind Punk

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 3, platoon, humanoid, time raider
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 251

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 40, Speed 5, Stability 2, Free Strike 5. The mind-crushing hexer — psychic-damage specialist.

**Tactics:** **Repelling Psihander** (Signature, Melee 1, two adjacent targets) — damage with dazed save-ends on tier 2/3 M<1/<2. *A target who ends their next turn adjacent to the other target falls prone.* Hit both, force them to separate or fall prone — a positioning dilemma. **Mindpunk** (2 Malice, Main action, 3-burst area) — psychic damage with prone on tier 1 R<0, prone + can't stand save-ends on tier 2/3 R<1/<2. The party-wide crowd control. **Foresight**.

### Time Raider Nemesis

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 3, platoon, humanoid, time raider
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 251

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 50, Speed 7, Fly, Free Strike 5. Flying scythe-at-range harrier with a hook from the planes.

**Tactics:** **Golden Scythe** (Signature, Ranged 10) — damage with pull 1/2/3 and restrained save-ends on tier 3 A<2. *This ability can target creatures on parallel planes of existence and pull them onto the nemesis's plane.* The nemesis yanks ethereal heroes, phasing characters, and plane-shifted allies back onto the battlefield. **Kinetic Crush** (2 Malice, Ranged 10) — psychic damage with slowed save-ends on M<0/<1/<2. *Any creature slowed this way takes 2 damage for each square they move or are force moved until the condition ends.* Combined with gravity wells, kinetic lanes, and forced-movement strikes from the platoon, a slowed hero takes a *lot* of damage just existing. **Foresight**.

### Time Raider Vertex

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 3, platoon, humanoid, time raider
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 252

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 62, Speed 5, Fly/hover, Free Strike 5. The armor-suit pilot — buffs allies, bends space, empowers the platoon.

**Tactics:** **Psionic Slam** (Signature, Melee 2) — damage + extra psychic, and *any power roll made against the target gains an edge until start of the vertex's next turn.* Marks a priority target for the whole platoon; every time raider strike against that hero gets an edge for a round. **Split Space** (5 Malice, Main action, 2-cube within 10) — opens a portal connecting to a location the vertex has experienced on any plane of existence, in person or otherwise. Any creature entering for the first time in a round or starting their turn there teleports to any unoccupied space in the portal at the chosen location. Lasts until the vertex uses this ability again, dismisses it, or is transported by it. The *retreat button* — vertex uses it to dump an injured kuran'zoi back to the mindship, or the whole platoon exfiltrates at once. **Invigorated March** (Maneuver, 4-burst area) — each ally target shifts up to half their speed. Mass reposition. **Foresight**.

## Leader

### Time Raider Tyrannis — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 3, leader, humanoid, time raider
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 253

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 120, Speed 10, Hover + Teleport movement, Stability 2, Free Strike 5. Psychic immunity 5, Reason +3. The psi-captain — an agile, tactically-brilliant space-punk admiral who teleports and hovers across the battlefield as easily as thinking about where they want to be.

**Tactics:** **Gatling Blaster** (Signature, Melee 2 or ranged 10, two targets) — corruption damage across tiers. *Each target takes a −2 penalty to speed until start of the tyrannis's next turn.* Movement debuff stapled to every Signature. **Air Raid!** (3 Malice, Maneuver, Ranged 10) — three time raiders fly up to their speed and make a free strike. *If a target doesn't land in an unoccupied space, they fall.* Mass-air-drop the platoon onto hero positions, with free strikes mid-drop. **Precog Reflexes** (triggered action, Ranged 10) — when a creature within 10 strikes the tyrannis, the strike takes a bane *and* the tyrannis makes a free strike against the attacker. **2 Malice upgrade:** double bane. Precognition as reaction economy. **End Effect** (5 damage self to clear a save-ends). **Foresight**.

Villain actions:
- **We Will Won! (VA1, Ranged 10, self and three allies).** Each target gains 15 temporary Stamina and *has their speed doubled until end of their next turn.* Bulletproof mass-sprint. The ability name is pure "creature outside time" flavor — the tyrannis's tense scrambling manifests in the middle of a battle cry.
- **Stick to the Plan! (VA2, 10-burst area).** Each target (self and allies) can *end one effect on them* or move up to their speed. Mass cleanse-or-reposition. Combined with Kuran'zoi Heraldry (Armiger's passive), the time raider squad has redundant condition clearing at multiple levels.
- **Armageddon (VA3, 5-burst area).** The tyrannis fires sensor mines into each unoccupied square in the area *and creates a gravity well whose area contains one or more squares of the tyrannis's space* (see Gravity Well Malice). Whenever an enemy enters a square with a sensor mine, the mine explodes for 3 damage. The board-denial finisher: the tyrannis stands at the center of a gravity well that pulls heroes *in*, while the ground between them and the party is carpeted with mines that explode on entry.

Counterplay: (1) **Kill the Armiger first.** Their Kuran'zoi Heraldry passive is the main reason time raider conditions don't stick — deny it before committing to debuffs. (2) **Don't plan around stealth.** Foresight means every time raider sees through concealment; only the Cannonfall also sees through cover, but rely on full break-line-of-effect rather than half-measures. (3) **Kinetic Lane + Gravity Well is the kill zone.** Watch the Helix's first Kinetic Lane go down; it tells you where they intend to funnel the party. Deny that funnel or break one of the two zone-shaping elements. (4) **The Tyrannis teleports.** Precog Reflexes means melee-burst strategies get punished; open at range. (5) **Psi-Cage (10 Malice) is the losing-the-fight condition.** Monitor Malice accumulation through the fight and burst the highest-HP kuran'zoi to drop the cage; the highest-HP is usually the Tyrannis (120) or Armiger (60) depending on composition.
