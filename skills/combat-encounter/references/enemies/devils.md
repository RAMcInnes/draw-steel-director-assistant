# Devils

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 76–83. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

In the lower plane of Hell, devils of the Seven Cities vie unceasingly for power and control — a conflict fought foremost through cunning and schemes. Azure or crimson-skinned humanoids marked by horns, tails, hooves, wings, and other hellmarks. Virtuosos with words; strike deals, forge contracts, exploit whichever mortal weakness is closest to hand (greed, revenge, vanity, desperation). In the heat of battle, devil charisma can compel a mortal to do their bidding before they realize what they've agreed to.

- **Precarious Pyramids.** The Seven Cities stand on strict hierarchy. Every devil serves a more powerful devil, seven archdevils at the top. Lawful, but always climbing — every rule gets twisted to find a loophole against those above.
- **True Names.** Every devil has a common name and a secret true name. The true name can be spoken to aid in summoning the devil, or to strip them of power. Every non-minion devil stat block has a **True Name** trait: if a creature within 10 squares speaks it, the devil loses their damage immunities, the nondamaging effects of their signature ability, and their Devilish Charm trigger until end of encounter. This is the major counterplay lever — make the players' infernal-name research *matter*.
- **Hell's Defectors.** Not every devil wants to climb. Some get stranded in the mortal world when a summoner overestimates themselves and dies mid-ritual — a cheap adventure hook for a devil NPC who'd rather not go back.
- **Languages.** Anjal and Caelian are the baseline. The more powerful a devil, the more languages — easier contracts with more mortals.

## Negotiations with devils (hook)

Devils are eager to deal but demand concrete evidence and compelling arguments. When running a devil NPC negotiation (Draw Steel: Heroes, Chapter 11): motivations are **Greed, Power, Legacy**; pitfalls are **Benevolence, Freedom, Protection**. A devil NPC starts with +1 impression and patience (to max 5), interest cap raised from 5 to 10. When ready to make an offer, divide interest by 2 for the final response — devils never settle without something desirable in return.

**Temptations** — devils primarily trade in bargaining chips: archdevil wings, archdevil blood, soul chalk, true names. Gifts freely given by a devil last only until the devil revokes them at some inconvenient moment. Sample infernal wares: **G'Allios Visiting Card**, **Thief of Joy** treasures; titles **Diabolist** and **Maestro**; project notes for **Devil's Bargain** armor enhancement, **Hellcharger Helm**, or a **Mediator's Charm**. Each hero can earn 1 wealth from a devil in exchange for a small favor.

## Shared devil mechanics

- **Fire Immunity.** Every devil has fire immunity (typically 5, or immunity-piercing Hellfire on the jurist). Don't open with the fire caster.
- **Devilish Charm (2 Malice triggered action, Ranged 5, non-minions).** Every non-minion devil has this: when targeted by a strike, the target makes a Presence test. Low — devil redirects to a new target. Mid — damage halved. High — target takes a bane on the strike. Effectively *every* devil melee/ranged attack the heroes throw is negotiating against their Presence.
- **Devil Legate's Hellish Bailiff.** The legate carries +3 damage immunity while within 10 squares of a higher-level non-minion devil or while in the Seven Cities — this is the anchor trait that makes mixed-level devil encounters bite harder than the math suggests.

## Devil Malice features

At the start of any devil's turn, Malice may be spent to activate one of the following.

- **Bureaucratic Tape (3 Malice).** One devil acting this turn uses a signature ability against an adjacent creature. On a tier 3, the target takes a **double bane on strikes (save ends)**. Cheapest way to neuter the party's striker for a round.
- **Underhanded Tactics (5+ Malice).** One or two devils teleport adjacent to non-hidden targets and make a free strike. Each additional 2 Malice adds one more devil to the teleport. This is the ambush-backline button — save it for when the party has committed frontline resources away from the casters.
- **Read the Small Print (7 Malice).** Every enemy must choose: **damage weakness 5**, or **bane on power rolls**. Lasts until end of encounter. Heavy tax — land this after the devils have established table position.

## Devil Clerk

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 5, minion, EV 7 per 4, devil, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 77

**Flavor:** Size 1M, horned bureaucrat with a pushed-ink edge — Speed 6, Stamina 10, fire immunity 5, Might +3. With Captain: +2 damage bonus to strikes.

**Tactics:** **Quill Pushing** (Melee 1 signature, one target per minion) is a push-stick — 3/6/7 damage, push 1/2/3. Real value is **positional**: any target adjacent to **two or more clerks** is **taunted** until end of their next turn. Clamp a hero between a pair of clerks and they're locked onto the minion line, which is exactly where the elites want them. Pair with Devil Legate's Law and Order taunt to stack forced-attention effects.

## Devil Notary

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 5, minion, EV 7 per 4, devil, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 77

**Flavor:** Size 1M, scribe-imp with a Ranged 5 fire bolt — Speed 6, Stamina 8, fire immunity 5, Reason +3. With Captain: +5 bonus to ranged distance.

**Tactics:** **Importunity** (Ranged 5 signature, one target per minion) deals modest fire but the rider is what matters — on 12+, **bane on target's next strike** (R<2 / R<3). More importantly, the **effect** is always on: **one non-minion devil within 5 of the notary gains an edge on their next strike**. Every notary is a buff beacon. Screen them behind the adjudicator or jurist and fire every round. Captain-adjacent, their 10-square effective range turns the whole mid-field into an edge-delivery zone.

## Devil Scrivener

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 5, minion, EV 7 per 4, devil, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 77

**Flavor:** Size 1M, flying devil-harrier — Speed 6 **Fly**, Stamina 9, fire immunity 5, Agility +3. With Captain: +3 bonus to speed.

**Tactics:** **Litigation** (Charge, Melee 1 signature, one target per minion) deals 3/5/6 **corruption** + **slowed (EoT)** on 12+. **Effect:** shift 1 square after the strike. Fly + Charge means a scrivener swarm bypasses frontline and lands on the caster, slows them, then shifts 1 to avoid the full opportunity window. With a captain (speed 9 + fly) they reach virtually anywhere on the map. The corruption damage type bypasses many fire-defense plans built for the other devils.

## Devil Adjudicator

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 6, elite, EV 32, devil, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 78

**Flavor:** Size 1M flying devil-bureaucrat wrapped in a cloud of floating paper. Speed 6, **Fly**, Stamina 140, Stability 1, fire immunity 5, Presence +3.

**Tactics:** The debuff engine. **Infernal Injunction** (Ranged 10, two targets, signature) hits for 10/15/18 fire + **frightened (save ends)** on I<1, then **slides a frightened target up to 2 squares** — position-editing that skates around stability. **Adjudicator's Interdiction** (Ranged 10, one target, main action) is the disabler: on ≤11 the target is **slowed, banes on power rolls, AND can't regain Stamina (save ends)** — a surge-denier and healer-blocker in one, which is why this should be mid-encounter not opener. **Quid Pro Quo** (Ranged 10 maneuver, one ally *or frightened creature*) teleport-swaps places — the ally clause is obvious, the **frightened** clause means a hero the adjudicator frightened last turn can be yanked into the devil's pile and the adjudicator can exit. **Vexatious Litigation** (aura): any creature within 10 with **P<3** takes **–2 on saving throws** — strips debuffs off Injunction/Interdiction *much* more slowly than the action math implies. Pairs with notaries (edge feed) and legate (draws fire). Counterplay: high-Presence hero targets the adjudicator first to counter Vexatious Litigation and Devilish Charm, and speaking the True Name cuts Injunction's frightened rider and Devilish Charm dead.

## Devil Jurist

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 5, elite, EV 28, devil, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 79

**Flavor:** Size 1M flying fire-caster with two flaming handfires. Speed 6, **Fly**, Stamina 120, Stability 0, fire immunity 5, Presence +3. **Hellfire** — the jurist's fire damage *ignores damage immunity*, which matters mirror-match or against fire-resistant heroes.

**Tactics:** The fire-artillery nuke. **Fire and Brimstone** (Ranged 12 signature, two targets) does 10/15/18 fire + **burning (save ends)** on 12+ — burning means 1d6 fire at start of *each* target turn, stacking with Ashes to Ashes. **1+ Malice: +1 target per Malice spent** — a spare 3 Malice turns this into a 5-target AoE from 12 squares away. **Ashes to Ashes** (Ranged 12 maneuver, one burning target) automatically deals +6 fire on top of the burning tick — same turn. **Dismissal with Prejudice** (2 burst main action) hits each enemy for 6/10/12 + **slide 1/3/5**, and if the target has M<2 the slide gets **+3** (so a tier 3 M<2 hit slides 8 — almost off any small map). The jurist wants to fly high, drop Dismissal to create distance, and chain Fire and Brimstone + Ashes to Ashes on the parked target. Counterplay: Stability and M<2 counters; speak the True Name to drop fire immunity and the Charm trigger. Focus-fire early — 120 Stamina, Stab 0, and no heavy defender of its own.

## Devil Legate

**Role:** defender
**Tags:** level 5, elite, EV 28, devil, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 80

**Flavor:** Size 1M armored polearm devil with a flaming infernal pike. Speed 6, Stamina 160, Stability 2, fire immunity 5, Might +3.

**Tactics:** The anchor. **Infernal Pike** (Melee 2 signature, two targets) — 9/14/17 damage + **slowed (save ends)** on 12+; if both targets are adjacent to each other, **+3 damage**. Reach 2 with a two-target hit means the legate taxes any hero pair that tries to bracket the back line. **Writ of Execution** (Charge, Melee 2 main action, two targets) is the opener — 6/11/14 + **prone / prone and can't stand (save ends)**; used as a Charge, the legate **ignores difficult terrain** and every creature/object whose space they pass through takes the damage. Build encounter maps with rubble/furniture corridors and watch the legate plow through three heroes in one charge. **Law and Order** (Melee 1 maneuver, one target) **taunts (save ends)** — only one taunted target at a time, but combined with clerks' double-adjacent taunt, the legate picks which squishy eats the focus. **Hellish Bailiff** — damage immunity 3 while in the Seven Cities *or* within 10 of a non-minion higher-level devil. In a mixed encounter with the Judge or a Magistrate/Adjudicator nearby, that's "always on." Counterplay: break the 10-square tether before focus-firing, or speak the True Name to kill the Pike's slow rider and the Charm trigger.

## Devil Magistrate

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 6, elite, EV 32, devil, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 81

**Flavor:** Size 1M stealth-duelist devil with twin flame-blades. Speed 7, Stamina 160, Stability 0, fire immunity 5, Agility +3, Presence +2.

**Tactics:** The assassin. **Edge of the Law** (Melee 1 signature, two targets) — 10/15/18 fire + **dazed (save ends)** on R<3; **shifts up to 3 squares before, after, or between strikes**. Freedom of movement baked into the signature — zero opportunity attacks on the way in or out. **Verdict** (Melee 1 main action, one target) — straight damage 11/17/21; **double edge if hidden** from the target, **+5 damage if target is dazed**. **Justice Turns Its Gaze** (maneuver) — **invisible until start of next turn** + attempt to **hide as a free maneuver** before end of current turn. Pipeline: Edge of the Law dazes the target → Justice Turns Its Gaze to vanish → next turn Verdict with double edge + dazed bonus for a massive single-target nuke. **Leading** trait — when the magistrate leaves an enemy adjacent to an ally, they **shift instead** of moving (no opportunity). Set up with a legate or clerk so the magistrate always has an adjacent ally to skate off. Counterplay: reveal effects, AoE around suspected hide spots, and True Name (drops fire immunity, Edge-of-the-Law's daze rider, and Charm).

## Devil High Judge

**Role:** (leader — no additional role)
**Tags:** level 6, leader, EV 32, devil, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 83

**Flavor:** Size 1M flying archmagistrate with a gavel-authority toolkit. Speed 7, **Fly**, Stamina 181, Stability 2, fire immunity 8, Presence +4.

**Tactics:** A charm engine. All three main abilities write charmed-this-way onto targets, which the Judge then exploits. **Infernal Decree** (Ranged 12 signature, three targets) — 10/15/19 psychic + **target can't hide (save ends)** on P<2/3/4, and while unhideable this way **any devil's strike against them gains an edge (2 Malice)**. **Compel the Jury** (Ranged 12, two targets, maneuver) — **charms on save-ends** across I<2/3/4; charmed creatures treat the Judge as an ally, and the Judge can **spend 1 Malice on their turn to move a charmed creature up to 3 squares**. **Devilish Suggestion** (Ranged 5, triggered, 2 Malice) — on trigger, target makes a Presence test. Low: charmed (save ends). Mid: Judge picks a new target for the strike. High: target banes the strike. All three trees dump into the same "charmed" pool. **Villain Actions** weaponize the pool:
- **VA1 All Rise** (3 burst) — Presence test against each enemy; on ≤11 **15 psychic + charmed (save ends)**; charmed again feeds the pool.
- **VA2 Heed My Decree** (5 burst, self + allies) — each target **shifts up to speed**; each charmed-pool creature **moves up to half speed** too. Mass reposition without triggering opportunity.
- **VA3 Deceptive Stratagem** (Ranged 12, one target) — if ally or charmed, Judge and target teleport-swap; **every ally within 12 makes a free strike**, and **every charmed-pool creature makes a free strike at a target of the Judge's choice**. This is the payoff round — stack charmed from rounds 1–2, then swing the whole battlefield.
- **End Effect** — at end of each turn, Judge can take **10 unresistable damage** to end one save-ends effect. The escape valve for stacked debuffs (notably conditions from the party's own controller).
- **True Name** — speak within 10 and they lose damage immunities, signature nondamaging effects, and Devilish Suggestion for the rest of the encounter.

Counterplay: cleanse charmed on save-ends quickly (each charmed hero is literally a pawn the Judge can move and strike with). The true name is the biggest single lever — research it in downtime. Bring Presence buffs against Decree, and AoE break charmed-adjacent positioning before the Judge can land Deceptive Stratagem's free strike wave.
