# Arixx

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 44–45. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Pebbles dance as the ground quivers. A spray of rock and earth shatters the afternoon's peace, and an arixx blooms from the earth — mandibles dripping sizzling acid as a talon impales its next meal, sometimes a fat sheep, other times a shepherd.

An arixx is a chitinous burrowing insectoid beast who stands taller than a horse. Hooked claws and serrated mandibles are fearsome weapons, but even more dangerous is the stream of caustic spittle they use to dissolve their prey. A single arixx is a full encounter on its own.

- **Pastoral Pest.** Arixxi can be found anywhere, but they're a particular plague in settled lands. An arixx hunts alone, lurking a few feet below roadways and pastures. Sensitive antennae detect movement above; the arixx bursts from the earth, drags living prey into its tunnels, and devours them. Hit-and-run tactics make arixxi virtually impossible to eradicate — unless they have the bad luck to ambush a party of well-armed adventurers. Many communities pay a handsome bounty for a slain arixx.
- **Terror of the Thaw.** Arixxi are a year-round menace in the warmest lands. In temperate places they hibernate through winter, granting farmers a season of peace — paid back on the first thaw of spring, when every arixx awakens ravenous on the same day and bursts from the ground to feed.
- **Burrower.** Moves underground at full speed; **Earthwalk** means earth or loose rock doesn't cost extra movement.
- **Solo economy.** Takes **two turns each round** (non-consecutive) via Solo Turns, plus villain actions — the action economy of a small warband in one body.
- **Soft Underbelly.** A prone creature gets a *double edge* on melee strikes against the arixx instead of the usual bane for attacking from the ground. Knocking it prone (or being prone beside it on purpose) is the counterplay.
- **End Effect.** At the end of each of its turns, the arixx can take 5 unresistable damage to end one save-ends effect on itself.

## Arixx Malice features

At the start of an arixx's turn, Malice may be spent to activate one of the following features.

- **Burning Maw (3 Malice).** The arixx dribbles acid over their mandibles; their next strike gains an edge and deals an extra 3 acid damage.
- **Geyser (5 Malice).** The arixx's tunnels swell with pressure, bursting hot gas from a 3×3-square area anywhere on the surface. Each enemy in the area makes an Agility test — on ≤11, 4 damage + vertical push 5; on 12–16, 4 damage + vertical push 3; on 17+, shift to the nearest unoccupied space outside the area.
- **Solo Action (5 Malice).** The arixx takes an additional main action on their turn. Usable even while dazed.
- **Earth Sink (7 Malice).** The map quakes and begins to sink. Each ground-bound creature with A<1 is knocked prone. For the rest of the encounter, each non-burrowing creature starting their turn on the ground spends 1 extra square of movement to leave their starting position (2 squares if prone or underground). A non-burrowing creature who starts and ends their turn in the same ground space sinks 1 square into the ground.

## Arixx

**Role:** solo
**Tags:** level 1, solo, EV 36, arixx, beast
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 45

**Flavor:** A horse-tall chitinous predator with hooked claws, serrated mandibles, and antennae that track ground vibration. Bursts from the earth in a spray of rock and acid; disappears back into the ground just as fast.

**Tactics:** Opens from underground — **Sinkhole** (Villain Action 2) shifts the arixx up, Bites a surface target, then re-burrows via Dig. **Bite** grabs a size-1 target and ticks 3 acid damage per turn; **Claw Swing** grabs *two* targets at once and can vertical-slide them up to 3 squares (pulling them underground if terrain allows). **Spitfire** is ranged acid (Ranged 10) that leaves a burning-acid patch until end of encounter — denies lanes and forces reposition. **Dirt Devil** (3 Malice) is the area control — 3-burst, difficult terrain, with double edge if the arixx started underground. **Dust Cloud** (maneuver) blinds a 1-burst and lets the arixx move, producing a moving concealment screen. **Skitter** halves incoming damage and shifts 3 squares — the arixx is hard to pin down between its two turns. Villain ladder escalates: **Acid Spew** (10×2 line, heavy acid, persistent puddle), **Sinkhole** (reposition + Bite + Dig), **Acid and Claws** (2-burst, heavy acid, weakens save-ends). Parties win by forcing it prone (Soft Underbelly trigger) and stacking save-ends effects faster than it can End-Effect them off.
