# Radenwights

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 225–228. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Small, bold ratfolk whose coordination reads like a circus act. Radenwights live in warrens called *meddles*, usually tucked under cities where the noise and distraction of daily life make raiding the surface above look more like sport than survival. They'll happily rob, but they prefer to knock their targets down, knock them out, and leave — lethal force is impolite.

- **Bonds and Bravado.** Radenwights are raised from birth to throw themselves fearlessly at any obstacle — the bravery is only possible because every radenwight trusts that every *other* radenwight will follow through instantly. In a scrap, it's never one rat's blade that does the damage; it's the instant instinctual follow-up from their comrades. This is the whole flavor of their mechanical kit (see **Ready Rodent** in shared traits).
- **The Magic of Music.** Arcane study is rare among radenwights, but *musical* magic is not — the meddle's culture prioritizes music and dance, and talented radenwights often discover magic through a flute or a set of panpipes. The legend is of a radenwight flutist who enraptured an evil dragon with her playing while her meddle rolled a suitably sized boulder off the cliff above. That story informs the way radenwight casters show up in a fight: distraction, rhythm, and a second shoe dropping.
- **The Great Maclette.** Never named without "The Great" in front. A maestro-criminal who leads city-wide robberies, always one step ahead of the ratcatchers. Some say he does it to provide for his meddle; others say he's after the criminal crown. The truth is that Maclette loves the composition of a heist the way a bandleader loves a new piece — each robbery is a new overture.
- **Size 1S, Climb everywhere.** Every radenwight in this section is small and has climb movement. The meddle fights from ceilings and walls as readily as floors.
- **Languages.** Most radenwights speak Caelian and Szetch.

## Shared traits

- **Ready Rodent.** *Every* radenwight stat block — minion and platoon alike — has this triggered action: when an ally deals damage to a target, the radenwight makes a free strike against that target. This is the defining mechanic of the category. A single damaging strike from one radenwight becomes a cascade — every other radenwight adjacent to the target tags in for a free strike. Heroes who run into a meddle are not fighting individual rats; they're fighting one organism with many blades. Plan every radenwight encounter around setting up Ready Rodent chains, and note that the Piper's **Vivace Vivace** and the Maestro's **Cacophony** both refresh Ready Rodent mid-round for extra waves.
- **Climb movement.** Walls, ceilings, rafters, alley-roofs — radenwights treat every vertical surface as open ground. Design encounter maps with climbing opportunities; that's what the meddle's built for.

## Radenwight Malice features

Spent at the start of any radenwight's turn.

- **Trouser Cut (3 Malice, Main action, Melee 1).** Damage with push 3 (tier 3 push 5) and taunted EoT at tier 2/3. *If the target is wearing clothing that covers the lower half of their body, they must use a maneuver once to pull it up before they can move.* Low-grade humiliation that consumes a maneuver action from the target — meaningful against heroes whose turn economy depends on maneuver-tagged abilities. *Minions can't use this one.*
- **Rat Race (5 Malice).** Each radenwight in the encounter shifts up to their speed. *If a radenwight ends this shift adjacent to one or more other radenwights, they make a melee free strike against each enemy adjacent to them.* Formation tightening plus free-strike cascade — a meddle that's been pulled apart can reassemble in one action and open the round with free strikes on any hero who's adjacent to the cluster.
- **Rally the Rodents (7 Malice, Area).** A radenwight uses music to coordinate living rats into a 10-square wall of scurrying rodents, placed anywhere on the map. The wall *doesn't* block line of effect for radenwights and their allies — but it does for everyone else. Each wall square has 10 Stamina. If the last radenwight dies while the wall still stands, the rats screech and disperse: each enemy on the map makes an Intuition test — tier 1 is 7 sonic damage + can't take a respite activity during their next respite; tier 2 is 5 sonic; tier 3 no effect. A multi-purpose tool: break line of effect for ranged heroes, asymmetric cover for the meddle's own ranged fire, and a post-TPK guilt-trip for whoever's still standing.

## Minions

Four types, all size 1S, climb-moving, 3–5 Stamina. All have **Ready Rodent**. Mix freely.

### Radenwight Mischiever — minion

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 1, minion, humanoid, radenwight
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 226
**With Captain:** +1 damage bonus to strikes.

**Tactics:** Size 1S, Stamina 4, Speed 7. **Dagger Dance** (Signature, Melee 1 or ranged 5, one target per minion) — base damage across tiers. *If the mischiever is hidden when they use this ability, they can target two creatures.* Start every encounter with mischievers hidden on the ceiling or in the rafters (Climb) and double their turn-1 output. Ready Rodent.

### Radenwight Redeye — minion

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, minion, humanoid, radenwight
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 226
**With Captain:** gain an edge on strikes.

**Tactics:** Size 1S, Stamina 3, Speed 5. **Eyes-On-Me-Shot** (Signature, Ranged 10, one target per minion) — reliable low-damage ranged chip. The minion's actual value is Ready Rodent on the targets their melee allies hit — four redeyes mean four free strikes across the room each time any ally lands damage on an adjacent target.

### Radenwight Scrapper — minion

**Role:** defender
**Tags:** level 1, minion, humanoid, radenwight
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 226
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to melee distance.

**Tactics:** Size 1S, Stamina 5, Speed 5. **Buckler Bash** (Signature, Charge, Melee 1, one target per minion) — damage with taunted EoT at tier 2/3. Minion defenders work in numbers — four scrappers taunting four different heroes split the party's attention; a single hero who damages *any* scrapper unlocks Ready Rodent from every other adjacent radenwight.

### Radenwight Swiftpaw — minion

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, minion, humanoid, radenwight
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 226
**With Captain:** gain an edge on strikes.

**Tactics:** Size 1S, Stamina 4, Speed 7. **Rapier Flunge** (Signature, Melee 1, one target per minion) — damage with slide 1/2/3 *and* the swiftpaw shifts 1/2/3. So a swiftpaw can repeatedly poke-and-reposition, teeing up flanking or clearing line of effect for a Redeye. Ready Rodent.

## Platoon

### Radenwight Bruxer

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, humanoid, radenwight
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 227

**Flavor:** Size 1L, Stamina 40, Speed 5, Climb, Stability 2, Free Strike 4. The meddle's jaw. A radenwight bigger than the rest, with a bite that clamps.

**Tactics:** **Lockjaw** (Signature, Melee 1) — damage with grab at tier 3. *A grabbed target takes 2 damage at the start of each of the bruxer's turns.* Bite-and-hold damage-over-time. **Flurry of Bites** (3 Malice, Main action, 1-burst area) — damage with bleeding save-ends on failed Agility tests. Small burst, but every adjacent minion gets a Ready Rodent free strike off the bite damage, so a 1-burst Flurry can turn into five or six follow-up strikes. **Lockdown** (passive) — any enemy who shifts adjacent to the bruxer has that shift end, and no adjacent enemy can shift. The bruxer is a melee anchor — once a hero is in grabbing range, they stay there. **Ready Rodent**.

### Radenwight Piper

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, humanoid, radenwight
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 227

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 30, Speed 5, Climb, Free Strike 3. The meddle's musician — the flutist who inspires every fighter in range.

**Tactics:** **Piercing Trill** (Signature, Melee 1 or ranged 10) — sonic damage with push 1/3/4. *The piper or one ally within distance regains Stamina equal to the damage dealt.* Built-in healing every turn. **Vivace Vivace** (Maneuver, 3-burst area) — *each ally target who has used Ready Rodent this round regains the use of their triggered action.* The meddle's multiplier: one Piper means every adjacent radenwight can Ready Rodent *twice* per round. **2 Malice upgrade:** area increases to a 6-burst, covering essentially the whole encounter. **Musical Suggestion** (passive) — at end of turn, the piper slides one adjacent creature up to 2, *ignoring stability*. Free reposition every round. **Ready Rodent**.

### Radenwight Ratcrobat

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, humanoid, radenwight
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 228

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 30, Speed 7, Climb, Free Strike 3. The troupe's acrobat — tumbles in, strikes two targets, tumbles out.

**Tactics:** **En Garde** (Signature, Melee 1, two targets) — damage across tiers, *the ratcrobat shifts up to 2 after striking the first target, then can shift 1 after striking the second.* So a ratcrobat threads between two heroes, cutting both, and exits in a different position entirely. **Over Here, Thanks** (Maneuver, Melee 1) — slide target up to 3, then shift into any square the target left. The ratcrobat swaps places with a hero who was counting on where they stood. **Gymratstics** (passive) — an edge on strikes against larger creatures. Every hero is "larger" to a size-1S ratcrobat. **Ready Rodent**.

## Leader

### Radenwight Maestro — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 1, leader, humanoid, radenwight
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 228

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 80, Speed 5, Climb, Stability 1, Free Strike 4. The bandleader — flautist, tactician, occasional criminal maestro. The Great Maclette is the most famous named example, but any meddle can have its own maestro conducting the fight.

**Tactics:** **Cacophony** (Signature, Main action, 5-burst area) — sonic damage with slide 1/3/5 per tier, and *the maestro shifts 1/3/5 squares after the power roll.* Area damage + self-reposition in one action. *Each ally within distance can use Ready Rodent as a free triggered action once before end of round.* This is the Maestro's core combo — drop a sonic burst, refresh Ready Rodent across the whole meddle, watch the next hero strike trigger free strikes from every radenwight on the map. **Tempo Change** (Maneuver, Ranged 10, two enemies) — slowed save-ends on failed Presence tests at rising tier difficulty. **3 Malice upgrade:** allies within 3 of any target gain +2 speed EoT. Cheap speed boost for the whole meddle. **Ever-Ready Rodent** (2 Malice, free triggered action, Ranged 5) — when a creature within 5 deals damage to an ally *or takes damage from an ally*, the maestro makes a free strike against that creature. The maestro gets a free strike every time combat happens within five squares. **End Effect** (5 damage self to end one save-ends).

Villain actions:
- **Overture (VA1, 10-burst area).** Each ally can shift up to their speed *or* take the Defend action. Tempo manipulation — spread out or dig in, meddle's choice.
- **Solo Act (VA2, Ranged 15, one creature).** Target ally halves any damage they take, gains +4 damage on strikes, and *doubles their speed*, all until end of their next turn. Spotlight one radenwight — usually the Bruxer for the damage bonus or the Ratcrobat for the doubled-speed-plus-shifts flourish. That ally becomes a short-term mini-boss for one round.
- **Rondo of Rat (VA3, 10-burst area, each dead ally in the area).** *Each target stands, makes a free strike, and then falls dead again. Any ally of the targets can use Ready Rodent as a free triggered action once in conjunction with these free strikes.* The meddle's finale: every fallen rat on the battlefield gets one last dramatic swing, and the living allies chain Ready Rodent off those strikes. Reserve for round 3+ when the heroes have stacked up a pile of dead radenwights and think the fight is winding down.

Counterplay: (1) Kill the Maestro fast — Ever-Ready Rodent and the refresh-Ready-Rodent clause on Cacophony make them the single biggest force-multiplier in the encounter. (2) Spread out against Cacophony — 5-burst can hit a clumped party, and Tempo Change's slow combined with Cacophony's slide drags heroes back into it. (3) Radenwights that are about to die mid-round are still valuable to the Maestro — if Rondo of Rat looks imminent, prioritize corpse removal (forced movement, disintegration, or holy damage that leaves no body).
