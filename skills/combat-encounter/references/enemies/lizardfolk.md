# Lizardfolk

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 198–201. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Reptilian humanoids who settle wherever the air is damp — tropical coasts, marshes, bogs, any wetland, and even abandoned ruins and caverns when they want privacy. Lizardfolk cultures tend to specialize: one tribe builds boats, another fletches arrows, another weaves or navigates. Respectful travelers find them fair trading partners. Trespassers who take too much food, cut too much wood, or open the wrong tomb door get one warning.

- **Homeland Protectors.** Not naturally aggressive — most lizardfolk ignore outsiders until the outsiders become a threat. Lizardfolk who swear to destroy an enemy will follow that quarry to the ends of the earth, so the heroes should hope the encounter doesn't escalate to oaths.
- **Focused and Loyal.** Blunt honesty is a sign of respect. A lizardfolk vow is binding.
- **Marshland Tyrants.** In crisis — famine, flood, plague — an evil leader can rally a tribe toward expansionist aggression. Other tribes oppose this and will accept help from outsiders who share a distaste for tyranny. This is the standard lizardfolk villain shape: a warlord pretending that conquest is defense.
- **Languages.** Most lizardfolk speak Caelian and a lizardfolk dialect of Khamish.

## Shared traits

- **Reptilian Escape.** Every lizardfolk stat block — minion through leader — can drop their tail to end a grabbed, prone, slowed, or weakened condition on themselves, then shift 2 squares. The tail doesn't grow back mid-fight unless the Water Pit Malice feature is active. The lizardfolk leader has a stronger **Rex Reptilian Escape** that works on *any* save-ends or end-of-turn effect. Plan around: grapple-and-lockdown strategies are built to fail once per fight per lizardfolk.
- **Swim movement.** Every lizardfolk in this section has Swim. The entire category is built around water — see the Tactics sidebar below.
- **Tail rotation.** When a lizardfolk drops their tail, the tribe rotates them to the rear and fresh-tailed allies take their place. Plan to describe this between combat rounds; it matters narratively even when it's not mechanically tracked.

## Lizardfolk Tactics (from the book's sidebar)

Lizardfolk prefer to fight in or near water whenever they can. If the battlefield doesn't start with water, they delay and feint while they spend Malice to flood it. Once there's water on the map, they split the party using grab-and-pull abilities (Tonguer tongues, Bloodeye bolas, Deathrex spear-pulls) — divide the defenders from the back rank, grab the back rank, drag it into the marsh. Tailless lizardfolk rotate rearward; healthier ones screen.

### Sample encounters (from the book)

- **Marsh Hunters, 18 EV.** 8 shellguard, 8 tonguers, 1 scaletooth.
- **Ambushers, 24 EV.** 16 grunts, 2 skyterrors.
- **Scalesworn Detachment, 30 EV.** 8 shellguard, 16 tonguers, 1 scaletooth, 2 bloodeyes.
- **War Party, 48 EV.** 8 shellguard, 8 grunts, 8 tonguers, 1 scaletooth, 1 skyterror, 1 bloodeye, 1 deathrex.

## Lizardfolk Malice features

Spent at the start of any lizardfolk's turn.

- **Net Trap (3 Malice, Maneuver, 1-cube within 3).** A lizardfolk drops a net snare; the first enemy to step in makes an Agility test (bane if unaware) — restrained save-ends / restrained EoT / no effect across tiers. An adjacent ally not also trapped can free the victim as a maneuver. The cheap zone-denial tool — drop two on the doorway of a lizardfolk hut and wait.
- **Water Pit (5 Malice).** Unearth a size-2 water pit, 2 squares deep. Any lizardfolk who dives into and out of it gains 10 temp Stamina, *regrows their tail*, and ends one save-ends effect. Enemies with burrow or the Nature skill can test (Might or Reason) to drain it — with tier 1 they fall in prone, tier 2 they fail, tier 3 they drain it. The tribe's healing pool *and* the counter to tail-loss rotation — once a Water Pit opens on the map, Reptilian Escape is reusable.
- **Flood the Shores (7 Malice).** Waist-high water covers the entire encounter map. Any lizardfolk submerged gains an edge on abilities and doubles swim speed. Drains at the end of the round unless at least one open Water Pit exists on the map. The "we chose when this fight happens" button: drop a Water Pit first to make the flood permanent, then spend 7 Malice to drown the party's footing.

## Minions

### Lizardfolk Grunt — minion

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, minion, humanoid, lizardfolk
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 199
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to speed.

**Tactics:** **Snap and Toss** (Melee 1, one target per minion) — low damage with a 2-square slide at tiers 2/3. The positioning minion — slide a hero one square toward the Tonguer or into a Water Pit. Four grunts with Captain-boosted speed 8 can herd a whole front rank into a chokepoint. Reptilian Escape.

### Lizardfolk Shellguard — minion

**Role:** defender
**Tags:** level 1, minion, humanoid, lizardfolk
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 199
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to Stamina.

**Tactics:** Size 1L (larger than grunts, tougher). **Shield Smash** (Melee 1, one target per minion) — low damage with a bane on the target's *next* strike. Four shellguards converging on the party damage-dealer impose four banes across their next four swings. Reptilian Escape. The screen — slot them between heroes and the Tonguer/Bloodeye line.

### Lizardfolk Tonguer — minion

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, minion, humanoid, lizardfolk
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 199
**With Captain:** +1 bonus to strikes.

**Tactics:** **Tonguelash** (Melee 8 — yes, eight, the reach of their tongue, one target per minion) — damage with pull 1/2/3 per tier, *or* the tonguer shifts toward the target. *If the forced movement or shift leaves the target adjacent to the tonguer, the target is grabbed.* Four tonguers posted around a lake can reach anywhere on a small battlefield and grab back-rank heroes one at a time. This is the signature lizardfolk kidnap move — Tonguer grabs the wizard, next round a Scaletooth bites them for the grab-upgrade damage. Reptilian Escape. Counterplay: close into melee with the tonguer (they're 1S and 3 Stamina) or break the tongue's line of effect with allied bodies.

## Platoon

### Lizardfolk Bloodeye

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, humanoid, lizardfolk
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 200

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 20, Speed 5, Swim, Free Strike 3. Intuition-positive hexer who spits acid.

**Tactics:** **Bola Knock** (Signature, Ranged 5) — damage with restrained save-ends on failed Agility tests. **Bloodshot** (2 Malice, Ranged 10) — acid damage with a line-of-effect-only-within-N-squares effect on failed Might tests at tier 1/2/3 (4 / 3 / 2 squares). Effectively a "blinding" — the target can still target people nearby but can't see across the battlefield. Pair Bloodshot with Flood the Shores and a Water Pit: the hero can't target the pit-diving tonguer because they're out of the shrunken line of effect. Reptilian Escape.

### Lizardfolk Scaletooth

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, humanoid, lizardfolk
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 200

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 46, Speed 5, Swim, Free Strike 4. The muscle of a lizardfolk warband — the "drag the kidnapped hero under and bite them" specialist.

**Tactics:** **Razor Bite** (Signature, Melee 1) — strong damage with bleeding save-ends at tier 3 on A<2. *If the scaletooth already has the target grabbed, the ability's potency increases by 1.* So the combo is: Tonguer grabs the hero, Scaletooth bites — the grab makes the bite more likely to bleed. **Tail Whip** (2 Malice, Melee 2, two targets) — damage with slide 1/2/3 and a grab rider on M<1/<2 if the slide leaves the target within 2 squares of the scaletooth. A two-target grab on one action — the whole party's back rank can end up grabbed if positioning is bad. Reptilian Escape.

### Lizardfolk Skyterror

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, humanoid, lizardfolk
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 200

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 30, Speed 7, Swim, Free Strike 3. The winged-glider scout — runs or falls a bit and sails the rest of the way.

**Tactics:** **Glaive Rush** (Signature, Melee 1) — damage with prone at tier 3. *If the skyterror is flying, they shift up to 4 squares* after. **Poison Blow Dart** (Ranged 5) — damage with weakened save-ends at tier 1/2/3 on M<0/<1/<2, *and any creature who ends their turn adjacent to a target weakened this way is also weakened EoT.* A contagious weakness — the skyterror poisons one hero and it spreads to their frontliner. **Glider** passive — whenever the skyterror moves 2+ squares along the ground or falls 2+, they can fly until end of their next turn. So a cliff-or-treetop encounter lets them sail into position, Glaive Rush + 4-shift out, and repeat. Reptilian Escape.

## Leader

### Lizardfolk Deathrex — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 1, leader, humanoid, lizardfolk
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 201

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 80, Speed 5, Climb + Swim, Stability 2, Free Strike 4. The tyrant. A stout, armored, spear-wielding reptile the size of two people — think alligator-shaman-warlord.

**Tactics:** **Ripper Spear** (Signature, Melee 3, two targets) — big damage with pull 1/1/2 per tier and bleeding save-ends on A<1/<2/<3. **1 Malice upgrade:** one target adjacent to the deathrex is grabbed in the deathrex's mouth. This is the pull-and-grab combo — spear hits two heroes, pulls both, the closer one ends up in the mouth. **Death Roll** (3 Malice, Melee 1, one grabbed target) — heavy damage with dazed save-ends on failed Might tests at every tier. *The grab ends and the deathrex slides the target up to 5 squares.* Bite the hero, throw them through the tribe's feeding grounds. **Trundle** (Maneuver, Self) — move up to speed, with a free strike against each creature who makes an opportunity attack during the movement. **Swat the Fly** (Triggered action, Melee 1) — when a creature within reach moves or shifts away, slide them up to 5 squares. The deathrex punishes heroes trying to escape the mouth or the grapple. **Rex Reptilian Escape** — stronger than the standard version; ends *any* save-ends or EoT effect, not just the four named conditions.

Villain actions:
- **Snack Attack (VA1, 10-burst area).** Each target (self and allies) moves up to speed and makes a free strike. *Each target gains temporary Stamina equal to the damage they deal.* The tribe eats the bleeding heroes to recover.
- **Shed Some Skin (VA2).** The deathrex shifts up to speed, leaving behind a shed-skin duplicate in their starting square. Duplicate acts on the deathrex's turn, has the deathrex's characteristics, 10 Stamina, and no villain actions. A feint + pseudo-Duplicate combo (and thematically great).
- **Thresher Thrasher (VA3, 10-burst area).** Each target (self and allies) moves up to speed. *Until end of encounter, whenever a creature comes adjacent to a target or starts their turn there, the target can make a free strike against them.* Permanent reaction zone on every lizardfolk on the map — close at your peril.

Counterplay: (1) Kill the deathrex while they're grabbed onto your ally (the grab ends when you drop the grabber — trading damage for freedom is often the right call). (2) Prevent the VA3 permanent-free-strike ring with a mass-daze or mass-prone before round 3. (3) Break up water terrain — no Flood the Shores, no Water Pit, no tail regrowth; Reptilian Escape becomes one-shot across the whole tribe.
