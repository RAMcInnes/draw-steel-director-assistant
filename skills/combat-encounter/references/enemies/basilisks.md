# Basilisks

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 48–49. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

With eight legs, a scaled rough hide, and razor-sharp teeth coated in poison, the basilisk is a deadly threat — even aside from their petrifying eye beams. A single look can fell the mightiest of foes, turning them into an immobilized stone statue who remains thankfully unaware when the basilisk later consumes their petrified form at leisure.

- **Raising Young.** Basilisks live in mated pairs and raise small clutches of younglings in damp caves. Adults subsist on berries, fish, fowl, and carrion, resorting to petrified victims only when starving. Younglings can *only* eat petrified victims, so adults travel far from the nest — even into populated areas — to hunt prey for the babies.
- **Dangerous Servitors.** Crime lords and overminds hatch basilisk eggs to raise the creatures as bodyguards and pets. Training is dangerous, and many handlers end up turned to stone when a basilisk escapes. Medusas in particular keep basilisks as pets — they feed their own victims to the basilisk and its younglings, immune to the eye beams themselves.
- **Alchemical Properties.** An oil in the basilisk's mouth gland reverts stone to flesh. Unsavory alchemists pay good money to those willing to steal a basilisk youngling — collected oil can be distilled into a **Restorative Salve**.
- **Restorative Salve.** After a basilisk dies, a character can make a **Reason test** to claim oil from its mouth. One dose applied as a main action un-petrifies a creature after 1 minute. Outcomes: **≤11** — must complete the *Find a Cure* downtime project (Draw Steel: Heroes) to create one dose; **12–16** — creates one dose, with two more available via *Find a Cure*; **17+** — creates three doses outright.

## Petrification shared ability

Both basilisk variants use **Petrifying Eye Beams** (2d10 + 2, Area/Magic, 5×2 line within 1 square, maneuver). The area extends from *both* eyes, and the ability targets the first creature without cover on either side of the line. A target **restrained** by the beams magically begins to turn to stone; a target who ends **two consecutive turns restrained this way is petrified**. The restrained creature — or an adjacent creature — can use a main action to cut away the encroaching stone, dealing **8 unresistable damage** and ending the effect. Already-slowed targets have the potency bumped by 1 tier, letting the tonguesnapper's hexer toolkit pipeline into locks.

## Basilisk Malice features

At the start of any basilisk's turn, Malice may be spent to activate one of the following features.

- **Stone Swim (3+ Malice).** The basilisk can burrow until the start of their next turn and moves up to their speed; they have damage immunity 2 while underground. The basilisk can burrow through stone, but can't drag other creatures underground when they do so. At the start of each of the basilisk's turns, the Director can spend 1 additional Malice to let the basilisk continue burrowing.
- **Upchuck (5 Malice) — 2d10 + 2.** *Area, Weapon — 3-cube within 10 — Main action — Each enemy in the area.* The basilisk spits up a chunk of partly digested stone. On ≤11, 4 damage; on 12–16, 4 damage + A<1 2 damage, prone; on 17+, 4 damage + A<2 5 damage, prone and can't stand (save ends).
- **Walleye (7 Malice).** The basilisk spews reflective spittle across an adjacent vertical surface in a 3×3 area. The basilisk can then use **Petrifying Eye Beams** targeting a square in the area, causing the ability's area and distance to become a **20 × 3 line within 1 square of the wall** — effectively weaponizing a whole corridor via mirror geometry.

## Basilisk

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 1, elite, EV 12, basilisk, beast
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 49

**Flavor:** An eight-legged, rough-scaled brute the size of a bear. Red eyes lock and hold before the petrifying beams trigger; teeth drip poison even when it isn't using them. Stamina 80, Speed 8, Immunity Poison 4, Calcifying Presence makes nearby ground difficult terrain for enemies.

**Tactics:** Opens with **Petrifying Eye Beams** on the heaviest armored target — maneuver-cost means it fires on the same turn as a Bite, and the 5×2 line targets the first uncovered creature on *either* side (position carefully to catch backline squishies). **Noxious Bite** (Melee 1, two targets) hits anyone the basilisk has previously poisoned with an edge, so a first-round Bite into a second-round double-Bite spikes damage fast. Spend 5 Malice on **Poison Fumes** (3-cube within 1) to weaken + slow a cluster — synergizing with the petrification pipeline (slowed targets bump Eye Beams by a tier). **Lash Out** (triggered action when hit by melee, 1-burst) deals 5 damage to everyone adjacent and bleeds anyone with A<2. Pairs aggressively with tonguesnappers: the basilisk restrains, the tonguesnapper pulls victims in with Prehensile Tongue while they can't escape. Counterplay: block line of sight to deny the eye beams, wear high-stability armor to dodge the tongue pull, and bring adjacent-ally healers to cut stone off trapped heroes.

## Basilisk Tonguesnapper

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 1, elite, EV 12, basilisk, beast
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 49

**Flavor:** A smaller, nimbler basilisk variant — faster and craftier, with a long prehensile tongue that drips acid. Eyes still petrify, but the tonguesnapper is the one that yanks you into range. Stamina 40, Speed 8, Immunity Acid 2 + Poison 2, **Petrifying Fumes** slows anyone starting their turn adjacent with M<1.

**Tactics:** Sets up the petrification combo from Melee 3 range: **Prehensile Tongue** (2d10+2 acid, pull 1/2/3) can target creatures **restrained by Petrifying Eye Beams** and **ignores their stability** if it does — meaning once a hero is caught in the beams, the tonguesnapper drags them in regardless of Might. Spend 3 Malice on the signature to target two additional creatures. **Wink** (2 Malice, Melee 1 or Ranged 10) is the solo control lever — heavy corruption damage + dazed (save ends), and dazed-this-way targets **can't benefit from edges or double edges and can't gain or use surges**; it's the tool for shutting down a hero's big turn. **Neurotoxin Splash** (triggered, 2-burst) punishes melee hits with 4 acid + slows M<2 targets. Because the Tonguesnapper has half the Basilisk's Stamina and no poison immunity, it wants to stay behind/beside the brute. Counterplay: stack stability so the tongue can't pull through it, keep a save-ends cleanser ready for Wink, and focus fire when it commits to a melee Wink.
