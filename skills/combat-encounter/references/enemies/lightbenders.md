# Lightbenders

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 196–197. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Light-warping predator cats that hunt in any sunlit wilderness — desert, plains, open forest. A lightbender at distance looks like a regular lion; up close, their eyes glow, their mane shimmers with iridescence, and their twin tails end in refractive crystals. Their pelt magically refracts surrounding light, so when a lightbender teleports, they leave a visual afterimage behind. Prey usually doesn't realize what they're looking at until the real cat lands on them.

- **Hidden Hunters.** The afterimage trick is the core of every lightbender's kit — both stat blocks have a triggered action that halves damage, ignores non-damage effects, and teleports them 5 squares, with an immediate free hide attempt if they land in cover or concealment. Bringing a lightbender below half Stamina often just puts them across the map in a shrub.
- **Ghostly Echoes.** Lightbenders also bend sound — delaying their own footfalls into silent prowls, or throwing guttural trills across a field to bait prey out of cover. Shadows of the College of the Harlequin Mask still teach techniques said to have been adapted from observing lightbenders hunt.
- **Protective Companions.** Usually solitary, but lightbenders will cross territory lines to help guard another's newborn kittens. A few have been tamed as guards or hunting beasts and become loyal protectors — often treating their smaller humanoid companions as surrogate kittens, with everything that implies about possessiveness and discipline.

## Shared traits (both stat blocks)

- **Size 2, Speed 10, Stamina 100, Stability 1.** Elites — one lightbender handles about two same-level heroes. Two of them (one of each variant) form a natural encounter for a 4-hero party.
- **Afterimage triggered action.** When a lightbender takes damage from a strike, they halve the damage, ignore any non-damage effects attached, teleport up to 5 squares, and can free-hide if they land in cover or concealment. This is the defining cat-move — the first big strike against a lightbender almost always relocates them into hiding.
- **Avoidance.** Any effect on a lightbender that would end on a saving throw instead ends automatically at the end of their next turn. Save-ends debuffs are functionally worthless; plan for instantaneous damage or conditions with different durations.
- **Afterimage + Avoidance + an afterimage-on-damage reaction** stack into a creature that doesn't stay debuffed, doesn't stay hit, and doesn't stay where it was.

## Lightbender Malice features

Spent at the start of any lightbender's turn.

- **Silent Prowl (3 Malice).** Each lightbender acting this turn can teleport up to their speed as a move action and free-hide afterward, all until the start of their next turn. The opener: teleport 10 squares, hide, set up the ambush for next round.
- **Duplicate (5 Malice).** Each lightbender acting this turn spawns a duplicate adjacent to them. The duplicate is visually identical except by supernatural means, has 1 Stamina, and shares the lightbender's speed. Duplicates act on the lightbender's turn but can only take move actions — and *once per round before or after using an ability, a lightbender can trade places with any duplicate.* The swap is the real play: heroes commit to attacking what looks like the cat, deal 1 Stamina of damage to a decoy, and the actual lightbender appears somewhere else. In a fight with a duplicate on the board, the lightbender is effectively untargetable by focus fire until the heroes work out which one is real.
- **Everything the Light Touches (7 Malice).** Each lightbender in the encounter shines radiantly, distorting senses within 5 squares. Each affected enemy makes a Reason test: tier 1 loses line of effect to any lightbender save-ends, tier 2 loses line of effect to any lightbender EoT, tier 3 no effect. The mid-fight "now you can't target me" button — ranged parties suddenly have no valid target, melee parties have nothing to melee. Spend when the cats want a free round to reposition.

## Lightbender — elite ambusher

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 3, elite, beast, lightbender
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 197

**Flavor:** The "patient predator" variant. Walks up unseen, opens the throat of whoever's standing watch. Size 2, Stamina 100, Speed 10, Free Strike 6.

**Tactics:** **Flash Swipe** (Signature, Melee 2, one target) — high single-target damage across tiers, *with +4 extra damage on any tier that gains an edge or double edge.* So an Exploit-Opening-equivalent (a prone target, a condition, flanking via Duplicate) turns the Signature into a big single-target nuke. **Piercing Tails** (Melee 2) — lower base damage but applies bleeding save-ends on failed Might tests, *and while bleeding this way, the target takes a bane on tests to search for the lightbender while they're hidden.* The "I am the bleed, and the bleed helps me disappear" combo. **Hypnotic Mane** (5 Malice, Maneuver, 3-burst area) — Intuition test, dazed save-ends at tier 1/2/3 on I<0/1/2. *While dazed this way, the target has speed 0; the daze ends if the target takes damage or if an ally spends a main action to shake them out.* A party whose back rank is mane-dazed suddenly has a choice: waste main actions shaking each other or let the ambusher free-strike immobile wizards. **Stalker's Afterimage** triggered action + **Avoidance** (shared). Counterplay: the daze ends on damage, so *attack dazed allies* to free them; prioritize the main cat over any duplicates you can verify; bring multi-target damage to catch the real lightbender alongside a decoy.

## Lightbender Pouncer — elite harrier

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 3, elite, beast, lightbender
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 197

**Flavor:** The hit-and-run variant. Bounds across the field, knocks down whoever's standing, free-strikes on the way past. Size 2, Stamina 100, Speed 10, Free Strike 5.

**Tactics:** **Pounce** (Signature, Charge, Melee 2, two targets) — damage across tiers with prone on failed Agility tests at tier 2/3. *The pouncer makes a free strike against each target they knock prone.* One charge = two hits + up to two free strikes on proned targets. Combine with Silent Prowl Malice to teleport-and-hide into ambush position, then emerge into a charge. **Sparking Tail Whip** (Main action, 2-burst area) — damage with *dazzled* save-ends on failed Agility tests at tier 2/3. *Dazzled targets take a bane on strikes and have line of effect only within 1 square.* A dazzled wizard can't hit anyone past their own square. **Illusory Feint** (5 Malice, Maneuver, 3-cube within 10) — same daze mechanic as Hypnotic Mane, but at range. So the pouncer can daze a ranged cluster from 10 away, then Sparking Tail Whip the melee cluster in a separate pulse — two different debuffs on two halves of the party. **Striking Afterimage** triggered action + **Avoidance** (shared). Counterplay: (1) the Illusory Feint daze ends on damage — same trick as the other cat's Hypnotic Mane, have a clear-headed ally swing at the dazed teammate. (2) Close the distance so dazzled targets can still hit in their 1-square line of effect. (3) Don't stand in a line when the pouncer charges — spread reduces two-target Pounce to one.

## Encounter building

A pair of lightbender + lightbender pouncer is a natural Level 3 elite encounter for four heroes. The variants synergize hard — Flash Swipe wants heroes exposed (dazed, flanked, or behind cover the Pouncer has just knocked down), and Pounce wants heroes grouped to double-tap. Open with Silent Prowl (3 Malice), burn Duplicate (5 Malice) the turn a hero gets isolated, and hold Everything the Light Touches (7 Malice) for the point where the party is about to burst one of the cats — it flips the round's tempo cleanly. The afterimage reaction on both stat blocks means focus-fire nukes *still* only land half damage and immediately relocate; plan for a fight where the heroes can't just pick a target and commit.
