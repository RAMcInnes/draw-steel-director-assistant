# Dragons

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 93–103. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Terrifying behemoths. Harbingers of destruction and woe. Dragons claim the world for themselves, throw the innocent into despair, and inspire heroes to draw steel. Mechanically, every dragon in this chapter is a **Solo** with Solo Turns (two non-consecutive turns per round) and End Effect (unresistable damage to shed one save-ends condition). Narratively, each is crystallized elemental energy shaped by a specific kind of anguish — and the anguish keeps making more of them even after the current one dies.

- **Where the Elements Meet Anguish.** Elementals entangled in rancor or grief crystallize into dragons. Thorn dragons grow from famine and drought; gloom dragons from terror and nightmares; crucible dragons from creative failure and jealousy; omen dragons from bound, restless souls; meteor dragons from complacent civilizations grown too comfortable. Killing the dragon doesn't fix the underlying condition — another will take its place in time. Use that when writing adventures.
- **Wyrmscale.** Most dragons wear wyrmscale — armor plating that both absorbs and emits elemental energy. When threatened, it radiates out as a **Wyrmscale Aura** trait that shapes the battlefield (bleeding briars, dark fog, magnetism, etc.). Every dragon has one; it's the single most important battlefield feature in the encounter.
- **Dragonseal.** Dragons magically mark creatures and lands as their own. To be "dragonsealed" is to become an extension of the dragon's might — targets of dragonseal take extra damage or effects specific to the species (e.g., an omen-sealed target can't treat allies as allies; a gloom-sealed target has psychic + cold weakness 3). Most dragon AoEs apply dragonseal on tier 1–2, and the payoff abilities key off it. Strip dragonseal with save-ends and Remove Affliction-style tools.
- **Domain.** If a dragon has occupied an encounter map for **1 week or more**, the whole map changes — overgrown and speed-reduced (thorn), haunted (gloom), steel-coated (crucible), soul-stagnant (omen), airless and unstable (meteor). This is a pre-encounter state, not a cost — plan around it or don't put the party in the lair.
- **Hoards.** Regular dragons hoard wealth partly as a hunting strategy — the better the treasure, the more adventurers walk in. Sample hoard contents by echelon: **1st** dragon components (scales/horns/venom glands), title *Dragon Blooded*, 1 wealth per hero; **2nd** Kuran'zoi Prismscale, Purified Jelly; **3rd** project notes in Vastariax for **Dragon Soul** armor, notes in Zaliac for a **Thunderhead Bident**; **4th** Blade of Quintessence, Page from the Infinite Library: Solaris.
- **Draco Nobilis.** The dragons in this chapter are *draco vulgaris* — terrifying, but not true dragons. True dragons can devour whole worlds and are a future-product problem.

## Shared dragon mechanics

- **Solo package.** Every dragon has **Solo Turns** (two non-consecutive turns/round) + **End Effect** (take N unresistable damage at end of turn to shed one save-ends effect on them). The End Effect tax scales with the dragon — 5 on the bredbeddle, 10 on the thorn/gloom/crucible, 15 on the omen, 20 on the meteor. Controllers who stack save-ends conditions force the dragon to bleed Stamina to shed them; conversely, dragons that dump Stamina into End Effect are accepting the trade to stay online, which is fine as Director tempo.
- **Solo Action (5 Malice).** Universal Malice feature on every dragon stat block: spend 5 Malice to take an **extra main action this turn**, usable even while dazed. This is the "break glass" button — save it for the round when a hero's big ability has the dragon locked down, and Solo Action punches through daze.
- **Wyrmscale Aura.** Each dragon's signature aura trait defines the encounter. Stand inside to suffer; stand outside to lose tempo.

## Thorn Dragon

Thorn dragons take root in the destitution of nature — drought, fire, flood, famine. Born of wood and bedecked in torturous thorns, with tangled briar wings and horns. They cultivate their lairs in lush farmlands or flourishing forests, engulf them in massive briar thickets, and choke out everything but their own brambles. Local folk either relocate or starve.

- **Conquerors of the Green.** Thorn dragons drive out existing flora and fauna. A thorn dragon over village farmland means famine or relocation.
- **Natural Curators.** No eye for worldly treasure — they hoard the wealth of the wild: uncut gemstones, minerals and ore, wood, oils, spices, and food (even food they won't eat).
- **Bestial Instincts.** Closer to beasts than to more powerful dragons; incapable of speech, driven by territorial instinct. Some settlements survive by dependency — trading the dragon its preferred hoard goods for meager food rations.
- **Territorial Brutes.** Defend their lairs by dragonsealing intruders, pushing and pulling them across briars, and bleeding them dry. When brambles aren't enough, they breathe poison.
- **Domain.** After 1 week, all surfaces on the map are covered in overgrowth: every non-dragon creature starting their turn on the map has speed **−2 (min 1)**, and any creature made **restrained** while on the ground is also made **bleeding**.

### Thorn Dragon

**Role:** (solo — no additional role)
**Tags:** level 2, solo, EV 48, dragon, elemental
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 95

**Flavor:** Size 3 briar-draconic solo. Speed 8, **Fly**, Stamina 250, Stability 6, poison immunity 5, Might +2 / Agility +3.

**Tactics:** The bleed/restrain/pull machine. **Withering Wyrmscale Aura** (2 aura) halves Stamina regain for non-dragons in it, and winded creatures take corruption on entry/turn-start — punishing heals keeps pressure on the party's support. **Virulent Breath** (10×1 line within 1 signature, Might test) deals 12/9/5 poison and on tier 1–2 **dragonseals** (save ends), with the rider that dragonsealed creatures take +1d3 damage on any d6/d3 roll — exactly the damage dice the dragon's own AoEs/Malice features roll. Open with breath, then everything that follows gets a damage bump. **Spinous Tail Swing** (Melee 2 charge, two targets) — 8/12/15 + **push 2/4/8**; spend 2 Malice for +1d3 and bleeding on A<2. Combo: push into **Bramble Barricade** walls (5 Malice) for line-of-effect denial + 1 damage/square and bleeding on forced entry. **Provoking Nettles** (trait) — once/turn, shift up to 5 squares through enemy spaces, first space costs the enemy 3 damage — no opportunity, through-move positioning. **Prickly Situation** (triggered) — any dragonsealed creature that shakes the seal gets **pulled 5 squares + restrained on A<2** — a self-protect clause that punishes the cleanser. Villain actions: **VA1 Briar Bindings** (4 burst) dragonseal-range restrain AoE; **VA2 Thorned Armor** — 3 damage to adjacent melee attackers + auto-Provoking Nettles; **VA3 Malign Thicket** — repeats Bramble Barricade twice free and, if Domain is in effect, every non-dragon takes 1d3 poison at turn start for the rest of the encounter. Key Malice: **Investiture of Verdure** (5 Malice, 10 burst maneuver) — forces dragonseal on everyone in burst, pulls each **up to 5 squares** toward dragon, and gives the dragon **5 temp Stamina per target pulled**; a heal + reposition in one button. **Affilictive Overgrowth** (7 Malice) — every enemy on map makes an Agility test for 12/9/5 poison + restrained/bleeding. Counterplay: keep a save-ends cleanser on the front line (to strip dragonseal before Briar Bindings compounds), avoid adjacency so Thorned Armor doesn't free-strike, and burn Bramble Barricade walls (fire weakness 5).

## Gloom Dragon

Where twilight spills long and cold and chilling fog hangs heavy through long autumns and winters — there you find gloom dragons. Wreathed in churning shadows, wrapped in glittering dark scales, they bring heavy fog and mist that manifests hallucinations, trapping prey in nightmares.

- **Thrill of the Hunt.** Lair in sprawling, murky wilds. Clever as ravens, cunning as hunting beasts; use mimicry and hallucinations to draw travelers deeper into the mists. Areas where gloom dragons settle have more nightmares than usual.
- **Intangible Treasures.** Collect voices, memories, and nightmares alongside material goods. Can speak, but only by repeating exactly what they've heard — a broken mirror from a dead lover fascinates one more than a perfect ruby brooch.
- **Unnerving Neighbors.** Instinct and curiosity; rarely reasoned with but can coexist with nearby settlements. Individual terrorizing over mass destruction — "don't follow voices into the fog" becomes a local folktale.
- **Languages.** Can mimic any sound they've heard, including sounds their targets' minds produce — a hallucinated loved one screams in *everyone's* ear at once, not just the target's.
- **Domain.** After 1 week, creatures on the map take **−2 on saves to end frightened**, and any frightened enemy who takes damage takes an extra **3 psychic**.

### Gloom Dragon

**Role:** (solo — no additional role)
**Tags:** level 4, solo, EV 72, dragon, elemental
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 97

**Flavor:** Size 4 fog-wreathed solo. Speed 8, **Fly, hover**, Stamina 350, Stability 2, psychic immunity 5, Presence +4 / Intuition +3.

**Tactics:** The hallucination/terror controller. **Gloaming Wyrmscale Aura** (3 aura) — dark fog that **concealment-hides the dragon from everyone inside**, deals 2 psychic/turn to enemies within, and **+3 psychic on all the dragon's abilities while any enemy is in it**. The 3-aura baseline + Shadow Skulk dark-cubes means the dragon is almost always fighting from concealment. **Breath of Brume** (4 cube within 10 signature, Agility test) — 14/11/6 cold + **dragonseal (save ends)** on tier 1–2; dragonsealed creatures gain **psychic weakness 3 AND cold weakness 3** and the area fills with magical darkness. **Phantom Tail Swing** (Melee 3 charge main action, two targets) — 10/15/18 psychic + **pull 2/4/6**; 3 Malice makes the pull **vertical** — drop heroes to death or into hazards. **Shadow Skulk** (trait, 1/turn) — shift full speed, leaving a **4-cube magical darkness** in starting space that lasts encounter; enemies who end turn in it and have I<3 are **frightened until end of next turn**. Pipeline: Breath of Brume → dragonseal + cold weakness → move away leaving Skulk cube → Phantom Tail pull into stacked weakness. **Visions in the Dark** (5 Malice, 10 burst maneuver) — dragonseal all, and every I<3 enemy **immediately makes a free strike against one ally of the dragon's choice** — turns the party against itself. **Encroaching Darkness** (1 Malice triggered, any creature within 10 moves) — relocate **two darkness cubes** up to 10 squares each. Cheap and always-on; reshape the Skulk-cube minefield every round. Villain actions: **VA1 Enveloping Umbrage** (5 burst) — pull 2/4/6 + frightened; **VA2 Pall of Nightmares** (10 burst) — psychic damage, dragonseals all, I<3 targets also dazed (save ends); **VA3 Absence of All Light** — dragon + 3 hallucinatory illusions appear in unoccupied spaces, each uses Breath of Brume, illusions share stats but have 1 Stamina, and the dragon can swap places with any illusion once per round. Key Malice: **Dread and Terror (3)** — bane on strikes into the aura; **Doleful Visions (5)** — four 2-cubes of nightmares in anywhere on map, Intuition test for 14/11/6 + dazed; **Phantasmagoria (7)** — 10 cube of phantasms, enemies entering take 6 psychic (8 if dragonsealed), **Intuition score −1 for potencies** encounter-long — stack this before dumping Pall of Nightmares. Counterplay: a cleanser who can strip frightened and dragonseal on the party's controller; AoE clears of Shadow Skulk cubes; and a light-source / concealment-pierce ability to strip the dragon's aura concealment (key to avoid Enveloping Umbrage one-shots). Save-ends cleansing is doubly important because Domain penalizes it by –2.

## Crucible Dragon

Metallic elementals touched by the toxic combination of fiery rage and ice-cold grief — often the result of failure in the pursuit of perfection. Each is a dark forge of abandoned creativity melted down in fires of spite, jealousy, and hate. Scales range from gleaming silver to blackened iron; long tail terminates in a hammer-like tip that can freeze and cool hot metal.

- **Bastions of Steel.** Lair high in the mountains, usually in peaks veined with iron ore. Consume vast amounts of iron, slag it in their bellies, and line a fortress of steel around themselves. A reserve stays in the gut for melting intruders.
- **Discerning Collectors.** Narrowly focused — Bynirak, the Rain of Ten Thousand Spears, collects only magic polearms; Joris'nyrathi, the Scorching Aegis, collects legendary shields. Hoards are armaments and armor, not gems or gold (except as melted filigree).
- **Flawed Pursuits.** Endlessly attempt to reproduce their most prized treasures, but only ever forge flawed copies at a fraction of original power — discarded projects and scrap surround their lairs.
- **Languages.** Older ones speak some Vastariax. Younger ones are usually silent, occasionally mumbling "not enough" and "mine" in Caelian.
- **Domain.** After 1 week: melted metal coats every surface. Non-dragon creatures starting their turn on the ground take **5 damage**, and **+5 more** if force-moved into a surface. Any enemy who uses **lightning damage** takes **1d6 + AoE to everyone adjacent** — a magnetism-chaos clause that punishes electrical strikers.

### Crucible Dragon

**Role:** (solo — no additional role)
**Tags:** level 6, solo, EV 96, dragon, elemental
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 99

**Flavor:** Size 4 metallic-forge solo. Speed 8, Stamina 450, Stability 6, fire immunity 6, Might +4 / Reason +3.

**Tactics:** The melted-metal brute/controller hybrid. **Magnetized Wyrmscale Aura** (3 aura) **pulls anyone wearing metal or slagged** 2 squares toward the dragon on first entry / turn start; **M<3 targets can't willingly move away**. This is an anti-martial zone. **Slag Spew** (10×2 line within 1 signature, Agility test) — 13/10/6 fire + **slagged (save ends)** on tier 1–2. Slagged targets take **2d6 fire / turn start** and, **on cold damage while M<3, are restrained (save ends)** — the dragon's own tail hammer deals the cold. **Forge Hammer Tail Slam** (Melee 3 main action, two targets) — 11/17/20 + **prone on M<2/3/4**; free strike against each slagged target knocked prone; **1 Malice → add 1d6 cold** (triggers slagged→restrained on M<3). Read: Slag them, hammer them, they fall, they can't stand, free strike, then cold triggers restrain. That's the loop. **Thermodynamic Flight** (1 Malice maneuver) — 2 burst self, 7 fire to each enemy in burst, then dragon shifts up vertically and flies until end of round. **Heat Buffer** (trait, 1/round on Thermodynamic Flight) — extends flight + 7 fire in a 4 cube below. **Hammer and Anvil** (1 Malice triggered, on flying turn start/move) — **drop + Forge Hammer Tail Slam with +4 damage per square descended** — this is the alpha strike; get to high altitude via Flight and drop on a cluster for catastrophic damage. **Polarize Aura** (1 Malice triggered, on two melee strikes this turn, 3 burst) — push 5/7/10 (tier 3 ignores stability) on size 2 or smaller — punishes melee swarm. Villain actions: **VA1 Heart of the Forge** (6 burst) — 4/6/8 fire + **frightened (save ends)** on I<2/3/4 — AoE debuff lead-in. **VA2 Subdermal Shielding** — damage immunity 6 refreshes at start of each round encounter-long but **only if dragon took no damage the current round** — the encounter's strategic lever: force-focus the dragon every round to strip it. **VA3 Polarity Chaos** (10 burst, Might test) — 16/13/7 + massive pull or push (10/8/5, ignoring stability on tier 1) — reposition the party + stacks of slagged + forced-movement damage on the domain. Key Malice: **Swordfall (3)** — the dragon falls-as-blade from the air into a 6×4 line for 7 damage, +4/square fallen to A<4 targets with restrained; **Shower of Blades (5)** — 6×4 line, 16/13/7 + bleeding; **Meltdown (7)** — every enemy starting turn on map is **auto-slagged** encounter-long until end of round. Counterplay: strip metal armor or accept the pull lever; keep the dragon honest every round to prevent Subdermal Shielding stacking; pay attention to the dragon's altitude (Hammer and Anvil's damage scales with square-count dropped); AoE heal the slag tick before it restrains on cold. Lightning damage is a trap — Domain hurts your own strikers.

## Omen Dragon

Known by many names: Reaper, Blight, Sorrow, Stillness, Vengeance, Grief. A coalescing of wayward souls — the stench of death solidified. They are death embodied, and they wield restless souls like weapons.

- **Life and Death.** The opposite of life isn't death — it's **stagnation**. Omen dragons form when hundreds of souls refuse the cycle and bind to an elemental. Stopping them means destroying what they've become; you cannot shepherd them back.
- **Life Outside Life.** Their dragonseal brings stagnation. Growth stops; creatures in their domain don't age. Lich-researchers sometimes lair near them on purpose. **New life can't begin** — no civilization, no crops.
- **Wicked Visions.** Bestow omens of the attacker's own death. Tarry too long near the dragon and risk fulfilling the prophecy.
- **Languages.** Speaks with the voices of all collected souls — Vastariax plus whatever languages those souls knew in life.
- **Domain.** After 1 week: Stamina regain on the map is **halved**, and when any souled creature dies on the map the Director **gains 1 Malice** + that creature **can't be raised** until the dragon dies.

### Omen Dragon

**Role:** (solo — no additional role)
**Tags:** level 8, solo, EV 120, dragon, elemental
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 101

**Flavor:** Size 5 soul-wreathed solo. Speed 10, **Fly**, Stamina 550, Stability 6, corruption immunity 6, Presence +5 / Agility +4.

**Tactics:** The death-clock. Core mechanic: **Deathcount**. Several abilities place a Deathcount on a target; at end of every turn, any target with a Deathcount **inside the dragon's Stagnant Wyrmscale Aura** (4 aura) has Deathcount reduced by 1. **When Deathcount hits 0, they die.** Only the lowest Deathcount on a creature counts; all Deathcounts are lost when the dragon drops to 0 Stamina. So the encounter is a race: whittle the dragon down before your timer expires. **Stagnant Wyrmscale Aura** (4 aura) — difficult terrain for enemies, no Stamina regain inside, and any dragonsealed creature inside without a Deathcount **gains Deathcount 12**. The dragon wants you dragonsealed and inside the aura; you want to be outside the aura or unseal yourself fast. **Corroding Breath** (5 cube within 10 signature, Agility test) — 18/14/9 corruption + **dragonseal** on tier 1–2. Dragonsealed creatures (**souled only** — constructs/oozes immune) look ghostly, have **Presence −1 for potencies**, and **can't treat other creatures as allies** (no healing buffs from allies on them). **Barbed Tail Swing** (Melee 4 charge, two targets) — 14/19/23 + **bleeding (save ends)** on M<3/4/5; 3 Malice bumps potency and **pulls each target up to 5 squares** — into the aura. **Death or Victory** (trait, 1/turn) — the dragon chooses a creature with a Deathcount; they can **take 1d6 damage + lose a recovery to INCREASE their Deathcount by 5**. This is the "buy time" decision heroes have to weigh. **Don't Turn Away** (1 Malice triggered, creature leaves aura) — dragon shifts full speed and **any dragonsealed creature made adjacent during the shift has Deathcount −1**. Leaving the aura costs you a Deathcount tick. **Repent!** (2 Malice triggered, dragonsealed damages dragon) — target chooses: **free strike against themself** or **gain Deathcount 5**. Tax for attacking while sealed. Villain actions: **VA1 What You Deserve** (5 burst) — pull + **assigns Deathcount 10/8/6** outright (no save, no seal required); this is the timer's start. **VA2 Souls of the Broken** (Ranged 10, 5 creatures) — P<5 rider on separate rolls: frightened / moves toward dragon / free strike against nearest ally. **VA3 So Long and Goodnight** (6 burst) — dragonseal all + **any target with a Deathcount has Deathcount reduced to 1** — the payoff. Land this with 3+ heroes at Deathcount 3–6 and they're dying next round unless they burn Death or Victory. Key Malice: **Detonation (5)** — 10 burst maneuver, every target dragonsealed + 9 corruption, dragon heals half total damage + targets lose dragonseal; **Rise and Fall (5)** — fly up 10, vertical-pull each creature in aura (4/6/10); **Burn It Right Down (10)** — map edges ignite purple flame; 5 corruption per square of flames an enemy enters, flames expand 1/turn. Counterplay: **Remove Curse–style dragonseal strip** is the single most important ability in the fight; stay outside the aura so Deathcounts don't tick; rush the dragon (all Deathcounts clear at 0 Stamina). Souled-only means swarms of constructs, oozes, or undead companions bypass the whole Deathcount clock — a worthy hook for a themed party.

## Meteor Dragon

They dwell in the cold, airless depths of space in lair-cities built of stolen kingdoms for centuries at a time. Then, without warning or mercy, a meteor dragon attacks. When a city grows too great, an empire too powerful, a culture too advanced — the meteor dragon descends. Cold and calculated violence: breach dams, trigger landslides, devastate granaries, knowing the chaos outlasts them.

- **Kingdom Killers.** Territory is hard to define — attacks span ages. Not every dead civilization is their work, but when one is, nothing lives.
- **Cities in the Sky.** Hoard infrastructure. Palaces, fortresses, monuments, and towers are stolen whole and fused into a labyrinthine spacefaring lair-city, secured by the dragon's glittering crystal dragonseal.
- **Avatar of the Infinite.** Innate mastery over gravity; smallest of the dragons by size but densest, radiating **cold airless void** wherever they go. Body infused with crystalline starlight convertible to white-hot energy rays.
- **Voidlight Annihilation.** The dragon's breath is *voidlight* — anathema to existence. Anything bathed in it is inverted and ceases to exist, regardless of material, strength, or potency.
- **Languages.** Not conversational. When they do speak, it's Vastariax and the First Language.
- **Domain.** After 1 week: every non-dragon creature on the map has **Stability reduced to 0 and auto-tier-1 on Agility tests**; any suffocating creature takes **+1d6 damage at end of round**.

### Meteor Dragon

**Role:** (solo — no additional role)
**Tags:** level 10, solo, EV 144, dragon, elemental
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 103

**Flavor:** Size 3 void-wreathed solo. Speed 15, **Fly**, Stamina 650, Stability 6, Might +5 / Agility +5 / Presence +5.

**Tactics:** The annihilation-tier boss. **Voidshroud Wyrmscale Aura** (1 aura, **scaling**) — enemies starting turn in it take **10 cold + suffocating**. Every time the dragon takes damage, the **aura grows +1 (max 5)** and the dragon deals **+5 damage the next time they use a rolled-damage ability**. The aura wants to be big; Field Collapse is the reset valve. **Gravity Well** (4 cube within 10 signature, Might test) — 20/16/10 sonic + **dragonseal** on tier 1–2. Dragonsealed target emits a **golden aura** and **takes +2 damage per square moved when falling or force-moved into an obstacle** — multiplies vertical pulls catastrophically. **Cosmic Tail Ray** (Melee 2 or Ranged 15 main action, two targets) — 15/21/25 holy + **weakened (save ends)** on A<4/5/6; **already-weakened target becomes dazed instead** — the second-strike upgrade. **Crescent Claws** (trait, 1/turn) — free strike on any target within 3, **ignores banes** against it until start of next turn. **Investiture of Gravity** (5 Malice maneuver, 15 burst) — dragonseal all, then **vertical slide each 10 squares** in a chosen direction; hitting an obstacle deals the full forced-movement damage. With Gravity Well's +2/square rider, this is unsurvivable for a low-Stamina hero in a constrained space. **Field Collapse** (triggered, on damage taken while aura ≥2) — **halves the damage**, every enemy in the aura takes **5 sonic + pulled 5 toward dragon**, aura resets to 1 — the reset/heal button. **A Hero Faces the Void** (2 Malice triggered, creature spends Heroic Resource) — 10/16/20 psychic + **frightened (save ends)** + **can't use the triggering ability while frightened** — ability-denial on the party's big finisher. Villain actions: **VA1 Impactful Arrival** (1-MILE burst, usable before the encounter begins) — 30 fire + knock prone on M<5. This is the meteor strike that opens the encounter narratively and damages the battlefield. **VA2 Burning Aurora** — encounter-long aura: every dragonsealed + weakened enemy in line of effect **loses 1 Heroic Resource at turn start**, dragon auto-casts **Cosmic Tail Ray with double edge targeting four creatures/objects**. The long-duration lever. **VA3 Voidlight Breath** (∞×3 line within 1, Agility test) — 25/21/15 + **annihilated** on I<6/5/4. An **annihilated** target retakes the test with potency −2 on subsequent hits; a creature reduced to 0 Stamina this way **dies and their soul is destroyed** — no raise, no reincarnate. The ultimate attack. Key Malice: **Liftoff (3)** — next Crescent Claws also **slides target 5 squares**, vertical-slides if dragonsealed; **Starfall (5)** — five 2-cubes anywhere on map, 20/16/10 holy + slowed/prone; **Event Horizon (10)** — black hole 1 cube within 20 squares, M<5 creatures + size-3-or-smaller objects **vertical pulled 2 toward it at round start** ignoring stability, anyone starting turn inside takes **Voidlight Breath effect**. Counterplay: prevent dragonseal stacking (strip every round); keep the Voidshroud aura small by clustering damage into a single round (Field Collapse only triggers at aura ≥2); don't use Heroic Resources adjacent to the dragon without a save-ends cleanser queued; spread out for Voidlight Breath and Event Horizon; treat the opening narrative strike (Impactful Arrival) as landscape-altering and plan terrain around it. This is a campaign-capstone encounter — six 10th-level heroes minimum, and they should still be scared.
