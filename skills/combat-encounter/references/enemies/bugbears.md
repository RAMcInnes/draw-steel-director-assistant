# Bugbears

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 52–55. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Bugbears are the *bu'gath* in Khelt — "the bearers of the great fear." Descended from early goblins who stepped into the wode centuries ago, fey magic twisted them into imposingly tall, hairy goblinoids whose footfalls make no sound. Many become legendary hunters and mercenaries; many more become reclusive and hide their whole lives in shadow; a rare few dive into their magic and become living nightmares.

- **Thrown Into the Fray.** Bugbears seldom trade or ally with outsiders and tenaciously defend their own homes — a reputation for being covert, insurgent warriors, when really they're just fighting to keep their solitude. When compelled to fight, they take the high ground and use their powerful arms to throw *anything* down at the problem: daggers, iron balls, each other, and sometimes the enemy at the enemy.
- **Goblin Bond.** Bugbears consider themselves goblinoids first and humanoids second. Shared origin with goblins and hobgoblins gives common ground when cloistered bugbears emerge from isolation.
- **Bu'gathic Magic.** Most bugbears have some control over inherent magic — a trick or two. Dedicated channelers evoke powerful shadow and rot magic, reshaping the appearance of the world and temporarily altering foes as defensive warnings. Bu'gathic magic also hides settlements behind shadowy veils in canyons and impenetrable swamplands.
- **Languages.** Most bugbears speak Caelian, Khelt, and Szetch.

## Throw/Catcher toolkit (shared)

Nearly every non-minion bugbear stat block has the same two maneuvers plus one triggered action, and they interlock into the signature "throw the enemy at the enemy" pipeline:

- **Catcher (free triggered).** When a size 1 creature/object is force moved into Melee 1, or a size 1 ally willingly moves into Melee 1, the bugbear grabs them.
- **Throw (maneuver).** Requires grabbed. Vertical push 3–5 (Channeler 3, Commander/Sneak 4, Roughneck 5). Allies don't take fall damage.
- The chain: ally force-moves next to a bugbear → Catcher grabs → Throw launches them onto an enemy. Also works with enemies the bugbear grabbed via their Signature or Malice. Roughneck's Haymaker (12–16) grabs *and* pushes one target, so it can self-fuel the next Throw.

## Bugbear Malice features

At the start of any bugbear's turn, Malice may be spent to activate one of the following features. Bugbears also have access to all **Goblin Malice Features (3–7 Malice)**.

- **Grab Iron Ball (3+ Malice).** For every 3 Malice spent, one non-minion bugbear acting this turn grabs an iron ball and uses a maneuver to throw it at a creature within 5 squares: damage = `8 – squares thrown`; if M<1, slowed (save ends).
- **Grab Javelin (5+ Malice).** For every 5 Malice spent, one non-minion bugbear grabs a javelin and throws it at a creature within 5 squares: damage = `12 – squares thrown`; if M<1, bleeding (save ends). While bleeding this way, any ally within 2 squares can use a **free maneuver to pull the bleeding creature up to 2 squares** — a cheap repositioning lever that folds into the Catcher/Throw pipeline.
- **Show Them the Great Fear (10 Malice) — villain action.** A bugbear infuses the encounter map with bu'gathic magic. Until end of encounter, all bugbears and allies have **doubled speed and can automatically climb at full speed while moving**. Additionally, any bugbear or ally's strike against an I<1 target also frightens them (save ends) and forces a full-speed straight-line retreat from the attacker. This is the "fear + mobility" button for a cloistered lair defense.

## Bugbear Roughneck

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 2, elite, EV 16, bugbear, fey, goblin, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 54

**Flavor:** The 1L wall of fur and tattooed arms at the front of the line. Stamina 109, Speed 6, M/A +2. Opens its own Throw chain without needing allies.

**Tactics:** **Haymaker** is the engine — Melee 1, two targets, and the 12–16 tier grabs one while pushing the other 2 squares, which combos directly into Throw next turn. 17+ upgrades the push to vertical. **Leaping Fury** (jump 5 before striking) is the opener when you need to bypass the front line to get onto a squishy controller: lands prone on M<1/2/3 depending on tier. **Drag Through Hell** (3 Malice, maneuver, requires grabbed) moves the roughneck at full speed while dragging the victim, dealing 2 per square and leaving difficult terrain behind them for enemies — position it to cross a choke point so the hero ends up away from their party *and* the party's chasing lane is slowed. **Flying Sawblade** (triggered) punishes any enemy who tries to force-move the roughneck: it uses Haymaker mid-flight, so knocking it back means eating a Haymaker and then falling. Counterplay: don't force-move it; bring stability so Throw/Drag fail; focus fire before it builds a Drag chain.

## Bugbear Sneak

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 2, elite, EV 16, bugbear, fey, goblin, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 54

**Flavor:** The shadow that finds your healer. Stamina 80, Speed 7, M/A +2. Built to open from hiding and to never be where you last saw them.

**Tactics:** **Sucker Punch** (Melee 1) grabs at every tier (A<1/A<2/auto) and **locks out triggered actions until the next round** — critical for disabling opportunity attacks and reaction-based defenders before the sneak peels away. If the sneak started the turn hidden from the target, it deals +4 damage; pair this with **Shadow Cloak** (3 Malice, 2-burst, Main action) which hits each enemy in the area, then **shifts the sneak up to their speed and lets them attempt to hide** — the Cloak is *both* the escape tool and the rearming of the hidden bonus for next turn. **Carving Dagger** (Ranged 8, two targets) bleeds on all tiers and the effect states *while bleeding this way, the target can't hide from the sneak or their allies* — so the sneak denies stealth-based counterplay while retaining their own. **Clever Trick** (1 Malice, triggered) redirects an incoming strike to another enemy within range of the attacker — cheap and bonkers for breaking focus fire. Counterplay: area attacks and reveal/detection effects, because as long as the sneak starts its turn hidden it keeps value-stacking the Sucker Punch.

## Bugbear Channeler

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 2, elite, EV 16, bugbear, fey, goblin, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 53

**Flavor:** The bu'gathic mage — shadow and rot magic, Stamina 60, Speed 5, R/I/P +2. The softest elite; hangs behind Roughnecks and Knightmares.

**Tactics:** **Shadow Drag** (Ranged 8, two targets) pulls 2/3/4 on tier and leaves difficult terrain behind each target for enemies — excellent for separating a hero from their pocket healer, or for setting up the Throw pipeline (pull into Catcher range of an adjacent ally bugbear). **Blistering Element** (3-burst) picks acid/cold/corruption/fire/poison on the fly and bleeds on M<0/1/2 — pick the damage type to exploit party weaknesses or dodge resists. **Twist Shape** (5 Malice, Ranged 5, one creature) is the signature terror move: on 12–16+ the target is **shapechanged (save ends)**, becoming slowed with fire weakness 10 as their limbs stretch and skin goes paper-thin — this is the bu'gathic "warning" flavor made mechanical, and it turns a melee hero into a crunchy fire-vulnerable piñata for the rest of the squad. **Shadow Veil** (triggered) halves damage on an ally within 5 squares *and* makes them untargetable until their next turn — one free "no" per fight for the priority target. Counterplay: break line of effect, close the range, and focus the channeler before it burns 5 Malice on Twist Shape.

## Bugbear Commander

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 2, elite, EV 16, bugbear, fey, goblin, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 53

**Flavor:** The veteran calling shots from the melee line. Stamina 80, Speed 5, M +2, R +2. Grants allies actions and conditions cleansing.

**Tactics:** **Inspiring Swordplay** (Melee 1, two targets) deals damage *and* grants an ally within 5 squares an edge on their next strike until the commander's next turn — the base turn enables another bugbear's next-turn burst. **You Next!** (Ranged 8) costs a main action but **lets a chosen ally move up to their speed and use a signature ability** — this is the big tempo lever: fire a Roughneck's Haymaker, a Channeler's Shadow Drag, or a Sneak's Sucker Punch a second time per round. **Fall Back!** (5 Malice, 5-burst) shifts each ally in the area up to their speed and lets them each use Throw — map-wide reposition that fuels the Catcher/Throw pipeline across the whole warband. **The Commander's Watching** (passive): any ally with line of effect to the commander can end one condition on themselves at the start of each of their turns — this is a save-ends/control-defeater that must be addressed by denying line of effect or killing the commander. Counterplay: focus the commander first, block line of effect with terrain, and spend finite stun/restrain effects on targets not adjacent to commander-sight.

## Bugbear Knightmare — minion

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 8, minion, EV 10 for four, bugbear, fey, goblin, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 55
**With Captain:** Gain an edge on strikes.

**Flavor:** The elite minion — heavy corruption, auras, used as bodyguards around a leader. Stamina 12 each, Free Strike 3, M +4 / P +4.

**Tactics:** **Corrosive Blade** (Melee 1) grabs on 17+ and can then vertical push 5. As a level-8 minion surrounded by level 2–5 heroes they bleed more heroes than they kill, but the auras are the point: **Bu'gathic Inspiration** gives any adjacent ally a +1 to power rolls, saves, or damage rolled as d6/d3 — *per knightmare adjacent*, so a captain with three knightmares around them gets +3 to everything relevant. **Magic Terror** mirrors the aura on enemies: −1 per knightmare adjacent. Position knightmares so their auras overlap onto a leader and also engulf the front-line hero. Counterplay: AoE that thins the bodyguard screen collapses both auras simultaneously, so the knightmare is the priority target for any 3-burst in the party.

## Bugbear Mob — minion

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 5, minion, EV 7 for four, bugbear, fey, goblin, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 55
**With Captain:** +2 damage bonus to strikes.

**Flavor:** Size 3 swarm of smaller bugbears behaving as one unit. Stamina 10, Speed 6, Stability 2.

**Tactics:** **Mug and Tear** (Melee 1) pulls 2/3/4 on tier and grabs on 17+. The effect clause says *if the target is pulled into the mob, that forced movement deals damage only at the Director's determination* — so a mob dragging a hero through its own space can pound them on the way in. **Swarm** lets the mob move through spaces as if 1L and occupy other creatures' spaces, with a free strike at the start of its turn against each creature sharing space. This is the board-state minion — uses up hero action economy without being easy to disengage from. Pair two mobs with a Commander: Fall Back! lets both mobs re-engage through the party's back line. Counterplay: forced movement to eject the mob from shared squares; multi-target attacks remain the best minion-clearer.

## Bugbear Snare — minion

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 5, minion, EV 7 for four, bugbear, fey, goblin, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 55
**With Captain:** +3 bonus to speed.

**Flavor:** Small stealth minion — Stamina 9, Speed 6, A +3. Built to open from concealment next to a heavier bugbear.

**Tactics:** **Cut 'Em Low!** (Charge, Melee 1) slows on A<3 at 17+. The kicker: if the snare started its turn **hidden from the target**, it auto-grabs on hit — and an already-grabbed target can be vertical pushed 4. Four snares + a Sneak (to keep hide value up) means four auto-grabs per round, piped straight into Throw or into a Commander's Fall Back! chain. With Captain's +3 speed they sprint 9, so placement before the first round defines how far they reach. Counterplay: reveal effects, AoE, and positioning heroes so no bugbear has a hiding spot within 9 squares before round 1.
