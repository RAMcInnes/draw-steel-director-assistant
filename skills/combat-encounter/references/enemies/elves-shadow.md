# Elves, Shadow

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 127–133. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Children of the Twilight Celestials, native to the parasitic manifold of Equinox ("Dusk") — a world of perpetual twilight, an unseen sun, no noon and no midnight. Equinox is dying, and Her Majesty Every Strike of Lightning a Lover Betrayed (the Shadow Queen) plans to rewrite Orden's topography to create a new home with shadow elves at the top. Scouts and assassins scour Orden seeking kidnap, assassination, theft, and "skullduggery" targets; other factions are exiled rebels, sages, or pacifists seeking a quieter resettlement. Every shadow elf is *incompatible with sunlight* — their skills, magic, and weapons falter under a true sun, but dissolve into darkness naturally.

- **In Equinox's Shadow / Of the Umbra.** Every non-minion *and* every minion in the line has the **Of the Umbra** trait: ignore concealment created by darkness, **damage weakness 3 in direct sunlight**, **damage immunity 3 while in concealment**. This is the single lever that defines the faction — *where you fight them* matters more than almost any other positioning choice. Pull the fight into noon daylight and the warband bleeds; let them keep torches snuffed and they soak 3 off every hit.
- **Manifold Weaponry.** Blades of solid shadow that wound across dimensions at once. Mechanically expressed as the **Extra Dimension** Malice feature — high Intuition is the counter to "shadow's bleed."
- **Brush Stalkers.** Domesticated dwimmerbeasts ridden into Orden. Newly-born don't survive translation, so the ones the party sees are *old* — moss-grown, antler-racked, cloven-hooved. Their glamor masks them as mundane deer; when it falls, they devour the light around them (Suneater aura).
- **Fractured Factions.** Not every shadow elf serves the Queen. Use this to seed the rare defector NPC — a sage, a pacifist, a rebel plotting a return. Cover for any "shadow elf who isn't trying to kill the party" story.
- **Duskcalling.** A "Lay" (humming + throat singing) aligns celestial bodies across manifolds. Can temporarily induce an eclipse in the manifold the duskcaller is performing in, letting allies gain the advantage — narratively the setup for a shadow elf *ambush* that engineers darkness on the map.
- **Languages.** Illyvric baseline; platoon leaders might know Caelian or Hyrallic.

## Shared shadow elf mechanics

- **Of the Umbra (universal trait).** On every shadow elf stat block — ignore darkness concealment, weakness 3 in sunlight, immunity 3 in concealment. Build the map around this: torches, windows, the time of day, and whether the duskcaller has had a turn to drop darkness cubes all move the encounter's difficulty meaningfully.
- **Climb movement (nearly universal).** Almost every shadow elf (minion to leader) has Climb. Vertical maps — cathedral rafters, cliff walls, canopy — favor the elves; flat open ground is the party's friend.
- **Deployments.** Sample encounter templates from the book: **Infiltrator Wing** (44 EV — eight cloaks, four dusk mages, one moondancer, one luminator), **Colony Guard** (64 EV — eight nightstrikes, eight snipers, one knightfell, one brush stalker, one noctis mage), **Kill Wing** (84 EV — eight dusk mages, two assassins, two panthers, one mournblade), **Shadow Incursion** (122 EV — sixteen cloaks, eight snipers, two moondancers, one luminator, one duskcaller, one eclipse).

## Shadow elf Malice features

At the start of any shadow elf's turn, Malice may be spent to activate one of the following.

- **Watch Me Disappear (3 Malice).** Each shadow elf acting this turn can attempt to hide as a free maneuver if they have concealment. With a duskcaller's darkness cube down, this is practically every elf on the map vanishing at once — setting up next round's ambusher/artillery edges.
- **Extra Dimension (5 Malice).** Every shadow elf strike against a target with **I<2** this turn tacks on **bleeding (save ends) or slowed (save ends)** in addition to the strike's regular effects. Low-Intuition heroes get stapled with conditions *per strike* — lean on this in rounds where multiple elves commit to the same target.
- **Home Is Where the Hurt Is (10 Malice).** Until end of encounter: every creature sees shadow elves in full color *but* shadow elves *no longer need* Of the Umbra's concealment bonus — potency of all shadow elf abilities **+2**, and enemy saves against shadow-elf-imposed effects require **8+** (instead of 6+). The nuclear option for the late-encounter turn where darkness has been dispelled; trades their darkness dependence for raw numbers.

## Shadow Elf Cloak

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 4, minion, EV 6 per 4, fey, humanoid, shadow elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 128

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Speed 8 Climb, Stamina 8, Might +3. **With Captain:** +2 speed bonus.

**Tactics:** **Stick and Poke** (Melee 1 signature) — 2/4/6 + **shift up to 2 squares**. The cloak's role is motion: dart in, poke, relocate into cover before free strikes land. Speed 8 (10 with captain) + Climb means a cloak swarm reaches virtually any hero on the map round one. Paired with a duskcaller's darkness cube, Watch Me Disappear lets them hide mid-swarm to re-stage next round's edges.

## Shadow Elf Sniper

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 4, minion, EV 6 per 4, fey, humanoid, shadow elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 128

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Speed 5 Climb, Stamina 7, Agility +3. **With Captain:** +2 damage bonus to strikes.

**Tactics:** **Lumina Arrow** (Ranged 7 signature) — 3/5/7; **Effect:** *next strike against the target gains an edge*. Snipers are edge-feeders, not damage-dealers. Four snipers tag four heroes in round 1 → every non-minion elf hitting those heroes the same round swings with an edge. Pair with an assassin (Ranged 15 high-damage follow-up) or a mournblade (Knife in the Dark into a tagged target) for a coordinated burst.

## Shadow Elf Dusk Mage

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 4, minion, EV 6 per 4, fey, humanoid, shadow elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 128

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Speed 5 Climb, Stamina 7, Agility +3 / Reason +2. **With Captain:** gain an edge on strikes.

**Tactics:** **Gloom Bolt** (Ranged 5 signature) — 2/4/6 + **slowed (save ends)** on A<2/A<3. Slow-stapler; eight dusk mages (the Kill Wing or Infiltrator Wing template) can effectively lock the whole party's movement. Gloom Bolt's Ranged 5 keeps mages out of melee — park them behind the knightfell for survival.

## Shadow Elf Nightstrike

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 4, minion, EV 6 per 4, fey, humanoid, shadow elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 128

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Speed 8 Climb, Stamina 8, Agility +3 / Intuition +1. **With Captain:** gain an edge on strikes.

**Tactics:** **Vault** (Melee 2 signature) — 3/5/7; **Effect:** the nightstrike *leaps over the target* into the unoccupied square on the other side. Vault past a defender to reach a backline, past an ally to relocate, past a pit — Melee 2 reach means the nightstrike never needs to end adjacent, so free-strike punishes don't land. Eight nightstrikes + one captain (Colony Guard template) is a leapfrog wave that always ends adjacent to the target the captain wants dead.

## Shadow Elf Luminator

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 4, platoon, EV 12, fey, humanoid, shadow elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 130

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Speed 5 Climb, Stamina 60, Stability 0, Intuition +3 / Presence +2.

**Tactics:** The mark-and-heal engine. **Lumina Mark** (Ranged 3 signature) — 8/12/15 lightning; **Effect:** *next strike against the target deals +5 damage*. Marks, then sits behind the knightfell while the rest of the warband cashes in. **Mourning Till Dusk** (3 Malice, 2-burst main action, allies only) — regains 6/9/12 Stamina + **each target gains an edge on next strike**; at tier 3 the Director also gets 3 Malice back, recycling the Malice economy. A luminator is the reason a shadow-elf warband outlasts an equally-costed encounter without healing. Focus-fire early or the luminator's Ranged 3 means it's always in tight formation.

## Shadow Elf Moondancer

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 5, platoon, EV 14, fey, humanoid, shadow elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 130

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Speed 7 Climb, Stamina 70, Stability 0, Agility +3 / Intuition +2.

**Tactics:** **Crescent Sweep** (Charge, Melee 1 signature) — 9/13/16; **Effect:** until end of current turn, *moondancer ignores opportunity attacks from the target*. Charge-in, strike, leave through the same square with no free strike; ideal for edge-feeding a luminator-marked backliner then exiting to cover. **Dissolve** (triggered on taking damage) — teleport up to **10 squares to a space with concealment created by darkness**. As long as a duskcaller darkness cube or Of the Umbra concealment exists somewhere on the map, the moondancer cannot be pinned. Counterplay: take out the darkness source *first*, then the moondancer has no landing pad.

## Shadow Elf Mournblade

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 6, platoon, EV 16, fey, humanoid, shadow elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 130

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Speed 5 Climb, Stamina 80, Stability 0, Might +2 / Agility +3 / Intuition +2.

**Tactics:** The knife-in-the-dark finisher. **Knife in the Dark** (Melee 1 signature) — 10/15/18; **Effect:** *mournblade is invisible to the target until the start of the mournblade's next turn*. Strike-and-vanish baked in. **Shadow Step** (maneuver) — if the mournblade has concealment, teleport up to 10 squares to a space with darkness concealment. The pipeline is obvious and devastating: edge-fed strike from concealment → invisible until next turn → Shadow Step to a new darkness cube → repeat. Pair with a duskcaller to keep darkness cubes refreshing. Counterplay: sunlight neutralizes the Shadow Step landing pads, reveal effects on invisible strip Knife in the Dark's Effect.

## Shadow Elf Noctis Mage

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 6, platoon, EV 16, fey, humanoid, shadow elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 131

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Speed 5 Climb, Stamina 70, Stability 0, Agility +2 / Reason +3 / Intuition +1 / Presence +1.

**Tactics:** Bane-stacker and dominate-lite. **Blotting Bolt** (Ranged 5 signature) — 9/14/17 + **bane on next strike**; **3 Malice:** upgrades to **double bane on next ability** (strike or maneuver/main action). Load a noctis mage's turn into the Bredbeddle/Judge pattern — 3 Malice into Blotting Bolt on the party's striker, shutting down their big-damage turn before it lands. **Enemies in the Dark** (3 Malice, Ranged 5, two enemies) — 8/10/13; on R<1/R<2/R<3 the *target* makes a free strike (tier 3: uses a *signature ability*) against one enemy of the noctis mage's choice. Two heroes attacking each other with a signature is the hard dominate-substitute. Counterplay: high-Reason heroes resist; cleanse before the mage's second turn lands a second Enemies in the Dark.

## Shadow Elf Panther

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 4, platoon, EV 12, fey, humanoid, shadow elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 131

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Speed 5 Climb, Stamina 70, Stability 0, Might +3 / Agility +2 / Presence +1.

**Tactics:** Cleaver with daze-synergy. **Dusk Cleave** (Melee 1 signature) — 9/13/16 + **bleeding (save ends)** on I<3; **Effect:** *panther makes a free strike against a creature/object adjacent to the target*. Position the panther between two heroes and every hit cleaves through both. **Bladestorm** (3 Malice, 2-burst main action) — 5 / 8 corruption + I<2 dazed / 10 corruption + I<3 dazed; **Effect:** *double edge on strikes against targets dazed this way*. Bladestorm round 1, Dusk Cleave on dazed targets round 2 with double edges chaining into cleaves. Counterplay: the panther is 70 Stamina with zero immunity — focus fire survives.

## Shadow Elf Assassin

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 6, platoon, EV 16, fey, humanoid, shadow elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 129

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Speed 5 Climb, Stamina 70, Stability 0, Agility +3 / Reason +2.

**Tactics:** The long-range spike. **Lumina Assault** (Ranged 15 signature) — 10/15/18; **Effect:** *next ability used against the target has a double edge*; **5 Malice:** each non-minion ally within 3 of the target makes a free strike. Ranged 15 means the assassin fires from nearly any map position; the 5-Malice clause turns a cluster of cloaks/mournblades around the target into a coordinated burst. **Splitbow** (2 Malice, 4×1 line within 10) — 5/10/12 + **bleeding (save ends)** on I<1/I<2/I<3; **Effect:** *push each target up to 4*. Clears clustered heroes off a control point or off a ledge. Pair with a sniper's edge-tag for a round 1 opener that deletes a squishy outright.

## Shadow Elf Duskcaller

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 5, platoon, EV 14, fey, humanoid, shadow elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 129

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Speed 5 Climb, Stamina 60, Stability 0, Agility +3 / Reason +3 / Intuition +2.

**Tactics:** The darkness engine. **Night Knife** (Melee 1 signature) — 9/13/16; **Effect:** *if duskcaller has concealment, target one additional creature*. **The Lay of Cor'thoroth** (2-cube within 3, maneuver) — fills the area with **darkness** until start of next turn; **2 Malice:** cube grows by 3 squares (→ 5-cube). The darkness cube is the lever the whole warband runs on: everyone inside gets concealment (→ immunity 3 from Of the Umbra), the moondancer has a Dissolve landing pad, the mournblade has a Shadow Step landing pad, and Watch Me Disappear lets the whole warband hide as a free maneuver. Protect the duskcaller like the luminator — without either, the faction's synergy collapses. Counterplay: a single *Daylight*-style reveal on the cube ends it; range the duskcaller early.

## Shadow Elf Knightfell

**Role:** defender
**Tags:** level 4, platoon, EV 12, fey, humanoid, shadow elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 129

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Speed 5 Climb, Stamina 70, Stability 0, Intuition +3 / Presence +2.

**Tactics:** The protector. **Suffusing Strike** (Ranged 3 signature) — 8/12/15 corruption + **taunted (EoT)** on R<2/R<3. A Ranged 3 taunt is unusual — the knightfell pulls aggro from 3 squares away instead of having to adjacent-cling. **Trick of the Eye** (Melee 2 triggered action) — when an enemy strikes an ally within 2, *target takes half damage, knightfell takes the other half*. Stand the knightfell next to the luminator/duskcaller/noctis mage and the warband's fragile back line soaks half damage instead of full. Counterplay: force-move the knightfell out of Melee 2 of the protectee *before* striking, or AoE around the protectee to hit multiple non-knightfell targets.

## Brush Stalker

**Role:** mount
**Tags:** level 4, platoon, EV 12, animal, fey, shadow elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 133

**Flavor:** Size 2, Speed 8, Stamina 60, Stability 3, Might +3 / Agility +2. A skeletal dwimmerbeast of glowing blue antler and moss, carrying an entire ecosystem on its rack. Glamor makes it look like a mundane deer; when the glamor drops, it devours light.

**Tactics:** The shadow elf mount. **Gore** (Charge, Melee 2 signature) — 7/10/13. **Reclamation** (2 Malice, 2-burst main action) — 4/7/10 corruption + **weakened (save ends)** on M<1/M<2/M<3. The mount's real contribution isn't damage — it's traits. **Suneater** — the 2-square area around the brush stalker is **devoid of light and provides concealment**, permanently. This is a *mobile darkness cube* that doesn't cost the duskcaller a maneuver and doesn't end at start of next turn. Where the brush stalker walks, shadow elves get immunity 3. **Wyrd Dyr** — while line of effect to the brush stalker exists, any **animal** except another brush stalker is **frightened**. Counters druid companions, paladin mounts, ranger pets, and any summoned animal. Counterplay: block line of effect, AoE through the Suneater aura (the shadow elves' damage immunity 3 still lets them tank one AoE comfortably), or focus-fire the brush stalker to collapse the mobile darkness node.

## Shadow Elf Eclipse

**Role:** (leader — no additional role)
**Tags:** level 6, leader, EV 32, fey, humanoid, shadow elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 132

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Speed 6 Climb, Stamina 180, Stability 1, Might +4 / Agility +3 / Reason +2 / Intuition +1 / Presence +2. Silver-haired, blade-bearing warrior-diplomat with glowing eyes — the field commander the Shadow Queen sends when she means it.

**Tactics:** The warband's capstone. **Manifold Blade** (Melee 1 signature, two targets) — 11/16/19 + **bleeding (save ends)** on I<2/I<3/I<4 (extra-dimensional wound); **2 Malice:** potency +1. Hits two targets, bleeds both, and the bleed ticks through save-ends saves that Extra Dimension may already have reduced to "8+ required." **Grasping Shadow** (Ranged 5 maneuver, three shadow-casting creatures/objects) — pull 5/7/10 + **slowed (save ends)** on I<2/I<3/I<4. A maneuver that pulls three heroes toward the eclipse is positioning gold — set up Manifold Blade's two-target Melee 1 by yanking three heroes into a clump, then sweep. **Put It Out!** (triggered, Ranged 10) — when an enemy within 10 uses an ability that **emits light** (including **fire or lightning damage**), the ability takes a **double bane**. The party's torchbearer and fire caster both hit walls simultaneously. **End Effect** — at end of each turn, eclipse can take 10 unresistable damage to end one save-ends effect (the leader escape valve). **Villain Actions:**
- **VA1 From the Shadows** (Ranged 5) — calls a **brush stalker** into an unoccupied space; each ally in range shifts up to speed and makes a free strike. Mid-encounter reinforcement + mass repositioning in one button.
- **VA2 Cast Away All Hope** (3 burst, each enemy) — target **loses all surges**; until end of round, allies ignore edges/double edges/nondamaging effects on *any* targets' abilities. The surge-stripper + debuff-cleanse-on-allies combo; save for the round the party has stacked buffs and is about to cash them in.
- **VA3 Umbral Hunger** (3 cube within 5, each enemy) — 7/12/15 corruption + **speed 0 (save ends)** on R<2/R<3/R<4; **Effect:** *area shrouded in darkness that creates concealment until end of encounter, and any enemy starting their turn in the area takes 5 corruption*. Speed 0 is a near-petrification for the whole area, and the darkness cube persists — this is how the eclipse permanently terraforms the map into shadow elf territory for the rest of the fight.

Counterplay: the eclipse's whole kit runs on darkness and the party's own light sources. Bring *sunlight* effects to trigger Of the Umbra weakness and neutralize the umbral cubes from VA3; remove the brush stalker summoned by VA1 before it anchors a mobile darkness zone; cleanse the VA3 speed-0 fast because Umbral Hunger becomes a tick-damage execution chamber. A hero with no light-emitting abilities sidesteps Put It Out! entirely — lean on weapons and physical damage when attacking the eclipse directly.
