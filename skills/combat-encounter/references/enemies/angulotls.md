# Angulotl

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 34–38. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Found in freshwater swamps, rivers, and rainforests, angulotls (ang-wa-LAH-tuls) are diminutive, brightly colored amphibious humanoids. Rather than defend their settlements and breeding grounds against hostile creatures, these poisonous frog folk prefer to establish their homes in places nigh-impossible for other humanoids to find — underwater caves and other enclosed spaces accessible only by water. Angulotls are happy to hide and watch a gaggle of noisy adventurers stumble past the entrance to their clutch without realizing it. But when threatened or pressed, angulotls can turn deadly.

- **Deadly Toxins.** Contact toxin on their skin gives them an edge in close combat; they also distill it into potent forms for coating blades and tipping darts.
- **Indicator Species.** Delicate physiology. Pollutants — especially runoff from magical experimentation — corrupt them into aggressive, confused creatures that attack anything, even other angulotls. Corruption sometimes grants strange abilities.
- **Moisture Dependent.** They breathe through their skin. They avoid dry areas and stay near freshwater unless it's raining.
- **Clawfish (q'ukutxal).** Trained partner-animals — moray-eel-like creatures with eight reptilian legs. Pull rafts, fight by grabbing and electrocuting.
- **Languages.** Most angulotls speak Filliaric.

## Wet (family effect)

Angulotls use a unique condition called **wet**. A wet angulotl doesn't provoke opportunity attacks by moving. A non-angulotl who is wet and ends their turn with no movement remaining slips and falls prone. Several angulotl abilities impose wet; a creature can also become wet by entering water, as the Director determines.

## Angulotl tactics (family)

Angulotls aren't generally aggressive. Confronted, a patrol exchanges a few blows before harrying retreat to their hidden villages. In defense of their homes they become fiercer and more determined, and try to force fights in or near water to exploit their amphibious nature and the wet effect.

Simple patrols: slippery captains leap into the backline and harass low-Stamina heroes while minion hordes keep higher-Stamina heroes engaged. Larger engagements: more defensive, protecting high-value units. Stay close and coherent to leverage support abilities.

## Angulotl Malice features

At the start of any angulotl's turn, Malice may be spent to activate one of the features listed on p. 34 — **Leapfrog** (3 Malice), **Resonating Croak** (5 Malice), and **Rainfall** (7 Malice). See the book for exact effects.

## Angulotl Cleaver

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for 4 minions
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 35

**Flavor:** A leaping, machete-swinging frog in warpaint, trailing the toxic slick of its skin. Fast, darty, and small enough to dismiss — until it lands in your backline.

**Tactics:** Jumps 4 squares to reach the softest target, swings once, hops away. Thrives adjacent to a captain for the damage bonus. Punishes anyone who grabs or melees it with passive poison.

---

## Angulotl Pollywog

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for 4 minions
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 35

**Flavor:** A tadpole-small, half-developed angulotl. Bounces in and nips at heroes, mostly a nuisance — until an ally swallows one whole to regenerate.

**Tactics:** Low damage, high mobility; shifts after each hit. Value is as a consumable — an adjacent angulotl can eat the pollywog to heal Stamina and become wet.

---

## Angulotl Dart

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for 4 minions
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 35

**Flavor:** A crouching frog-archer with a poison-tipped blowdart. Picks off the weakest target it can see from deep in the brush.

**Tactics:** Range 8 poison damage at a distance. Hits harder on wounded targets — gains an edge against anyone with less than full Stamina. Keeps back; relies on Toxiferous to punish melee pursuers.

---

## Clawfish

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for 4 minions, angulotl-associated
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 35

**Flavor:** A moray-eel body on eight reptilian legs, slithering through grass and up trees as well as it glides through currents. Trained to grab and shock enemies.

**Tactics:** Grabs and holds wet targets so Shocking ticks lightning damage at range 2. Pairs viciously with wave, rainfall, or any tonguelash that leaves enemies wet. Strikes gain an edge when beside an angulotl captain.

---

## Angulotl Needler

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, horde, EV 3
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 36

**Flavor:** A larger frog-archer with a longer blowgun and a quiver of poison tips. Patient, precise, and cruel.

**Tactics:** Extreme reach (Ranged 15) keeps the needler out of threat range. Spend Malice to weaken low-Might targets — ongoing poison damage each turn stacks quickly if heroes can't break line of sight.

---

## Angulotl Slink

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 1, horde, EV 3
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 36

**Flavor:** A sleek, knife-armed angulotl that leaps from cover, laps at heroes with a long sticky tongue, and leaves adhesive slime behind wherever it lands.

**Tactics:** Opens with Tonguelash (Melee 6) to pull a hero out of formation and soak them wet; teammates close on the isolated target. Hop To It repositions to cover and hides. Adhesive glues up any square it vacated, gating pursuit.

---

## Angulotl Wave

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 1, horde, EV 3
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 37

**Flavor:** A shaman-caste angulotl wreathed in motes of sun-magic. Commands light and toxic vapor equally.

**Tactics:** Refulgent Beams illuminate heroes, denying hide/invisibility and granting edge on all strikes against them — a devastating setup for the rest of the warband. Noxious Bubble drops a zoning 2-cube gas pocket near allies (they become wet, benefiting from slink/clawfish synergy); any hero who touches or damages it takes poison and wet.

---

## Angulotl Daybringer

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 1, leader, EV 12
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 38

**Flavor:** A gilded, sun-worshipping angulotl priest. Their throat expands into a radiant disc that blazes like a miniature sun, and pollywogs burst from their back on command.

**Tactics:** Anchor of the warband. Sun Lamp heals and speed-boosts every angulotl in 10 squares — use it whenever multiple allies are present. Acid Grasp punishes strikes against the daybringer with splash acid. Tongue Slap downshifts incoming strike tiers, making it survivable in melee. Villain actions escalate: **New Dawn** summons 4 pollywogs, **Plague of Frogs** jumps the whole warband forward with free strikes, and the capstone **It Is Day** dries the map, illuminates every enemy, ends wet on them (for acid damage), and grants every angulotl double edge on their next strike.
