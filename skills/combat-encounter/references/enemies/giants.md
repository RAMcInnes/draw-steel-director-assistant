# Giants

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 144–152. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Supremely well adapted to harsh environments, giants thrive in extreme conditions. Fire giants build blazing cities in the hearts of volcanic calderas while stone giants stalk silent mountain peaks; frost giants haul enormous prey out of the arctic tundra and hill giants squat across grasslands, sharing borders with humanoid communities. Most people go their whole lives blissfully unaware of the local tribe of giants living a valley over — giants keep to themselves, and a handful of sufficiently motivated giants can handle almost any other threat in their environment.

- **Adaptable and Emotional.** Giants physically resemble the territory they inhabit — a fleshy heat-giant in a volcano, a frost-rimed hunter in the tundra, a moss-crowned brute on the hills. Each kind embodies the dominant emotion of its habitat: fire giants blaze with passion, frost giants are stoic and proud, hill giants are convivial and cruel by turns, stone giants are quiet and patient.
- **Distant Cousins.** Humanoid scholars like to assume that all giants share ancestry with one another — and with the largest humanoids. Giants do not care for such theories, and humanoids who voice them in a giant's presence should have a ready excuse and a plan to run.
- **Fire Giants.** Hail from sweltering deserts and the volcanic hearts of the Wallgate Mountains. Passionate and easily angered, quick to extend affection and equally quick to rage. Caldera-spanning cities of fire and stone, legions, festivals of controlled burn.
- **Frost Giants.** Stalk arctic tundra hunting enormous prey. When not hunting or feasting they spar and boast of great deeds done by their ancestors. Strength comes from family bonds and pride of physical or intellectual feats.
- **Hill Giants.** The giants most likely to interact with smalls. Share the rolling plains and flatlands with humanoid neighbors; they can be friendly, they can be cruel, and they are *clever* despite the common slander to the contrary. A small village on a hill giant's good side has a powerful neighbor.
- **Stone Giants.** Mountain-dwelling and quiet. Mind their own business, dislike being drawn into other people's fights, and are deeply attuned to their stone environment — many can swim through solid rock.
- **Languages.** Most giants speak Khoursirian.

## Shared traits

- **Size 4.** Every giant stat block in the section is Huge; minions, elites, and the leader alike. Plan for sweep attacks, reach, and squeeze points on the battlefield.
- **Immunity by kind.** Fire giants have fire immunity, frost giants have cold immunity, stone giants take reduced damage from Agility/Might tests. Plan hero damage types accordingly.
- **Stability.** Most elite giants sit at Stability 10 (5 for lithe frost/fire variants). Shoving or proning them via force movement is not a cheap option.

## Giant Malice features

Malice spent by any giant acting this turn.

- **Hurl Landscape (3 Malice).** A giant rips a chunk of environment loose and hurls it — ranged area attack that deals damage and creates difficult terrain where it lands. The cheap Malice button: use it to convert environment into ammunition and to clog up hero movement corridors.
- **Titanic Tear (5 Malice).** A giant tears a fissure in the ground in a short line, with adjacent enemies punished as the earth splits. Excellent for splitting a hero party across a map seam and for setting up a Pillar / Pillar-adjacent Stone Giant play.
- **Growing Rage (7 Malice).** A giant swells with fury until end of encounter — gains bonus damage and an edge on Might tests. Spend on the Chief or a Red Fist when the fight is going long and you want the damage curve to keep rising instead of flattening.

## Fire Giants

Aggressive, volcanic-born, emotional — every fire giant's kit burns heroes who get close and rewards giants for stacking bodies and flames together.

### Fire Giant Fireballer — minion

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 5, minion, fire giant, giant
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 151

**Flavor:** Smoldering apprentice, size 4 like every giant, with a passive **Searing Skin**: any enemy who ends a turn adjacent takes fire damage.

**Tactics:** **Blazing Leap** is the minion's whole value — a jump-and-slam Signature that deals fire damage in a small burst around the landing square. Four fireballers leaping into a clustered hero formation deposit one minion, one chip-damage AoE, and one ring of searing-skin retaliation per landing. Use as openers when heroes are stacked; scatter the party before the Chief or a Red Fist charges in.

### Fire Giant Red Fist

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 8, elite, fire giant, giant
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 150

**Flavor:** Heavy-weapon elite in blazing plate, size 4, Stamina ~240, Speed 8, Free Strike 10. The iron bodyguard of a fire giant formation. Kingdom-of-volcano body — **Searing Skin** on contact.

**Tactics:** **Flaming Punch** (Signature, Melee) is a high-damage single-target strike that also leaves a burning lane — a trail of burning ground squares that each hero in its path steps through. **Caldera** (3 Malice) converts a burst around the Red Fist into difficult terrain scorched ground that deals fire damage to each enemy inside when used and each round they remain. **Blazing Leap** (Maneuver) jumps up to five squares while dealing fire damage to the landing zone. **Heat and Pressure** (triggered action) lets the Red Fist give free strikes to an ally within distance when an enemy starts their turn near the fire giant. **Guardian Block** (triggered) soaks a hit aimed at an adjacent ally — this is a defender-flavored brute, built to escort the Chief or a Lightbearer and punish anyone who tries to skip them. Counterplay: cold damage, ranged kiting, don't cluster for the Caldera.

### Fire Giant Lightbearer

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 8, elite, fire giant, giant
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 146

**Flavor:** Clergy/flame-keeper elite, size 4, Stamina 200, Speed 8, Free Strike 9. Carries a consecrated torch whose light is a weapon and a blessing in one.

**Tactics:** **Flamelash** (Signature, ranged line) damages along a line and leaves a glowing mark on hit targets — other fire giants get extra damage or an edge versus anything marked. **Living Blaze** (3 Malice) buffs the whole fire giant line by adding fire damage to their strikes and granting them an edge for a round. **Travel By Fire** is the key positional: the lightbearer can teleport into any burning square within range, letting them ride their own Flamelash trails or the Chief's Burning Kick ground to cross the map. **Emergency Beacon** (triggered) grants an adjacent bloodied giant healing at a cost — use this to keep the Chief or Red Fist on the board past the threshold where the party expects the win. **Healing** action for out-of-combat reset. Counterplay: the Lightbearer dies fast if heroes can reach them before a Red Fist screens — burst them down early or the fight's damage curve keeps climbing.

### Fire Giant Chief — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 9, leader, fire giant, giant
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 149

**Flavor:** Size 5, Stamina 240, Speed 10, Stability 10, Free Strike 9. Fire immunity. Lava-blooded warlord of a volcanic city. **Scorching Skin**: any enemy who grabs or is grabbed by the chief takes fire damage and is weakened (save ends).

**Tactics:** **Rolling Fist** (Signature, Main action, Melee/Ranged area, 3-cube within 4) damages every enemy and object in the area and — on the tier's save line — applies damage, knocks prone, and weakens. **Burning Kick** (Main action, Charge — Melee) lets the chief move straight through up to two creatures or objects, damaging and pushing each. **Lava Pillar** (3 Malice, Maneuver, 3-cube within 10) drops a burning pillar that deals fire damage in an area and leaves difficult terrain. **Fuel the Fire** (1 Malice, triggered) — when a fire giant ally within 12 squares makes a strike, the chief pumps it into a double-edge Signature that also deals extra fire damage.

Villain actions ratchet the tempo:
- **Forward! (VA1).** The chief shifts up to their speed and makes a free strike; any enemy who starts a turn within 1 square of the chief's lane takes damage from the burning wave.
- **Burning Legion (VA2).** Call in a squad of five fire giant fireballers from unoccupied spaces within range. This is the summon button — a mid-fight wave that re-floods the board with minions.
- **All to Cinders (VA3).** A huge fire burst from the chief. Every enemy in the area takes heavy fire damage; anyone reduced by this burst also affects allies near them with a chained secondary strike. The finisher.

Counterplay: cold damage, ranged focus fire, don't let minions pile up before VA2 refills them, break line of sight to interrupt Fuel the Fire.

## Frost Giants

Stoic hunters who fight as skirmishers and artillery — mobile, patient, and at their best when they can keep a cold ring of ground around themselves.

### Frost Giant Snowblaster — minion

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 4, minion, frost giant, giant
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 151

**Flavor:** The ranged minion — size 4, Speed 7, hurls bolts of packed snow and ice at distance.

**Tactics:** **Cold Bolt** (Signature, Ranged) is straightforward cold damage with tier effects that slow heroes. Spread four Snowblasters around the back of the map as a steady chip-damage and slow field; pair with a Wind Sprinter or Storm Hurler who wants enemies slowed before they trigger reaction abilities. The minions are fragile — keep them behind terrain or behind a Red Fist equivalent.

### Frost Giant Wind Sprinter

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 8, elite, frost giant, giant
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 147

**Flavor:** Size 4, Stamina 200, Speed 10, Stability 3, Free Strike 8. Leaner and faster than most giants — blade runner of the tundra.

**Tactics:** **Cold Axe** (Signature, Melee/Ranged) damages plus applies a bleeding cold effect that ticks when the target moves. **Wizard Surge** (3 Malice) grants the wind sprinter an edge on strikes and adds bleeding cold damage to hits until end of their next turn — spend when a hero is already exposed. **Ice Dance** (Maneuver) lets the wind sprinter shift through enemy squares and leave each shifted-past hero prone. **Crash Underfoot** is the mobility punisher: when the wind sprinter moves at least three squares in a straight line and ends adjacent to an enemy, the enemy falls prone. **Kingdom of Isolation** — the wind sprinter is surrounded by a natural bane zone in which any enemy starting a turn within 2 squares makes the next strike with a bane. Counterplay: grab them, slow them, deny straight-line movement.

### Frost Giant Storm Hurler

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 8, elite, frost giant, giant
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 152

**Flavor:** Size 4, Stamina 180, Speed 7, Stability 5, Free Strike 9. Axe-wielder who throws storm-wrapped javelins of ice.

**Tactics:** **Ice Javelins** (Signature, Ranged area) damages a line and leaves slick frozen ground in each square the javelins cross. **Flower of Frost** (3 Malice) places a frozen burst on the battlefield that deals cold damage and applies slowed/restrained tier effects to enemies inside it. **Ice Dance** (Maneuver) — same cross-the-party ability as the Wind Sprinter version. **Frozen Retribution** (triggered action) — when an enemy within range hits a frost giant ally, the Storm Hurler returns cold damage. **Kingdom of Isolation** (passive) — same bane zone described above. Counterplay: close the distance fast, break line of effect to the javelin arcs, don't cluster inside Flower of Frost.

## Hill Giants

The giants most likely to share borders with humanoids. Clever in their own way, and violent when they decide to be.

### Hill Giant Mosstooth — minion

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 4, minion, hill giant, giant
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 151

**Flavor:** Size 4, moss-crowned minion brute. Passive **Distracted** — when this minion is the target of an ability, the triggering creature is also distracted (it's a dumb punching bag but the targeting hero pays a cost for swinging).

**Tactics:** **Swing** (Signature, Melee) — cheap, big melee damage. Crowd four mosstooths around the front line; they aren't precise, they aren't fast, they eat hits and return huge damage on whoever picks them off.

### Hill Giant Clobberer

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 7, elite, hill giant, giant
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 148

**Flavor:** Size 4, Stamina 200, Speed 6, Stability 8, Free Strike 8. The quintessential big dumb brute — a giant with a club and a bad attitude, wired to drag a single hero through the terrain.

**Tactics:** **Clobberin' Club** (Signature, Melee) damages and knocks prone. **Stomp** (3 Malice) quakes a burst around the clobberer dealing damage to each enemy in the area; enemies can't stand from prone until their next turn — combos off Clobberin' Club. **Hill Quake** (Maneuver) — each enemy taken down by a major line or an Agility test. **You Ain't Getting Away** (Triggered action) — when an adjacent enemy tries to move away, the clobberer grabs them and makes a free strike. **Destructive Path** lets the clobberer tear through terrain when they charge, paying a small Stamina cost to wreck a wall or object on the way to their target. **Distracted** (passive) — same "hits to me are also bad for you" clause as the minion version. Counterplay: don't end your turn adjacent, save a push or teleport to break the You Ain't Getting Away lock.

## Stone Giants

Quiet, patient, stone-bonded. Most stone giants can **swim through stone** — they can't drag allies underground with them, but they treat walls and floors as terrain to move through and hide in. Stoneshaker / Stonebreaker Flesh: half damage from Agility and Might tests.

### Sand Stone Giant — minion

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 5, minion, stone giant, giant
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 151

**Flavor:** Size 4 minion who lurks buried in loose sand until the moment of ambush. Burrow movement (sand).

**Tactics:** **Buried in Sand** (Signature) lets the sand giant pop out of a sand square adjacent to a hero and strike — an ambusher-style opener as a minion. **Stoneshaker Flesh** (passive) halves damage dealt by Agility/Might-test abilities. **Stone Swim** — burrow through sand, don't drag others along. Use four of these to seed a desert map: heroes step onto sand tiles and get ambushed one at a time. Counterplay: concentrated area damage rather than precise picks; avoid the loose ground.

### Basalt Stone Giant

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 8, elite, stone giant, giant
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 146

**Flavor:** Size 4, Stamina 207, Speed 6, Stability 10, Free Strike 8. Rune-carver elite — blades inscribed with singing stone sigils.

**Tactics:** **Razor-Sigiled Blade** (Signature, Melee) damages and applies a rune-echo effect that deals secondary damage when the target moves or uses an ability. **Cobblestone Shape** (Maneuver) throws up a temporary cluster of difficult terrain cobbles in an area, and enemies who end their turn inside take an additional strike. **Resonate Rune** (triggered action) — when the basalt giant is hit, the runes on their blade flare and return damage to the attacker within distance. **Stoneshaker Flesh** and **Stone Swim** shared passives. Play as the zone controller of a stone giant warband: place Cobblestone, then force heroes into it with a Granite Stone Giant's Pillar.

### Granite Stone Giant

**Role:** defender
**Tags:** level 8, elite, stone giant, giant
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 147

**Flavor:** Size 4, Stamina 247, Speed 7, Stability 10, Free Strike 8. The stone giant defender — slow, enormous Stamina pool, forces heroes to deal with *them* or deal with consequences.

**Tactics:** **Jagged Stone Club** (Signature, Melee) damages and applies weakened. **Long Burst** (3 Malice) a larger Melee burst against each enemy adjacent to the granite giant. **Castle Stone Shape** (Maneuver) raises a wall of stone in an adjacent line — uses the existing floor to build battlefield terrain. **Pillar** — raises a size-2 pillar in an adjacent square; any enemy in the square is lifted on top and gains falling-prone risk. **Stoneshaker Flesh** and **Stone Swim** passives. This is the crowd-shape elite: Pillar + Castle Stone Shape let a single granite giant redesign a map mid-fight to protect a Basalt's rune-zone or a Marble's sling-lane.

### Marble Stone Giant

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 8, elite, stone giant, giant
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 147

**Flavor:** Size 4, Stamina 207, Speed 6, Stability 10, Free Strike 8. Artisan-statued elite — polished marble hide, slings enormous shards.

**Tactics:** **Marble From a Great Sling** (Signature, Ranged area) hurls a worked stone in a cube area, damaging each enemy and object, and — critically — **ends save-ends effects on the marble giant that tick at end of turn after the strike resolves**. The hexer self-cleanse. **Polish Stone Shape** (1 Malice, Maneuver, cone) makes the ground in the area slick: any enemy who starts their turn there falls prone and slides back. **Break Armor** (triggered) when the marble giant takes damage, they reduce it and splash damage onto adjacent enemies. **Far Flung** (triggered, 1 Malice) when a prone enemy tries to stand up within the giant's reach, the giant strikes them with a double edge and keeps them down. **Stonebreaker Flesh** (passive). Counterplay: focus the save-ends debuffs expecting them to be wiped by the Signature; don't cluster beneath the sling's arc.
