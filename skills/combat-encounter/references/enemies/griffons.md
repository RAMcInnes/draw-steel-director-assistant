# Griffons

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 166–167. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Eagle-headed, lion-bodied apex air predators. A griffon's hunting range spans hundreds of leagues and they consider any trespassing humanoid a threat — or a meal. They nest alone or in pairs on inaccessible cliffs, treetops, and mountainsides, laying up to three eggs in a three-month brooding season each spring.

- **Coveted Mounts.** Wild griffons can't be captured without casualties, but those hatched and hand-raised from eggs bond loyally to their handlers. Some militaries and city guards field griffon cavalry; griffon eggs fetch steep prices. Daring poachers raid wild aeries and are usually killed by the parents — enough die that many regions have outlawed egg poaching outright.
- **Inaccessible Aeries.** Nests are hidden in enormous treetops, cliff-clinging thickets, or sheltered nooks unreachable without flight. Chicks hatch after forty days, learn to fly forty days after that, and strike out to find their own territory within days of their first flight.
- **Rare Breeds.** The common leonine-eagle griffon shares the sky with falcon-panther, bearded-vulture × clouded-leopard, and striped condor-tiger variants. Scholars disagree whether these are natural or magically fashioned, and pay well for evidence of undiscovered breeds.

## Shared traits

- **Fly.** Every griffon in this section flies. Plan for vertical movement, line-of-effect across a 3D battlefield, and kite-impossible positioning.
- **Beast of Prey.** A creature grabbed by a griffon has a double bane on the Escape Grab maneuver. Once a griffon grabs you, you're going up and you're going somewhere you don't want to go.
- **Steady.** Power rolls that would knock a griffon (or their rider, on the mount variant) prone take a bane. Forced-movement-to-prone strategies are blunted.

## Griffon Malice features

Spent at the start of any griffon's turn.

- **Swoop (3 Malice, Maneuver, Self).** The griffon flies up to their speed and makes a free strike against each creature who makes an opportunity attack during the movement. The "punish defenders for reacting" button — turns any attempted lockdown into a tax on the lockers. Cheap and nearly always worth it.
- **Piercing Cry (5 Malice, Maneuver).** One griffon acting this turn shrieks at a single enemy within 5 squares; target makes an Intuition test and is frightened save-ends or EoT on tier 1/2. Burst on the party paladin or the hero who stabilizes morale.
- **Wildwinds (10 Malice).** Until end of encounter, each creature who isn't flying or mounted on a flier takes −3 to stability, and any forced-movement effect against them pushes them an additional 5 squares. The stakes-raise for a final act — combine with the Griffon's Crack the Earth or the Striped Condor's Power Wing Buffet to send grounded heroes flying far across the map.

## Griffon — elite mount

**Role:** mount
**Tags:** level 2, elite, beast, griffon
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 167

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 80, Fly 9, Stability 2, Free Strike 5. The standard leonine-eagle variety. Trained as military or city-guard mount; deadly as a wild predator; essential as a scene element.

**Tactics:** **Claw Swipes** (Charge, Melee 1, two targets) damages and lets the griffon shift 1/2/3 squares per tier after the strike — hit-and-fly incarnate. *If used as part of the Charge main action, the griffon can grab one target.* This sets up the whole combo: charge in, grab a hero, fly away. **Crack the Earth** (Maneuver, 3-cube within 8 *requires flying + a grabbed target*) — fly halfway to the ground and drop the grabbed target into an impact crater. Tier 3 adds a<2 push 4 + prone on adjacent enemies. The dragon-on-the-scale kill combo: grab the healer, fly into the air, drop them into the party. **Wing Buffet** (3 Malice, Maneuver, 4×2 line) pushes each enemy 3/4/5 with vertical push on failed Agility saves — grounded heroes leave the ground. **Zephyr Feint** (1 Malice, triggered action) — when the griffon takes damage, halve it, ignore any nondamaging effects, and shift up to 2. Reaction nearly impossible to lock down. **Beast of Prey** + **Steady** passives. Counterplay: kill the grabbed-hero drop before it resolves (interrupt the rise); break line of sight; stay near the ground where Wildwinds doesn't pile up.

## Striped Condor Griffon — elite brute

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 2, elite, beast, griffon
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 167

**Flavor:** Size 3, Stamina 100, Fly 7, Stability 3, Free Strike 5. The tiger-condor variant — larger, heavier, wings wider. Hunts by gliding circles overhead, then collapsing out of the sky.

**Tactics:** **Violent Thrashing** (Signature, Melee 2, two targets) — pushes both targets, with *vertical* pushes on the secondary target at every tier, plus prone at tier 3. The grab-two-heroes-and-throw-one-upward ability, done in one action. **Bound Ahead** (5 Malice, Main action) — shifts up to speed along the ground in a straight line; each enemy who comes adjacent during the shift either takes 5 damage or is knocked prone. Ground-level board sweep, useful when Wildwinds is active because the prone effect is doubly punishing. **Power Wing Buffet** (3 Malice, Maneuver, 5×3 line) — pushes 2/4/6 and converts to vertical on M<0/1/2 per tier. A bigger, harder Wing Buffet — heroes end up in the air with nowhere to go but down. **Circle and Strike** (Triggered action, Ranged 5) — when the griffon flies *directly above* a creature within 5, they dive, taking no fall damage, dealing 3 damage per square dove, and grabbing or proning at a<2. The overhead-diving reaction — discourages running *under* the condor as cover. **Banded Predator** — can attempt to hide even while observed, and can attempt to hide at end of turn whenever no enemy has line of effect. The striped pattern works; a hidden size-3 flier is nightmarish. **Beast of Prey** + **Steady**. Counterplay: don't end a turn directly beneath the condor; deny line-of-effect breaks to prevent hide attempts; bring something with reach to punish Bound Ahead.
