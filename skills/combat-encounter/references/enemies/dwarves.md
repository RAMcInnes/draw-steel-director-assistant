# Dwarves

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 105–111. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Dwarves are children of the Elder God Ord — silico-organic hybrids whose flesh is infused with stone, physically denser than humans or elves. Orden-renowned as engineers and technologists, they inherited pneumatic steam power and mechanized suit lore from their long-extinct elder siblings, the **steel dwarves**. Most of the stat blocks in this section are the **press-gangs of Kal Kalavar** — dwarves who, under Ajax's rule, capture prisoners for forced labor or the Body Banks. Every ability is designed to pipeline captives out of combat alive.

- **Restraint Pipeline (the shared clause).** Nearly every non-minion dwarf signature carries the same effect: **"A target restrained by a dwarf can be force moved by this ability. This forced movement doesn't end the restrained condition unless the Director determines otherwise."** This turns restrain-then-push into the whole squad's primary trick — one dwarf restrains, every other dwarf's abilities then drag the captive across the board without freeing them. Mix platoons freely; the clause stacks.
- **Press-Gangs of Kal Kalavar.** Dwarf marauder lords wear salvaged valok communication arrays like mohawks — giving them magnetomancy to shape and levitate metal. They lead press-gangs who deliver captive bodies to Ajax for forced labor or body-bank harvest. *Ajax Will Pay Well for These Specimens* is the marauder lord's signature villain action for a reason.
- **Stone Whisperers.** Dwarves born with an innate talent to communicate with stone — taken young to a secretive enclave, trained to manipulate stone with only whispers. Legends warn of stone *singers* who nearly ended the world; modern whisperers stay quiet out of fear of their own potential.
- **Servitor War Walkers.** Mechanized valok-derived constructs used for public transit in dwarven cities and as elite mounts in war. Three size-1 allies can occupy the same space atop one war walker, with cover.
- **Languages.** Most dwarves speak Caelian and Zaliac.

## Dwarf Malice features

At the start of any dwarf's turn, Malice may be spent to activate one of the following features.

- **Breaching Charge (3+ Malice).** For each 3 Malice spent, a dwarf destroys one adjacent object or square of wall; each enemy adjacent takes the object's Stamina in damage (3 wood / 6 stone / 9 metal). Use to collapse cover a hero is hiding behind *and* damage them for doing so — wall-breaking as AoE opener.
- **Rappelling Barrage (5 Malice).** Each dwarf acting this turn can auto-climb at full speed while moving, with one free strike at any point during the movement. Lair defense for cliff-lined, multi-level press-gang ambushes.
- **Snaring Line (7 Malice, 10×1 line within 10, Main action).** Agility test; ≤11 is 8 damage + restrained (EoT), 12–16 is 6 damage + slowed (EoT), 17+ no effect. The line **remains until end of encounter** — any enemy who enters or starts their turn there repeats the test. Map-wide restraint zone that feeds the restraint pipeline for the rest of the fight. Can't be used by minions.

## Dwarf Axethrower — minion

**Role:** defender
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, dwarf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 106
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to Stamina.

**Flavor:** Line-holder minion. Stamina 7, Speed 5, M/I +1. Locks down one hero's reaction economy per hit.

**Tactics:** **Whistling Axes** (Melee 1 or ranged 5, one target per minion) — 1/2/3 damage, but the effect is the real value: **the target can't use triggered actions until the start of the next round**. Four Axethrowers can disable the reaction-heavy defender *and* pick off weaker targets at range in the same turn. At 17+, an ally adjacent to the target gets a free strike. With Captain's +2 Stamina and the restraint clause on nearly every other dwarf, use Axethrowers to lock out a paladin's Intercept / shield bash reaction before the Warden or Shieldwall steps in for the heavy hit. Counterplay: kill the Axethrower screen early to re-enable the defender's reactions.

## Dwarf Catchpole — minion

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, dwarf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 106
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to Stamina.

**Flavor:** The melee capture specialist. Stamina 7, Speed 5, M +2. Built to finish the restraint-pipeline chain.

**Tactics:** **Maul** (Charge, Melee 1, one target per minion) grabs or prones at 17+, and — critically — **if the target is already restrained, it takes +2 damage**. Catchpoles are the payoff for every dwarf who set up the restrained condition first. Four Catchpoles ganging on one hero already restrained by Trapper/Warden/Snaring Line deal a compounded beating. Counterplay: free the restrained condition before the Catchpole turn cycles.

## Dwarf Driver — minion

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, dwarf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 106
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to Stamina.

**Flavor:** Mobile skirmisher minion, Speed 7. Stamina 6, M +2, A +1.

**Tactics:** **Handaxes** (Charge, Melee 1 or ranged 5, one target per minion) pushes 1/2/4 and carries the restraint-pipeline clause — so a restrained hero can be pushed without losing restraint, and the Driver is the cheap "relocate the captive" tool. Four Drivers can drag a restrained hero 16 squares across a round while other dwarves pile on. Counterplay: force the restrained hero's save before the Driver chain fires.

## Dwarf Hunter — minion

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, dwarf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 106
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to Stamina.

**Flavor:** Snaring-javelin minion for dragging heroes out of formation. Stamina 6, Speed 5, M/A +1/+2.

**Tactics:** **Snaring Javelin** (Ranged 5, one target per minion) pulls 1/2/4 and carries the restraint-pipeline clause. The best minion for isolating a healer or controller from their front line — pull 4 squares then hand off to the Catchpole / Warden who is now adjacent. Four Hunters on one round can relocate an entire back-line. Counterplay: stand adjacent to multiple front-liners; forced movement over 2+ squares triggers reactions from allies.

## Dwarf Gunner

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, EV 6, dwarf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 107

**Flavor:** Portable-ballista crew chief at range. Stamina 26, Speed 5, A/I +2/+1.

**Tactics:** **Portable Ballista** (Ranged 10) pushes 1/3/5 and — if the target ends adjacent to an object or wall after the push — restrains them until end of their next turn. Combined with Breaching Charge (collapse a wall, then push a hero into another wall edge) this is a map-exploiting sniper. **5 Malice kicker**: if the push drives the target *into another creature*, both are restrained until EoT. **Ensnaring Chains** (5 Malice, Ranged 10, one prone/restrained/slowed creature, Maneuver) makes a free strike, *ends* the prone/restrained/slowed condition (freeing up allies to retarget), and **restrains the target (save ends)** — hard-lock upgrade for a hero already soft-controlled. **Split Shot** passive: whenever the gunner rolls damage on a target, one creature or object adjacent to the target takes 3 damage — tax for clumping near the front-line hero. Counterplay: keep ranged heroes in open terrain away from walls/objects; don't stack adjacent to the target the gunner is shooting.

## Dwarf Launcher

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 2, platoon, EV 8, dwarf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 107

**Flavor:** Grenadier who ignores cover. Stamina 39, Speed 5, M/I +1/+2.

**Tactics:** **Concussive Grenade** (3-cube within 5, Signature, each enemy) pushes 1/3/3 and slows M<1/2. **Sleep Grenade** (3 Malice, 3-cube within 5, Main action, each enemy) is the real toolkit: dazes on I<0/1/2 and the effect is the kicker — **dazed-this-way targets treat their characteristic scores as 1 lower for the purpose of resisting potencies**. This opens up the next round's harder potencies from every dwarf in the squad (the Stone Whisperer's M<2 restraint, the Marauder Lord's 3 Malice restraint kicker, etc.). **Indirect Fire** passive: the launcher ignores concealment/cover and doesn't need line of effect as long as a size-1 opening exists — so hiding behind a shieldwall or pillar doesn't help. Counterplay: close the range so the launcher is in melee with their soft Stamina, or waste grenades with allies spread out.

## Dwarf Reel Winch

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, EV 6, dwarf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 108

**Flavor:** Crossbow-line specialist who yanks heroes into the trap. Stamina 36, Speed 5, A +2.

**Tactics:** **Snaring Crossbow** (Ranged 10, Signature) — slows M<0/1/2 and **pulls up to 5 squares** via the restraint-pipeline clause; the range and the pull dwarf most other controllers' reach. **Reel Them In** (3 Malice, Ranged 10, three creatures, Maneuver) pulls each **up to 8 squares** (an extra 2 squares if already restrained or slowed) — this is the map-wipe repositioning move: drop three ranged heroes into the front-line's melee grill simultaneously. **We Have a Quota!** passive: if a target made slowed by the reel winch is *already grabbed or slowed*, those conditions end and the target is **restrained (save ends)** — converting slow-stacking into hard restraint that feeds every other dwarf's pipeline. Counterplay: don't stand slowed next to the reel winch's line; a single cleanse breaks the restraint upgrade; focus fire the reel winch before they spend 3 Malice on Reel Them In.

## Dwarf Shieldwall

**Role:** defender
**Tags:** level 3, platoon, EV 10, dwarf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 108

**Flavor:** The Kal Kalavar line defender, Stamina 72, Stability 4, M/P +2/+1.

**Tactics:** **Wide Axe** (Melee 1, Signature) slides 1 at every tier and **the shieldwall can shift 1 square to remain adjacent to the target** — so the slide-then-chase lets them re-engage any hero who tried to disengage. 3 Malice kicker adds an additional target. **Intercepting Shield** (1 Malice, triggered, self) makes the shieldwall the target of a strike against an adjacent ally and halves the damage — a cheap and always-available ally-protect, especially for the Gunner/Launcher/Reel Winch in the back. **Call to the Wall** passive: whenever a creature deals or takes damage from the shieldwall, the shieldwall can make them **taunted until end of the creature's next turn** — lock a hero onto the defender before they can break toward the Marauder Lord. Counterplay: avoid melee entirely; ranged attacks sidestep Call to the Wall; spend reactions on non-adjacent allies.

## Dwarf Stone Whisperer

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 3, platoon, EV 10, dwarf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 109

**Flavor:** The stone-manipulating mage — Stamina 52, Speed 5, Movement: Burrow, R/I +2.

**Tactics:** **Tile Slide** (Signature, 2-cube within 1, each creature and object, Main action) slides 1/3/5 and slows on M<0/1, **restrains (save ends) on M<2 at 17+** — the best restraint-opener in the platoon pool, and it triggers on *every creature* in area, so a clump of heroes gets hit at once. **Stone Wave** (3 Malice, 3-cube within 10, Ranged Maneuver) pushes 2–3 and slows R<1/2/3, leaving **difficult terrain for enemies** in the area — combine with the Launcher's potency-reducing Sleep Grenade so R is easier to beat. **Stone Walker** passive: whenever the whisperer willingly moves, they can **phase through up to 2 squares of stone** as part of that movement — stone walls are not cover from them, and they can exit combat through a solid wall at will. Counterplay: the whisperer has no Stability improvement and soft Stamina for their level — focus fire before they establish the Tile Slide → Catchpole chain; terrain-blasting spells eat their cover.

## Dwarf Trapper

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, EV 6, dwarf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 109

**Flavor:** Mobile melee-range skirmisher-trapper. Stamina 36, Speed 7, A +2, I +1.

**Tactics:** **Concussive Bolts** (Signature, Charge Melee 1 or ranged 10) pushes 2/4/6 — the most push-per-attack in the platoon tier, carrying the restraint-pipeline clause for relocating captives. **Steam-Powered Snare** (3 Malice, 3-cube within 5, Ranged Maneuver, each enemy) — Might test; ≤11 deals 7 damage + restrained (EoT), 12–16 is 5 damage + slowed (EoT), 17+ no effect. **The snare remains until end of encounter**, and any enemy who enters or starts their turn there repeats the test — a persistent mini-Snaring-Line that can be stacked with the 7 Malice version for layered terrain denial. Counterplay: mark and avoid the snare square; a single cleanser covers the first round of repeat tests.

## Dwarf Warden

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 2, platoon, EV 8, dwarf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 109

**Flavor:** The prisoner-escort heavy. Stamina 59, Stability 3, M/I +2/+1.

**Tactics:** **Concussive Maul** (Melee 1, Signature) pushes 1/3/5, and **17+ restrains the target (save ends)** plus pushes 5 — huge swing that opens the restraint pipeline by itself. **Concussive Shockwave** (5 Malice, 3-cube within 1, each enemy, Main action) pushes 2 and slows A<0/1/2 — AoE slow-setup for the Reel Winch's upgrade pipeline (slowed → restrained via We Have a Quota!). **Escort the Prisoners** passive: whenever the warden moves, they can carry **an adjacent restrained enemy as if the enemy were grabbed** — the press-gang truck. The whole fight-design assumption for this platoon is that the warden walks a restrained hero out of the engagement zone while allies cover his escape. Counterplay: free the restrained condition *before* the warden's turn starts; stability prevents the maul's 17+ push from stacking.

## Dwarf Marauder Lord — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 3, leader, EV 20, dwarf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 110

**Flavor:** Salvaged-valok magnetomancer with a psionic mohawk array, levitating axes, Ajax's quota collector. Stamina 132, Stability 4, M +3, R/I +2/+1.

**Tactics:** **Levitating Axes** (Signature, Melee 1 or ranged 10, two targets) slides 1/3/5, with the restraint-pipeline clause. **3 Malice kicker**: a target force moved adjacent to an ally of the marauder lord is **restrained until EoT** — the cleanest way for the marauder lord to restrain two heroes in one action by sliding them next to Wardens/Catchpoles. **Magnetomancy** (Maneuver, Psionic Ranged 10, one creature or object) vertical-slides the target up to 5 squares — off a ledge, off a cliff, off the war walker cupola. **5 Malice kicker** converts it to a 10-burst area targeting **each restrained creature in the area** — the whole press-gang's captives get yanked simultaneously toward the Marauder Lord for delivery. **Your Weapon Is Useless** (triggered, Psionic Ranged 10, self or one ally) when a creature makes a melee strike on the target: the target halves the damage and the attacker takes 4 damage — one free retaliatory reaction per round saved for the heaviest-hitting hero. **End Effect** passive: at end of each turn, take 5 damage to end one save-ends effect, unreducable — the Marauder Lord never stays debuffed.

Villain actions:
- **Ajax Will Pay Well for These Specimens (VA1).** 5-cube within 10, **Levitating Axes against each target, one power roll against all** — the opening round-wipe for a clustered party.
- **Don't Let Them Escape! (VA2).** 5-burst, each ally shifts up to their speed, then the marauder lord uses Levitating Axes. This is the reposition + strike: the whole press-gang repositions to surround a restrained hero, then the marauder lord adds two slides on top.
- **Test Your Metal! (VA3).** The marauder lord creates **three size-2 metal objects in unoccupied spaces within 10** — from then on, Magnetomancy can *additionally* target one of these objects per use. Map-level metal terrain for magnetomancy chains; the objects can be hurled, slammed, or used as cover. Combos monstrously with Breaching Charge (9 damage per adjacent enemy when metal objects are destroyed).

Counterplay: keep heroes un-restrained before the 5 Malice Magnetomancy fires; kill the metal objects from VA3 before the next round; don't chain melee strikes with Your Weapon Is Useless up; push the Marauder Lord off the war walker cupola to deny its cover.

## Servitor War Walker — elite mount

**Role:** mount
**Tags:** level 1, elite, EV 12, construct, dwarf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 111

**Flavor:** The multi-legged mechanical mount — three size-1 allies can ride in the cupola with cover. Size 3, Stamina 60, Speed 8, Stability 2, M +2, R/P −2, Movement: Climb.

**Tactics:** **Grasping Claws** (Signature, Charge Melee 2, two creatures or objects) — 6/9/12 damage, **restrains (save ends) on M<2 at 17+**. The effect clause is the nastiest in the whole section: **"a target already restrained or restrained by this ability is pulled up to 3 squares"** — so once the war walker has restrained a hero, each subsequent Grasping Claws both damages *and* repositions. Combined with the restraint-pipeline clause and Mobile Prison Harness, the war walker is a mobile jail with a damage output. **Stunning Blast** (3 Malice, 3-burst, each enemy, Maneuver) is lightning damage + slowed on A<0/1/2 — perfect setup for the next Grasping Claws (slowed targets feed the restraint pipeline). **Cupola** passive: up to three size-1 allies ride in the same space with cover — stack a Marauder Lord + two platoon dwarves on one war walker and they're all in cover. **Mobile Prison Harness** passive: any *restrained or slowed* creature who comes adjacent to the war walker is **automatically restrained (save ends) with a bane on power rolls, and moves with the war walker**. Combined with the Reel Winch's "restrain slowed targets" and Snaring Line / Launcher dazing, the harness turns the war walker into a rolling captive-collector. Counterplay: never approach the war walker while slowed; ranged heroes can kite the 60 Stamina down from safe distance; stability + Might saves break the restraint pull chain.
