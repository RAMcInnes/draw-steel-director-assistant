# Orcs

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 215–222. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

In a long-lost language, *oruk* — "blood fire" — described the glowing vein-light an orc puts out when they push themselves to the limit. Orcs know one another as *kanin*, "the people," but wear "orc" with pride when dealing with outsiders. Their history spans every age and climate of the world; most live in mixed humanoid societies, a few settle secluded tropical communities, and the orcs heroes tend to cross swords with are the same roles other humanoids fill — mercenaries, bounty hunters, garroters, cultists, and the occasional corrupt king.

- **Relentlessly Tough.** Tall, tough-skinned, tusked, with hair draping their shoulders like mantles and veins that brighten when they're excited. Endurance is their signature — few humanoids can match a trained orc for sustained combat. When pushed to the brink of death, the blood-fire surges and an orc finds one last strike in them (Relentless, see shared traits). The glow is red for most orcs, blue for mountain orcs, white for underground orcs.
- **Innate Power.** Orc spirit occasionally manifests as raw magic. **Eyes of Grole** specialize into a single elemental affinity. **Terranovas** channel magic through their feet into the earth. **Godcallers** use song to spark the latent magic in every creature's spirit.
- **Mohlers.** Spined, pig-like burrowing animals that corkscrew through the earth, native to deserts and forests. In peace they plow fields and eat pests; in war they reshape battlefields to trip and swallow enemy footing. Orcs gift a mohler companion to individuals of exceptional merit — treat one well and they're a steadfast protector, treat one poorly and the mohler flees home and the orc community takes it as a personal offense.
- **Scyzas.** Giant bipedal lizards from the tropics, with trunk-like legs, claws that cleave through terrain, and battering head crests that ring like bones in a storm. Adorned with great war harnesses, a single scyza can carry dozens of orcs.
- **Languages.** Most orcs speak Caelian and Kalliak.

## Shared traits

- **Relentless (every platoon+ orc).** When reduced to 0 Stamina, the orc makes a free strike before dying — and *if that strike reduces its target to 0 Stamina, the orc is reduced to 1 Stamina instead of 0 and survives.* This is the blood-fire mechanic in stat-block form: every named orc on the board might pop back up if their last swing connects cleanly. Never leave a "dead" orc surrounded by wounded heroes.
- **Bloodfire Burn (every minion orc).** The minion version — free strike before dying, without the revive. Plan for the last swing on *every* orc on the board.
- **Wide role coverage.** The orc category is the most mechanically diverse in this book after humans — they field every role (artillery, ambusher, brute, controller, defender, harrier, hexer, support, plus the elite mount and the leader). Any faction built from orcs can cover every combat niche.

## Orc Malice features

Spent at the start of any orc's turn.

- **Overwhelming March (3 Malice).** Each orc shifts up to their speed, moving through enemy spaces if they can. Each enemy passed through makes a Might test — tier 1 is 6 damage + prone, tier 2 is 4 damage + prone, tier 3 is push 2. The formation sweep: the whole warband walks over the hero line in a single action. Combine with Juggernauts and Bloodrunners, who want prone targets for their bonus damage and mobility clauses.
- **Mohler Trench (5 Malice).** Two mohlers surface and dig a 10×1 trench — 2 squares deep, difficult terrain, can't be placed directly beneath creatures. The map-cutting tool: split the party across a trench that costs movement to cross and drops heroes into difficult terrain. Bloodrunners' prone-entry Unimpeded clause gets free triggers out of a trench full of fallen heroes.
- **Mohler Cavity (7 Malice).** A group of mohlers dig a 5-cube pit beneath any area containing at least one ground-standing creature. Area becomes difficult terrain. *Each orc in the area can shift into the nearest unoccupied space outside the pit before it completes.* Each non-orc in the area makes an Agility test — tier 1 falls + prone + can't stand EoT, tier 2 falls, tier 3 shifts out safely. The lair's nuclear option. An orc warband can collapse the ground beneath a hero cluster while their own people leap free.

## Minions

### Mohler — minion

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 1, minion, animal, orc
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 216
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to speed.

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 4, Speed 7, Burrow. The spined corkscrew-pig, minion-grade. Not technically humanoid — the only Animal-keyword creature in the orc roster.

**Tactics:** **Earth Bump** (Signature, Melee 1, one target per minion) — modest damage, *distance increases to Melee 2, and if the mohler is 1+ squares beneath the target before using the ability, M<1 also knocks prone.* The mohler's whole game is burrowing under a hero and surfacing beneath them. **Seismic Sense** — no line of effect needed against any creature or object touching the ground. **Ground Grinder** — the mohler can use the Dig maneuver at the start of the encounter, *and while burrowing within 1 square below the ground, the ground above is difficult terrain.* Four mohlers starting an encounter pre-burrowed under the battlefield convert whole zones into costly movement. Pair with Overwhelming March Malice for horrifying opener turns.

### Orc Blitzer — minion

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, minion, humanoid, orc
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 216
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to speed.

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 4, Speed 7. Fast spear-and-shield minion — the Warleader's Familial Reinforcements villain action drops *four* of these onto the board mid-fight, so expect them to refill.

**Tactics:** **Lugged Spear** (Signature, Melee 1, one target per minion) — light chip damage, but *a target who starts their next turn adjacent to three or more blitzers takes 3 damage.* Four blitzers swarming one hero extract 3 off-turn damage on top of their own strikes. **Bloodfire Burn**. Use as the cheap swarm that converts the Warleader's Familial Reinforcements into board-wide adjacency pressure.

### Orc Bloodspark — minion

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, minion, humanoid, orc
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 216
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to forced movement distance.

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 3, Speed 6. Spellcaster minion — the blood-fire expressed as ranged magical bolts.

**Tactics:** **Explosive Mote** (Signature, Ranged 10, one target per minion) — chip damage with push 1/2/4 per tier, *or* the bloodspark shifts 1/2/4 squares away. Either kite or knockback — useful at either end of the priority list. Captain's +2 forced movement turns the push 4 into a push 6 across tiers. **Bloodfire Burn**.

### Orc Glorifier — minion

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 1, minion, humanoid, orc
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 216
**With Captain:** +5 bonus to ranged distance.

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 3, Speed 6. The crier — wields sonic magic to point out the day's important target for the warband.

**Tactics:** **Call to Victory** (Signature, Melee 1 or ranged 5, one target per minion) — low sonic damage with slowed save-ends on P<1/<2 per tier. *Allies gain an edge on melee strikes against the target until the glorifier and each other glorifier in their squad are killed.* The permanent-edge-on-a-priority-target effect — four glorifiers marking the hero defender means the entire orc warband has a melee edge against them for most of the fight. With Captain's +5 ranged distance, the Glorifier marks heroes from 10 squares away. Kill *every* glorifier to drop the buff. **Bloodfire Burn**.

### Orc Razor — minion

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 1, minion, humanoid, orc
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 216
**With Captain:** +1 damage bonus to strikes.

**Flavor:** Size 1L (larger than most orc minions), Stamina 5, Speed 6. Meat-and-push.

**Tactics:** **Boot and Blade** (Signature, Melee 1, one target per minion) — damage with push 3 at tier 2 and push 3 *or* prone at tier 3. *The razor gains an edge against any target affected by a condition.* So combine with the Glorifier's slowed rider, any prone from Mohlers, or any dazed from elsewhere and the razor's swinging at edge. **Bloodfire Burn**.

## Platoon

### Orc Bloodrunner

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 3, platoon, humanoid, orc
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 217

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 50, Speed 8, Free Strike 5. The sprinter-skirmisher. Highest speed on the orc roster.

**Tactics:** **Shield Bash** (Signature, Melee 1, one target) — damage across tiers with push on tier 1/2/3 and prone on tier 3. **The forced movement distance equals the number of squares the bloodrunner moved on their turn before using the ability.** So Bloodrunner charges 8 squares, Shield Bashes, pushes 8. *An ally targeted by this ability ignores the damage and can move up to the same distance.* Use Shield Bash on an ally to mass-reposition the warband. **2 Malice:** the ally also free-strikes after the forced movement. **Unimpeded** — can end movement in a prone creature's space, and the first time on a turn the bloodrunner enters *any* creature's space, that creature takes 3 damage. **Relentless**.

### Orc Chainlock

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, humanoid, orc
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 217

**Flavor:** Size 1L, Stamina 20, Speed 5, Stability 2, Free Strike 3. The dockside hook-and-chain specialist.

**Tactics:** **Hook and Chain** (Signature, Melee 3, one target) — damage with pull 1/2/3 and hooked save-ends on M<0/1/2. *A hooked target can't move more than 3 squares away from the chainlock's position when the ability was used.* The target is magnetized to that square. **Heavy Crossbolt** (3 Malice, Ranged 5) — damage + slowed save-ends on A<0/<1/<2, with prone at tier 3. **Chain Link** (passive) — whenever the chainlock is force moved by a creature's melee ability, *the creature is pulled the same distance toward the chainlock after the forced movement resolves.* Try to push a chainlock into a pit — you come with them. **Relentless**.

### Orc Eye of Grole

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, humanoid, orc
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 218

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 20, Speed 6. The elemental-focused orc mage. Pick one of cold, fire, or lightning — the Eye has *immunity 5* to that type and deals it on their strikes.

**Tactics:** **Elemental Discharge** (3 Malice, either single-target Ranged 10 or 5×2 line) — damage with push/slide/kite rider. The area form applies *damage weakness 3 to the same damage type* save-ends on any enemy targeted. So lining up three heroes on a fire Eye's line discharge sets them all up to take +3 from every other fire Eye's strikes for the rest of the fight. Build a fire-Eye squadron and stack the weakness. **Relentless**.

### Orc Garotter

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, humanoid, orc
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 218

**Flavor:** Size 1L, Stamina 30, Speed 5, Free Strike 4. The assassin — dagger, wire, and a magical cloak.

**Tactics:** **Dagger Feint** (Signature, Melee 1) — damage across tiers with shift 1/2/3 after, *and +4 damage if the ability gains an edge or double edge.* Combines with the Glorifier's permanent-edge mark. **Strangle** (Main action, Melee 1) — damage with dazed save-ends at tier 2/3 on I<1/<2, and **grabbed** at tier 3. *While grabbed this way, the target can't communicate or use magic abilities.* The dedicated anti-caster lock — drag the party wizard out of the fight with a grab they can't spellcast their way out of. **Chroma Cloak** (1 Malice, Maneuver) — the garotter turns invisible until end of their turn; ends early if they take damage or use an ability. So: Chroma Cloak in, Strangle the wizard, Relentless if killed. **Relentless**.

### Orc Godcaller

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, humanoid, orc
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 219

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 30, Speed 6, Free Strike 3. The bard-priest. Song-magic that sparks the blood-fire in allies.

**Tactics:** **Power Chord** (Signature, Melee 1 or ranged 10) — sonic damage with weakened save-ends on tier 3 P<2. **Cadenza** (Main action, Ranged 10) — target ally moves up to their speed and uses a main action. Free action economy for the warband. **3 Malice:** target a second ally. So one Godcaller Cadenza with 3 Malice gives two allies a full turn each outside their initiative. **Rallying Ostinato** (2 Malice, Maneuver, Ranged 10) — self and three allies regain 15 Stamina *and ignore difficult terrain until end of encounter.* The heal-and-strategic-movement combo. Pair with Mohler Trench Malice; the orcs ignore the trench, the heroes don't. **Relentless**.

### Orc Juggernaut

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 3, platoon, humanoid, orc
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 219

**Flavor:** Size 1L, Stamina 60, Speed 6, Free Strike 5. Not to be confused with the Ogre Juggernaut. This one's a Reason/Intuition-negative chaos swinger with a giant two-handed axe.

**Tactics:** **Haymaker Greataxe** (Signature, Melee 1) — heavy damage with prone at tier 2/3 and bleeding save-ends at tier 3 on M<2. *A target who is already prone takes +6 damage.* So: Mohler trips someone, Juggernaut finishes them. **Hrraaaaaagh!** (1 Malice, free triggered action) — when the juggernaut takes damage, they move up to their speed and make a free strike. Damage-them-get-punished reaction. **Blood in the Water** — whenever the juggernaut willingly moves, they can move +3 additional squares if they end closer to a prone creature. Combines with Relentless and Hrraaaaaagh! for a juggernaut that closes on wounded heroes even as the party tries to kite. **Relentless**.

### Orc Rampart

**Role:** defender
**Tags:** level 2, platoon, humanoid, orc
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 220

**Flavor:** Size 1L, Stamina 59, Speed 6, Stability 2, Free Strike 4. Reason/Intuition/Presence all positive — a thoughtful defender, not a dumb tank.

**Tactics:** **My Spear, My Foe** (Signature, Melee 1) — damage with taunted EoT at tier 2/3. *Double edge against any target who dealt damage to the rampart this round.* Encourages the party to hit the rampart (that's the taunt) and then punishes them double for doing so. **Castling** (Maneuver, Self) — the rampart moves/shifts up to their speed adjacent to a target ally, then *swaps places with them*. Positional rescue: bad-spot ally goes where the rampart was, rampart takes the heat. **No.** (Triggered action) — when a creature targets an ally adjacent to the rampart with an ability that doesn't also target the rampart, *the rampart becomes the target of the triggering ability instead.* The complete defender package: taunt, reactive redirect, swap positions. **Relentless**.

### Orc Terranova

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 2, platoon, humanoid, orc
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 220

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 30, Speed 6, Burrow, Stability 2, Free Strike 4. The earth-magic orc mage — where Eyes of Grole pick an element, the Terranova picks *the ground itself*.

**Tactics:** **Earth Pillar** (Signature, Ranged 10, three targets) — damage with prone + can't stand save-ends on failed Agility tests. *Each target must be on the ground, and the ground in each target's space rises 1 square.* Three heroes end up stranded on 1-square pillars, unable to stand, unable to climb down easily. **Sinkhole** (3 Malice, Main action, 3-burst area) — damage with restrained save-ends + permanent difficult terrain. **Seismic Step** (passive) — ignores difficult terrain, and *no line of effect needed against creatures touching the ground.* Fire Earth Pillar through walls at any grounded hero. Combine with Mohler Trench / Mohler Cavity — the Terranova stands up and launches from inside terrain the party can't clear. **Relentless**.

## Leader

### Orc Warleader — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 3, leader, humanoid, orc
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 221

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 120, Speed 6, Stability 2, Free Strike 5. The tactical commander — all characteristics positive, optimized for shouting orders as much as swinging.

**Tactics:** **Go.** (Signature, Ranged 10) — target ally moves up to their speed and uses a main action. The Warleader's cornerstone: free ally turns every turn. **1 Malice:** two allies. **3 Malice:** one ally and a minion squad (the entire squad acts on the Warleader's turn). **Mace Lariat** (Main action, Melee 1 each enemy in reach) — damage across tiers with push 1/3/5 and dazed save-ends on M<1/<2/<3. A 1-burst-style melee that hits *every* enemy adjacent. **Lockdown** (3 Malice, Maneuver, self and Ranged 10) — self and three allies move up to speed and use the Grab maneuver with an edge. Mass grab-and-hold button. **Courtesy Call** (triggered action, Ranged 10) — when the target gets a tier 1 outcome on a power roll, *the target has a double edge on their next power roll before end of encounter.* The Warleader pats a struggling ally on the shoulder and the ally's next swing is devastating. **End Effect** (10 damage to clear a save-ends). **Relentless** (same as any other platoon+ orc — the Warleader can survive a lethal hit if their free strike kills).

Villain actions:
- **Close In (VA1, 10-burst area).** Each ally target moves up to their speed. Each enemy who ends up adjacent to a target after the move makes an Intuition test — tier 1 frightened of the Warleader save-ends, tier 2 frightened EoT. Fear-cone set up through positioning.
- **Familial Reinforcements (VA2).** The Warleader shifts up to speed, and *four orc blitzers appear in unoccupied spaces within 10 squares.* A fresh minion swarm in the middle of the fight; immediately threatens Tiny Stabs–style adjacency damage via the Blitzer's 3-damage passive.
- **I'll Do This Myself (VA3).** *Three times in succession, the Warleader shifts up to speed and can use Mace Lariat.* Three map-sweeping AoE strikes in one action — a finisher that can cross the battlefield hitting different clusters per swing.

Counterplay: (1) The Relentless + End Effect + "survives if the free strike kills" stack means the Warleader is the hardest same-level kill in the orc book. Plan for burst damage to overkill past the 0 Stamina threshold in one commit. (2) Go.'s free main actions are the real damage engine; silence the Warleader or drop them before they cast it across multiple turns. (3) VA3's triple Mace Lariat wants adjacent targets; if the fight is approaching round three, spread out. (4) The Warleader's "survives via lethal free strike" can be defused if their free strike target is already at full Stamina (then it's unlikely to reduce them to 0) — stay topped up on healing through the fight's final round.

## Elite mount

### Scyza — elite mount

**Role:** mount
**Tags:** level 3, elite, animal, orc
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 222

**Flavor:** Size 4, Stamina 100, Speed 6, Stability 3, Free Strike 5. A giant bipedal war-lizard with a battering head-crest and a platform harness that seats *three size-1 allies in the same space.*

**Tactics:** **Clawed Kick** (Signature, Melee 2, two targets) — damage with prone at tier 2/3, *and a roar that frightens save-ends on I<2.* **Whiptail** (Melee 3, one target) — damage with prone at tier 2 and bleeding save-ends on A<2 at tier 3. *Against a target on top of the scyza, the ability gains an edge, and the target is pushed into an unoccupied adjacent square and knocked prone.* So heroes who try to jump on the scyza and fight from the platform get whipped off. **Crestfall** (2 Malice, Main action, 3-cube within 2) — damage + sonic secondary + dazed save-ends on R<0/<1/<2. **Sandstorm** (3 Malice, Maneuver, 3-burst) — kicks up a sandstorm granting concealment to the scyza and any ally in the area until end of the scyza's next turn, plus an Intuition test for each enemy in the area (tier 1: 10 damage + prone + slowed EoT). The "hide in a dust cloud while the allies fire out of it" button. **Brace and Break** (triggered action) — when the scyza or any rider is targeted by an ability, *halve the triggering damage; if the attacker is within 3 squares, the scyza makes a free strike against them.* **Terrible Beast** (passive) — +6 damage against objects. Scyzas chew through walls, doors, and siege pieces. **War Harness** (passive) — three size-1 allies can share the scyza's space. Stack a Bloodspark, a Chainlock, and a Garotter in one harness for a single moving piece with ranged magic, hook-and-chain control, and an assassin all firing from one square.

Counterplay: (1) Target the scyza — 100 Stamina is not infinite, and killing the mount grounds three riders. (2) Don't cluster inside Sandstorm's concealment — you're blind in it, the orcs are not. (3) Brace and Break only triggers on targeted abilities; area abilities still land at full damage. (4) Size 4 means the scyza is a huge target profile, and the only orc stat block in this section that size-matters for AoE targeting.
