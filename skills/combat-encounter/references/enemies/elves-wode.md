# Elves, Wode

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 135–141. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Wode elves are the children of the Sylvan Celestials and masters of the elf-haunted wilds for which they are named. They see all forests as their domain by birthright — and take pride in the human reputation for snatching children who wander too far in. Their natural glamour lets them strike from cover and melt back into the underbrush; they bait attackers into disadvantageous fights with false retreats and hidden reinforcements in dense, vertical terrain.

- **Masking Glamor (shared trait).** Nearly every non-minion wode elf has this passive: **abilities targeting them that would take a bane from cover or concealment take a *double* bane instead**. A wode elf in brush, behind a tree, or just partially hidden is punishingly hard to hit. Combine with the druid/chirurgeon's teleport-to-cover move and the sentry's mark to keep the whole platoon perpetually hidden.
- **Into the Green.** The guerrilla, warleader, and wode elf scout minion can **attempt to hide at the end of each of their turns** — and with Forest Network Malice, any wode elf who ends their turn hidden can *shift up to their speed while remaining hidden*. Hiding is the central reset.
- **Wodeworking.** Wode elves sing plant-creatures to life — the **wodenelg** (moose-like mounts) are the most common, but legends tell of ancient plant-beings sung into existence by entire choirs of sixteen-part wode elf harmony, still wandering the world. A wodenelg's rider is typically also its singer, creating a lifetime bond.
- **Blightless.** A hunter sect who view *any* interloper as a direct threat to the wode and seek to eliminate outsiders by any means. Most mortal encounters with wode elves are with the Blightless — they set the tone for mortal perception of an otherwise peaceful people.
- **Mark mechanic.** Wode elf **sentries** and **gweiadur** mark enemies; while a target is marked by any wode elf, all wode elf allies gain an **edge on abilities against them**. This is the focus-fire designator — and it pipelines into Death Blossom (AoE damage only to marked enemies).
- **Languages.** Most wode elves speak Caelian and Yllyric.

## Wode Elf Malice features

At the start of any wode elf's turn, Malice may be spent to activate one of the following features.

- **Forest Network (3 Malice).** Each wode elf who ends this turn hidden can **shift up to their speed while remaining hidden** — a hidden relocation that never breaks concealment. Round after round, hidden wode elves reposition to flank, set up Into the Green ambushes, and stay invisible.
- **Punishing Regrowth (5 Malice).** Until end of round, each wode elf **gains an edge on abilities, and their strikes can slide a target up to 2 squares in addition to their usual effects**. If a strike already imposes forced movement, the slide happens *after*. **A creature force-moved by a wode elf's strike who ends that forced movement in difficult terrain is restrained (EoT).** Turns Overgrowth / Entangling Vines / Vines Everywhere terrain into restraint traps for the whole round — drop a zone, then push heroes into it.
- **Vines Everywhere (7 Malice, Main action, each enemy in encounter).** Agility test: ≤11 is 8 damage + restrained (save ends), 12–16 is restrained (EoT), 17+ no effect. Map-wide restraint bomb — the round-opener for a decisive ambush.

## Wode Elf Lookout — minion

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, fey, humanoid, wode elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 136
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to speed.

**Flavor:** Canopy-hidden spotter. Size 1M, Stamina 4, Speed 7 + Climb, A +2, P +1.

**Tactics:** **Longbow** (Ranged 8, Signature, one target per minion) 1/2/3 damage. **There!** passive: **any wode elf within 5 squares can make ranged strikes as if occupying the lookout's space** — lookouts become firing perches for every other wode elf with a ranged weapon. Post four lookouts on high ground and every archer in the warband effectively shoots from all four spots. **Masking Glamor** shared. Counterplay: kill or displace lookouts to deny the shared firing perch; climbing heroes can trade positions directly.

## Wode Elf Scout — minion

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, fey, humanoid, wode elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 136
**With Captain:** Gain an edge on strikes.

**Flavor:** Fast dagger-stealth minion. Stamina 4, Speed 10, A +2, Free Strike 2.

**Tactics:** **Daggers** (Melee 1 or ranged 5, Signature, one target per minion) 2/4/5 damage. **Into the Green**: scout can **attempt to hide at end of each turn** — a hidden minion is immune to focus fire and retains the glamour-bane advantage. Four scouts all hiding every round is an action-economy nightmare for the party's reveal options. **Masking Glamor** shared. ## Wode Elf Yeoman — minion

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, fey, humanoid, wode elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 136
**With Captain:** +1 damage bonus to strikes.

**Flavor:** Long-ranged archer minion. Stamina 3, Speed 7, A +2, P +1. The pushiest minion archer.

**Tactics:** **Heavy Longbow** (Ranged 12, Signature, one target per minion) 2/4/5 damage + push 1/2/3. Extreme range plus push — yeomen sit on elevated terrain and shove heroes away from the objective or into prepared difficult terrain. With a captain's +1 damage and four yeomen attacking the same target, that's 12 push *and* 24 damage from range. **Masking Glamor** shared. Counterplay: close the range fast; hide behind true cover (yeomen are still limited by line of effect, just not banes).

## Wode Elf Chirurgeon

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 2, platoon, EV 8, fey, humanoid, wode elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 137

**Flavor:** *kai-RUR-jinz* — master strategists who enact picture-perfect plans mid-battle. Stamina 40, Speed 7, Climb, M/A/P +1/+2/+1.

**Tactics:** **Wild Ax** (Signature, Charge Melee 1 or ranged 5, one target) 6/9/12 damage + push 1/3/5. Effect: **the chirurgeon can make a ranged free strike before using this ability** — two attacks per Main action in effect. **5 Malice kicker: uses Wild Ax again** — triple strike per main action for 5 Malice. **The Wode Protects Us** (3 Malice, Ranged 5, self and three allies, Magic Ranged Maneuver) — **each target teleports up to 10 squares to a space that has cover or concealment**. The whole platoon repositions into cover simultaneously; combined with Masking Glamor, that's four wode elves with double banes against them on the same round. **Masking Glamor** shared. Counterplay: area-effect attacks bypass the cover teleport; close the range to deny chirurgeon's push.

## Wode Elf Druid

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 2, platoon, EV 8, fey, humanoid, wode elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 137

**Flavor:** Vine-calling nature mage. Stamina 30, Speed 7, Climb, A +1, P +2.

**Tactics:** **Entangling Vines** (Signature, 3-cube within 10, each enemy, Area Magic Ranged) 5/8/10 damage + pull 1/3/5 + M<1/2 slowed (save ends). Effect: **while slowed this way, a target can't search for hidden creatures** — so the druid's slow is also the glamour-protection upgrade, since every hidden wode elf stays hidden against the slowed hero. **3 Malice kicker**: cube size and potency both +1. **The Wode Protects Us** same 10-square teleport-to-cover reposition as the chirurgeon — duplicate the cover lockdown. **Masking Glamor** shared. Counterplay: Might saves block the anti-search slow; AoE through cover works; focus fire while the druid has spent 3 Malice on the upgraded vine cast.

## Wode Elf Green Seer

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, EV 6, fey, humanoid, wode elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 138

**Flavor:** Oracle-caster who punishes reactive play. Stamina 20, Speed 7, A/I/P +1/+2/+1.

**Tactics:** **The Forest's Embrace** (Signature, Ranged 10, Magic Ranged Strike, one creature) 5/7/9 damage + I<1/2 restrained (save ends). Effect: **while restrained this way, a target can't search for hidden creatures** — same glamour-protection synergy as the druid's slow. **The Natural Cycle** (3 Malice, Ranged 10, two creatures, Magic Ranged Strike Maneuver) 2/4/6 damage + P<1 weakened / P<1 bleeding + **double bane on strikes** at 17+. Effect: **while bleeding or weakened this way, a target is covered in lichen** — another vector for the wode elf "target can't see properly" theme. **Foreseen Punishment** (free triggered, Ranged 5) when a creature uses a triggered action targeting the green seer or an ally within range — **the green seer makes a free strike against the triggerer after the triggered action resolves**. Punishes reaction-heavy defenders. **Masking Glamor** shared. Counterplay: Intuition saves dodge restraint; ranged attacks that don't use triggered actions bypass Foreseen Punishment entirely.

## Wode Elf Greenskeeper

**Role:** defender
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, EV 6, fey, humanoid, wode elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 138

**Flavor:** Plant-mage bladed defender. Stamina 40, Speed 7, A/P +2/+1.

**Tactics:** **Growing Longsword** (Signature, Melee 1, one target) 5/7/9 damage. Effect: **the target is taunted (EoT) and the greenskeeper shifts up to 3 squares** — taunt + reposition in one move; chase a hero who tries to disengage. **2 Malice kicker**: distance increases to **Melee 5** — reach-weapon mode, strike heroes from outside their melee. **Overgrowth** (3 Malice, 3-cube within 10, Area Ranged Maneuver, special) — **the area is overgrown with heavy brush and brambles until end of encounter, providing cover and concealment for the greenskeeper and allies**. The area is **difficult terrain for enemies**, and any enemy who starts their turn there takes **3 damage**. This is the "make your own glamour-cover zone anywhere on the map" move — combine with Punishing Regrowth for restrain-on-push into Overgrowth zones. **Masking Glamor** shared. Counterplay: area-effect spells ignore cover; stay out of Overgrowth zones; kill greenskeepers before they establish the cover field.

## Wode Elf Guerrilla

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 3, platoon, EV 10, fey, humanoid, wode elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 139

**Flavor:** Teleporting hit-and-run captain. Stamina 50, Speed 7, Teleport, A/P +2/+1.

**Tactics:** **Splinter Dagger** (Signature, Melee 1 or ranged 5, Magic Ranged Strike Weapon, one target) 7/11/14 damage + M<2 bleeding (save ends) at 17+. Effect: **the guerrilla can teleport up to 3 squares after striking** — hit and reposition into hidden cover each turn. **3 Malice kicker**: adds a second target, +3 damage if both adjacent. **Do Not Hesitate in the Wode** (3 Malice, Ranged 20, free triggered, self and each ally, *only when acting as captain*) when an ally ends their turn while the guerrilla hasn't acted: **the targets take their turn immediately, each with an edge on abilities until end of turn**. Turns the guerrilla into a turn-order disruptor — the platoon can cascade multiple turns before the heroes get to act. **Into the Green**: hide at end of each turn. **Masking Glamor** shared. Counterplay: kill the guerrilla early to disable the turn-cascade; reveal effects neutralize Into the Green; ranged AoE that catches multiple hidden squares.

## Wode Elf Gweiadur

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 3, platoon, EV 10, fey, humanoid, wode elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 139

**Flavor:** The long-range sniper, climbing to vantage. Stamina 40, Speed 7, Climb, A/I +2/+1.

**Tactics:** **Snare Bow** (Signature, Ranged 15, Strike Weapon, one target) 7/11/14 damage + **A<2 restrained (save ends) at 17+**. Effect: **the gweiadur shifts up to 3 squares after striking** — snipe and reposition. **3 Malice kicker**: if the target is restrained, **one enemy adjacent to them is also restrained (save ends)** — contagion restraint pulls in the nearest ally. **You Activated My Trap!** (3 Malice, 3-cube within 10, each enemy, Area Magic Ranged Maneuver) 4/6/9 damage + R<0/1/2 **marked (save ends)**, slowed on tiers 2–3. Effect: **allies gain an edge on abilities against a target marked by any wode elf** — feeds the whole platoon's focus-fire mark mechanic. **Masking Glamor** shared. Counterplay: Agility saves block the restraint; stay spread out to avoid the 3-cube mark/slow; focus fire the gweiadur before they establish the first mark.

**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 139

## Wode Elf Warleader — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 3, leader, EV 20, fey, humanoid, wode elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 140

**Flavor:** The finest tactician of a wode elf court, sent into battle only if the situation is dire. Size 1M, Stamina 120, Stability 2, Teleport, M/A/R/I/P +2/+3/+2/+2/+2.

**Tactics:** **Wodeblade** (Signature, Melee 1, two targets, Magic Melee Strike Weapon) 8/12/15 damage + M<1/2/3 restrained (save ends). Effect: **the warleader can teleport up to 3 squares between each strike** — teleport, strike, teleport, strike. **2 Malice kicker**: restrained targets take +3 damage. **Fairness Is a Human Concept** (5 Malice, 10-burst, Maneuver, each ally) each non-minion target **makes a free strike, then shifts up to 3 squares, and can attempt to hide at end of warleader's turn if they end with cover/concealment**. Platoon-wide strike + reposition + hide cascade. **Wode Sickness** (triggered, Ranged 10, one enemy who hasn't taken their turn this round) — when an ally ends their turn: **the target takes their turn immediately, and if they have P<2 they are bleeding and take a bane on strikes until end of turn**. Force-feeds an unready hero into their turn early, before their allies can set them up. **Into the Green** + **Masking Glamor**: hide at end of each turn, double banes from cover/concealment. **End Effect** passive: 5 unreducable self-damage at end of turn to clear one save-ends effect.

Villain actions:
- **You Will All Witness my Blade (VA1).** 5-burst, each enemy — Wodeblade against each target with an edge on the power roll. The opening restraint-wave for a clustered party.
- **Suppressing Volley (VA2).** 5-burst, each ally — the warleader uses Wodeblade, then each ally in the area makes a free strike. Multi-target strike cascade; the warleader's strike is the setup, ally strikes are the payoff.
- **Is It Now or Is It Then? (VA3).** 5-burst, self and each ally — **each target becomes invisible until the start of the next round**. The warleader then uses Wodeblade. The whole platoon disappears, one ally reappears to strike, then vanishes. Massive defensive reset + ambush setup.

Counterplay: reveal effects break Is It Now; area attacks through cover bypass Masking Glamor's double bane; cleanse the save-ends Wodeblade restraints before Fairness fires to prevent restrained-target +3 stacking; save burst damage for the warleader emerging from invisibility after VA3.

## Wodenelg

**Role:** mount
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, EV 6, plant, wode elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 141

**Flavor:** Moose-like plant-construct mounts sung to life by wodeworking — the rider is typically also its singer, bonded for life. Size 2, Stamina 30, Speed 10, Stability 1, M/A +2/+1.

**Tactics:** **Gore** (Signature, Charge Melee 1, one target) 5/7/9 damage. Effect: **the wodenelg's rider can make a free strike at any point during the charge** — lance-and-horse damage stack every charge. **Sure-Footed** passive: **ignores difficult terrain and doesn't provoke opportunity attacks by moving** — immune to Overgrowth / Vines Everywhere / Entangling Vines' difficult terrain (the platoon creates terrain that traps heroes but not mounts). **Where I End the Woods Begin** (3 Malice, self, Maneuver) **the wodenelg and their rider become invisible until the start of the wodenelg's next turn** — invisible charge setup for the next round. **Mounted Stability** passive: the wodenelg's rider has **damage immunity 2** — reinforced mount armor. **Shared Glamor** passive: if the rider has Masking Glamor, the wodenelg does too — ride a warleader/gweiadur/guerrilla and the whole 2-size mount is also wrapped in the double-bane cloak. Counterplay: hit the rider from ranged before the invisible-charge round; dismount via force movement; AoE catches the mount's 2-size profile.

## Wode Elf Sample Encounters

*Source: p. 137. Ready-made EV-balanced deployments.*

- **Scout Patrol (23 EV).** Four lookouts, eight scouts, one chirurgeon, one green seer. Ambush patrol for a forest-edge encounter.
- **Blightless Militia (32 EV).** Eight yeomen, eight runners, one druid, one greenskeeper, one sentry. A hunter warband with long-range pushback and marked focus-fire.
- **Hawkeye Cavalry (48 EV).** Three gweiadur, three wodenelg. All-mounted snipers — hit-and-run from moose-back through difficult terrain.
- **Reclaimer Force (74 EV).** Sixteen yeomen, sixteen scouts, one guerrilla, one druid, two greenskeepers, one warleader. Full-court assault — the flagship Blightless reprisal.
