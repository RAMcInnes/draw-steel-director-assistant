# Kingfissure Worm

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 187–188. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Size-5 ambush burrowers that terraform the earth as they move — tunnels, crevasses, and collapsed countryside are their hunting ground. Named for their trick of lying in wait under the surface, sensing vibrations, and shooting their serpentine tongues up through any crack to drag prey down into a cavernous gut. They eat indiscriminately: people, wagons, siege engines, gems. The oldest specimens are truly immense — mistakable for a mobile mountain range — and their intestines are a legendary hoard of swallowed treasure. A few intrepid (or unlucky) communities have even survived inside the lower abdomen of one of the great worms, eking out lives in the spacious gut-biome.

- **Ambush from below.** Kingfissures spend most of their existence subterranean, waiting for surface vibrations. Any "the ground trembles" signal the GM foreshadows a round or two before combat is doing narrative work — the attack will come from the floor, not the horizon.
- **Sensitive giant.** They sense the world through ground-conducted vibration. Eyesight is terrible, and **they cannot pinpoint flying creatures** — birds nest undisturbed in worm territory. The fly/hover party has a genuine strategic advantage before the fight starts.
- **Unstoppable Force.** Once a worm has sights on prey, only death turns it back. No terrain obstacle, no display of power, no diplomacy diverts it. The single vulnerability is the tongues — left exposed above ground while dragging victims down.
- **Territorial and asexual.** Two kingfissures in the same region means one of them is about to eat the other. Dying worms are theorized to grow new worms from detached tongues; juveniles follow herds and caravans eating stragglers.
- **Swallowed communities.** A kingfissure corpse is a mobile treasure vault. Entire armies mobilize when a worm dies somewhere accessible. Abandoned dens become defensible villages and workshops. Good hook-seed for any post-combat aftermath: what happens to the tunnels, and who claims the gut?

## Kingfissure Worm Malice features

Spent at the start of the worm's turn or when an action's trigger occurs.

- **Aftershock (3 Malice, triggered).** When anything damages the worm or one of its tongues, each creature within 5 takes 5 damage and is proned on A<4. Once per round. Cheap, ever-present tax on melee attackers; budget one use per round of the fight.
- **Solo Action (5 Malice).** One additional main action this turn, usable **even while dazed**. The daze-bypass matters because Spontaneous Regeneration dazes the worm — Solo Action is how a regenerated worm still swings. Also counters save-ends daze from heroes.
- **Spontaneous Regeneration (5 Malice).** Lose 35 Stamina, regrow one tongue (max three). Worm becomes dazed until end of turn. The tongue-economy reset — if the party has killed two tongues to shut down grabs, the worm trades 35 Stamina to put them right back. Pair with Solo Action to attack through the daze.
- **Megaquake (7 Malice, Main action, 5-burst).** Tier-scaling damage + slide 5 on M-fail. **Effect until end of next round:** each target has -3 stability, treats all terrain as difficult, and takes 10 damage whenever proned. The setup/punish combo — Megaquake → King's Fissure fall → 10 bonus damage from the prone. Also disables most heroes' stability against subsequent forced-movement swallow attempts.

## Kingfissure Worm

**Role:** (solo — no additional role)
**Tags:** level 7, solo, EV 108, beast, worm
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 187–188

**Flavor:** Size 5, Stamina 420, Speed 10 **Burrow**, Stability 5, Free Strike 8. Might +5 / Agility +1 / Reason -5 / Intuition +2 / Presence -3. **Solo Turns** — two turns per round, not consecutive. **End Effect** — at end of each turn, take 10 unresistable damage to end one save-ends effect.

**Tactics:** A grab-drag-swallow solo built around three semi-independent tongues and a crushing burrow-and-breach game. The worm doesn't chase; it positions the map so the party has nowhere to stand that isn't about to become its mouth.

**Traits:**
- **Multiple Tongues.** Three tongues, each a 5×1 line within 1 square of the worm, 35 Stamina, **psychic immunity all**, can't be force-moved. Each tongue grabs one Size 3-or-smaller target. **Tongues can only be targeted while grabbing** — the party can't preempt them; they have to take the grab, *then* carve the tongue off. Psychic-damage builds are pointless here; physical and elemental only.
- **Seismic King.** Line of effect only within 3 squares — but the worm **ignores concealment for any ground-touching creature and doesn't need line of effect against them**. Cover, darkness, and invisibility mean nothing to a hero standing on dirt. The only way to hide is to leave the ground.
- **Swallowed.** A swallowed creature is restrained and takes 1d6 acid per turn. **If the worm takes 25+ damage in a single round from swallowed creatures, it regurgitates everyone** — they land prone within 3 squares. Intentional self-swallow into the belly with a big-damage PC is a legitimate party plan on this fight.
- **Titanic Tunneler.** Burrows through stone, leaving stable Size 3 tunnels. The worm permanently reshapes the map; the GM should track the tunnel network for the post-fight aftermath and for round-to-round re-emergence points.
- **Unstoppable Crawler.** Cannot be frightened or proned. While restrained or slowed, the worm takes -2 speed *instead of* the condition's normal speed effect. Hard-CC locks don't exist on this fight — damage and save-ends debuffs on Might-saves (for the Megaquake/Tongue Grab riders) are what move the needle.

**Signature — Tongue Grab** (Melee 5, one target per tongue, 2d10+5): 13/18/22 damage + grab on M<3/M<4/M<5, tier 3 also **banes the Escape Grab**. Requires at least one tongue. **Maneuver rider:** pull up to two grabbed creatures adjacent to the worm. Round one opens with three simultaneous tongue grabs at 5-square range — three heroes immediately disadvantaged before the worm even swings its maw. Then pull two adjacent and ready a Consume/Maw on the following solo turn.

**Maw** (Charge, Melee 1, 2d10+5): 15/20/25 damage + push 3 / push 5 prone / **swallowed on tier 3**. **2 Malice upgrade:** the charge ignores difficult terrain and destroys mundane Size-3-or-smaller objects in the path; the first hero whose square the worm crosses during the charge takes 8 damage and is pushed 3. The opening-round move when the worm surfaces in the middle of the party — charge through the frontline on the way to the squishy, tearing a trench through any wagons, buildings, or barricades in between.

**Consume** (2 Malice, Main action, Melee 1, one grabbed creature): swallows, period. The follow-through to Tongue Grab — grab with a tongue, pull adjacent with the maneuver, Consume on solo turn two. Three tongues + Consume + solo turns = up to three party members inside the worm on round two. This is how total party knockouts happen.

**Tongue Whip** (Maneuver, Melee 5 or Ranged 10, one target): only usable while something is grabbed. Slams the grabbed creature into the target, dealing 13 to both. **At range, +5 damage and the grabbed creature is released.** Two uses: (1) melee slam to stack damage on two heroes with one action; (2) ranged slam to launch the grabbed hero as a projectile at an ally across the map, then release them — the tongue-catapult. Opens the door to throwing a party member 10 squares into an environmental hazard.

**Tearing Recoil** (Triggered, when a tongue takes damage without dying, 2d10+5): 5 damage to the grabbed creature or object, release, retract the tongue. The worm's *choice* — any time a player targets a tongue below-kill threshold, the worm can convert the hit into "5 damage + release + tongue-back-in-mouth" and set up a fresh Tongue Grab next turn. Means the only way to permanently remove a tongue is to kill it outright (35 Stamina in a single attack chain, or survive multiple rounds of grabs + damage + regrab).

**Villain actions:**
- **VA1 King's Fissure** (20×4 line within 1, ground creatures only). Area *becomes* a 10-square-deep fissure. Agility test: ≤1 takes 10 damage, falls in, lands prone, can't stand EoT; 2 takes 10 damage and is left hanging at the fissure edge; 3 shifts to nearest unoccupied space outside. The map-rewriting open — a canyon through the party's formation. **Combined with Megaquake's "10 damage when proned" rider, the fall itself ticks for an extra 10 on the prone-landing.** Heroes hanging at the edge are one more push away from falling.
- **VA2 Earth Breach.** Usable only while burrowing. Worm burrows up to half speed, breaches, and moves 5 straight up before dropping back. Any creature whose space the worm passes through takes 10 damage + prone on A<4; **any creature made winded by this is swallowed.** The submarine-into-carrier-strike — the worm dives in round one, breaches under the party in round two, surfaces through the healer, and swallows them if winded. The "winded → swallowed" clause targets characters already taking damage.
- **VA3 Better Out Than In** (5-burst, 2d10+5): 8/13/17 acid damage + weakened save-ends on P<3/<4/<5. **Effect:** every swallowed creature is regurgitated and automatically gets the tier-3 outcome, landing prone within 5. The "I'm tired of you in my stomach" button — high-Presence heroes who've been stabbing the worm from inside get ejected, take full damage, and land weakened in the worm's 5-burst threat range. Closes the self-swallow strategy once the worm has had enough.

**Counterplay:**
1. **Fly or hover.** Kingfissures cannot pinpoint flying creatures — Seismic King and most of the tongue-grab pipeline requires ground contact. A party with even one flier drastically changes the fight's shape; an all-flying party plays a different game entirely.
2. **Target tongues strategically.** Each tongue is 35 Stamina, psychic-immune, can't be force-moved, and auto-triggers Tearing Recoil when not killed outright in one strike chain. Focus-fire to overkill; don't trickle damage.
3. **Self-swallow as offense.** The "25+ damage in a round from swallowed creatures forces regurgitation" clause is a legitimate win condition for melee brutes. Get eaten on purpose, burst the gut, everyone pops out prone-but-alive. Be aware of VA3 — once the worm sees the strategy, it ejects everyone with weakened save-ends.
4. **Save the burst for the breach.** VA2 Earth Breach is signaled by the worm burrowing at all — if the worm goes under, expect a surface strike at the party's softest target in one or two rounds. Have a stability check and action ready.
5. **Save-ends pressure is weak.** End Effect clears one save-ends at 10 unresistable per turn, and Unstoppable Crawler trivializes restrained/slowed. Stick to damage, daze, and forced-movement (within the Stability-5 budget, which Megaquake lowers to 2).
6. **Don't clump.** Aftershock and King's Fissure and Better Out Than In are all AoEs. Spread the formation — the worm's signature move is punishing any hero who stands next to another hero.
