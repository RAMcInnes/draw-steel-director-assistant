# Fossil Cryptic

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 142–143. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Cave-diving humans who first found fossil cryptics thought they were fossilized reanimated skeletons predating recorded history. A priest tried to turn them — learned they aren't undead seconds before being crushed by a pillar of stone. Fossil cryptics are **elementals**: primal spirits from Quintessence inhabiting the bones of creatures who once guarded the site. They're found in places of otherworldly beauty connected to Quintessence — caves untouched for eons, tunnels laced with fractal veins of priceless metal. Archaeologists, miners, and tomb-raiders who trespass get ground into dust and absorbed into the cryptic's own form.

- **Primal Sentries.** Inheritors of an ancient guardian's duty. They often *warn* trespassers in ancient languages before attacking — good hook for a parley attempt before initiative. Turn negotiation into the first round's decision point.
- **Bones of the Earth.** Cryptics vary wildly in shape but share a central cluster of fossils atop a churning column of rock and metal. Malleable limbs extend from the earthy core — unsettling speed despite the size.
- **Ancient Intelligence.** They weaponize columns of stone at range and manipulate the ground around them, pushing and pulling friend and foe into the best positions for battle.
- **Languages.** Phorialtic most commonly, plus the ancient languages of whichever ruins they inhabit. Useful if the party has a linguist — lets them decode inscriptions in the cryptic's territory *and* attempt negotiation in a dead tongue the cryptic respects.

## Fossil Cryptic Malice features

At the start of the fossil cryptic's turn, Malice may be spent to activate one of the following.

- **Floor Mosaic (3 Malice).** Slide one creature on the ground up to 5 squares. **If the creature is prone, this forced movement ignores stability** — the Stone Bone Storm / Sand Slam prone-locker pipeline sets up Floor Mosaic, and the slide is unblockable. Use this to drag a hero into a Stone Pillars trap or into the churning aura.
- **Solo Action (5 Malice).** Additional main action this turn, usable **even while dazed**. The daze clause matters — the End Effect only clears save-ends, so Solo Action is the escape valve when a controller has stapled dazed on without a save.
- **Stone Pillars (5 Malice).** Two 1-square pillars thrust up or down anywhere on the map, up to 5 squares tall. A creature in a pillar's square before it appears is **knocked prone on its surface**; if the creature contacts ceiling or floor and has **M<2** they are **restrained (save ends)**. Map-shaping tool — carve sightlines, ceiling-pin a hero at altitude, or drop a pillar behind a retreating party to block escape.
- **Choking Dust (10 Malice).** Encounter-long: air across the whole map thickens with dust, **every enemy in the dust is suffocating**. At end of each round, every enemy makes a **Might test** — ≤11 stability reduced to 0 + force movement against them gains +2 squares, 12–16 stability halved (EoT), 17+ no effect. The tier-3 escape valve for Might-strong heroes means this feature doesn't brick low-Might parties but is an easy round-1 cleanse for high-Might ones. Spend when the party is stacked around a Stone Pillars trap — the stability-0 clause makes Floor Mosaic and Sand Slam's forced-movement riders devastating.

## Fossil Cryptic

**Role:** (solo — no additional role)
**Tags:** level 2, solo, EV 48, elemental
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 143

**Flavor:** Size 1L cluster of fossils atop a churning column of rock and metal. Speed 8 **Burrow**, Stamina 250, Stability 3, Might +3 / Agility +2 / Reason +1 / Intuition +1. **Solo Turns** — two turns per round, not consecutive. **End Effect** — at end of each turn, take 5 unresistable damage to end one save-ends effect.

**Tactics:** A forced-movement solo that owns vertical and horizontal space. **Traits:**
- **Churning Trunk (aura 1).** Ranged abilities targeting the cryptic take a bane, and any enemy entering the aura for the first time in a round or starting their turn in it takes 5 damage. Close combat punishes sustained adjacency — park the cryptic in the middle of the party and let the aura tax every round.
- **Seismic Step.** Ignores difficult terrain, and has **line of effect to any creature with concealment if that creature is touching the ground**. Combined with VA3's ceiling pillars, this means the cryptic can target a hero hiding behind cover, in smoke, in a Duskcaller's darkness cube — so long as their feet are on the floor. Only *flying* truly blocks it.

**Signature Ability — Sand Slam** (Melee 2, two creatures/objects) — 8/12/15 + **slide 2 / slide 2 + prone / slide 3 + prone and can't stand (EoT)** on A<1/A<2/A<3; **Effect:** *already-prone targets become restrained (EoT) instead*. The combo: Sand Slam round 1 prones a low-Agility hero, Sand Slam round 2 (or on the cryptic's second solo turn) upgrades to restrained — then Floor Mosaic slides the restrained hero into the Churning Trunk aura or a Stone Pillars zone.

**Stone Bone Storm** (6×1 line within 1, main action) — 4/7/10 + **M<1 push 2 / M<2 prone / M<3 prone**; **Effect:** *the cryptic reforms and appears in an unoccupied space in the area*. The teleport clause is the hook — Stone Bone Storm is both AoE and a relocation, letting the cryptic dive into a backline cluster, prone the squishies, and land inside the line so next turn's Sand Slam adjacency is free.

**Stoneshift** (Ranged 5 maneuver, one ground-touching target) — slide 3; **2 Malice:** additional target. A cheap maneuver re-position that threads into any main action — slide the party's defender *out* of Sand Slam range, then Sand Slam the exposed controller.

**Dissipate** (1 Malice triggered, on taking damage) — halve damage, ignore nondamaging effects, shift 3. The defensive escape valve; pair with End Effect to roll through two save-ends debuffs in the same round while still repositioning.

**Shatterstone** (5 Malice triggered, 2-burst, on using the Dig maneuver to resurface) — before resurfacing, move up to speed then burst; 4/7/10 + **push 2 / push 3 + prone / push 4 + prone**. Use Burrow to travel under the party, then resurface in their middle with Shatterstone. With Speed 8 + Burrow, the cryptic can cross a 16-square map underground and breach into the backline.

**Villain Actions:**
- **VA1 First Warning Quake** (10 burst, each ground enemy) — Might test: ≤11 prone and can't stand (EoT), 12–16 prone, 17+ nothing. An opener that prones the entire ground-based party — sets up Sand Slam → restrained, and makes Floor Mosaic's "ignores stability on prone targets" universally live.
- **VA2 Final Warning Fissure** (5 burst, each ground enemy) — area drops 2 squares, becomes difficult terrain; *target enemies fall, target allies drop safely*. Then Agility test: ≤11 9 + prone, 12–16 5, 17+ move to nearest space outside the area. The map literally caves beneath the party — *drops* means fall damage on top of the listed damage if the GM adjudicates the pit depth.
- **VA3 No Escape** (Ranged, two power rolls) — first roll on two targets in Ranged 10: 5/9/12 + prone + **M<1/M<2/M<3 restrained (save ends)**. Second roll on two ground targets: 2/3/4 + vertical slide 2/4/6; **at tier 3, if the vertical slide contacts the ceiling, the target is restrained (save ends)**. The "vertical slide 6 into ceiling for restrained" on a map with a ≤6-square ceiling height means the party's brute and striker get pinned to the ceiling, restrained, adjacent to *nothing* they can hit. The cryptic then slow-grinds them with Stoneshift + Stone Pillars while the rest of the party scrambles to reach them.

**Counterplay:** fly or climb to break Seismic Step's ground-touching clause and the VA3 ceiling pin; high-Might and high-Agility builds weather the prone/restrained pipeline; bring save-ends cleansing because the cryptic's own End Effect outpaces most single-target debuffs. Focus-fire between the two solo turns to force End Effect trades and Dissipate commits. Stay mobile — Churning Trunk punishes stationary melee, so the cryptic *wants* the party to clump adjacent to it. The negotiation window before initiative is a genuine out: if the party speaks Phorialtic (or the ruin's ancient tongue) and commits to leaving, a cryptic may let them go.
