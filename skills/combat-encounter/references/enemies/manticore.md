# Manticore

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 202–203. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

A lion-bodied, dragon-winged, scorpion-tailed apex predator with the unsettling trick of wearing a humanoid face. Manticores develop ancestry-typical features from whichever humanoid population dominates the region they were born in — a manticore in devil-infested lowlands sprouts horns and fiendish eyes; one in dwarven mountains looks something like a bearded kinsman. They're born knowing the regional tongue well enough to mimic, though they rarely form full sentences — hunger does most of their talking. Travelers in manticore territory hear a friendly voice in the woods, or a shout of "help!" from a rockshelf, and walk into serrated teeth.

- **Lured by voice.** The classic manticore encounter begins before initiative: a mimicked plea, a shopkeeper's greeting, a child's laugh in an alley. Good hook material for stealth/perception tests or full-on social scenes before the fight. A party who fails their check walks into Carnivorous Bite range; one who succeeds can stage their own ambush.
- **Sky hunter.** Fly is standard movement. The manticore's preferred opening shoots poison-tipped tail spikes from altitude to weaken prey, then closes with its howl and bite. An encounter without meaningful vertical play sells the fight short.
- **Heralds of death.** A magical howl that unnerves and frightens. Frightened heroes are easier to bite (edge on Carnivorous Bite) and the howl chains into the Solo's best round.
- **Companions, rarely.** A manticore that has tasted humanoid flesh is ruined for domestication; a juvenile raised from before first kill can be a fiercely loyal mount or companion, and remains so even after it eventually eats a person. Plot lever for druid / ranger / beast-tamer character hooks.
- **Treasure-cave.** Manticores hoard inadvertently — they drag prey to their lair, and prey brings gear. Lairs are strewn with bones, broken weapons, coins, and odd relics. Abandoned old lairs are unguarded loot for anyone willing to trek into manticore country. The book lists sample treasures (Blade of the Luxurious Fop, Bull Shot, Stop-'n-Go Coin) and components (adamantine spearheads, a priest-blessed mirror, pixie wings) for the GM to seed.
- **Languages.** Most manticores mimic Caelian plus one regional language. Rarely negotiate — hunger first.

## Manticore Malice features

Spent at the start of the manticore's turn.

- **Uncanny Mimicry (3 Malice).** The manticore mimics a voice to unnerve one target in line of effect: target with R<4 takes a bane on power rolls against the manticore, save ends. **Each subsequent use on the same target weakens by 2** (R<2, then R<0, then fails). So the first Mimicry against a low-Reason hero lands clean; repeat uses become less reliable. Target-switch the mimicry across the party, don't spam on one.
- **Solo Action (5 Malice).** One additional main action, usable **even while dazed**. Pairs with Solo Turns (two turns per round) — a 5-Malice round lets the manticore take three main actions spread across two turns, typically Tail Spike + Carnivorous Bite + Carnivorous Bite on a softened target.
- **Desperate Howl (5 Malice).** Every enemy in line of effect makes an Intuition test: ≤1 frightened save-ends, 2 frightened EoT, 3 no effect. A cheaper map-wide fear effect than VA1 (no burst cap, but weaker ceiling). Spend when the party is spread out and VA1's 5-burst won't catch them.
- **Barrage of Barbs (7 Malice).** Sprays a 5-square zone around the manticore: A<3 targets in the area are bleeding save-ends; the area becomes difficult terrain and any enemy takes 3 poison per square of the area they enter. **Taking 9+ poison from this in one turn = weakened until end of encounter.** This is the "lock the ground" button — combine with fly+repositioning so the manticore sits above the barbs while the melee frontline has to wade through them to reach it.

## Manticore

**Role:** (solo — no additional role)
**Tags:** level 4, solo, EV 72, beast, manticore
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 203

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 350, Speed 10 **Fly**, Stability 2, Free Strike 6. Might +4 / Agility +3 / Reason 0 / Intuition 0 / Presence -1. **Solo Turns** — two turns per round, not consecutive. **End Effect** — at end of each turn, take 10 unresistable damage to end one save-ends effect.

**Tactics:** A flying apex predator with a four-phase game: lure (Uncanny Mimicry pre-fight), soften (Tail Spike from altitude), unnerve (Howl/VA1), devour (Carnivorous Bite on frightened target for edge). Use Fly + Agile Predator to stay out of opportunity-attack range while cycling strikes.

**Traits:**
- **Agile Predator.** Whenever the manticore deals damage to a creature, it doesn't provoke opportunity attacks from that creature during that turn. Bite a hero, fly away; Tail Spike a hero from altitude, drift backward; no tax. Essentially free disengage against anyone already on the hit list.

**Signature — Carnivorous Bite** (Melee 1, 2d10+4): 12/17/21 damage + bleeding save-ends on A<2/A<3/A<4. **Effect:** *if the target is frightened, this ability gains an edge.* The reason Trumpeting Howl and Desperate Howl exist — fear-then-bite is the central combo. Bleeding save-ends stacks with the manticore's End Effect (it doesn't clear *its own* debuffs, only heroes').

**Tail Spike** (Ranged 10, two targets, 2d10+4): 6/11/14 damage + poison and weakened save-ends on M<2/M<3/M<4 riders. **1 Malice rider:** a weakened target takes 1d6 poison at start of each of their turns. The ranged softener. Opens the fight from altitude against two heroes at once; typically the healer and the controller. **VA3's Debilitating Poison upgrades Tail Spike's weakened rider to "speed halved + 3 poison per turn" for the rest of the encounter.**

**Harrying Claws** (Maneuver, Melee 1, two targets): slide 1/2/4 + damage on A<2/A<3/A<4. The repositioning maneuver — knock a hero out of their defender's coverage, drag a controller into Barrage of Barbs, split the party formation. Cheap and works alongside a main action.

**Reflexive Instinct** (2 Malice, Triggered, when a creature deals damage, Ranged 10): manticore shifts up to 5 squares *into the air*, then uses Tail Spike against the target. The "don't punch me" response — any strike that damages the manticore gets a Tail Spike counterblast plus the manticore gains altitude. Costs 2 Malice, so the GM budgets maybe 2–3 uses across an encounter; pick the moments when a melee hero commits.

**Villain actions:**
- **Trumpeting Howl (VA1).** 5-burst, frightens each creature EoT with a save-ends upgrade on I<2/I<3/I<4 (tier 3 upgrades to *dazed* save-ends instead). The primary frightener — opens the fight or closes out a round where the party has clumped. Frightened → Carnivorous Bite edge is a massive damage amp.
- **Cornered Predator (VA2).** Manticore shifts up to speed, then Tail Spikes every enemy within distance. Movement-plus-multi-target. The all-hero damage burst when the party has spread to threaten from multiple angles. Land this when the manticore is low on Stamina and wants space.
- **Debilitating Poison (VA3).** Until end of encounter, the manticore has **double edge on power rolls against weakened creatures**, and any creature weakened by Tail Spike has speed halved and takes +3 poison at start of each turn. A cumulative end-game buff: after Tail Spikes have seeded weakened across the party, VA3 converts that into a permanent status and gives the manticore double-edge-on-bite for every weakened hero. Typically round 3 or later.

**Counterplay:**
1. **Ranged damage.** Fly + Agile Predator make melee frustrating — strike, manticore drifts, strike again, manticore's already at altitude. Archers, casters, and anyone with throw/reach is built for this fight.
2. **Presence saves for fear.** Every key attack pipeline runs through frightened-from-howl into Bite-with-edge. A character who can dispel fear or grant bonuses against frightened conditions carves the encounter's damage budget in half.
3. **Control the ground.** Barrage of Barbs + Cornered Predator punish clumped or stationary parties. Spread out; keep movement options open; bring forced-movement-resistance where possible.
4. **Mitigate weakened.** Tail Spike seeds weakened to set up VA3's double-edge. Cleanse weakened early, or bring high-Might characters to make the M<-saves.
5. **End Effect spends are the pacing meter.** Each round the manticore trades 10 Stamina to clear a save-ends. Against a 350-Stamina solo with Solo Turns, that's 20+ damage per round in self-inflicted cleansing. Stack save-ends debuffs to force trades even if they'll be cleared.
6. **Mimicry is pre-fight.** If the GM is using the manticore's mimicry as a hook, a high-Presence social character who sees through the lure flips the encounter from ambush to prepared fight — a genuine narrative outcome. Bring it up in session prep.
