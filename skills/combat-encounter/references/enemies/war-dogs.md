# War Dogs

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 297–320. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book. 1st Echelon entries are detailed; 2nd–4th Echelon entries are descriptions only.*

Ajax the Iron Saint's patchwork army — stitched, welded, and alchemically fused soldiers built from body parts harvested by the Body Banks. They aren't undead: they have minds, wills, and drives. But any remnants of a soul are destroyed in the making. War dogs are metaphysically unstable, incapable of love or empathy, and highly malleable — *the ideal disposable soldier for a discerning tyrant*. Fresh recruits undergo brief training camps, are fitted with explosive **loyalty collars** (unremovable neck pieces rigged with fuse-iron charges), and sent to join a legion. Insubordination, inefficiency, or even questioning an order sends a war dog back to the Body Banks.

> **Content Warning: Brainwashing and Body Horror.** War dogs are explicitly evil soldiers built from stitched-together humanoids — Frankenstein's monster as shock trooper. Check in with players first; modify the appearance and lore as needed for table comfort.

- **Loyalty Collar.** Every war dog has one. When reduced to 0 Stamina, the collar explodes — damage scales with Echelon: 1d3 at Echelon 1 minions, 1d6 at Echelon 1 horde, 2d6 at Echelon 2, 3d6 at Echelon 3–4. Dying war dogs are *landmines*. Adjacency to a low-Stamina war dog is a trap.
- **Posthumous Promotion.** Most 1st Echelon horde war dogs have this maneuver — Ranged 10 on one war dog ally with a loyalty collar, reducing them to 0 Stamina (triggering their collar explosion). The pack weaponizes its own casualties: Posthumous Promotion a wounded minion adjacent to a hero, detonate the collar, 1d6 damage + whatever follow-up Malice features compound on top.
- **Soulless.** War dogs share the *soulless* keyword with undead and constructs. Soul-targeting effects (certain holy/corruption interactions) don't latch on.
- **Captain-centric tactics.** Minion frontlines backed by captains with control/support abilities. Captains throw minions into the fray without caution, then manually detonate collars when the minions are spent. Non-captain war dogs rarely show initiative — without an officer, a war dog squad stagnates.
- **Body Bank logistics.** Dead war dogs are recycled. The fiction supports waves — if the party wipes a squad, the next fight in a war-dog-held zone can reasonably feature freshly-rebuilt versions of the same fallen units.
- **Languages.** Most war dogs speak Caelian and one Vaslorian human language.

## Shared traits

- **Loyalty Collar.** On every war dog. Plan kill-order around it: ranged kills over melee; don't cluster heroes adjacent to a low-Stamina war dog; the Posthumous Promotion → collar explosion combo shapes how fast a pack closes in.
- **Captain-multiplier kit.** Many minions have "With Captain" bonuses (+damage, +speed, +range, edge) that scale based on whether a captain is attached to the squad. The captain choice transforms which minion squad is dangerous.

## War Dog Malice features

Tiered by war dog level. Higher-tier war dogs access any prior tier via **Prior Malice Features** 3–7 Malice.

### Level 1+

- **Reconstitute (3 Malice).** One war dog this turn tears apart a nearby humanoid corpse and regains Stamina equal to 5× their level. Self-healing that keys off the corpses the party has been dropping — combat produces war dog fuel.
- **Fire for Effect (5 Malice, Area, 4-cube within 10, Maneuver).** Fire damage + slowed OR weakened (Director's choice of one condition for all affected) on Agility-fail. The map-wide fire-support button.
- **Fodder Run (7 Malice).** Each war dog minion shifts up to their speed and makes a free strike. Each minion who does so is then reduced to 0 Stamina. **Literally trades the minion line for one round of frontline damage and triggers every Loyalty Collar across the pack simultaneously.** Spend when minions are all in melee adjacent to heroes — the cascade of collar detonations is the primary event.

### Level 4+

- **Loyalty Unto Death (5 Malice, Ranged 10, 2 war dogs, Maneuver).** Two collared war dogs shift up to speed and are reduced to 0 Stamina. After each Loyalty Collar resolves, each adjacent enemy makes a Presence test: tier 1 push 4 + frightened save-ends, tier 2 push 2 + frightened EoT, tier 3 push 2. Directed bomb-delivery — pick two expendable horde units, ship them across the map into the party cluster, detonate.

### Level 7+

- **Alchemical Cloud (7 Malice).** Chemical bank sweeps the encounter map. Each enemy makes a Might test: tier 1 8 poison + dazed EoT, tier 2 7 poison + weakened EoT, tier 3 4 poison. Map-wide condition spray.

### Level 10+

- **Cry Havoc (7 Malice, 3-burst, non-minion only).** Psychic damage + frightened save-ends on P-fail. **Effect:** each war dog within distance deals +15 damage on strikes until end of next turn, ends one save-ends effect on themselves, and shifts+free strike. The alpha-round button — spend when every war dog is positioned for a strike.

## 1st Echelon

The Body Banks are filled with enemies of the state, conquered peoples, war dogs who have died twice, and animal/monster scraps. The flesh sculptors take pride in crafting a highly destructive, single-mindedly fanatical army. Most 1st Echelon war dogs are simple amalgams: patchwork skin, mismatched limbs, unsettling proportions — they look artificial, and they know it.

### War Dog Ground Commander — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 3, leader, EV 20, humanoid, soulless, war dog
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 303

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 120, Speed 5, Stability 2, Free Strike 5. The 1st Echelon captain who actually thinks.

**Tactics:** **Conditioning Spear** (Signature, Melee 1 or Ranged 5, two targets) — damage + pull 1/2/3. **Effect:** one ally within 10 makes a free strike. **1 Malice rider:** target with I<2 adjacent after resolution is grabbed save-ends, **can't be escaped with Escape Grab maneuver** (can hold two at once). The ground commander pulls heroes into auto-grab and hands out pack free-strikes every turn. **Highest Posthumous Promotion** (10-burst Maneuver): *every* collared war dog in the area is reduced to 0 Stamina. The nuke button — wipe the pack's remaining minions for map-wide Loyalty Collar detonation. **Final Orders** (Triggered, Ranged 10, one ally): target who is damaged/force-moved/reduced to 0 Stamina *gets one more turn* — moves speed and free-strikes — then dies. Refuses to let a dying soldier stop fighting. **End Effect** (5 unresistable self-damage to clear save-ends).

Villain actions:
- **Combined Arms (VA1).** 10-burst; each ally makes a ranged free strike *then* Charges. Mass opener.
- **Make an Example of Them (VA2).** Ranged 10 one enemy; every ally within 5 of target moves up to speed + free strike; I<2 target is frightened of the commander save-ends. Focus-fire button.
- **Claim Them for the Body Banks (VA3).** 10-burst; each ally shifts 2 + Grabs; each enemy takes a bane on Escape Grab until end of encounter. Sets up the post-battle "you lose" narrative — the war dogs don't kill, they *recover* for the Body Banks.

Counterplay: kill the ground commander first to collapse free-strike economy; Presence saves for VA2 fear; damage the pack's own minions to cash their Loyalty Collars back at the war dogs.

## 2nd Echelon (descriptions only)

Ajax's army bears extraordinary equipment: **shrikeguns** (torsion-powered rapid-fire bolt throwers whose stakes scream on release — the "shrike scream" breaks less-disciplined armies); **fuse-iron** (unstable alchemical metal that converts force into heat and light — flames, explosions, concussive bursts); **houndweapons** (rare living weapons made from insubordinate war dogs themselves — horrific blends of flesh, machine, and spirit, requisitioned only by the highest ranks).

- **War Dog Sparkslinger** (Level 4 Minion Artillery, EV 6 for four) — Galvanic Arc (Ranged 7) lightning with spread that damages nearby enemies; captain increases spread. Chain-lightning minion.
- **War Dog Sweeper** (Level 4 Minion Harrier, EV 6 for four) — Shrikegun Shot (Melee 1 or Ranged 3) push + *+3 damage to targets within 2 squares of the sweeper*. **Shrapnel-Laced Loyalty Collar:** 1d6 damage to each enemy/object *within 2 squares* (not just adjacent).
- **War Dog War Frog** (Level 4 Minion Ambusher, EV 6 for four) — Size 1S, Climb + Swim, Poison Immunity. Poisoned Dagger (Melee 1 or Ranged 4), and jumps 3 before/after striking; if ending in cover/concealment, can hide.
- **War Dog Arachnite** (Level 6 Horde Artillery, EV 8) — Size 1L, Climb, Psychic Immunity 6. Longarm Shrikegun (Ranged 15, ignores cover/concealment, **chooses damage type per strike**: acid/cold/fire/lightning/poison/psychic/sonic). Web Vial maneuver paints an area difficult terrain until encounter end. **Eight-Eyed Sight:** automatically knows every hidden creature within 10 at turn start.
- **War Dog Doomthief** (Level 5 Horde Defender, EV 7) — Size 1L. Ripper Shrikegun (10×3 line) push + tier-3 slowed. **Doom Magnet:** 3-aura of warped fate **blocks line of effect for any enemy ability that doesn't include the doomthief as a target.** The defender that forces a hero to either attack them or be unable to target anything behind them. **Expanding Doom** (4 Malice Maneuver) adds +3 to the aura and damage immunity 4.

- **War Dog Tormentite** (Level 5 Horde Hexer, EV 7) — Mark of Agony (Ranged 10) corruption + marked save-ends with edge on strikes against mark; whenever the tormentite takes damage, each marked target takes 3. **Vortex of Pain** (3 Malice, 4-cube within 10) corruption + tier 3 lets one ally in the area transfer a save-ends effect. **Persistent Pain:** self-damages 1 at start of each turn — runs on hurt.

Houndblade (Charge, Melee 1 or Ranged 3, two targets) — taunted EoT with bane-on-strikes; 3 Malice loses 1d3 Recoveries. **Get Them, You Dolts!** (1 Malice/target, three creatures, Maneuver): each target shifts + free strike; +4 damage vs tetrarch-taunted targets. **Sneering Disregard** (Triggered): a non-taunted creature attacking the tetrarch gets a double bane; tier 1 = tetrarch ignores effects other than damage and target is frightened. **End Effect.** Villain actions: Enter the Fray (2-burst with 7-square jump, frightened), Lay Waste (five 2-cubes within 20, fire + slowed, area stays ablaze for encounter), You Would Dare?! ## 3rd Echelon (descriptions only)

War dogs who prove loyalty get special Body Bank attention — smoothed construction, higher-quality parts, *nearly ordinary appearances*. **Happy accidents** produce unusual deviants given extra attention. **Made to Order** war dogs radically alter creation processes, fusing with inorganic materials to fulfill specific roles — often bearing little resemblance to any humanoid, and considered badges of honor.

## 4th Echelon (descriptions only)

At the apex of the war dog command are infamous named figures — brutal tactics, legendary prowess, unbreakable loyalty. **Castellan Hoplon** is the master defender whose scars come from combat rather than the Banks, famous for harrying retreats, holdfasts, and last stands. **Iron Champion Doru**'s accidental creation required Ajax himself to stop his initial rampage; his untempered aggression and mysterious regeneration make him a battlefield monster. **Logostician Vesper** is a living portal network — the emergent personality of several dozen psychic minds working in concert from within a mobile flesh chassis, managing supply lines and transport. **Soulbinder Psyche** ("The Goddess of the Banks") can return from the Banks again and again without losing her core self; master of the soul-flesh connection. **Strategos Alkestis** ("The Silver Wolf") is one of the most brilliant war-dog commanders ever, willing to stoop to any depths for tactical edge.

- **War Dog Blood Jumper** (Level 10 Minion Harrier, EV 12 for four) — Fly. Jumplance (Charge, Melee 1) tier-3 bleeding. **Drop Troop:** falls prone if they don't end their turn on the ground.
- **War Dog Hunter-Killer** (Level 10 Minion Artillery, EV 12 for four) — Fuse-Iron Rocket (Ranged 10) damage + push. **Effect:** each enemy adjacent to the target *before* the push takes 5 fire damage — punishes cluster formations.
- **War Dog Socialite** (Level 10 Minion Hexer, EV 12 for four) — Psychic Immunity 10, P +5. Call to Self-Sabotage (Psionic, Ranged 5) — **the target makes a free strike against themself at a tier 1/2/3 result**. Plus +5 damage per surge the target has. Self-harm compulsion minion.
- **Castellan Hoplon** (Level 10 Elite Defender, EV 48) — Stamina 260, Damage Immunity 3.
