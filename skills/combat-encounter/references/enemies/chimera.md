# Chimera

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 56–57. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Massive flexing claws, a lashing draconic tail, and nimble leathery wings — plus three menacing heads: a snarling lion, a helix-horned ram, and a dragon with fire sparking in their throat. Most see a chimera as an aggressor, but few know the creature's origin as a divine guardian. A single chimera is a full encounter on its own.

- **The First Chimera.** A deity gifted the first chimera to a devout queen — a young guardian with a dragon's cunning, a lion's bravery, and a ram's tenacity. When a conquering army slaughtered the beast, the queen's fury was said to be felt by the gods: a nearby volcano erupted, spewing basalt and chimeras across the land. The chimeras destroyed the opposing army, then dispersed — each finding a territory to claim as divine protector over the place, its inhabitants, and its treasures.
- **Tactical Warriors.** Chimeras keep themselves and their charges from being cornered. The lion head snaps up enemies in massive jaws and hurls them away, the ram head pummels them to the ground, and the dragon head bathes them in fire. The three heads crying out together can shake even the most stalwart warrior.
- **One Being, Three Heads.** Unlike some other multiheaded creatures, a chimera possesses a single unified mind. The heads work together — never snapping at one another over food, favors, or personality conflicts — and often express the chimera's emotions in unison, braying together in battle or purring all at once to show affection.
- **Flyer.** Speed 10, **Movement: Fly**, Immunity Fire 6. No weaknesses. **Volant** lets the chimera move their speed toward an enemy whenever they make a creature winded or drop one to 0 Stamina — after the Bite-and-Toss, the chimera flies to the next victim for free.
- **Solo economy.** Takes **two turns each round** (non-consecutive) via Solo Turns, plus villain actions. **End Effect** — at the end of each of its turns, the chimera can take 5 unresistable damage to end one save-ends effect on itself.

## Chimera Malice features

At the start of the chimera's turn, Malice may be spent to activate one of the following features.

- **Defensive Snapping (3 Malice).** Until the start of their next turn, the chimera can make a free strike against each enemy who comes within 2 squares — a reach-2 zone of control that punishes approach.
- **Solo Action (5 Malice).** The chimera takes an additional main action on their turn. Usable even while dazed.
- **Unstable Terrain (5 Malice).** The chimera shakes the ground, creating three size-2 stone objects anywhere on the map. Each creature adjacent to one or more objects when they appear who has A<2 takes 5 damage. Pillar-drops for line-of-sight denial and immediate damage in one feature.
- **Ashen Clouds (7 Malice).** Until the start of the chimera's next turn, the entire map is shrouded in thick ash. All creatures and objects have concealment that the chimera ignores, and each enemy in the cloud with I<1 has line of effect only within 3 squares. **Potency increases by 1 each use** — spam it mid-fight and the Intuition bar keeps creeping.

## Chimera

**Role:** solo
**Tags:** level 3, solo, EV 60, chimera, beast
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 57

**Flavor:** A size-2 flying beast with a lion's jaws, a ram's horns, and a fire-breathing dragon's maw, crowned with leathery wings and a draconic tail. Stamina 300, Speed 10 (Fly), Stability 1, Immunity Fire 6. Might +3 / Agility +2 / Reason –2 / Intuition +1 / Presence 0.

**Tactics:** Opens with **Bite** (signature, Melee 2, two targets, 2d10+3 — 9/13/16 damage; +3 if it has edge or double edge) to stack pressure on the frontline, then maneuvers with **Lion's Toss** (Melee 2, vertical push 2/3/5) to fling a victim off a wall or into a cluster — Volant then lets it fly its full speed toward the next target, turning each kill or winded trigger into repositioning. **Dragon's Eruption** (5 Malice, 3-cube within 10, fire + A-weaken save-ends) is the area pressure; **Roar** (5 Malice, 5-burst psychic + frightened save-ends on I<2 / I<3) is the lockdown — ram-head fear plus dragon-head fire stacked onto Bite. **Ram's Defiance** (triggered on tier-1 strike against the chimera) shifts up to 5 squares and counter-strikes with push and slowed (save ends) — the chimera punishes missed melee attempts by re-establishing spacing. Villain ladder escalates hard: **Overture of Destruction (VA 1)** is a 1-burst that repeats Bite + Lion's Toss on every enemy in the area — AoE tossing; **Fire Solo (VA 2)** fires Dragon's Eruption *and* Roar for free (no Malice); **Chorus of Destruction (VA 3)** casts Roar, then shifts full speed with free strikes against anyone it passes adjacent to, ending the shift with a free Dragon's Eruption. Because End Effect auto-cleans save-ends in 5-damage increments, stacking one condition isn't enough — parties need overlapping save-ends pressure to keep anything sticking. Counterplay: force it to land (fire-immune won't save it from being grappled or grounded), spread out to blunt AoE (Eruption, Roar, Overture, Ashen Clouds all punish clumping), and save big interrupts for the villain-action turn rather than the Bite turn — that's where the real damage spike lives.
