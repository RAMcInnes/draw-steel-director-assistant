# Humans

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 180–185. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Humans live in every habitable corner of the world and turn up in every faction, band, and cult a Director might need. They're the mercenaries, the bandits, the cultists, the city guard, the bandit chief, and sometimes the dark-armored knight leading the whole thing. The stat blocks in this section are the adversarial side of that range — the ones who draw weapons on heroes.

- **Villains and Heroes.** Short lives push humans to seek immortality through empire, monument, or apotheosis. Violence and greed are close cousins in the family. When ambition outstrips circumstance there's always some ancient evil willing to make a deal, so human adversaries frequently come in cultist flavor. Fortunately many humans go the other way and become heroes.
- **Risks and Rewards.** Humans gamble — at cards, at war, at life. They spot opponent mistakes fast and press advantages hard. Encounter-wise this reads as *opportunistic tactics*: humans chain off conditions the party applies to themselves, exploit a prone or slowed hero, and punish overcommitted defenders.
- **Swords for Hire.** Humans are the face of most mercenary bands. Mixed human adversaries — brawler, trickshot, storm mage, blackguard — read as a merc company for hire by whoever the villain-of-the-week is.
- **Connected to the Natural World.** Humans have an uncanny sense for detecting magic and psionics near them, and a related resistance to supernatural effects. This manifests mechanically as the **Supernatural Insight** trait (see below) and the corruption/psychic immunity on every human stat block.
- **Languages.** Most humans speak Caelian and one Vaslorian human language.

## Shared traits (every human)

- **Supernatural Insight.** Every human ignores concealment when the concealment is granted by a supernatural effect. Invisibility spells, shadowy illusions, fog-cloud magic — none of it hides a hero from a human enemy. Plan around the assumption that magic stealth simply does not function against this category.
- **Corruption and psychic immunity.** Every human stat block carries corruption and psychic immunity scaling with level (1/1 at L1, 2/2 at L2, 3/3 at L3, up to 4/4 on the Bandit Chief). Casters whose whole kit is psychic or corruption damage will bounce off. Bring a secondary damage type.
- **Malice-friendly structure.** The three Malice features combine well with almost any human mix, because most humans are single-role specialists and the Malice features are the cross-role glue. Exploit Opening in particular is the reason a mixed-party human encounter feels so *human* — everyone piling on whoever just got hit by a condition.

## Human Malice features

Spent at the start of any human's turn.

- **Alchemical Device (3 Malice, Maneuver, 3-cube within 10).** Area corruption damage with slowed/restrained save-ends on failed Agility tests across tiers. *Can't be used by a minion.* The horde/platoon zone-control tool — drop it on the back rank before a melee push.
- **Exploit Opening (5 Malice).** Each human acting this turn gains an edge on abilities until end of turn, *or a double edge on any ability that targets an enemy already affected by a condition.* The "kick them while they're down" button — fires off the round after Alchemical Device, a Knave's taunt, a Storm Mage's slow, or a hero's own misstep. This is the single most important Malice feature to plan around.
- **Staying Power (7 Malice).** Each non-minion human regains Stamina equal to 5× their level. The mid-fight reset — L2 heal 10 each, L3 heal 15. Spend when the party has just bloodied the platoon front line and expects to finish them off next round.

## Minions

Pick one or two minion types to form a base layer. All minions are size 1M with scaling immunity; all have **Supernatural Insight**.

### Human Apprentice Mage — minion

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 2, minion, human, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 181
**With Captain:** +5 bonus to ranged distance.

**Tactics:** **Lightning Strike** (Ranged 10) — chip lightning damage, and *if the apprentice doesn't use a maneuver or move action this turn, the target is also slowed EoT*. Encourage the apprentice to stay put for the slow rider; with Captain's +15 range the apprentice can slow a back-rank hero from most of the way across the map. Combine with Exploit Opening Malice to convert the slow into double-edge strikes across the rest of the platoon.

### Human Guard — minion

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 1, minion, human, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 181
**With Captain:** +5 bonus to speed.

**Tactics:** **Halberd** (Melee 2, one target per minion) — straightforward damage with a 2-square reach. *If the guard is flanked, they make a free strike against a different adjacent target.* Punishes parties who spread around a single guard; reward for holding the line. With Captain's +5 speed guards can sprint across an open street to the party's back line.

### Human Archer — minion

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, minion, human, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 181
**With Captain:** +5 bonus to ranged distance.

**Tactics:** **Crossbow** (Ranged 10) — basic ranged chip across tiers. Four archers on a rooftop with Captain's +5 range can suppress a 15-square zone. The dedicated range chaff — pair with a Knave or Blackguard who needs the party looking the wrong way.

### Human Raider — minion

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, minion, human, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 181
**With Captain:** gain an edge on strikes.

**Tactics:** **Handaxes** (Charge, Melee 1, one target per minion) — low damage, but *if used as part of a charge, the raider can make a ranged free strike before using the ability*. So a charging raider throws a handaxe, then closes and swings. Four raiders on a charge = four ranged free strikes + four melee hits. Captain's edge applies to the charge. Very efficient opener.

### Human Death Acolyte — minion

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 1, minion, human, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 181
**With Captain:** +5 bonus to ranged distance.

**Tactics:** **Necrotic Bolt** (Ranged 10) — low chip corruption damage with a rider that restores 1 Stamina to one creature within 5 squares. The acolyte is minion-grade healing — pair them with a Death Cultist or Blackguard to smooth out the platoon's damage curve. Combines with the Death Cultist's Rise My Minions if acolytes are the minions being revived.

### Human Rogue — minion

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 1, minion, human, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 181
**With Captain:** gain an edge on strikes.

**Tactics:** **Concealed Dagger** (Melee 1 or Ranged 5, one target per minion) — base damage unremarkable, but *if the rogue is disguised or hidden when they use it, extra 3 damage*. Hide in crowd pre-initiative (they're disguised), then reveal on turn 1 for the +3. This is a canonical "humans in a tavern / on a street / in a crowd" setup — heroes walk in, one of the patrons is a rogue, and the first strike of the fight is supercharged.

## Platoon

One platoon creature per hero in an all-platoon encounter, or sprinkle them through a mixed fight.

### Human Brawler

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, human, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 182

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 40, Speed 5, Free Strike 4. The bar-fight specialist.

**Tactics:** **Haymaker** (Melee 1) — solid base damage with grab + bane-on-Escape at tier 3. *A target who is already grabbed takes +2.* Grab, then Haymaker the grabbed target next turn. **Throw** (1 Malice, Maneuver) — push a grabbed target up to 5 squares. Grab, throw into difficult terrain or into a Storm Mage's Gust of Wind zone. **Shoot the Hostage** (passive) — when the brawler takes a strike while they have something size-1S+ grabbed, they halve the damage and *the grabbed creature takes the remainder.* This is the defining brawler move and a hero-psyche test: attacking a brawler holding your ally's cleric. Counterplay: separate the grab before attacking, or accept the damage split.

### Human Death Cultist

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 2, platoon, human, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 182

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 40, Speed 5, Free Strike 4. The cult-robed necromancer.

**Tactics:** **Death Scythe** (Melee 1 or Ranged 10) — corruption damage, with weakened save-ends at tier 3 on I<2. **2 Malice upgrade: the cultist regains Stamina equal to half damage dealt.** Sustain button — spend when the cultist is below half. **Rise, My Minions** (1 Malice per minion, 5-burst, Maneuver) — each dead minion in the area revives with full Stamina. Dies permanently at end of encounter or if the cultist is killed. The long-haul minion pump: kill a human minion field, watch it come back at the end of the round. Counterplay: drop the cultist before the revive, and remember that killing the cultist kills the revived minions outright.

### Human Knave

**Role:** defender
**Tags:** level 2, platoon, human, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 182

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 50, Speed 5, Free Strike 4. The mercenary front-liner.

**Tactics:** **Morningstar and Javelin** (Melee 1 or Ranged 5) — damage with double-bane-on-next-power-roll at tier 3 on M<2. *Effect: target is taunted EoT.* **I'm Your Enemy** (passive) — whenever an adjacent taunted creature deals damage to someone other than the knave, the knave makes a free strike against them. **Overwhelm** — an enemy who starts adjacent to the knave can't shift. The full defender package: taunt, punish for ignoring, lock in place. Pair with a Brawler who grabs the hero who tried to shift, or with a Storm Mage who can Gust of Wind anyone who'd break the taunt.

### Human Scoundrel

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, human, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 182

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 30, Speed 5, Free Strike 4. Rapier-and-dagger swashbuckler.

**Tactics:** **Rapier and Dagger** (Melee 1) — clean damage across tiers, and *2 Malice: if the ability gains an edge or double edge, +2 damage.* Combos with Exploit Opening to pile on conditioned heroes. **Dagger Storm** (5 Malice, Main action) — Rapier and Dagger against up to three targets with a 2-square shift before or after each strike. The scoundrel dances across a cluster, striking three heroes and ending in a new position. Counterplay: stand apart (dagger storm needs reachable targets), and don't let the scoundrel leave the fight with Dagger Storm's shift-after-strike.

### Human Storm Mage

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 3, platoon, human, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 183

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 40, Speed 5, Free Strike 5. The weather-turner.

**Tactics:** **Lightning Bolt** (Ranged 15) — single-target lightning damage. **5 Malice upgrade:** drops Ranged/Strike, becomes a 10×1 line area targeting each enemy and object in it. Save 5 Malice for when the party lines up. **Gust of Wind** (3 Malice, Maneuver, 5-cube within 1) — slide 2/4/6 per tier with slowed save-ends on failed Might tests; *disperses gas or vapor and extinguishes flames, including supernatural effects.* The controller and the anti-caster. Clears Hag oven zones, druid fire fields, etc. **Arcane Shield** (passive) — any melee ability targeting the storm mage takes a bane; whenever an adjacent enemy deals damage to the mage, that enemy takes 2 lightning damage and is pushed 2 on r<1. Self-defense built in. Counterplay: ranged attacks bypass the bane; don't cluster if Lightning Bolt goes line-mode.

### Human Trickshot

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, human, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 183

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 20, Speed 5, Free Strike 4. The crack shot.

**Tactics:** **Trick Crossbow** (Melee 1 or Ranged 15) — strong damage, and *ignores cover and concealment.* No terrain plan stops a trickshot. **3 Malice:** one additional target. The crossbow also fires in melee — it's a true hybrid weapon. Counterplay: the trickshot only has 20 Stamina. Close and kill.

## Mounts

### Giant Hawk — platoon mount

**Role:** mount
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, animal, human
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 185

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 30, Fly 5, Free Strike 3. The noble hunting companion — also makes a great rider mount for a raider or trickshot.

**Tactics:** **Talons** (Melee 1) — damage with grab at tier 3. **2 Malice:** extra damage on any tier that gains an edge or double edge — combos with Exploit Opening perfectly. **Dive** (1 Malice, Maneuver) — move up to speed. **Mounted Platform** — *once per turn when the hawk moves, any creature riding it can make a free strike during or after the movement.* Perfect combat engine for human cavalry: raider climbs on a hawk, Dive to close, raider free-strikes mid-flight, hawk lands, raider charges for its handaxe + ranged-free-strike combo.

## Leaders

### Human Blackguard — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 1, leader, human, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 184

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 80, Speed 5, Stability 2, Free Strike 4. The armored zweihander-swinging knight-commander. Reason-positive — a tactician, not just a bruiser.

**Tactics:** **Zweihander Swing** (Signature, Main action, 1-burst area) — damage across tiers with slowed save-ends on failed Might tests. *Effect: one ally within 10 squares can make a free strike.* **1 Malice:** upgrade the ally's free strike to their *signature ability.* So every Blackguard turn spins up a minion or platoon-level signature for free — Death Cultist's Death Scythe, Storm Mage's Lightning Bolt, Trickshot's Trick Crossbow, etc. This is the Blackguard as force multiplier. **You!** (Ranged 10, Maneuver) — mark one enemy; the blackguard and allies have an edge on abilities against marked targets. Pair with Exploit Opening Malice for stacking edges. **Parry!** (triggered action) — when a creature strikes the blackguard or an adjacent ally, halve the damage. **End Effect** for save-ends stripping.

Villain actions:
- **Advance! (VA1).** Shift up to speed, and during/after this movement use Zweihander Swing twice. Two bursts across the shift = potentially two different clusters hit on one action.
- **Back! (VA2).** 5-burst area — slide each target up to 5 squares. Reposition the entire engagement zone before a big round.
- **I Can Throw My Blade and So Should You! (VA3).** 3-cube within 5 — Zweihander Swing against each target, then *each ally within 5 squares can make a free strike (one per ally) against a target.* Map-shredding finisher that fires every adjacent ally's best strike at once.

Counterplay: (1) Break line of effect before the Blackguard can mark with You! (2) Kill or isolate the signature-reliable allies first — the 1 Malice upgrade on Zweihander Swing is the real engine, and it needs allies in range. (3) End Effect means save-ends debuffs don't land long; focus on damage.

### Human Bandit Chief — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 3, leader, human, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 184

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 120, Speed 5, Stability 2, Free Strike 5. The most statistically complete human stat block in the book — all five characteristic bonuses positive, with a magic longsword and a whip. Corruption/psychic immunity 4. A roadside warlord with blood magic.

**Tactics:** **Whip and Magic Longsword** (Signature, Melee 2, two targets) — heavy damage with pull 1/2/3. *Effect: any target adjacent to the chief after the power roll takes 3 corruption damage.* So the whip's pull often drags heroes into the corruption effect for extra damage. **2 Malice:** extra target. **Kneel, Peasant!** (Maneuver, Melee 1) — push 1/2/4 + prone on failed Might tests. **2 Malice:** becomes a 1-burst Area hitting every enemy in the burst. Cheap mass-prone. **Bloodstones** (triggered action, self) — when the bandit chief makes a power roll, they can take 5 unresistable corruption damage to *increase the tier of the result by one.* Always-on tier pump at the cost of self-damage, which Staying Power Malice then refunds. **End Effect** for save-ends stripping.

Villain actions:
- **Shoot! (VA1, 10-burst area).** Each artillery ally in the area makes a ranged free strike. Bandit Chief + four Archers + Trickshot = five free ranged strikes on one villain action.
- **Form Up! (VA2, 10-burst area).** Each ally target shifts up to speed, and *until end of encounter, while the bandit chief or any ally is adjacent to a target, that target has damage immunity 2.* The defensive phase-shift — the whole band becomes meaningfully tougher for the rest of the fight once they're in formation. Counterplay: isolate allies or kill the chief to reduce the stack.
- **Lead From the Front (VA3).** The chief shifts up to 10 squares. During or after, uses Whip and Magic Longsword against up to *four* targets, and one ally adjacent to each target makes a free strike. The act-three crescendo — massive repositioning, 4-target damage with corruption rider, plus 4 ally free strikes.

Counterplay: (1) Don't stand adjacent to the chief after a Whip and Longsword pull (the corruption rider). (2) Pull allies *away* from the chief before Form Up! locks in the damage immunity. (3) Burst the chief before VA3 — Bloodstones tier-pumps make every late-game roll worse than the one before.
