# Voiceless Talkers

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 286–291. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

The **synlirii** (sin-LEER-ee, singular *synliroi*) are among the most powerful psions in the timescape. The denizens of the World Below — and the unlucky surface-dwellers who've met them — know them as voiceless talkers. Descended from boneless aquatic cephalopods, they are genuinely alien in thought and motivation, and they loathe humanoid species with a specific, taxonomic hatred. They call humanoids "the barking ones" — creatures who squeeze air through meat flaps to communicate — and they consider this an abomination that requires *correction*.

- **Uneasy Alliances.** Synlirii and overminds are hereditary enemies, but their mutual obsession with the barking ones regularly pushes them into uneasy alliances. Both groups recruit humanoid agents from the World Below, trading lore and technology for intelligence on surface foes.
- **Olothec Hatred.** Synlirii's antipathy toward humanoids is surpassed only by their deep fear of the **olothec**. Ancient myths claim the olothec created both synlirii and overminds. Overminds shrug this off as harmless fable. Synlirii — who style themselves as the future masters of the timescape — rage against the implication that their powers come from anywhere but their own superior development.
- **Psionic Spies.** Voiceless talkers consider *information* their single greatest weapon. They manipulate memory and perception, obscure their forms, and pass undetected among other cultures. Expect them in unexpected places.
- **Psi-Tech.** Synlirii breed mollusks whose secretions they fashion into plastic-like psionic weapons — handheld pistols, portable rifles — all powered by psionic crystals and keyed to synlirii minds. Only a voiceless talker can fire one.
- **Creature Engineers.** Synlirii create **hulking brains**, **mindkillers**, and more exotic servants through a psionic technique they call the *Interlace*. Most Interlace experiments don't survive long. The World Below is littered with unnatural singletons who escaped the synlirii **Body Banks** — often creatures who can't reproduce and might never die of their own volition.
- **Graywarpers.** Voiceless talkers whose psionic abilities — and free will — have been drained away as punishment for insolence or incompetence. Treated as expendable tools who exist solely to boost the power of other voiceless talkers.
- **Hulking Brains (thylin'oa).** Engineered pearlescent-skinned humanoid-shaped horrors with four massive arms and a pulsing brain in place of a head. Serve as bodyguards and psionic batteries for their creators.
- **Mindkillers.** Floating clawed brains with trailing spinal columns and nerves. Amorphous parasites who force their way into a humanoid body through ear, eye, or nose; devour the central nervous system; replace it with their own tissue. When the process is complete, the mindkiller has all the victim's knowledge, memories, and appearance, and can puppet the body as the perfect spy. **Mindkiller whelps** are a lesser, faster-to-create variant that can't body-jack, but their mere presence weakens enemies' mental defenses.
- **Languages.** Most voiceless talkers communicate telepathically via Mindspeech and also speak Variac.

## Shared traits

- **Psionic Conductor.** Every non-minion voiceless talker (and some minions) has this passive. *Whenever a non-minion voiceless talker within 5 squares uses a psionic ability, they can do so as if they were in this creature's space.* So any ability origin-point can be shifted to any conductor within 5 squares of the caster — the battlefield is effectively a distributed psionic antenna. Lines and areas fire from wherever the party's defensive positioning is *weakest*. The Invader has an upgraded **Psionic Amplifier** that also grants a double edge to any ability routed through them.
- **Psychic immunity.** Most voiceless talkers have psychic immunity 6; the leader has 8. Psychic-damage-focused hero kits will bounce; diversify damage types.
- **Hover / teleport movement.** The Artillerist, Invader, and Evolutionist all hover and teleport. Terrain doesn't slow the command staff, and melee-lock strategies rarely stick.

## Voiceless Talker Malice features

Spent at the start of any voiceless talker's turn.

- **Guise (3 Malice, Maneuver).** One non-minion voiceless talker projects a psionic screen over their body, *preventing other creatures from treating them as an enemy* until end of next turn. Target the party cleric as a friend for a round, walk past the paladin unhindered, set up a melee position that would otherwise be denied.
- **Memory Thief (5 Malice, Maneuver, Psionic Ranged 5).** Psychic damage plus a Reason test: tier 1 the target can't treat their allies as allies save-ends, tier 2 and 3 the target *perceives their allies as enemies* save-ends. Minions can't use. The "turn the party against itself" button; combines with Memory Thief-layered Cloud-of-Deceit-style positional trickery.
- **Evolutionary Circuit (10 Malice).** All voiceless talkers link minds, creating a circuit that empowers them while two or more voiceless talkers remain in the encounter. While active: (1) any psionic strike made by a voiceless talker deals +5 damage; (2) when a non-minion voiceless talker takes damage, they can free-trigger to swap places with any voiceless talker *minion* on the map, and that minion takes the damage instead. The late-fight buff + damage-redirect button. Spend when the Evolutionist is at half HP and multiple voiceless talkers remain.

## Elites (Level 6)

### Hulking Brain

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 6, elite, horror, voiceless talker
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 288

**Flavor:** Size 1L, Stamina 180, Speed 5, Stability 4, Free Strike 7. Four arms, pearlescent skin, a pulsing brain for a head. The bodyguard and psionic battery.

**Tactics:** **Four-Way Grasp** (Signature, Melee 1, *four* targets) — damage with grab at tier 2 and 3 on failed Agility tests. *The hulking brain can have up to four size-1 creatures grabbed.* **2 Malice:** potency +1. The iconic move: grab four heroes simultaneously. **Cerebral Suplex** (Main action, Melee 1, each grabbed enemy) — damage plus secondary damage on failed Might tests; all grabbed targets are *ungrabbed* after the power roll. So the grab sets up a single big damage round, then releases. **Lumber** (Maneuver, Self) — shift up to 4 squares ignoring difficult terrain. **Brawny Buffer** (1 Malice, free triggered action) — when an ally voiceless talker within 5 takes damage from an enemy, the hulking brain shifts adjacent and becomes the new target. **2 Malice:** the enemy is also knocked prone. **Biceps to Spare** (passive) — can carry up to four grabbed size-1 creatures with no speed penalty. **Psionic Conductor**. The tank + relocator + grab-all-four brute.

### Mindkiller

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 6, elite, horror, voiceless talker
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 289

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 140, Speed 6, Fly/hover, Free Strike 7. Psychic immunity 6. The full-grown parasite — the adult form the whelps transform into.

**Tactics:** **Brain Latch** (passive) — the mindkiller can grab creatures *size 4 or smaller*, using Reason in place of Might. Grabbed targets take a bane on Escape Grab rolls. The mindkiller can reach up and grab a dragon. **Killer Claws** (Signature, Melee 1) — heavy damage (11/17/21) with grab at tier 2/3 on A<2/<3. **Concealing Strike** (2 Malice, Psionic Ranged 5, two creatures) — damage with *mindkiller invisible to target save-ends* on failed Reason tests. Any hero who resists fails to see the mindkiller for the rest of the fight. **Mindwipe** (Maneuver, Melee 1, one grabbed creature) — the target must be grabbed. If R<2, *the target takes a -1 penalty to one of their Reason, Intuition, or Presence scores, and the mindkiller gains a +1 bonus to the same score, until end of encounter.* Permanent characteristic swap per hit. **Meat Shield** (triggered action, Self) — when the mindkiller takes damage, they halve it; any creature they have grabbed takes the other half. **3 Malice upgrade:** the grabbed creature takes the *full* damage instead. **Psionic Conductor**. **Nimble** — moves through enemy spaces at normal speed.

### Voiceless Talker Artillerist

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 6, elite, horror, voiceless talker
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 290

The sniper-turned-AoE. **Mind Jolt** (Main action, 10×1 line within 10) — lightning damage with slowed save-ends on failed Intuition tests. **In Our Sights** (Maneuver, Ranged 10) — until start of next turn, *the next psionic ability used against the target automatically treats its initial power roll as a 17.* The setup-for-a-kill button: mark a hero with In Our Sights, then the Evolutionist or another elite fires Psionic Intrusion with a guaranteed tier 2+ hit. **Tactical Reposition** (1 Malice, triggered action, Self) — when the artillerist takes damage, teleport up to 5 squares and ignore any effects from the damage. **Psionic Conductor**. **Locked On** (passive) — ignores invisibility, cover, and concealment. *A creature can't hide from the artillerist while the artillerist has line of effect to them.* No positional counterplay — the artillerist sees everyone.

### Voiceless Talker Invader

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 6, elite, horror, voiceless talker
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 290

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 140, Speed 5, Hover/teleport, Free Strike 7. Psychic immunity 6. The tentacle-limbed psionic controller.

Voiceless talker abilities routed through the invader's space also *gain a double edge*. The Invader is the priority psionic-routing target because they don't just relay, they amplify.

## Leader

### Voiceless Talker Evolutionist — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 6, leader, horror, voiceless talker
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 291

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 180, Speed 5, Hover/teleport, Stability 2, Free Strike 7. Psychic immunity 8. Reason +4, Presence +2. The synliroi commander — an evolutionary supremacist who sees every combat as a demonstration.

**Tactics:** **Psionic Intrusion** (Signature, Melee 1 or ranged 5, two targets) — psychic damage with slowed save-ends on failed Reason tests across tiers. **Carpe Quadratum** (Maneuver, Psionic Ranged 5) — *the evolutionist teleports to swap places with a target.* Takes the target's favorable position and puts them in a worse one. **Adaptability** (triggered action, Self) — when the evolutionist takes typed damage, they gain *damage immunity 5 to that damage type* until start of their next turn. Parties shouldn't try to chain the same damage type. **End Effect** (10 damage self to clear a save-ends). **Witness Evolutionary Superiority** (passive) — *the evolutionist has any trait of the Director's choice from any ally minion in the encounter.* If there's a Mindkiller Whelp squad, the Evolutionist may have picked up Feast. If there's a Graywarper, Psionic Conductor. Pick a trait per encounter to match the fight's needs.

Villain actions:
- **Show Me Who You Are (VA1, 5-burst area).** Each enemy makes an Intuition test. Tier 1: the target uses a signature ability against the *nearest enemy* within distance. Tier 2: the target makes a free strike against the nearest enemy within distance. Tier 3: frightened save-ends. Forces the party's best signature abilities against their own frontliners.
- **Release the Thralls (VA2, Ranged 5).** The evolutionist teleports *eight minions of level 4 or lower* into unoccupied spaces within 5 squares. All eight minions can be of any monster type but must share the same name. Instant reinforcement wave — voiceless talker spy networks mean the evolutionist can pull bodies from anywhere in the setting.
- **Brainstorm (VA3, 3-burst area).** Lightning damage across tiers. *Effect: until end of encounter, the evolutionist is surrounded by a 5-aura psionic electrical storm. The area is difficult terrain for enemies, and any enemy who enters for the first time in a round or starts their turn there takes 8 lightning damage.* A permanent 5-square-radius damage aura centered on the evolutionist — forces the party to deal with them at range, or pay 8 lightning per entry.

Counterplay: (1) **Kill the Invader first** — Psionic Amplifier's double edge on routed abilities is a much bigger damage multiplier than the other Psionic Conductors, and the Invader also has the Brain Drain potency escalator. (2) **Don't feed Adaptability** — diversify damage types or the evolutionist bounces every repeat hit. (3) **Mindkiller Whelps must not finish off allies** — a Feast transformation mid-fight creates a brand-new elite stat block. Don't leave low-HP heroes adjacent to a whelp cluster. (4) **Locked On is the ranged assassin's counter-to-stealth** — hiding from an Artillerist doesn't work; the answer is to break line of effect with cover, not illusion. (5) **Evolutionary Circuit (10 Malice)** is the losing-the-fight condition — +5 damage to every psionic strike, plus minion-swap damage redirect. If Malice is climbing toward 10, burst non-minion voiceless talkers fast so the redirect half of the effect loses targets. (6) **Carpe Quadratum means the Evolutionist is never where you want them** — plan for any melee commit against them to end with the evolutionist swapping you into a worse square.

## Named solo: Lord Syuul

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 293–294. Reference only — stat block lives in the book.*

> *"Yours is an interesting mind, Tyract. I wonder what it tastes like?"*

**Lord Syuul** (see-YOU-ull) is the Chief Design Architect of the living synlirii high-science vessel *Waking Nightmare*. He scours the timescape hunting novel genetic specimens he can torture into new species, and has spies, informants, and allies in every corner of the World Below and the surface world watching for subjects with unique hereditary traits his Interlace process might extract.

- **The Extractors.** Syuul's most trusted servants form his elite surgical commando squad — the Extractors. When *Waking Nightmare* detects a novel hereditary ability, Syuul dispatches a team of synlirii genetic surgeons and a single **scanner** (a 4-foot hovering glass cylinder full of brain matter and cerebrospinal fluid) that catalogs every living being in the area in an instant. The Extractors then apply the Interlace, siphoning off the victim's unique abilities. Subjects who survive find those abilities gone.
- **Architect of Change.** Syuul's thralls know him by this title. He claims to remember a time when the synlirii ruled the world of **Proteus, the Sea of Eternal Change**, as gods — before the time raiders rebelled, before the synlirii retreated to the World Below. Other genetic architects are content to rule the Dark Under All. Syuul burns with hatred — of the kuran'zoi, of the ungrateful proteans, and of every living being not under his direct control.
- **Past the Body Banks.** Syuul no longer values his original invention, the Body Banks, and traded them away to Ajax's wizard **Mortum** for more genetic material. The Body Banks required manually feeding specimens into the tanks; his new device lets him rewrite a living being's genetic material *from any distance*. When it's complete, he intends to remake the timescape in his own image — every living being a formless mass of mind and flesh thinking only Syuul's thoughts.
- **Current obsession: Talents.** Syuul's present research is focused on talents — heroes with the innate ability to turn thoughts into raw power. If the party includes a talent, assume Syuul has a file on them.
- **Languages.** Mindspeech (telepathic) and Variac.

### Lord Syuul Malice features

At start of Lord Syuul's turn, spend Malice to activate one of the following. These replace the standard voiceless talker Malice features in encounters where Syuul is the antagonist.

- **Guise (3 Malice, Maneuver).** Lord Syuul projects a psionic screen preventing other creatures from treating him as an enemy until end of his next turn. Same as the standard voiceless talker feature.
- **Do It for Me (5 Malice, Maneuver).** Lord Syuul psionically plunders the minds of each creature within 2 squares. Each makes a Reason test — tier 1 takes 13 psychic and uses a signature ability against a creature of Syuul's choice, tier 2 takes 10 psychic and makes a free strike against a creature of Syuul's choice, tier 3 no effect. Close-range friendly-fire burst. Positions the party to keep distance, which feeds Syuul's ranged control.
- **Solo Action (5 Malice).** Syuul takes an additional main action *even if dazed*. Dazed-proof.
- **Overpower (7 Malice).** Syuul sends out a psionic burst to overpower his greatest threats and makes a Reason test (2d10 + 4). Tier 1: Syuul has damage weakness 5. Tier 2: damage immunity 2. Tier 3: damage immunity 5. Once per round as a maneuver, Syuul can repeat this test, *replacing* the previous Overpower effect. Additionally, while an Overpower effect is active, any hero with at least one psionic ability can use a maneuver to make a Reason test that *replaces* the previous effect — with inverted tiering: tier 1 = Syuul immunity 5, tier 2 = immunity 2, tier 3 = weakness 5. The Overpower effect lasts until end of encounter. The core of a Syuul fight: the party's psionic-ability heroes play a tug-of-war over his damage immunity, and the hero who pushes back has the inverted roll distribution. Non-psionic parties can't push back at all and watch Syuul cycle his own rolls until he lands immunity 5.

### Lord Syuul — solo

**Role:** solo
**Tags:** level 6, solo, horror, voiceless talker
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 294

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 450, Speed 7, Hover/teleport, Stability 3, Free Strike 7. Psychic immunity 10. Reason +4, Intuition +4, Presence +3, Agility +3. A Level 6 Solo — stands against about six same-level heroes alone, and his entire character sheet is optimized around mental and psionic dominance.

**Tactics:**

**Solo Monster.** *End Effect* — end of each turn, Syuul can take 10 unresistable damage to end one save-ends effect. *Solo Turns* — two turns per round, never consecutive.

**Tentacle Grab** (Signature, Melee 2, two targets) — damage (11/17/20) with grab on failed Agility tests across tiers. **2 Malice upgrade:** distance becomes *Melee 10*, and each grabbed target is pulled up to 10 squares. With 2 Malice, Syuul can grab from across the battlefield and yank heroes to him.

**Dampening Grenade** (Main action, Psionic Ranged 5, 2-cube area) — damage with duration scaling by tier (2 turns / end of Syuul's next turn / end of encounter). *Any supernatural ability used by a creature in the area has a double bane. All reactive tests made against magic or psionic effects in the area have a double edge.* The anti-caster zone: tier 3 lasts the whole fight, and Syuul's own abilities ignore this restriction because the "creature in the area" phrasing targets enemies standing inside it.

**Mind Blown** (7 Malice, Melee 1, one grabbed enemy) — heavy damage (12/20/24). *If this action reduces the target to 0 Stamina and they have a brain, their brain explodes, instantly killing them.* The execution button — ignores the normal "reduced to 0 Stamina" survival path. A hero whose brain explodes is dead, not dying. Reserve for when a hero is low and Syuul has already grabbed them via Tentacle Grab.

**You Come With Me** (Maneuver, Self) — Syuul teleports up to 5 squares along with each creature and object he has grabbed. He can release grabbed creatures before or after teleporting. The kidnap tool: grab a hero with Tentacle Grab, teleport them into a Dampening Grenade zone, Mind Blown them next turn.

**Adaptability** (triggered action, Self) — when Syuul takes typed damage, he gains immunity 5 to that damage type until start of his next turn. Same clause as the Evolutionist's — diversify damage types.

**Mind Over Manners** (passive) — *whenever Syuul uses a psionic ability, he can do so as if he were in the space of any creature within his line of effect who he has observed using a psionic ability.* Not just allies — *any* creature. Once the party's psionicist has cast a single spell in Syuul's sight, he can originate abilities from their square for the rest of the fight. Routes Dampening Grenade, Mind Blown, and Do It for Me origination points through the hero's own body.

Villain actions:

- **See Only Me (VA1, Psionic, 10-burst area).** Each enemy makes an Intuition test. Tier 1 takes 16 psychic + *no line of effect to any creature except Syuul* and a bane on strikes targeting Syuul save-ends. Tier 2 takes 13 psychic + no line of effect except to Syuul save-ends. Tier 3 takes 7 psychic. The party-isolation opener — tier 1 heroes can't see each other, can only target Syuul, and have a bane doing it. Monologue fuel.
- **Phantom Pain (VA2, Psionic, Self).** Syuul becomes invisible, teleports up to 10 squares, and projects an illusory double within 10 squares. The double can't move or act, but *Syuul can use psionic abilities as if he were in the double's square*. Any creature who touches or damages the double with a melee strike takes 10 psychic damage. If Syuul takes damage, his invisibility ends and the double disappears. Double-faint: the real Syuul is elsewhere firing through the double, until a hero lands any damage on him.
- **Mindshatter (VA3, Psionic, 5-burst area).** Psychic damage across tiers. *Until end of encounter, each target has damage weakness 3.* Permanent damage-weakness debuff on everyone in the burst — combines with Syuul's own psychic damage output and any Psionic Conductor routing for a late-fight escalation.

Counterplay: (1) **Overpower is the fight's core puzzle.** A party with at least one psionic-ability hero should dedicate that hero's maneuvers to *pushing back* on the Overpower chain — the inverted tier distribution means hero-rolled Overpowers land on Syuul's damage weakness more often than on his immunity. A party with *no* psionic abilities will watch Syuul cycle his own rolls until he lands tier 3 immunity 5, and there is no counterplay from that point. (2) **Don't let Syuul see a psionic ability cast.** Mind Over Manners keys off the first observed psionic cast from any creature with line of effect. Psionic heroes should open from behind cover or save their abilities for later rounds — because once Syuul sees the cast, the rest of the fight has ability origins routed through the caster's square. (3) **Dampening Grenade at tier 3 lasts the whole encounter.** First-round focus fire to break the roll distribution on that strike, or burst Syuul before he uses it. (4) **Don't be grabbed.** Tentacle Grab + 2 Malice pulls from 10 squares. A grabbed hero fed into Mind Blown at tier 2+ with their brain showing is a permanent loss — the brain-explodes clause bypasses normal dying/stabilization rules. (5) **Adaptability forces damage-type rotation.** Between-strike damage type rotation is mandatory for any striker. (6) **Scanners and Extractors are the campaign's ongoing threat.** Syuul himself is a late-game encounter; the campaign tension with him is earlier — the party notices that subjects with unique talents keep disappearing, and follows the trail to him. Run Syuul as a persistent campaign antagonist whose scanners appear before he does.
