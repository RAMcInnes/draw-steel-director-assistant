# Elves, High

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 119–125. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

High elves are the children of the solar celestials, created to tend the sun elves as librarians and heralds. Now they sequester themselves in the fallen cities of the sky elves, obsessed with knowledge — discovering it, documenting it, and *forcefully acquiring* it. Their armies corral enemies together and bury them under waves of group-cast magic; the more exposure a victim has to high elf magic, the more effective that magic becomes.

- **Glamour.** High elves emit an innate glamour, letting them change physical features and aspects of personality at will. Each observer sees the glamour slightly differently, filtered by their own values. Useful roleplay hook: two PCs describing the same high elf may literally disagree on what they look like.
- **Elemental Summoners.** Many high elves bond to elemental creatures: soot crows (scouts/harriers), brambleguards (living vine-wall defenders), ceramic horses (fast mounts), and elemental motes (revive-flash sprites). Summon Elemental maneuvers feed reinforcements into the encounter every round a high elf spends Malice.
- **Otherworldly Grace (shared trait).** Nearly every non-minion high elf has this passive: at the start of each of their turns, they choose one save-ends effect on them and end it at end of their turn instead. This neutralizes save-ends control in one round — plan condition stacking around it. The *Palinode's Recall* also cleanses via teleport+temp-stamina, and the *Ordinator's Otherworldly Blessing* ends **multiple** effects per turn instead of one.
- **Group Casting & The Ordinator.** High elves practice a tradition of group-cast magic — the more elves casting in unison, the farther the spells reach (miles, when a whole platoon works together). The **Ordinator** is the leader who focuses this: when on the map, their *Chaincast* Malice is always active *and* abilities cast through the ordinator's space gain a **double edge**. Position the Ordinator centrally; the whole platoon fires magic through them.
- **The Unseen Hands.** A high elf radical sect who believe they've found sky elf texts detailing their own downfall at the hands of the sun elves. Now they manipulate noble courts from within, aiming to dismantle high elf society and plunge the world into anarchy. A dormant plot hook: "loyal" high elves may secretly be Unseen Hands agents.
- **Languages.** Most high elves speak Caelian and Hyrallic; some also speak Yllyric.

## High Elf Malice features

At the start of any high elf's turn, Malice may be spent to activate one of the following features.

- **Chaincast (3 Malice).** Until end of round, whenever a high elf uses a magic ability, they can use it **as if occupying the square of another high elf on the map** to whom they have line of effect. Range-extension + line-of-effect-bypass in one. An Ordinator on-map makes this permanent and doubles the edge on abilities cast through their square.
- **Gift From an Accursed Tome (5 Malice, 5×1 line within 1, each enemy, Main action).** Pick a damage/condition combo: **cold + slowed**, **poison + weakened**, or **corruption + frightened**. 5/9/12 damage on R<1/2/3. The damage/condition picker lets the Director shop for the party's weakest save — sniff out resistances and pick accordingly. Can't be used by minions.
- **In Defiance of Time (7 Malice).** Until end of round, every high elf in the encounter gets **+4 speed**, and whenever any high elf uses an ability against an enemy, every high elf adjacent to that enemy makes a free strike. Combined with Bloodletter/Zephyr/Brambleguard clustering and the Ordinator's Chaincast, this is the round-wipe button — drop it with 4+ high elves within melee reach of a clustered party.

## Elemental Mote — minion

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, elemental, high elf, soulless
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 120
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to speed.

**Flavor:** Tiny flying wisps summoned by high elf mages. Size 1T, Stamina 3, Fly, R/I 0, P +2. Designed to *die* to fuel elemental revives.

**Tactics:** **Dweomer Plume** (Melee 2, Signature, Magic) 1/2/3 damage, with **R<1/2 damage weakness 3 (save ends)** at tier 2+ — stacks with *Gift From an Accursed Tome* since any Reason-failing target takes +3 from the next round of high elf magic. **Spark of Life** passive: on the mote's turn, it can **leave the encounter in a flash of light to revive one adjacent dead soot crow, brambleguard, or ceramic horse with 3 Stamina** — the self-destruct-to-revive button. Four motes means up to four free resurrections for the elemental screen. Ordinator's *Elemental Uproar* explicitly calls out Spark of Life as its preferred effect on mote targets. Counterplay: kill motes *before* they stand adjacent to dead elementals; AoE clears the revive pool.

## High Elf Quiver — minion

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, fey, high elf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 120
**With Captain:** +5 bonus to ranged distance.

**Flavor:** Back-line archer minion. Stamina 3, Speed 5, A +2, R +1. Soft Stamina — their value is reach and reshuffle.

**Tactics:** **Heavy Arrow** (Ranged 10, Signature, one target per minion) 2/4/5 damage, and the effect kicker is the real value: **each ally adjacent to the target shifts up to 2 squares** — a free team reposition for every quiver shot. Four quivers = eight ally shifts per round, meaning the whole high elf platoon swims around the battlefield without spending actions. With a captain's +5 range, a quiver shoots from 15 squares and reshuffles the whole formation. **Otherworldly Grace** (shared trait): cleanses save-ends effects. Counterplay: focus fire on quivers before they enable the shift cascade; AoE is the only way to clear four at once.

## High Elf Dawn Mage — minion

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, fey, high elf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 120
**With Captain:** +5 bonus to ranged distance.

**Flavor:** Ranged caster minion. Stamina 3, Speed 5, R +2, P +1. The "anti-hide" minion — denies stealth-reliant heroes.

**Tactics:** **Bright Bolt** (Ranged 5, Signature, Magic, one target per minion) 1/2/3 holy damage with the effect clause **"Until the start of the dawn mage's next turn, the target can't hide"** — a whole squad of dawn mages locks the rogue out of hiding *cycles every round*. Combines with the Zephyr's Sweeping Blade (which wants A<2 targets together) and the Bloodletter's Razor's Edge (double edge on bleeding targets, so stealth attrition is denied as the setup). **Otherworldly Grace** shared. Counterplay: reveal- and hide-denial is their only trick — kill them first if the party has a rogue to protect.

## Soot Crow — minion

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, elemental, high elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 120
**With Captain:** Gain an edge on strikes.

**Flavor:** Aerial scout minion. Size 1T, Fly, Speed 7, Stamina 4, A +2, P +1. Built to harass and disengage.

**Tactics:** **Heckle** (Melee 1, Signature, one target per minion) 1/2/3 damage, **taunted (EoT) on 17+**. Effect: **until end of their turn, the soot crow ignores opportunity attacks from the target** — hit and fly free without triggering reaction chains. With a captain's edge, a squad of four soot crows can fly through an entire adventuring party, taunt the defender, and peel away without consequence, setting up the Ordinator or Wyrd to nuke the undefended back line. Counterplay: area attacks remain the best tool; if the soot crow lands in melee with an ally of the soot crow's target, opportunity attacks still work.

## Brambleguard

**Role:** defender
**Tags:** level 2, platoon, EV 8, elemental, high elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 121

**Flavor:** Living vine-wall. Size 2, Stamina 59, Speed 4, Stability 3, M/P +2. Summon them via Summon Elemental (2 Malice) for instant cover / healing stations.

**Tactics:** **Wall of Roses** (Signature, Self, Area Magic, Main action) — until the brambleguard's next turn, their speed is 0 and they extend into a **5-wall**. Each ally who starts their turn adjacent regains **5 Stamina** *and* can apply the **Magic keyword to their weapon abilities** until end of turn. This is the magic-weapon-on-demand fountain — every high elf warrior within reach converts mundane damage into magical damage for one turn. **Whip Frenzy** (Main action, 2-burst, each enemy) 4/7/10 + push 3 + A<2 bleeding at 17+, AoE pressure when not walled. **Thicket and Thorns** passive: **blocks line of effect for enemies**, and enemies starting their turn adjacent take **4 damage**. With Wall of Roses up, the brambleguard is simultaneously healing allies, granting magic weapons, blocking LoE for ranged heroes, and dealing aura damage — the whole platoon's anchor. Counterplay: force-move the brambleguard (Stability 3 is tough), or cut line of effect with flanking; ranged attacks from outside the LoE bubble are the clean answer.

## High Elf Bloodletter

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, EV 6, fey, high elf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 121

**Flavor:** Fast dual-blade assassin. Stamina 30, Speed 7, A +2, I +1.

**Tactics:** **Razor's Edge** (Signature, Melee 1) 6/9/12 + R<2 bleeding (save ends) at 17+. Effect: **the bloodletter and each of their allies have a double edge on abilities targeting a creature bleeding this way** — this is the whole platoon's focus-fire setup. One bloodletter bleed = every high elf piles on with +2 tiers. **Blood Haze** (2 Malice, 1-burst, Maneuver, self special) fills a 1-burst with **blood vapor: blocks LoE for enemies, and enemies in area have damage weakness 3**. Then the bloodletter shifts up to their speed *and can attempt to hide if they end with concealment*. The vapor is a re-stealth field that also softens up whoever walks into it. **Otherworldly Grace** shared. Counterplay: AoE through the Haze still hits (Haze blocks LoE but not area effects); healing removes bleeding and collapses the double-edge stack; kill the bloodletter before they re-hide on turn 2.

## High Elf Deathtouch

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 2, platoon, EV 8, fey, high elf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 121

**Flavor:** The assassin-caster at extreme range. Stamina 30, Speed 5, M/R/I/P +2/+1/0/+1.

**Tactics:** **Heartpiercer** (Signature, Ranged 15, one creature) 7/10/13 damage, and **17+ stacks three conditions at once**: **R<1 bleeding, I<1 frightened, P<1 restrained** (all save ends). Low tier 3 against a squishy caster can lock them out of movement, actions, and stealth in one shot. **5 Malice kicker**: ability becomes **3-cube within 10, each enemy** — a triple-condition AoE once per fight. **Kiss of Death** (Melee 1, Maneuver, one willing ally) is the named horror move: the ally gets **+5 speed and auto-tier-3 on every power roll**, but at end of their next turn **they immediately die**. Suicidal finisher for a winded ally about to die anyway — cash in the dying ally for one guaranteed tier-3 turn, then pack up the corpse as a resource for grave-robbing allies. **Otherworldly Grace** shared. Counterplay: close the 15-square range fast; avoid clustering inside the 3-cube AoE when Malice is high; don't assume winded high elf allies are "out of the fight" — they might be about to get Kissed.

## High Elf Orbweaver

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 3, platoon, EV 10, fey, high elf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 122

**Flavor:** Web-of-force condition caster. Stamina 40, Speed 5, R/I/P +2.

**Tactics:** **Awash** (Signature, 3-cube within 1, each creature, Main action) 4/6/9 cold damage + M<0/1/2 push 3 / push 5 or prone / slide 5 or prone — top tier is a clean 5-square slide *or* prone on the whole cube. **Aetherweb** (Main action, Ranged 8, two enemies or objects) 5/8/11 damage, **slowed on R<0/1 save-ends and restrained on R<2 save-ends at 17+**. The nastiest effect clause in the section: **"each enemy within 3 squares of a target suffers the same potency effect as the target unless they are already adjacent to them, or if they immediately shift into an unoccupied space adjacent to the target (no action required)."** A restrained target becomes a radioactive zone — any enemy within 3 squares who *isn't adjacent to the victim* is also restrained. To avoid, each nearby hero must *voluntarily shift adjacent to the victim*, which bunches them up for the Wyrd's *Twystrd* cube, the Ordinator's *Fountains Roar*, or the Deathtouch's *Heartpiercer* AoE. Counterplay: Reason-save heroes who can tank R<2; heal/cleanse the first restrained hero before the spread propagates.

## High Elf Palinode

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, EV 6, fey, high elf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 122

**Flavor:** Quiet cleric-analog, condition-remover and teleport anchor. Stamina 30, Speed 5, Immunity Psychic 5, I/P +2/+1.

**Tactics:** **Instill Regret** (Signature, Ranged 8, one creature, Magic) 5/7/9 psychic + weakened on I<1/2 (save ends). **2 Malice kicker**: potency +1, and **if the target is weakened at end of encounter they can't take a respite activity during their next respite** — a narrative punishment that matters far more between sessions than in-session. **Recall** (Maneuver, Ranged 5, two allies, Magic) — each target **teleports to an unoccupied space adjacent to the palinode, and all three gain 5 temporary Stamina**. The repositioning + soaking tool: pull two bleeding allies out of focus fire, give them fresh HP, re-anchor the formation. **Otherworldly Grace** shared. Counterplay: engage the palinode in melee so Recall's 5-range is less useful for the platoon's distant allies; interrupt Recall turns by forcing the palinode to cleanse Instill Regret's weakened kicker on themselves.

## High Elf Wyrd

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 3, platoon, EV 10, fey, high elf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 123

**Flavor:** Terrain-sculptor and elemental summoner. Stamina 40, Immunity Psychic 5, Speed 5, R/P +2, I −1.

**Tactics:** **Twystrd** (Signature, 1-cube within 5, each enemy, Main action, Area Magic) **vertical push 3/5/6** — basic forced-fall AoE. The effect clause is the scaling lever: **for each elemental mote adjacent to the wyrd, the cube size increases by 1**. With 4 motes adjacent, the cube is 5 wide — a whole party gets flung. This is why the Wyrd summons motes first, keeps them close, and fires Twystrd when the party clusters. **Summon Elemental** (2 Malice, Maneuver, Ranged 5) summons two elemental motes or two soot crows — feed the Twystrd scaling. **Wyrd Warp** (2 Malice, 8-wall within 8, Maneuver, Area Magic) shapes the land **as loose clay, either raising or lowering** a wall segment — creatures ride the terrain up, or fall if lowered beneath them. Build pits to dump flying heroes into, or raise elevation to split the party's melee line from their ranged. **Otherworldly Grace** shared. Counterplay: kill the adjacent motes to shrink Twystrd's area; force the wyrd to move (they have only Speed 5 and no stability improvement); ranged heroes who can stand outside the Twystrd cube.

## High Elf Zephyr

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, EV 6, fey, high elf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 123

**Flavor:** Flying staff-wielder with free-strike chains. Stamina 30, Speed 7, A/P +2/+1.

**Tactics:** **Sweeping Blade** (Signature, Melee 2, one creature or object) 5/7/9 damage. Tier-3 effect: **the zephyr can make a free strike against a creature adjacent to the target**, and **if both have A<2, both are prone**. **On any tier, the zephyr can then shift up to 2 squares** after resolving — so every Sweeping Blade ends with a reposition regardless of outcome. **Windwalk** (Maneuver, self) flies up to speed — **prone if not ending on solid ground** (so mind the drop-zone). **Like the Wind** passive: **doesn't provoke opportunity attacks by moving** — immune to the whole opportunity-attack economy, so they kite freely past defenders. **Otherworldly Grace** shared. Counterplay: ranged reach prevents Sweeping Blade's double-hit setup; force the zephyr to land mid-air (Wyrd Warp mimicry works against them too); A<2 saves block the double-prone.

## High Elf Ordinator — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 3, leader, EV 20, fey, high elf, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 124

**Flavor:** The platoon's spiritual and magical beacon. Size 1M, Stamina 120, Fly, R/P +3, A/I +2, M 0. Flight + the Magic Beacon passive turns the Ordinator into the center of the platoon's magic network.

**Tactics:** **Lightning Rod** (Signature, Ranged 10, one creature, Magic) 9/14/17 lightning + R<1/2/3 dazed (save ends). Effect: **until end of ordinator's next turn, every ally high elf in the encounter gains an edge on ability rolls against the target**. This is the painted-target designator — the whole platoon stacks attacks on the Ordinator's marked foe each round. **Elemental Uproar** (Maneuver, 10-burst, Area Magic, each elemental ally) — every elemental ally in the burst can **move full speed or make a free strike, and elemental motes instead use Spark of Life** to revive fallen elementals. Once per turn, the Ordinator rebuilds the elemental screen *and* triggers a round of free strikes from soot crows/ceramic horses. **Summon Elemental** (2 Malice, Maneuver, Ranged 10) — summons **four** elemental motes or soot crows (double the Wyrd's output). **3 Malice kicker**: instead summons one ceramic horse or one winded brambleguard — on-demand platoon defender. **Enough!** (triggered, Ranged 10, the triggering enemy) — when any enemy uses an ability against the Ordinator or an ally within 10, the Ordinator uses **Lightning Rod against them after the triggering ability resolves**. Massively punishes casters and focus-fire attackers; pairs with the Magic Beacon's double-edge since Enough! Lightning Rod is itself a magic ability. **Otherworldly Blessing** passive upgrade: cleanses **one *or more*** save-ends effects per turn (not just one — the Ordinator is uncleansable over 2 rounds).

Villain actions:
- **Fountains Roar, Now Free From the Earth (VA1, 10-burst, each ally).** Each ally ends one effect on themselves and moves up to their speed. Opening rally that resets conditions across the whole platoon simultaneously — save the hero's biggest debuff for *after* this fires.
- **And the Sun Forsook Her Children (VA2, 5-cube within 10, each enemy).** Presence test: 12/9/0 corruption damage + pull 5/3/1 toward center of the cube. **The area turns dark and distorted, becoming difficult terrain for enemies for the rest of the encounter**. Terrain-transformation + cluster-pull to feed the Orbweaver's Aetherweb radiation zone.
- **But We Will Change Her Mind (VA3, 10-burst, self and each ally).** For the rest of the encounter, **each ally's free strikes gain the Magic keyword and can target two creatures or objects, and each ally glows with magic**. Every free strike in the Brambleguard's Wall of Roses / In Defiance of Time / opportunity chain becomes a two-target magic hit — combined with the Bloodletter's bleeding double edge, this is the round-wipe amplifier.

**Magic Beacon (passive).** Whenever the Ordinator is on an encounter map, the Chaincast Malice feature is **always active**, and when any elf uses a magic ability as if they were in the Ordinator's space, that ability has a **double edge**. The Ordinator's position is the whole platoon's magical aiming point — kill the Ordinator or sever line of effect to them.

Counterplay: kill the Ordinator first (their 120 Stamina and flight make this hard — ranged focus fire through line of effect is the priority); don't trigger Enough! with low-impact abilities; hold cleanses until after Fountains Roar has fired; avoid standing in the And the Sun Forsook cube (and the difficult terrain it leaves).

## Ceramic Horse

**Role:** mount
**Tags:** level 1, platoon, EV 6, elemental, high elf
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 125

**Flavor:** Fire-maned elemental steed, size 2, Speed 10, Stamina 30, Stability 2, M/A +2/+2, P +1. Summoned via the Ordinator's 3 Malice Summon Elemental kicker, or pre-placed as a ridden mount.

**Tactics:** **Elemental Charge** (Signature, Charge Melee 1, one target) 5/7/9 — tier 1 physical, tier 2 fire, tier 3 lightning + M<2 prone. Every successful Charge is a different damage type (play dirty with type-resistance-checking parties). **Stomp** (Main action, 1-burst, Area Weapon, each enemy) 3/5/7 damage, with **+2 damage vs. prone targets** — so Charge (prone) → Stomp (bonus damage) in two actions. **Buck** (2 Malice, Maneuver, self, target the horse's rider) **vertical slide the rider up to 3 squares, ignoring stability**, with the rider able to **use a ranged ability mid-forced-movement and take no fall damage** — the Ordinator or Palinode rides the horse, Buck launches them into a tactical firing position, they cast, then land safely. This is the rider-launcher move. **Shared Otherworldly Grace** passive: if the rider has Otherworldly Grace, the ceramic horse *also* has it — so when the Ordinator/Palinode rides, the horse cleanses save-ends every turn too. Counterplay: dismount the rider before the horse's turn (force-move rider off); kill the horse first to deny the launch platform; elemental-damage-resistant gear matters across three types (fire, lightning, physical).

## High Elf Sample Encounters

*Source: p. 122. Ready-made EV-balanced deployments.*

- **Ruin Hunters (24 EV).** Eight soot crows, eight dawn mages, one palinode, one zephyr. Harassment + cleanse + aerial harrier — good for a library-infiltration ambush.
- **Grove Guardians (36 EV).** Eight elemental motes, eight soot crows, one brambleguard, one wyrd, one ceramic horse. Elemental screen + terrain sculptor + mounted wyrd — grove/wilderness defense.
- **Acquisitor Strike Team (46 EV).** Eight quivers, eight soot crows, one brambleguard, three bloodletters, one deathtouch. Ambushing a target to steal relics/knowledge — the bleed-double-edge focus-fire setup.
- **Relic Wardens (74 EV).** Sixteen elemental motes, eight dawn mages, two bloodletters, one brambleguard, one palinode, one orbweaver, one ordinator. The flagship full-platoon encounter — vault guardians with every trick online.
