# Hobgoblins

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 171–179. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Also called *demogoblins* — descended from ancient goblins who traded with an infernal power for size and strength. Tusked, horned, and bigger than their goblin cousins, hobgoblins settle hot country (deserts, tropics, volcanic uplands) and bring that heritage to the battlefield as fire immunity, corruption magic, and the occasional teleport. Where goblins win by swarming, hobgoblins win by coordinated tactics — they scout, they drill, they synergize, and their word, once given, is binding.

- **Goblin Kin, Infernal Training.** Every hobgoblin shares the Goblin keyword and can be combined with goblin units — runners, snipers, war spiders, worgs. Most hobgoblins have **Fire Immunity** (2–6 depending on level), so your own fire effects will largely bounce off the enemy half of a mixed fight.
- **Fire and Corruption.** Damage types skew almost entirely to fire and corruption. A party loaded with fire resistance fares well; a party with fire weakness will bleed out fast under Burning Witch volleys and Firerunner walls.
- **Infernal Ichor.** Nearly every hobgoblin detonates a small fire-damage burst to adjacent creatures when killed. Stacking melee on a low-Stamina hobgoblin costs the party chip damage. Plan kill-order accordingly.
- **Binding Bargains.** Hobgoblins honor their word — a GM lever for parley, truces, and hostage scenes before or during a fight. A hobgoblin who gave their word not to attack the cleric won't attack the cleric, even if it costs them the battle.
- **Grilp.** A housecat-sized green devil that serves as a hobgoblin scout and familiar. Flies, hides, and weakens saves on anyone nearby — the only non-Goblin-keyword unit in the section.
- **Slaughter Demon.** A grack'tanar — serpent-bodied, six-clawed, burrowing. War mages summon these from the Seven Cities of Hell to wipe an enemy off the map. Elite brute, ignores hobgoblin fire synergy but adds a completely different threat profile.
- **Languages.** Most hobgoblins speak Anjali, Caelian, and Szetch.

## Shared traits (nearly every hobgoblin)

- **Fire Immunity.** Scales with level (2 at minion, 4–5 at platoon, 6 at high platoon/leader). Your own party's fire effects may heal more than they harm in a mixed encounter.
- **Infernal Ichor.** Dying hobgoblins spray burning blood. Ranged kills are strictly better than melee kills when the target is the last adjacent unit to a hero.
- **Goblin synergy.** Because every hobgoblin has the Goblin keyword, Goblin Malice Features (Goblin Mode, Tiny Stabs, Swamp Stink) are all available — hobgoblin encounters are a strict superset of goblin encounters.

## Hobgoblin Malice features

Spent at the start of any hobgoblin's turn. Hobgoblin-specific features stack on top of the goblin suite.

- **Goblin Malice Features (3–7 Malice).** Any hobgoblin can spend to activate a goblin Malice Feature. Full access to Goblin Mode, Tiny Stabs, and Swamp Stink in addition to the hobgoblin list below.
- **Operation Goblin Mode (3 Malice).** Each goblin in the encounter gains +3 speed until end of round — stronger than the goblin version (+2). Buy when the pack needs a tempo swing and the encounter has any meaningful goblin count.
- **Operation Tactical Swarm (5 Malice).** Each hobgoblin shifts up to their speed and can take the Defend main action. Map-wide free reposition + defensive stance. Drop before the heroes' big turn to soak the alpha.
- **Operation Earth Sear (7 Malice).** Ground becomes blazing hot until end of round: each enemy takes 1 fire damage per square of ground they enter, and any enemy ending their turn on the ground has fire weakness 2 until start of their next turn. Punishes movement and amplifies every subsequent fire effect — pair with Incendiarist Fireball Volley or Burning Witch Burning Legion.

## Minions

### Hobgoblin Brandbearer — minion

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 4, minion, goblin, hobgoblin, humanoid, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 172
**With Captain:** edge on strikes.

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 7, Speed 5, Fire Immunity 2. The fire-weakness applicator.

**Tactics:** **Searing Grasp** (Melee, magic) chips fire damage and on tier 2/3 applies **fire weakness 5** save-ends. **Open Furnace** is the engine: whenever an enemy takes fire damage, they take +1 fire damage per adjacent brandbearer. Stack three or four brandbearers on a single hero, land one tier-2 Searing Grasp, and every subsequent fire tick from the encounter is amplified by +3 to +4 per hit. The brandbearer's job is to be adjacent and alive, not to deal damage themselves. Counterplay: area damage to clear the cluster; movement to break adjacency.

### Hobgoblin Recruit — minion

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 4, minion, goblin, hobgoblin, humanoid, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 172
**With Captain:** +4 bonus to Stamina.

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 9, Speed 5, Fire Immunity 2. The bodyguard-buff minion.

**Tactics:** **Sword Lunge** (Melee) does middling damage, tier 3 grabs or prones. The real work is **Tactical Positioning**: each non-minion ally deals +1 damage per adjacent recruit. Four recruits clustered around a Hell Trooper or Grandguard add +4 damage to every one of that elite's swings. Unlike brandbearers, recruits want to be next to *allies*, not enemies. Counterplay: kill the recruits before engaging the buff target, or pull the buffed target out of the cluster.

### Hobgoblin Lancer — minion

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 4, minion, goblin, hobgoblin, humanoid, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 172
**With Captain:** +2 damage bonus to strikes.

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 8, Speed 7, Fire Immunity 2. Fast corruption-damage melee-or-ranged stabber.

**Tactics:** **Grim Thrust** (Melee 2 or Ranged 5) deals corruption damage with push 1/2 on tier 2/3, plus **+2 damage if the lancer has high ground**. The lancer wants verticality — a tower, a cliff, a war spider's back — and the option to switch between poke-from-reach and ranged pelting as positioning shifts. Speed 7 on a minion is genuinely fast; combine with Operation Tactical Swarm to reposition four lancers onto high ground for a clean volley. Counterplay: force melee engagement on low ground; the high-ground rider is their whole damage budget.

### Grilp — minion

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 4, minion, devil, hobgoblin, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 172
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to speed.

**Flavor:** Size 1T, Stamina 8, Speed 7, **Fly**, Fire Immunity 2. Housecat-sized chameleon-skinned devil. Not a Goblin — Devil/Hobgoblin/Infernal only.

**Tactics:** **Flyby Bite** (Melee) does tier-scaled damage; on tier 3 the grilp shifts 2 squares after. Base effect: the grilp moves up to speed and can attempt to hide. **Shifting Camouflage** gives permanent concealment from all creatures — so they hide on command. **Bat Out Of Hell** is the reason to bring them: each enemy making a saving throw takes a -1 penalty per adjacent grilp. Four grilps on one hero = -4 to all save-ends rolls, which makes the party's save-ends conditions (fire weakness from Brandbearer, bleeding from Bloodlord, restrained from Redglare) nearly permanent. Counterplay: fire/area damage scatters the cluster; true-sight or detection bypasses the concealment.

## Platoon

### Hobgoblin Burning Witch

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 4, platoon, goblin, hobgoblin, humanoid, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 173

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 50, Speed 5, **Teleport** movement, Fire Immunity 4.

**Tactics:** **Soul Burn** (Ranged 10, two targets, choose corruption or fire per target) is solid two-target artillery. **2 Malice rider:** if a target has P<2, weakened save-ends, *plus* any enemy starting their turn within 3 squares of that target and who has P<2 is also weakened save-ends — a weakened-aura from a single hit. **Burning Legion** (1 Malice, Maneuver) teleports three allies up to 5 squares each and burns any creature adjacent to the destination. The hobgoblin teleport-chain engine: teleport three minions in, each detonates fire on adjacent heroes. Counterplay: high-Presence characters resist the weakened aura; spread the party out to prevent the Burning Legion + Open Furnace detonation chain.

### Hobgoblin Death Captain

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 4, platoon, goblin, hobgoblin, humanoid, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 173

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 60, Speed 5, Stability 2, Fire Immunity 4.

**Tactics:** **Blightblade** (Melee) delivers damage and — critically — **the next strike against the target has a double edge**. Every Death Captain turn hands the pack a double-edged swing at a named target. **3 Malice rider:** one adjacent ally uses their signature ability. Pair with a Hell Trooper or Grandguard for a free second activation. **On My Mark!** (Maneuver) makes one ally move up to speed and make a free strike — standard tempo buff. **Battle Ready:** any hidden creature striking the Death Captain or an ally within 2 takes a bane. Counter to rogue/assassin parties who lean on hide-and-strike. Counterplay: kill the Death Captain before the double-edge rider is used, or bait out On My Mark on a low-value ally.

### Hobgoblin Firerunner

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 5, platoon, goblin, hobgoblin, humanoid, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 174

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 70, Speed 8, Fire Immunity 5. Speed-8 is exceptional.

**Tactics:** **Flaming Kick** (Charge, Melee) deals hefty fire damage; tier 3 with A<3 dazes. **Blazing Trail** (Maneuver) moves up to speed and leaves an 8-square wall of fire along the path: enemies entering or starting their turn in the wall take 5 fire damage. **Hot to Go:** the first time per round the firerunner takes fire damage, their speed and the next wall they create are both +4 until end of their next turn. Hilariously, if your party *dumps fire on the firerunner*, you're feeding them — the hobgoblin strategy of running parties onto Operation Earth Sear's heated ground while the firerunner laps the map laying walls is the centerpiece combo. **Kindlestep:** ignores difficult terrain (including Swamp Gas, Unhallowed Ground, and their own Earth Sear). Counterplay: no-fire damage types only; force the firerunner into a chokepoint where they can't draw a long wall.

### Hobgoblin Grandguard

**Role:** defender
**Tags:** level 6, platoon, goblin, hobgoblin, humanoid, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 174

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 111, Speed 4, Stability 4, Fire Immunity 6. The wall.

**Tactics:** **Tower Shield Smash** (Melee 2) hits hard and prones on tier 3. **3 Malice rider:** each ally adjacent to a prone target gets a free strike — recruit-pile multiplier. **Thunder Rush** (3 Malice, Main action, 1×2 line within 1) damages and **pushes each target up to 10 squares**, then the Grandguard shifts into the area behind them. The canonical "shove the whole front line into a pit / off a cliff / into Unhallowed Ground" button; combined with the War Mage's Unhallowed Ground or an Incendiarist burn zone, Thunder Rush is the hobgoblin setup move. **Wide Guard:** any strike against an ally within 2 takes a bane. Counterplay: ranged attacks from outside the 2-square aura; stability ≥ target's forced-move to survive Thunder Rush.

### Hobgoblin Hell Trooper

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 4, platoon, goblin, hobgoblin, humanoid, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 174

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 70, Stability 2, Speed 5, Fire Immunity 4. The front-line bruiser.

**Tactics:** **Fire Flail** (Melee 2, two targets) is tier-scaling fire damage that also lets the Trooper move without provoking opportunity attacks from any target until end of turn — so they can cleave two heroes and walk out of both threat ranges. **Fight Me, Coward!** (Ranged 5, Maneuver) taunts a target with P<2 end-of-turn; while taunted, the target takes 1d6 fire damage whenever they use an ability or strike that doesn't target the trooper. Pure lock-on to a single hero who isn't a caster with good Presence. Counterplay: high-Presence ranged attackers ignore the taunt; keep ablative allies adjacent to the Hell Trooper to absorb Fire Flail's two-target spread.

### Hobgoblin Incendiarist

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 5, platoon, goblin, hobgoblin, humanoid, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 175

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 60, Speed 5, Fire Immunity 5.

**Tactics:** **Fire Crossbow** (Ranged 10) delivers high single-target fire damage, tier 3 with A<3 applies burning save-ends (1d6/turn). **Fireball Volley** (3 Malice, Area, 4-cube within 10) is the AoE: fire damage across tiers, tier 3 prones, and each tier applies burning save-ends on failed Agility. **Raining Cinders:** the ranged free strike of each ally within 3 squares has distance 10 and deals fire damage. Cluster two or three Incendiarists and every nearby minion suddenly has a fire-damage ranged free strike at 10 — turning the whole platoon into a secondary artillery battery. Counterplay: disrupt the cluster with area or forced movement; fire resistance; put the Incendiarist's targets behind line-of-effect cover.

### Hobgoblin Redglare

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 6, platoon, goblin, hobgoblin, humanoid, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 175

**Flavor:** Size 1L, Stamina 70, Speed 5, Stability 4, **Teleport**, Fire Immunity 6.

**Tactics:** **Eye Flash** (Ranged 10) is the slow/restrain engine: tier 1 slowed, tier 2/3 restrained save-ends — corruption damage on top. **Glare of the Old Judgments** (5 Malice, Ranged 10) marks the target as *judged* until end of encounter on tier 3 (or tier 2 if P<2): a judged target takes 10 corruption per turn *and regains 5 Stamina each time they use an ability that lets another creature spend a Recovery*. The punish-the-healer button — whoever casts a party heal is feeding the Redglare. Counterplay: burn the Redglare down inside the 10-squares line; positioning to break line of effect; the party healer avoids triggering effects that grant Recoveries while the judgment is active.

### Hobgoblin Smokebinder

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 5, platoon, goblin, hobgoblin, humanoid, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 176

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 70, Speed 7, **Fly, Hover**, Fire Immunity 5. Thin-bodied smoke-walker.

**Tactics:** **Choking Bolt** (Ranged 5) deals fire damage; tier 3 with R<3 slows save-ends. Rider: if the ability gains an edge or has a double edge, **the target can't communicate with anyone until end of their next turn** — silences bards, command casters, and cooperative tactics. Hunts for edge sources (Death Captain, flanking). **Smoke Bomb** (3 Malice, 3-burst Maneuver) forces Might tests; stronger failure = double bane on next roll. **Essence of Smoke:** the Smokebinder can move through spaces as if size 1T and occupy another creature's square, and hide at end of turn if they haven't taken damage. The perfect disengage-and-hide loop — move through a hero, hide behind them, reappear next turn. Counterplay: area damage breaks the hide; grab/restrain locks them in place; burst them fast before the communication lockout stacks.

## Elite

### Hobgoblin War Mage — elite

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 5, elite, goblin, hobgoblin, humanoid, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 176

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 120, Speed 5, **Hover, Teleport**, Fire Immunity 5. The hobgoblin brain trust and the thing that summons Slaughter Demons.

**Tactics:** **Hellfire** (Area, 3-cube within 10) is a fire-weakened-save-ends cone; rider: *before using this ability, the War Mage can teleport a creature within 10 up to 2 squares*. Drag a hero into their own party's line-of-fire or out of cover, then Hellfire them. **Enchantments of War** (Ranged 10, two allies) grants each 10 temporary Stamina + double edge on their next power roll; the War Mage can also spend any amount of current Stamina to add an equivalent amount of temp Stamina to each target. This is the pack buff — two Hell Troopers or a Grandguard + Bloodlord with 10 temp Stamina and a double-edge swing is a devastating combined turn. **Unhallowed Ground** (3 Malice, 5-cube within 10, Maneuver) consecrates the area until end of encounter: difficult terrain and **fire weakness 10** for any enemy inside. The lynchpin of hobgoblin fire strategy — drop Unhallowed Ground, then every subsequent fire tick from Firerunners / Incendiarists / Burning Witches is +10. **Magic Siphon** (Triggered, Ranged 10): halves damage/healing from a magic ability used within distance, and the War Mage regains Stamina equal to the remaining amount. Counters caster parties directly. **Despair, You Who Face Death:** enemies within 2 take -2 to saves. Counterplay: kill the War Mage before Unhallowed Ground lands — every encounter pivots on whether this ability resolves. Non-magic damage bypasses Magic Siphon. Keep distance from the -2 save aura.

## Leader

### Hobgoblin Bloodlord — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 6, leader, goblin, hobgoblin, humanoid, infernal
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 178

**Flavor:** Size 1M, Stamina 180, Speed 6, Stability 2, **Teleport**, Fire Immunity 6. Corruption-and-fire infernal warlord.

**Tactics:** **Soul Sword** (Melee, two targets) does corruption damage with bleeding save-ends across tiers. **2 Malice rider:** each target is marked until end of encounter or death; allies gain edge on strikes against marked targets; the Bloodlord can have up to three marks live at once. The party's front line stays permanently marked with edges flowing into the pack. **Take Point!** (Ranged 10, Maneuver) makes one ally move up to speed and use a signature ability. The pack-force-multiplier maneuver — hand a Grandguard a free Thunder Rush, or a Burning Witch a free Soul Burn. **An Army From Blood** (3 Malice, Triggered, Ranged 10): when a non-minion hobgoblin in distance takes damage, three Hobgoblin Recruits manifest adjacent to them. Effectively infinite minion economy as long as the Bloodlord has Malice. **End Effect:** at end of each turn, take 10 damage to end one save-ends effect — stays clean of debuffs.

Villain actions:
- **Advance! (VA1).** 10-burst. Self and each ally gain 10 temp Stamina and can move up to speed; each non-minion ally then makes a free strike. Pack tempo + durability buff. Open with this.
- **Skulls Abound (VA2).** 3-aura until end of encounter: any enemy entering or starting their turn in the aura takes 8 corruption and takes a bane on their next power roll. The Bloodlord becomes a walking no-go zone — the party must either stay out of melee (handing the pack initiative) or eat corruption every turn.
- **I Am Fire! I Am Death! (VA3).** 5-burst. Fire damage across tiers *plus* P-save rider for bonus damage, push up to 5, and prone. Also: until end of encounter, the Bloodlord is wreathed in black flames — any adjacent enemy who grabs them or uses a melee ability against them takes 5 corruption. The "don't touch me in melee" lock.

Counterplay: (1) Burst the Bloodlord before VA2/VA3 land — the aura and melee-punishment combined force the party into ranged-only attrition. (2) End Effect means save-ends debuffs are wasted — stack damage and mark-removal instead. (3) Killing all non-minion hobgoblins shuts off An Army From Blood's trigger. (4) Cleanse or kill marked targets fast; three simultaneous edge-marks compound with every pack swing.

## Summoned elite

### Slaughter Demon (grack'tanar) — elite

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 4, elite, abyssal, demon, hobgoblin
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 179

**Flavor:** Size 3, Stamina 140, Speed 7, Stability 3, **Burrow**, Fire Immunity 5. Serpent-bodied, six-clawed demon summoned from the Seven Cities of Hell by hobgoblin war mages. Not a Goblin — Abyssal/Demon/Hobgoblin.

**Tactics:** **Steely Skewer** (Melee 3, two targets) deals damage; tier 2 bleeding, tier 3 bleeding *and restrained* save-ends — and a creature restrained this way *moves with the slaughter demon*. The demon can drag up to six creatures or objects at once. Combined with **Drag Below** (free strike as part of the Dig maneuver; target with M<2 is grabbed with a bane on Escape Grab) and Burrow movement: the demon skewers the party frontline, dives underground, and drags them away from the healer. **Tail Stinger** (3 Malice, Melee 5) is the single-target finisher — high poison damage with weakened save-ends across all tiers; a target weakened this way also has **damage weakness 3**, stacking with all subsequent hobgoblin hits. **Devour Soul** (Triggered, 5-burst): when a creature with a soul dies in distance, that creature *can't be brought back to life*, and the slaughter demon gains an edge on power rolls until end of encounter. The revenant/raise-dead prevention button and an escalating damage amp. **Lethe:** while winded, edge on strikes *and* edge on strikes against them — same risk-reward as the Goblin Underboss's Get Reckless, but on a 140-Stamina elite. **Soulsight:** nothing within 2 can hide. Counterplay: ranged damage to avoid the melee-3 spread; non-living minions / constructs / undead bypass Devour Soul entirely; push the demon above winded before they hit the Lethe window; keep the healer at range 6+ so a Burrow-drag can't loop them to the wall.
