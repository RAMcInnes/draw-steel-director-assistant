# Demons

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 59–74. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book. 1st Echelon entries are detailed; 2nd–4th Echelon entries are descriptions only.*

Creatures of incarnate hate, demons spawn naturally in the Abyssal Wasteland. They crave violence and suffering the way other creatures need food. A demon cares only for themselves, and they torture and tear lesser demons apart for fun. Each demon's bestial appearance is unique — a chaotic arrangement of teeth, claws, and limbs meant for killing. Even demons of the same kind have unique features: one might bear an extra set of eyes or teeth, while another has a humanoid arm growing from their forehead.

- **Soul Reavers.** Demons feast not on food or liquids, but on souls. Souls fuel their anarchic powers, and while a demon is starved for souls, they can scarcely think. Whenever a demon kills a creature who has a soul, they consume that soul and keep its energy within their body — they can then burn that soul energy to enact their most devastating abilities.
- **Lethe.** When a demon's soul energy begins to flag, they fall into *lethe* — a violent hunger wherein they can only lash out in a desperate search for sustenance. Mechanically, every non-minion demon has the **Lethe** trait: while winded, they gain an edge on strikes, and strikes made against them gain an edge. This is the hexer's paradox — a winded demon is more dangerous and more vulnerable at once.
- **Demonic Hierarchy.** Mortal scholars classify demons into ten categories, each increasingly powerful. Each time a demon consumes a soul, they might evolve into a more powerful form — the jump can be instantaneous or take years. Stronger demons bully weaker ones into service.
- **Mortal Alliances.** Demons form temporary alliances with evil mortals in exchange for souls to consume. Such alliances create carnage efficiently, but inevitably collapse when the demons decide to devour their partners. The only creature who can truly keep a demon in line is a more powerful demon.
- **Languages.** Lower-category demons speak Proto-Tholl. As demons evolve and reach category 3 or higher, their speech turns into proper Tholl.

## Shared traits (non-minion demons)

- **Lethe.** While winded, an edge on strikes in *and out* — see above.
- **Soulsight.** Creatures within 2 squares of the demon can't be hidden from them (5 squares on the bale eye; 100 miles on the soulraker hivequeen's Hive Soulsight).

## Demon Malice features

Malice features are tiered by demon level — a higher-tier demon can spend Malice on any prior tier's feature (via the **Prior Malice Features** 3–7 Malice entry on each tier).

### Level 1+

- **Soulburn (3 Malice).** Each demon acting this turn has a double edge on abilities. Cheap turn-wide buff; combos into a horde wave that expects a single round of peak damage.
- **Abyssal Evolution (7 Malice).** One demon minion of your choice transforms into a non-minion horde demon of the same level. Converts minion chaff into a focused threat mid-encounter — save for when a single minion cluster survives long enough to be worth upgrading.
- **Abyssal Rift (7 Malice).** Place two size-2 rifts anywhere on the map. Any demon can use one rift as a portal to the other. A non-demon entering or starting their turn on a rift takes corruption damage equal to the highest-level demon on the map. Each rift has 25 Stamina, damage immunity 2, holy weakness 5. Rifts close automatically when no demons remain. A Magic or Psionics skill-user adjacent to a rift can spend a maneuver on a **Reason test** or **Intuition test** to destabilize it: ≤11 rift regains 5 Stamina and stays open; 12–16 stays open; 17+ closes. This is the map-warping hammer — use when you want heroes choosing between chasing demons or collapsing rifts.

### Level 4+

- **Abyssal Jaunt (3 Malice).** One demon acting this turn folds space until their next turn: they ignore difficult terrain, move through solid matter, and don't provoke opportunity attacks by moving. If they end their turn inside solid matter, they're shunted back out to the space they entered from. Useful for the ambusher or harrier you want to teleport-assassinate a backline hero.

### Level 7+

- **Seeping Blight (5 Malice).** One demon expels blight onto the ground in a 3-burst that lingers until the start of their next turn. Any enemy entering or starting their turn in the area takes 6 corruption damage, and has a double bane on power rolls until the start of their next turn. This is zone control + massive power-roll suppression — drop it on the hero caster's square.

### Level 10+

- **Soul Flense (7 Malice).** One demon unleashes pent-up agony on every non-demon in a 5-burst. Each target makes a **Presence test**: ≤11 takes 10 corruption and is soul flensed; 12–16 is soul flensed; 17+ no effect. Effect: the demon removes all save-ends conditions on themselves and transfers them to each creature soul flensed this way. This is the late-game Houdini — the demon dumps every debuff heroes have stacked onto them right back onto the party.

## 1st Echelon

A number of lesser demons have been identified across categories 1 to 3. These are the ones worth statting out; higher echelons are summarized below.

### Ensnarer — minion

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, abyssal, demon
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 61
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to melee distance.

**Flavor:** A pitling that got hold of a soul and bored through its own head to become a fanged appendage — mouths have opened across its body, each whipping out a long, barbed tongue. Stamina 5, Speed 5, M +2.

**Tactics:** **Barbed Tongues** (Melee 3) pulls 1/2/3, and if a target is pulled adjacent, the ensnarer makes a free strike against them. With Captain's +2 melee distance, reach goes to 5 — these are the board-controlling minions of the 1st Echelon, yanking isolated heroes into the main cluster. Pair with muceron or torlas to convert the pull into a grab or a control-zone trigger.

### Frenzied — minion

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, abyssal, demon
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 61
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to speed.

**Flavor:** A more-evolved ensnarer: fast, vicious, starving. Stamina 4, Speed 6, A +2.

**Tactics:** **Rip and Tear** is a Charge — the frenzied's whole value is getting into melee from across the board. With Captain speed goes to 8, and Charge can reach squishy back-rank heroes on turn 1. Use them as the distraction while ensnarers set up the pulls.

### Pitling — minion

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, minion, EV 3 for four, abyssal, demon
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 61
**With Captain:** +2 bonus to speed.

**Flavor:** Rodent-like demons with gleaming green eyes and terrible body odor. Fly 5. Stamina 3.

**Tactics:** **Spit** (Ranged 10, poison). The passive **Horrid Stench** is the real kicker: *any enemy with three or more pitlings within 2 squares can't regain Stamina* — drop a swarm of four on the party cleric and their healing stops. Fly movement lets you arrange the 2-square bubble around the priority target regardless of terrain.

### Bendrak

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 2, horde, EV 4, abyssal, demon
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 62

**Flavor:** An amalgamation of flesh and shards of broken mirror. Stamina 15, Speed 5, P +2. Uses dazzling reflections to confuse foes and hide allies.

**Tactics:** **Warp Perceptions** (Ranged 10, psychic) weakens on P<1/2, and the effect is the point: if the weakened target makes a strike, the bendrak can *split* the strike between the original target and a second target the bendrak picks — first target takes half the damage, second takes the rest. This turns the hero's own attack against the party; perfect with a Captain nearby to make sure the hero has something juicy to swing at. **Vanish** (1 Malice, Ranged 10) makes self or one ally invisible until the end of their next turn with a shift-3 and hide attempt — bendraks disappear before heroes can retaliate.

### Muceron

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 3, horde, EV 5, abyssal, demon
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 62

**Flavor:** Ensnarer evolved through repeated soul-consumption into a brutish 1M body covered in mouths. Stamina 30, Speed 5, M +2.

**Tactics:** **Barbed Tongues** (Melee 2) pulls 2/3/4 per tier; if the target is pulled adjacent, the muceron either free-strikes or uses **Grab** — pipeline the pulled hero straight into a restraint. **Tongue Pull** (2 Malice, Ranged 5, three targets, maneuver) pulls each up to 5 squares — an AoE repositioning button that sets up a whole round of melee chaos for the rest of the horde. Use one muceron as the anchor while ensnarers and frenzieds swarm the displaced heroes.

### Remasch

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 2, horde, EV 4, abyssal, demon
**Movement:** Teleport.
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 62

**Flavor:** Physical forms blended with the abyssal wastes. Teleport movement, Stamina 20, Speed 5, P +2. Rumored to be the source of the teachings of all shadows through the College of Black Ash.

**Tactics:** **Abyssal Strike** (Melee 1) damages *then teleports* the remasch up to 2/3/5 squares on tier — hit-and-run incarnate. Spend 5 Malice to drag an adjacent creature along on the teleport (they appear adjacent to the remasch's destination) — this is the kidnap/isolate button, great for yanking a hero into a prepared kill-zone far from their allies. **Grasping Shadow** (3 Malice, maneuver) teleports 2 *and then uses Abyssal Strike* — effectively three teleport-attacks chained in one turn.

### Ruinant

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, horde, EV 3, abyssal, demon
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 63

**Flavor:** Glowing eyes, tendril-ringed maws, inflamed scars across the body. Their breath comes as a sickening wheeze, and their wounds mirror onto their victims. Stamina 15, Speed 6, A +2, P +1.

**Tactics:** **Bloodletting Claws** (Melee 1, two targets) bleeds M<2 on 17+. **Salt Wounds** (3 Malice, Ranged 10, three targets, maneuver) requires each target to be below full Stamina — it's the *finisher*, cheap corruption damage chaining across wounded heroes. A pack of ruinants and a single heavier horde demon is a canonical low-level demon wave: the big demon draws the bleed/damage, the ruinants run cleanup.

### Torlas

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 1, horde, EV 3, abyssal, demon
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 63

**Flavor:** Piecemeal abominations whose bodies don't obey mundane geometry. Use living flesh to whip allies and enemies into position. Stamina 10, Speed 5, P +2.

**Tactics:** **Floor to Flesh** (Signature, 3-cube within 10) slides 3/4/5 — and the ground becomes difficult-terrain spongy flesh dealing 1 damage per square moved until the torlas's next turn. This is the *sticky zone* tool for splitting parties across a movement tax. **Grasping Tendons** (Ranged 10, three targets, maneuver) pulls each up to 3. The combo: slide enemies into the sticky flesh zone, then pull each turn to keep them in the mud. Perfect pairing with a muceron-anchored melee line.

### Chorogaunt — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 3, leader, EV 20, abyssal, demon
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 64

**Flavor:** A terrifying demon leader — an amalgamation of several demon bodies formed into a mobile musical instrument. Several heads embedded in the chest form a chorus; ribs upturn into a fleshy pipe organ. Attacks entrance and confuse enemies, making them more vulnerable to other demons. Stamina 120, Speed 5, Stability 2, Weakness: Holy 5.

**Tactics:** The chorogaunt is a solo-feeling leader for a 1st-Echelon squad. **Agonizing Harmony** (Signature, 5-burst each enemy) deals 4/7/10 psychic and slows on I<1/2/3 (save ends) — then *one ally within 10 squares shifts up to their speed*. Built to pile psychic pressure *and* reposition a pack of ruinants/frenzieds into strike range in a single action. **Chaotic Entrancing Harmony** (Maneuver, 10-burst each enemy) slides each enemy up to 3, **ignoring stability** — this is the map-reset button.

Villain actions escalate the tempo:
- **Frightening Tones (VA1).** Ranged 10, three enemies; choose between 5 psychic or frightened (save ends).
- **Bully the Weak (VA2).** Kills one allied demon outright; every other ally deals +3 strike damage until end of round; the Director gains Malice equal to the number of heroes in the encounter. This is the *sacrifice pump* — spend a bleeding-out horde demon to fuel Malice for a Seeping Blight or Soul Flense.
- **Running Cacophony (VA3).** Shift–Agonizing Harmony–Shift–Agonizing Harmony in one action — a mobile doom loop that can catch a spread party in two overlapping 5-bursts.

Defenses: **I Thrive on Pain (3 Malice, triggered).** When targeted by a strike, halve the damage and deal +3 on all chorogaunt abilities until end of next turn. Plus **End Effect** (spend 5 unresistable self-damage at end of turn to clear one save-ends effect) and **Lethe** (edge both ways when winded).

Counterplay: holy damage (weakness 5); burst down before Bully the Weak compounds Malice; spread the party outside the 5-burst cones; don't let an ally demon survive adjacent into late rounds, since it becomes sacrifice fuel.

## 2nd Echelon (descriptions only)

As demons consume more souls and their mercurial forms shift into more powerful ones, they begin to evolve more distinct personalities, desires, and intelligence — often based on the souls consumed. Across categories 4 to 6, demons develop a particular taste for certain types of souls, which eventually come to define them.

- **Grulqin** (Level 4 Minion Brute, EV 6 for four) — bulbous head, two powerful arms, and a circular serrated bone blade running down the middle of their head. Charge-forward minion; Signature gets an edge if they moved 3+ squares in a straight line first.
- **Orliq** (Level 4 Minion Harrier, EV 6 for four) — shifting bone plates around a core of corruptive energy. Fly. Signature deals corruption + slide/vertical-slide. **Imposing Energy:** any enemy starting their turn with two or more orliq adjacent is slowed (EoT).
- **Wobalas** (Level 4 Minion Artillery, EV 6 for four) — prefer to kill from afar while protected by allies, firing psychic bolts from bows of their own flesh and sinew. Ranged 20 with a bane-on-next-strike effect, doubled on winded targets.
- **Bale Eye** (Level 5 Horde Hexer, EV 7) — "flesh moons." Colossal (size 4) floating orbs of roiling dripping flesh with a seam that opens onto a demonic eye whose gaze turns minds and bodies upside down. Fly. Warps targets into double-bane-on-positive-characteristics husks.
- **Fangling** (Level 4 Horde Harrier, EV 6) — body overgrown with teeth, fangs, and tusks from countless creatures. Deadly in close quarters to attackers and bystanders alike — adjacent-enemy chip damage, damage-back on melee hits.
- **Fiktin** (Level 5 Horde Ambusher, EV 7) — cunning hunters who disguise themselves as mundane objects with a foul oily-red residue, then reveal their true humanoid shape of red oil in a violent burst. Opens encounters from concealment.
- **Gunge** (Level 4 Horde Controller, EV 6) — horrifically distended, maws big enough to swallow a horse. Vomits bilious slime that traps, dissolves, and bleeds; **Spew Slide** triggered action lets them eject from melee and leave a slime puddle behind.
- **Tormenauk** (Level 6 Horde Brute, EV 8) — hulking ape-shaped, a dozen extra mouths around the body. Brute-force grappler that also wails in the voices of devoured souls, dealing psychic pain (**Agony Wail** dazes in a 3-burst).
- **Lumbering Egress** (Level 6 Leader, EV 32) — mobile masses of warped flesh set around an enormous whirling portal to the demonic realms. Summons minions in bursts, folds space to teleport, and when its last ally minion dies or it drops below 25 Stamina, **Abyssal Protectors** dumps eight ensnarers onto the map. The siege engine of the 2nd Echelon.

## 3rd Echelon (descriptions only)

Demons of categories 7 to 9 continue to mutate, or are corrupted and spawned from other powerful creatures into the demonic hierarchy. In a plane of chaos, the **soulraker** hive is notable for fashioning a facsimile of order — demonic wasps in a single chaotic hive mind, serving a demonic hivequeen who cannibalizes her children to ingest any souls they've consumed.

- **Soulraker Scout** (Level 7 Minion Harrier, EV 9 for four) — demonic wasp progeny who emerge from the hivequeen's abdomen with an earsplitting buzz. Fly. **Abyssal Buzzing:** any enemy starting turn with two+ soulraker minions adjacent takes 3 sonic damage.
- **Soulraker Soldier** (Level 7 Minion Brute, EV 9 for four) — armored in dense chitin; favor driving foes before them with pushes on every tier.
- **Soulraker Stinger** (Level 7 Minion Artillery, EV 9 for four) — wings atrophied, barbed poisonous stingers launched like quills. Ranged 20 with a concealment-granting effect covering the rest of the soulraker team.
- **Blight Phage** (Level 7 Horde Controller, EV 9) — once angelic creatures, now cruel distended mockeries covered in pestilent boils seeping ruinous blight. Spreads **Seeping Blight** zones across the map.
- **Chimeron** (Level 9 Horde Brute, EV 11) — horrifying amalgam of multiple demonic forms melded into a unique monstrosity, unslakable hunger for souls. Signature (**Flux Gnash**) picks damage type *and* condition after the roll; **Pain Absorption** halves damage from any targeted strike.
- **Styrich** (Level 8 Horde Hexer, EV 10) — lanky bodies with manes of long razor-sharp hair whipped to shred enemies. Melee-4 reach grabs and pulls; **Tangled Nest** restrains a 4-burst.
- **Soulraker Handmaiden** (Level 8 Horde Ambusher, EV 10) — abyssal parasites implanted by a hivequeen's stinging ovipositor. Gestate inside a host 1d3+1 weeks without any physical or mental signs, then emerge as a horrifying tower of flesh and bone attached to the host's insides. While implanted they occupy the host's space and have no line of effect outside; as a maneuver they emerge and can use the host's signature abilities at the host's modifiers. The cure is the **Find a Cure** downtime project in *Draw Steel: Heroes* — the cure kills the handmaiden when consumed.
- **Soulraker Praetorian** (Level 7 Horde Harrier, EV 9) — elite guards of a hivequeen, dripping foul ichor and barbed-stinger strikes that break off and keep bleeding victims.
- **Soulraker Hivequeen** (Level 9 Leader, EV 44) — a demonic wasp whose abdomen houses their own soulraker hive. Implants handmaiden parasites with her stinging ovipositor. **Hive Soulsight:** no creature within 2 squares of *any* soulraker demon within **100 miles** of the hivequeen can be hidden from her (and she has line of effect to them). **Cannibal Queen:** consume an adjacent soulraker to end a save-ends effect. Villain actions summon minions, shift-and-daze enemies, and carpet the area with bomber-wasp strikes.

## 4th Echelon (descriptions only)

The more souls a demon consumes, the more they evolve, and the more refined their tastes become — until category-10 demons can be satisfied only by consuming souls that exemplify particular qualities. Many have their tastes honored by their titles: Gorgino, the Sensuous Feast; Tyx, the Obliviated Spiral; Lorikta, the Discarded Dream.

- **Optacus** (Level 10 Minion Artillery, EV 12 for four) — abyssal eyes atop spindly flesh stalks supported by innumerable legs. Climb movement, Ranged 20 fire beam that ignores concealment, and at 17+ ignores immunity. Winded targets take +5.
- **Tyburaki** (Level 10 Minion Harrier, EV 12 for four) — thresher shark × terror lizard, tail lined with tiny shark maws. Swim. Tail Bite (Melee 3) plus free strikes on every adjacent enemy after the attack.
- **Unguloid** (Level 10 Minion Brute, EV 12 for four) — centaur-like with demonic rhinoceros body; renowned charge specialists. Gore Horn pushes 2/4/6 on tier, +10 damage on a 2+-square charge.
- **Izyak** (Level 10 Horde Controller, EV 12) — mercurial psionic form that appears different to every observer; true form a brain on four bone legs. Teleport movement; Nostalgic Wanderlust restrains and vertical-slides (left-in-midair until the restraint ends).
- **Vicisitator** (Level 10 Horde Harrier, EV 12) — flesh and bone trapped in a state of perpetual agonizing flux; touch warps bone, flesh, and soul. **Warp Touch**'s slowed/weakened effects cannot be ignored.
- **Aurumvas** — the demon king (no stat block in the source pages summarized here). Craves wealth and highly avaricious souls; towering figure with a stack of crowns on an ophidian head. Lords over other powerful demons through the wealth in his vast Abyssal vaults, drawing lesser demons and wicked mortals with the promise of minor treasures. If left unchecked, his servile armies and glittering hoards might one day encompass the entire timescape.
