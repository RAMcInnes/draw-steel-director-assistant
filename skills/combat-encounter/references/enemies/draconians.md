# Draconians

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 85–90. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Draconians — *wyrmwights* to some, *dragon knights* in Orden — are a people created through an obscure ritual known as **Dracogenesis**. Most have an elemental affinity tied to their draconic scales, which they can change given enough time and training. The entries on these pages are not an adventuring party but six individual warriors whose reputations precede them; all are likely to be found serving in various military forces as high-ranking commanders.

- **Dracogenesis.** The ritual is closely guarded; a draconian's elemental immunity is the shape of their scales, and scales can be retrained. Every draconian in this section is level 6, size 1M (Dorzinuuth 2), Stamina 140–180, with Fly movement. Non-minion draconians do not have the Soulsight or Lethe traits that demons carry — a draconian is a humanoid first, not an Abyssal creature.
- **The Dragon Phalanx.** The dragon-knight fraternity founded under Good King Omund. Several of the draconians below either swore its oath or reject it entirely, and the oath itself is a hook: creatures who recite it can trigger reactions in Phrrygalax (punitive) and Dorzinuuth (guilt-struck bane on strikes).
- **Languages.** Most draconians speak Caelian alongside the tongues of their employers and creators. Lydixavus, Phrrygalax, and Dorzinuuth speak Caelian and Vastariax. Aeolyxria speaks Caelian and the First Language. Locratix speaks Caelian and Oaxuatl. Myxovidan speaks Caelian and Axiomatic.

## Draconian Malice features

At the start of any draconian's turn, Malice may be spent to activate one of the following features.

- **Guarding Gale (3 Malice, maneuver).** One draconian acting this turn flaps their wings; each creature adjacent is pushed up to 4 squares, and M<2 targets are knocked prone. Cheap AoE disengage and prone-setup.
- **Breath Weapon (5 Malice, 4×2 line within 1, each enemy, main action).** 6/10/13 damage of a type the draconian has immunity to (so the Phrrygalax/Locratix acid variant is thematic — their scales *are* the ammunition). Combines with **Armor of the Ancients** (Phrrygalax) to swap immunities mid-fight and change breath type accordingly.
- **Scaleshatter Burst (7 Malice, 2-burst, free maneuver).** AoE damage plus a massive self-cost: the draconian gains weakness 5 but **takes two turns per round until end of encounter**. This is the tempo-nuke button — spend when the draconian is above half and the party is clustered. Stacks monstrously with Phrrygalax's **Oathbreaker's Vengeance** (extra damage on the strike after any failed save) since the draconian now gets twice the saves to fail per round.

## Aeolyxria the Uncanny

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 6, elite, EV 32, draconian, dragon, humanoid
**Immunity:** Poison 6. **Movement:** Fly.
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 86

**Flavor:** A wandering occultist who hoards tomes and experimental artifacts — she takes mercenary work only to try out whatever new doohickey she's scavenged. A sharp tactical mind lets her treat any battlefield as a home map. Stamina 140, Speed 5, R +2, I +3.

**Tactics:** **Spittlesplash** (Signature, Ranged 10, two enemies) slows on M<1/2/3 and is the opening move. **Experimental Treasure** (Ranged 10, two creatures or objects) is the signature gimmick — on 12–16 it's corruption + weakened, on 17+ it's lightning + bleeding, and on ≤11 it *heals* the target 10 Stamina. The twist: the *first time* in the encounter she rolls this ability, she can then **reuse that outcome for every subsequent casting** for the rest of the fight. Always try to roll it early on a safe target to lock in a tier 3. 2+ Malice adds one target per 2 Malice. **Elevate** (2 Malice, 1-cube within 5, maneuver) lifts the ground 5 squares into a dirt pillar, carrying any creatures in the area to the new elevation — map sculpting mid-round; spend more Malice for more pillars, breaking line of effect and stranding flyers' prey. **Blood For Blood** (triggered, Ranged 5) punishes any enemy who makes *her ally* bleed: poison and bleeding on the offender. **That's Our Opening!** (passive) gives the Director +1 Malice every time Aeolyxria imposes a condition — her conditions are baked into every ability, so she's a Malice engine as much as a hexer. Counterplay: deny her the first roll (burst her before she casts Experimental Treasure), close the range, break line of effect to the pillars.

## Locratix the Morningstar

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 6, elite, EV 32, draconian, dragon, humanoid
**Immunity:** Acid 6. **Movement:** Fly.
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 87

**Flavor:** Traveler from a faraway land, sole progeny of her Dracogenesis clutch — focused on survival above all else, contemptuous of the dragon-knight oath. Takes mercenary work but is always one better deal away from putting her lance through her current employer. Stamina 160, Speed 8, A +3.

**Tactics:** **Skewer** (Signature, Melee 1, two targets) slows M<1/2 and *deals 6 damage to everything in a 2×1 line behind the target* — lance-through-and-past geometry; line up two heroes in a row for the extra damage. **Acidic Stun** (2 Malice, 3×1 line within 1) dazes on M<1/2/3, and dazed-this-way targets **take +6 damage from her subsequent abilities** — this is the setup-and-payoff cycle. **Takeoff** (maneuver) is the signature exit: fly up to her speed, and A<2 creatures adjacent to the takeoff square are knocked prone. **Stay Back!** (triggered) strikes a creature moving within Melee 2 and on A<3 sets their speed to 0 until end of turn — a brutal approach deterrent when she's guarding altitude. **Flighty** passive: any enemy she deals rolled damage to *can't use her as a trigger for their triggered actions* until her next turn — she peels away from reaction-chain defenders. **Absorbing Scales**: after she takes acid damage (her immunity), she gains damage immunity 6 against the next strike regardless of type. Counterplay: ranged pressure at altitude; don't stand in line behind an ally; hit her with non-acid *first* so Absorbing Scales can't fire.

## Lydixavus the Deadeye

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 6, elite, EV 32, draconian, dragon, humanoid
**Immunity:** Acid 6. **Movement:** Fly.
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 88

**Flavor:** Nothing escapes their aim — decades of training on a precision cold-breath rifle-shot. Every scar on Lydixavus's scales is a debt, and they're collecting tenfold. Stamina 140, Speed 5, R/I +3.

**Tactics:** **Breathsnipe** (Signature, Ranged 15) is the sniper shot — 10/16/19 cold plus bane or double-bane on the target's next strike. **Ice Lob** (2-cube within 10) dazes at every tier (M<1/2/3), the AoE for when multiple heroes are clumped. **Parting Gift** (maneuver) lets Lydixavus fly up to their speed and *leave a size-1S ice mine* in the takeoff square — the mine explodes when an enemy enters, using the Ice Lob power roll against the triggering creature and everything adjacent. Perfect for a reposition + area denial combo every turn. **Wasn't Aiming For You** (triggered): if Breathsnipe lands a tier 1 outcome, Lydixavus immediately uses Breathsnipe again against a creature within 5 squares of the original target — a miss becomes a hit on someone else. **Scorekeeping Scales**: Lydixavus knows the location of *every creature who has ever dealt damage to them* and always has line of effect to any such creature within 20 squares (as long as a size-1 opening exists). Old grudges become permanent tracking — use when prior-session NPCs show up again. Counterplay: close to Melee and don't let them take off; block the ice-mine lane; and if Lydixavus has a vendetta against a hero, position that hero with multiple allies between them and line of effect.

## Myxovidan the Sintaker

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 6, elite, EV 32, draconian, dragon, humanoid
**Immunity:** Corruption 6. **Movement:** Fly.
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 88

**Flavor:** Obsidian-black scales, nausea-inducing breath. Raised by a secluded order of monks who deemed him an evil to be purged through austere training; when he left, he left behind a trail of emaciated monk corpses that looked drained of life. Stamina 140, Speed 5, R/I +2.

**Tactics:** **Breaking Palm** (Signature, Melee 1, two enemies) weakens on M<1/2/3, and the top tier swaps to corruption damage. **2 Malice kicker: Myxovidan regains Stamina equal to half the damage dealt** — this is the self-sustain engine; every round he hits two heroes he heals back 15–20. **Expunging Exhalation** (2-burst, area main action) deals corruption and stacks corruption weakness 3 on save-ends — combos straight into his own corruption breath weapon from the shared Malice list, or with any other corruption-damage ally. **Step and Swap** (Ranged 3, maneuver) lets Myxovidan and a target ally *shift to swap places* — he walks a bleeding ally out of the front line and takes the heat himself while the Breaking Palm lifesteal refills him. **Anyone Can Do That** (2 Malice, triggered) activates when an adjacent creature melee-damages him: Myxovidan **recreates the triggering ability using his highest characteristic score** and uses it against the attacker; if he gets a higher tier than the original, the Director gains 2 Malice. This is the mirror — heroes with a big melee signature should think twice before committing. **Stench of Death** (passive): enemies within 5 squares regain 3 less Stamina when they heal. Counterplay: ranged damage avoids Anyone Can Do That; kill him fast enough that lifesteal can't stabilize him; fight outside his 5-square healing-debuff bubble.

## Phrrygalax the Subduer

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 6, elite, EV 32, draconian, dragon, humanoid
**Immunity:** Acid 6. **Movement:** Fly.
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 89

**Flavor:** Once a dragon knight in the service of Good King Omund — took the oath, but always knew he was made for violence. When Mandrake's betrayal fractured the Dragon Phalanx, Phrrygalax gleefully dropped his oath and picked up his true purpose. Stamina 180, Speed 5, Stability 3, M +3, P +3.

**Tactics:** **Baneful Blade** (Signature, Melee 1, two targets) bleeds M<1/2, and the top tier adds 3 extra bleed damage. **Spinning Spit** (2 Malice, 1-burst, each enemy, main action) is the close-in AoE fire. **Heavy Landing** (maneuver) flies up to his speed, lands, and knocks A<2 creatures prone around the landing square — gap-close + control. **Armor of the Ancients** (2 Malice, triggered) activates when he takes acid/cold/corruption/fire/lightning/poison damage: he takes **no damage, regains that Stamina, and swaps his current immunity to the triggering type**. Heroes with elemental damage spells are actively counterproductive. **Still Your Tongue!** (free triggered) fires when any creature within 5 squares *recites the oath of Good King Omund's Dragon Phalanx* — Phrrygalax shifts up to his speed and uses Baneful Blade for +7 damage against the reciter. This is a lore-triggered nuke for any scene where an NPC paladin or PC recites the oath. **Oathbreaker's Vengeance** passive: whenever he fails a saving throw, his *next* strike deals +7. Counterplay: *don't hit him with elemental damage*, don't recite the oath within 5 squares, bring weapons that bypass his 180 Stamina quickly rather than slow elemental chip.

## Dorzinuuth the Base — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 6, leader, EV 32, draconian, dragon, humanoid
**Immunity:** Lightning 6. **Movement:** Fly, hover.
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 90

**Flavor:** To those who know the old Dragon Phalanx, no name inspires more awe and fear — a mastermind who mounted daring campaigns and always returned with the same number of soldiers (often *more*). After Good King Omund's death, many were shocked to see him debase himself with banditry and petty mercenary jobs; others hold out hope that he's making unknown moves in the darkness, waiting for the day he can uphold his oath in the light again. Stamina 180, Speed 5, Stability 3, M +4.

**Tactics:** **Punishing Flail** (Signature, Melee 1, two targets) is prone-on-every-tier (M<2/3/4); 2 Malice kicker adds bleeding on M<4 targets. **I'll Cut A Path** (5×2 line within 1, maneuver) is the *lead-by-example* positional: Dorzinuuth **shifts to an unoccupied space adjacent to the end of the line before the roll**, so he escorts his line into a new formation and prones M<3/4 on hit. **Watch Your Six!** (triggered) halves damage to an ally within Melee 1 while Dorzinuuth isn't flying — wings-as-shield. **Sheltering Wings** passive: strikes against Dorzinuuth on the ground take a bane — reward him for staying grounded with allies. **Remember Your Oath** passive: if he hears a creature recite the Dragon Phalanx oath, he takes a bane on all strikes against that character for the rest of the encounter — the *opposite* reaction of Phrrygalax, and the cleanest way to neutralize Dorzinuuth in an encounter where a PC remembers the oath. **End Effect** (10 self-damage at end of turn to clear a save-ends effect).

Villain actions:
- **Roaring Gambit (VA1).** 5-burst Reason test; Frightened (save ends) / (EoT) / no effect. Allies in the area gain an edge on their next strike. The opening rally.
- **Wings of Second Wind (VA2).** 10-burst self and allies; each shifts or flies up to their speed and regains 10 Stamina. This is the reset button — use when the phalanx is scattered and bleeding; heals everyone including Dorzinuuth.
- **Snap, Crackle, Pop (VA3).** 5-burst on self and allies: an electrifying mesh that **deals 6 lightning damage back to any melee attacker** for the rest of the encounter. Combos with Watch Your Six and Sheltering Wings — melee heroes fighting Dorzinuuth's grounded formation hurt themselves every hit.

Counterplay: recite the oath early to impose the bane; keep him flying so Sheltering Wings can't protect him; save a hero's biggest burst for after Wings of Second Wind (otherwise he heals through it); avoid melee chains while Snap, Crackle, Pop is up.
