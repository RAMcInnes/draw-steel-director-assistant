# Goblins

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 160–165. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

One of the most numerous humanoid species in the world — they call themselves *rogabrin* (Szetch for "more of us"). Short, lithe, long-armed, prehensile-toed: built to climb and to flee, equally at home in treetop cities and cavern-warrens of stalactites and ledges. Most goblins live peaceful lives in goblin or mixed-species communities; the goblins heroes tend to meet are the thieves and murderers their own people exiled. A lone goblin in the wild is terrified. A dozen of them are supremely — perhaps *overly* — confident.

- **Encountered in Groups.** Goblins work together or they die. Exiled bands turn into roadside bandits who ambush travelers; larger hordes become unscrupulous mercenaries serving powerful villains. They fight as an overwhelming force and flee when they're the ones outnumbered.
- **Mobile and Sneaky.** Nearly every goblin on a battlefield has **Crafty** — they don't provoke opportunity attacks by moving. This *is* goblin tactics. They run through your line, hack at knees, shoot from cover, climb walls, and leave.
- **Goblin Magic.** Most goblin spellcasters are exiles who sold favors to fiends, archfey, or worse. Goblin assassins conjure darkness from the souls of their victims; goblin cursespitters throw hexes that hold a fight at bay from range.
- **Skitterlings.** Six-legged winged rodents the size of a housecat. Flapping feet, gripping claws — goblins train them to dive at a hero's face with toxin-coated toes that cause sluggishness.
- **War Spiders.** Enormous arachnids with blades bolted to their legs, carrying goblin archers on platforms lashed to their backs. On a raid they arch their abdomen and launch their riders bodily into the melee.
- **Worgs.** Canine mounts raised from pups by goblin communities. Loyal to the hand that feeds them, and they protect their handler to the last.
- **Languages.** Most goblins speak Caelian and Szetch.

## Shared traits (nearly every goblin)

- **Crafty.** No opportunity attacks triggered by movement. The whole pack can walk through a hero line, take positions behind the defender, and never pay for it.
- **Climb speed.** Every goblin stat block in this section (except the worg and horde mount) has Climb movement. On any map with verticality — trees, cliffs, ruin walls — goblins own the third dimension.
- **Size 1 Small.** Most goblins are 1S. Two goblins can share a square on a War Spider's Wide Back. Plan for tight clusters.

## Goblin Malice features

Spent at the start of any goblin's turn.

- **Goblin Mode (3 Malice).** Each goblin in the encounter gains +2 speed until end of round. Cheap map-wide movement buff — combine with Crafty for a round where the whole pack repositions freely without reaction cost.
- **Tiny Stabs (5 Malice).** Each enemy in the encounter takes 1 damage for each goblin adjacent to them. Scales with how densely the pack has mobbed a single hero — four goblins on one target = 4 unresistable chip, no roll. Drop it the round after Goblin Mode repositions the swarm.
- **Swamp Stink (7 Malice).** A green mist covers the map until end of round (wind can't clear it). All squares become difficult terrain for non-goblins. Each non-goblin makes a Might test: tier 1 takes poison damage + weakened while the mist lingers, tier 2 weakened while the mist lingers, tier 3 no effect. Map-wide weakened for one round while goblins move normally — the ambush-layer-two button when the party has their backs to terrain.

## Minions

### Goblin Runner — minion

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, minion, goblin, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 161
**With Captain:** gain an edge on strikes.

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 4, Speed 6, Climb. The sprint-in, sprint-out chaff.

**Tactics:** **Club Charge** (Charge, Melee 1, one target per minion) does low damage across tiers but the *Charge*-typed signature + **Crafty** means four runners can sprint across a map through an enemy line and swing before the defender has any answer. Summoned by the Monarch's **Get in Here!** — the Monarch's summon button drops two runners within 20 squares, so plan for the board to sprout fresh runners mid-fight.

### Goblin Skitterling — minion

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 1, minion, animal, goblin
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 161
**With Captain:** +3 bonus to speed.

**Flavor:** Size 1T (tiny), Stamina 3, Speed 5, **Fly**. Six-legged winged rat-things with poison feet. *Does not have the Crafty trait* (it's the Animal companion, not a goblin proper).

**Tactics:** **Claws** (Melee 1, one target per minion) deals poison damage and — critically — **the target takes a bane on their next strike**. Four skitterlings flying over to the party wizard or paladin impose four banes on four next-strikes. With Captain's +3 speed they fly 8. The priority-target debuffer: direct them at whoever the party most needs to hit cleanly next turn.

### Goblin Sniper — minion

**Role:** artillery
**Tags:** level 1, minion, goblin, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 161
**With Captain:** +5 bonus to ranged distance.

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 3, Speed 5, Climb. Bow sniper.

**Tactics:** **Bow** (Ranged 10, one target per minion) chips damage, and *gains an edge if the sniper doesn't use a move action this turn*. With Captain, range goes to 15. The stationary back-rank: post them on a cliff or a treetop (Climb movement is the whole point), let them sit still for the edge, and rain shots. **Crafty** still applies if you do need to reposition. Counterplay: close the distance, or make them move; an edge-denied sniper is weak.

### Goblin Spinecleaver — minion

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 1, minion, goblin, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 161
**With Captain:** +1 damage bonus to strikes.

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 5, Speed 5, Climb. The push minion.

**Tactics:** **Axe** (Melee 1, one target per minion) pushes 1/3/4 per tier. Four spinecleavers on one hero with tier 2 results shove them 12 squares — walk-a-hero-off-a-ledge is the canonical goblin move, and **Crafty** means the escort gives no reactions. Combine with the Monarch's back line or Underboss formation, or pair with terrain (chasms, water, spike pits) where forced movement does the real damage.

## Horde

### Goblin Assassin

**Role:** ambusher
**Tags:** level 1, horde, goblin, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 162

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 15, Speed 6, Climb. Dagger in the dark, fueled by pacts.

**Tactics:** **Sword Stab** (Melee 1) — mid-range damage across tiers, and *if the ability gains an edge or has a double edge, it deals an extra 2 damage*. So the assassin wants every possible source of edge — concealment from Swamp Gas, a skitterling's bane being worked around by flanking, etc. **Shadow Chains** (3 Malice, Ranged 10, three targets) restrains save-ends — the pack-wide crowd-control button. **Slip Away** — can attempt to hide even while observed. Combined with **Crafty**, the assassin can enter melee, sword-stab, disengage, hide, reposition — every turn. Counterplay: area damage defeats the hide-and-reappear; light sources and true-sight short-circuit Slip Away.

### Goblin Cursespitter

**Role:** hexer
**Tags:** level 1, horde, goblin, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 162

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 10, Speed 5, Climb. Surlach-pact hexer.

**Tactics:** **Eye of Surlach** (Ranged 15) — corruption damage with weakened save-ends on every tier (on I<0/1/2). The longest-range effect in the goblin kit; fire from a treetop across the map. **Dizzying Hex** (1 Malice, Ranged 10, Maneuver) — prones and on tier 3 the target *can't stand* save-ends. A maneuver that prones a hero for the rest of the scene on a good roll. **Crafty**. Hard to reach, hard to silence — the priority kill target for a party that needs their healer not to be weakened forever. Counterplay: close the distance or break line of effect; 15 squares is a long way if there's cover.

### Goblin Stinker

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 1, horde, goblin, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 163

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 10, Speed 5, Climb. Poison-gas controller.

**Tactics:** **Toxic Winds** (Signature, Area, 3-cube within 15) — poison damage + slide 1/2/3. **1+ Malice rider: for each Malice spent, force-move one additional square.** Cheap Malice sink that pushes heroes off ledges. **Swamp Gas** (Maneuver, 3-cube within 10) creates a lingering green-haze difficult-terrain zone — non-goblins take 2 poison damage per square moved inside it; lasts until the stinker's next turn or their death. Combo: Swamp Gas near the defender, Toxic Winds to slide the rest of the party into it. **Crafty**. Counterplay: kill the stinker to drop Swamp Gas instantly; ranged parties with forced-movement resistance fare best.

### Goblin Underboss

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 1, horde, goblin, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 163

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 15, Speed 5, Climb. Mid-rank commander.

**Tactics:** **Swordplay** (Melee 1) — solid single-target damage, and *one ally adjacent to the target can make a free strike against them*. Every Underboss turn hands a free strike to a nearby goblin. **Get Reckless!** (Maneuver, 5-burst) — until start of Underboss's next turn, each ally target has an edge on strikes *and any strike made against them has an edge*. The pure damage-pump with a risk clause. **2 Malice upgrade** removes the "strikes against targets gain an edge" drawback, so for 2 Malice the Underboss gives the whole pack a clean edge. **Crafty**. This is the engine for a big goblin turn — Underboss sets up, pack executes. Counterplay: kill the Underboss or take advantage of the "strikes against gain an edge" window if they didn't pay the Malice.

### Goblin Warrior

**Role:** harrier
**Tags:** level 1, horde, goblin, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 163

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 15, Speed 6, Climb. Spear-and-shield skirmisher.

**Tactics:** **Spear Charge** (Charge, Melee 1) — straightforward charge damage. **Bury the Point** (2 Malice) is the finisher: higher damage plus bleeding save-ends on tier 1/2/3 depending on Might check. **Crafty**. The all-rounder horde unit — spend Malice on bleeding when a hero is already below half so the DoT races their healer. Pair with Underboss's Get Reckless for clean edges on Bury the Point. Works beautifully as a War Spider rider (see below) since Ride Launcher's edge stacks with Crafty's mobility.

## Mounts and companions

### Worg — horde mount

**Role:** mount
**Tags:** level 1, horde, animal, goblin
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 165

**Flavor:** Size 1L, Stamina 15, Speed 5. Canine, pack-loyal, raised by goblin communities.

**Tactics:** **Bite** (Charge, Melee 1) — damage, Charge-typed so combines with Mounted Charger. **Sprint** (1 Malice, Maneuver) — the worg moves up to their speed. **Mounted Charger** — if a worg used as a mount charges, their rider gains an edge on melee strikes until end of rider's turn. **Shared Crafty** — if the worg's rider has Crafty, the worg has it too. So a Goblin Warrior on a worg charges, gets Mounted Charger's edge, and neither of them provoke — all for the cost of one base goblin + one worg. Build goblin cavalry out of these.

### War Spider — elite mount

**Role:** mount
**Tags:** level 1, elite, animal, goblin
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 165

**Flavor:** Size 3, Stamina 60, Speed 7, Climb, Stability 2. The ride-to-battle centerpiece. Platform on the back, leg-blades, web spinneret, terrifying.

**Tactics:** **Bite** (Melee 1) — poison damage with weakened save-ends on tier 3 (M<2). **2 Malice rider:** if target has M<3, weakened save-ends on *any* tier — pay the Malice when the bite lands on a bruiser whose Might save you'd otherwise fail. **Leg Blade** (Melee 1, two targets) — multi-target melee, +3/+3/+3 damage across tiers vs Bite. **Trample** (5 Malice, Main action) — the spider shifts up to their speed, uses Leg Blade against *each creature who comes adjacent during the shift*, single power roll against all targets. The board-clearing button for when the party has clumped up. **Web** (Maneuver, 3-cube within 1) — restrain save-ends across tiers *plus* difficult terrain for enemies in the area. **Skitter** (Triggered action) — when the spider or any rider takes damage, halve the damage and the spider shifts up to 2 squares. Pure survivability plus repositioning reaction. **Ride Launcher** — any ally jumping off the spider can leap up to 6 squares test-free, no fall damage, and *their first melee strike that turn has an edge*. **Wide Back** — two size-1 allies share the spider's space while riding. Canonical combo: Warrior + Spinecleaver both on the spider, spider Trample + Web, then both riders jump off for Ride Launcher edge + Crafty repositioning. Counterplay: target the spider — 60 Stamina is not infinite, and killing the mount grounds the riders mid-maneuver.

### Skitterling

See the minion entry above. Skitterlings double as mounts/pets depending on the encounter framing, but the stat block is the Level 1 Minion Hexer.

## Leader

### Goblin Monarch — leader

**Role:** leader
**Tags:** level 1, leader, goblin, humanoid
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 164

**Flavor:** Size 1S, Stamina 80, Speed 6, Climb, Stability 1, Free Strike 4. The king of an outlaw band — small, quick, surrounded by minions they won't hesitate to shove in front of a blade.

**Tactics:** **Handaxe** (Signature, Melee 1 or ranged 5, two targets) — strong damage across tiers, and *one ally within 10 squares makes a free strike* as an effect rider. The Monarch pumps the pack's damage output every turn. **Get in Here!** (1 Malice, Ranged 20, Maneuver) — two goblin runners appear in unoccupied squares within 20. Map-range reinforcement button that also refills the Tiny Stabs economy. **Meat Shield** (triggered action) — when a creature targets the Monarch with a strike, an adjacent ally becomes the target instead. This is *literal* ablative armor — every adjacent minion is potential damage redirection, and pairs obscenely well with the Monarch summoning runners on demand. **Crafty**. **End Effect** — at end of each turn, take 5 unresistable damage to end one save-ends effect. The Monarch never stays debuffed.

Villain actions:
- **What Are You Waiting For? (VA1).** 10-burst, each ally target moves up to their speed or makes a free strike. Mass tempo — whichever each gobbo needs.
- **Focus Fire (VA2).** Ranged 10, one enemy/object. Each ally within 10 of the target can move up to their speed *toward the target*. The "pick a hero, everyone converges" button. Follow with Tiny Stabs Malice next turn — the Focus Fire just placed every goblin adjacent to the target.
- **Kill! (VA3).** Each enemy in the encounter takes 2 damage for each goblin adjacent to them. Tiny Stabs but stronger and map-wide. If Focus Fire set up the cluster, Kill! finishes the job.

Counterplay: (1) clear the Monarch's meat shields before attacking — every adjacent minion is a free redirect. (2) Use ranged abilities that bypass the adjacency-for-Meat-Shield clause when targets extend beyond melee. (3) End Effect means save-ends conditions are worthless — stick to instantaneous debuffs or outright damage. (4) Burst the Monarch before VA3 lands with the pack adjacent.
