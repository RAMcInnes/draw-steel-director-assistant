# Valok

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 282–285. Reference only — stat blocks live in the book.*

Autonomous machines built by the long-vanished *steel dwarves* — forged from the truemetal *valiar* and powered by *prismacore* (iridoss), a gemstone infused with the power of other worlds. The steel dwarves perfected valiar smithing and used it for everything from weapons to translucent indestructible fabrics, but those secrets died with them. What remains is the valok: nearly indestructible, never tiring, and still executing millennia-old commands in the crumbling ruins they were left in. Activating a valok without the correct control phrases means facing a machine that is overloaded by time but undiminished in purpose.

Two classes of valok have been discovered — **servok** (huge labor/siege machines) and **multivok** (smaller humaniform supervisors and bodyguards). Ancient steel-dwarf carvings hint at a third, more advanced class built just before the makers' disappearance. No examples have yet been found.

- **Valiar Might.** The core trait on every non-minion valok. **While not bleeding, weakened, or winded, any power roll against them is automatically a tier-1 outcome.** Critical hits still trigger their additional main action, but normal damage tiers collapse. The only way to play properly against a valok is to apply bleeding / weakened / winded first, then commit real damage. Without the setup, the party fights a wall.
- **Crafted to Perfection.** Shape can't be changed by any external effect. Polymorph / ooze-transform / compel-shape abilities do nothing.
- **Prismacore power.** Valok don't rely on magic, spells, or prayer for operation. Anti-magic zones, dispels, and magic-suppression effects don't disable them. Prismacore never loses power — valok work ceaselessly, without rest.
- **Multivok Maintenance.** Multivok (Bodyguard, Chief) passively restore 15 Stamina at start of turn to each servok within 2. Multivok-plus-servok encounters self-heal unless the party splits them apart.
- **Servok Siege Machine.** Every servok ignores difficult terrain *and* deals +15 damage against objects. Valok encounters that leverage the environment (breakable walls, supports, siege objectives) favor the machines hard.
- **Languages.** Most valok communicate exclusively in Rallarian — the steel dwarf tongue. Parties with a linguist may be able to issue an old command phrase *before* initiative and short-circuit the fight. Good hook.

## Shared traits (every non-minion valok)

- **Valiar Might** — tier-1-only unless the valok is bleeding, weakened, or winded. This is the whole fight.
- **Crafted to Perfection** — shape-changing does nothing.
- **Soulless + Construct + Valok keywords** — resurrect/raise/mind-control effects that target souls do nothing; construct-bypassing abilities work as usual.

## Valok Malice features

Spent at the start of a valok's turn.

- **Repeater (3 Malice).** One valok acting this turn takes an additional main action. Cheap and always relevant — doubles a servok's Wrecking Ball or a multivok's Gatling Bolt Gun volley.
- **I Was Not Commanded to Wait (5 Malice).** Until end of round, at the end of each turn one valok can either shift up to 2 or make a free strike. Each turn — so across a full round with multiple hero turns, one valok gets multiple off-turn actions. Run this with a bodyguard next to a hero to tax every enemy turn.
- **Rapid Logic Overdrive (7 Malice).** Each valok in the encounter ends one save-ends effect on themselves, moves up to speed (with the new speed bonus), and **until end of encounter all valok deal +5 damage and have +5 speed**. **Stackable** — activate multiple times to keep piling on the bonuses. The "commit to finishing this fight now" button; the cost compounds with each stack but so does the damage and mobility.

## Multivok

### Multivok Bodyguard — elite

**Role:** defender
**Tags:** level 9, elite, construct, multivok, soulless, valok
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 283

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 240, Speed 5, Stability 5, Free Strike 9. Axe-bladed arms, shoulder-mounted repeating crossbow, still loyal to a long-dead charge.

**Tactics:** **Gatling Bolt Gun** (Signature, Ranged 10, two targets) — heavy damage across tiers, tier 3 bleeds save-ends on A<4. The default ranged threat, always on. **Valiar Axe** (3 Malice, Melee 2) — bigger damage, weakened save-ends on all tiers, tier 3 prones. **Effect:** *double edge if this ability was used against the same target earlier in this encounter* — the bodyguard commits to targets and punishes them harder over the fight. **Magnetic Pull** (Maneuver, 10-burst, metal-clad enemies or metal Size-3-or-smaller objects) pulls 8 (or 15 on M<3), then free-strike each target ending adjacent. Armored fighters get yanked across the map straight into the axe. **Valiar Cloak** (Triggered, Melee 2): when an ally within 2 is targeted, *after seeing the power roll*, the bodyguard becomes the target instead. The ablative defender ability — eats any hit that would hurt a ranged ally like a multivok chief. Combined with Valiar Might's tier-1 autocap, the bodyguard redirects a hero's big swing into a tier-1 outcome on the wrong target. Counterplay: apply bleeding/weakened/winded before swinging; attack targets outside the Valiar Cloak 2-square bubble; bring non-metal weapons to sidestep Magnetic Pull.

### Multivok Chief — elite

**Role:** support
**Tags:** level 9, elite, construct, multivok, soulless, valok
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 284

**Flavor:** Size 1L, Stamina 220, Speed 5, Stability 3, Free Strike 9. Built to oversee and coordinate — the foreman of a still-running steel dwarf construction site.

**Tactics:** **Pneumatic Punch** (Signature, Melee 2, two targets) — damage + push 3/5/8 per tier. The area-clearing melee. **Targeting Beam** (3 Malice, Ranged 10) — damage that **can't be reduced in any way**, slows save-ends on A-save, and *any strike against the slowed target has a double edge.* The focus-fire setup: chief beams a priority target, servoks dog-pile with double-edge swings. **Chief's Command** (Maneuver, Ranged 5, one ally) — ally shifts and uses a main action. The tempo-multiplier: hand a servok war engine a free Prismacore Cannon. **Quick Shield** (Triggered, Ranged 10) — target gains 15 temporary Stamina when hit by a save-ends effect; the amount decreases by 3 per use. Keeps allies above the Valiar Might threshold (not bleeding/weakened/winded) for longer. **Multivok Maintenance** (servoks within 2 regain 15 Stamina/turn) + **Valiar Might** + **Crafted to Perfection.** Counterplay: kill the chief first to collapse the Command / Quick Shield / Maintenance web; Targeting Beam's damage bypasses reduction, so resistance builds don't help; spread out so Pneumatic Punch can't hit two targets.

## Servok

### Servok Builder — elite

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 9, elite, construct, servok, soulless, valok
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 284

**Flavor:** Size 3, Stamina 240, Speed 5, Stability 8, Free Strike 10. A construction valok still clearing ground for an unfinished steel dwarf project.

**Tactics:** **Wrecking Ball** (Signature, Ranged 5, 3-cube Area) — Agility OR Intuition test: 15 damage + push 5 + prone on tier 1, 12 + push 3 on tier 2, 8 on tier 3. The zone-clearing bomb (note: Agility *or* Intuition, so high-Int heroes get a second save option). **Construction Arm** (Melee 3) — damage + grab on tier 2/3 + **tier 3 adds M<4 vertical push 5** — grab a hero and slam them up into the ceiling. **Lay the Foundation** (3 Malice, 6×3 line within 1) — wet concrete area, difficult terrain, and enemies starting their turn in it make a Might test: tier 1 restrained EoT, tier 2 slowed EoT. **Build Wall** (Maneuver, 6-wall within 3) — creates a concrete wall; can consume adjacent wet-concrete squares for 2 extra wall squares each. The map-rewriter — builders actively construct terrain mid-fight. **Sputter** (1 Malice, Free triggered, Melee 3) — when damaged, encases the attacker in wet concrete with a restrained save-ends (and the concrete square persists). **Servok Siege Machine** + **Valiar Might** + **Crafted to Perfection.** Counterplay: apply bleeding/weakened/winded before committing damage; use abilities that don't roll power (auto-damage bypasses Valiar Might); destroy wet-concrete squares before Build Wall harvests them; stay outside Wrecking Ball's 5-square range.

### Servok Miner — elite

**Role:** controller
**Tags:** level 9, elite, construct, servok, soulless, valok
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 285
**Movement:** Burrow, Climb.

**Flavor:** Size 2, Stamina 200, Speed 5, Stability 6, Free Strike 9. A tunneler that bored out the legendary steel dwarf mines — still looking for ore.

**Tactics:** **Drill Press** (Signature, Melee 2, two targets) — damage + restrained save-ends on M<2/<3 and prone on tier 2/3. **Effect:** *in suitably soft ground, a restrained target is entrenched in a 1-square-deep hole.* Restrained becomes literally buried — harder to extract, potentially narrative permanence. **Unload Rocks** (3 Malice, 4-cube within 1) — Agility test; tier 1 14 + slide 4 + *miner's allies have concealment from the target save-ends*. **Break Ground** (5 Malice, 20×1 line within 1, Maneuver) — 5-square-deep fissure; allies can shift out of the area; enemies make Agility tests: tier 1 fall into fissure + prone + can't stand EoT, tier 2 hang at edge prone, tier 3 shift out. The map-destruction button — 20-square canyon through the battlefield, literally opening the ground under the party. **Miner Inconvenience** (2 Malice, Triggered when struck) — dust billows 2-burst around the miner's starting space until end of round; while in the area the miner *ignores nondamaging effects of any strike against them*. Damage still lands, but riders and conditions from that round's strikes fizzle. **Valiar Tunneler:** burrows through stone *and metal* — including valiar. Creates stable Size-2 tunnels. **Servok Siege Machine** + **Valiar Might** + **Crafted to Perfection.** Counterplay: high-Agility builds weather Break Ground and Unload Rocks; melee characters must close before the miner burrows off the map; target with damage, not conditions, while Miner Inconvenience is active.

### Servok War Engine — elite

**Role:** brute
**Tags:** level 10, elite, construct, servok, soulless, valok
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 285

**Flavor:** Size 3, Stamina 260, Speed 5, Stability 8, Free Strike 10. The siege-grade valok. Bladed rake on the front, Prismacore Cannon mounted on top. Many went to war and never came home.

**Tactics:** **Blade Rake** (Signature, Melee 3, two targets) — damage + pull 3 on tier 2, pull 6 on tier 3. Drag the frontline heroes straight onto the rake. **Prismacore Cannon** (Ranged 20) — massive damage (22/29/34) + dazed save-ends on I<4/I<5. **Damage can't be reduced in any way.** **Can't be used again until next round** — the "once per round" finisher. Drop on the party's biggest-damage hero. **Destructive Rollout** (Maneuver) — move in a straight line, destroying mundane Size-1 objects/walls; the first creature whose space the engine crosses *chooses* fall prone or take 10 damage. The charge/trample maneuver; combine with Blade Rake's pull to drag targets into the next-turn rollout lane. **Burning Oil** (3 Malice, 20×1 line within 1, Maneuver) — Agility test; fire damage + burning save-ends; **area is burning until end of encounter** — difficult terrain and 3 fire per square moved. The persistent-zone fire spray. **Quick Blast** (1 Malice, Free triggered, Ranged 20) — when damaged, damage back + push 2/5/8; **damage can't be reduced.** The punishing counter-shot. **Servok Siege Machine** + **Valiar Might** + **Crafted to Perfection.** Counterplay: bleeding/weakened/winded collapses Valiar Might and opens the 260-Stamina health bar to real damage; avoid the straight lines that enable Destructive Rollout and Burning Oil; burst the engine between Prismacore Cannon cooldowns.

## Encounter notes

- **No minion, horde, leader, or solo tier.** Every valok in the source pages is an elite. Valok encounters build from mixing 2–4 elites — a bodyguard plus two servoks plus a chief is a canonical setup. No cheap minion chaff means every valok is a Valiar-Might wall.
- **Valiar Might is the whole puzzle.** The fight is a two-stage game: (1) apply bleeding, weakened, or winded to each valok to unlock real damage; (2) then actually damage them. A party with no bleed/weaken tools will chip tier-1 damage all day; a party with even one bleed source ends the encounter in reasonable time. Plan accordingly.
- **Siege encounters.** Servok Siege Machine (+15 damage vs objects, ignores difficult terrain) means these work brilliantly as the back half of a stronghold-defense encounter — the party protects walls while war engines and builders actively demolish them.
- **Command phrase hooks.** Pre-fight narrative leverage: a lost steel dwarf tablet, a Rallarian-speaker, or a surviving activation rod can flip a valok to inert before initiative. This is the intended answer to "the party can't win a straight fight against four elites."
