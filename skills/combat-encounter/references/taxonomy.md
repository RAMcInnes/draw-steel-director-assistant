# Enemy keywords

Sourced from Draw Steel creature types. The keyword scopes filename lookup under `references/enemies/<category>.md`.

- **Abyssal** — creatures from the Abyssal Wasteland (demons, gnolls); hunger for mortal souls. Chaotic, predatory.
- **Accursed** — creatures under powerful supernatural curses (medusas, werewolves); the curse changes their essential nature.
- **Animal** — ordinary beasts (bears, wolves, big spiders); animal-level sapience, natural defenses only.
- **Beast** — animal-level sapience plus supernatural traits (basilisks, chimeras); no society, but dangerous powers.
- **Construct** — manufactured creatures animated by magic or psionics (ashen hoarder, valok); sapience depends on the creator. Don't need to eat, drink, sleep, or breathe.
- **Dragon** — giant reptilian creatures with breath weapons, wings, claws, jaws; includes draconian kin.
- **Elemental** — creatures from Quintessence, the Manifold of Elements (crux of fire, meteor dragons); raw elemental power or partial elemental heritage.
- **Fey** — creatures from Arcadia, a manifold of nature and magic; innate connection to nature, magic, or both. All elves trace here.
- **Giant** — humanoid-shaped but much larger (ogres, trolls, fire/frost/hill/stone giants); no relation to most humanoids except the hakaan.
- **Horror** — alien creatures, unnatural on most worlds (overminds, voiceless talkers); usually potent psionic abilities.
- **Humanoid** — size 1 creatures with human-like limb arrangement and sapience (dwarves, time raiders); form societies.
- **Infernal** — creatures from the Seven Cities of Hell (devils, hobgoblins); plan to tempt mortals into soul contracts.
- **Ooze** — semisolid masses of moisture and malice (gummy brick); form in dark, damp environments; loose puddles to hardened shapes.
- **Plant** — creatures made of vegetation (shambling mound); can move and interact unlike plant objects.
- **Swarm** — many creatures moving and acting as one (swarm of spiders, swarm of minotaurs).
- **Undead** — reanimated flesh and spirits of the dead (ghosts, zombies); sapience depends on the creator. Don't need to eat, drink, sleep, or breathe.

# Enemy organization

Sourced from Draw Steel creature organization. The shapes (minion / horde / platoon / elite / leader / solo) drive encounter-building decisions about numbers, action economy, and which monsters anchor a roster.

- **Minion** — weakest tier; build encounters with them four at a time. Heroes are outnumbered and cut through minion fields. Some minions die before they can act.
- **Horde** — hardier than minions but still need numbers; outnumber heroes roughly 2:1 in an all-horde encounter. Fragile compared to platoon+; double or triple stat blocks when a specific horde creature is key.
- **Platoon** — well-rounded, self-sufficient monsters; roughly one platoon creature per hero in an all-platoon encounter. Often fight alongside minions plus an elite or two.
- **Elite** — functional opposite of minions; a single elite can handle about two heroes of the same level. Supports other organizations or bands with other elites.
- **Leader** — powerful monster who buffs allies, grants extra actions, and uses villain actions. Stands toe-to-toe with two or more same-level heroes. Typically one per encounter. Leaders have no additional creature role.
- **Solo** — an encounter on their own; stat blocks include solo-specific rules. A solo can typically stand against six same-level heroes. Solos have no additional creature role.

# Enemy roles

Sourced from Draw Steel creature roles. Roles are descriptive — most don't carry special rules, they just help build encounters and use creatures effectively.

- **Ambusher** — melee warriors who can slip past beefier heroes to reach squishier back-line targets.
- **Artillery** — best at range; can use their strongest abilities at great distance.
- **Brute** — high Stamina, high damage. Hard to ignore, hard to disengage from; can push enemies around.
- **Controller** — changes the battlefield with magic or psionics; repositions foes and alters terrain. Often squishy and needs protection.
- **Defender** — tough, takes a lot of damage, forces enemies to attack them instead of squishier targets. Often protects controllers and hexers.
- **Harrier** — mobile warriors specializing in hit-and-run; traits maximize battlefield positioning.
- **Hexer** — debuffer using conditions and other effects. Generally squishy; relies on defenders.
- **Mount** — mobile creature meant to be ridden; makes the rider more dangerous.
- **Support** — aids allies with buffs, healing, movement, or additional action options.

# Enemy categories

Sourced from Draw Steel monsters. Each of these "categories" will include monsters that have "keyword", "organizations", and "roles" as part of each's information.

- [Angulotls](enemies/angulotls.md) - Diminutive, brightly colored, poisonous amphibious frog folk of freshwater swamps, rivers, and rainforests.
- [Arixx](enemies/arixx.md) - Horse-sized chitinous burrowing insectoid beasts with hooked talons, serrated mandibles, and caustic spittle.
- [Ashen Hoarder](enemies/ashen-hoarder.md) - Hulking four-legged construct built from magically reinforced humanoid bones, with clawed arms and corpse-impaling back spikes.
- [Basilisks](enemies/basilisks.md) - Eight-legged, rough-scaled beasts the size of a bear, with poison-dripping teeth and petrifying eye beams.
- [Bredbeddle](enemies/bredbeddle.md) - Ogre-sized headless giants wielding the axes that beheaded them, able to magically adopt the forms of victims they behead.
- [Bugbears](enemies/bugbears.md) - Tall, hairy, silent-footed goblinoids descended from early goblins twisted by fey magic in the wode.
- [Chimera](enemies/chimera.md) - Size-2 winged beast with a lion head, a ram head, a fire-breathing dragon head, massive claws, and a draconic tail — all driven by a single mind.
- [Demons](enemies/demons.md) - Creatures of incarnate hate spawned in the Abyssal Wasteland, ranging from rat-like pitlings up through chorogaunts, soulraker hivequeens, and the demon king Aurumvas.
- [Devils](enemies/devils.md) - Azure- and crimson-skinned infernal humanoids marked by horns, tails, hooves, and wings, native to the Seven Cities of Hell.
- [Draconians](enemies/draconians.md) - Dragon-scaled winged humanoids (wyrmwights) created by the Dracogenesis ritual, each with an elemental affinity tied to their scales.
- [Dragons](enemies/dragons.md) - Crystallized elemental spirits shaped by anguish — thorn from famine and drought, gloom from terror and nightmares, crucible from creative failure and jealousy, omen from bound restless souls, meteor from complacent civilizations grown too comfortable; killing the dragon doesn't address the underlying condition, so another will take its place in time.
- [Dwarves](enemies/dwarves.md) - Stone-fleshed size-1 humanoids, physically denser than humans, who field pneumatic steam-powered weapons and valok-derived mechanized war walkers.
- [Elementals](enemies/elementals.md) - Mercurial spirits of air, earth, fire, and water drawn from Quintessence — cruxes of fire, essences of storms and tides, forces of earth, and fields of growth.
- [Elves, High](enemies/elves-high.md) - Tall, poised, glamour-shifting sorcerer-librarians who bond to elementals and summon soot crows, brambleguards, ceramic horses, and elemental motes to fight alongside them.
- [Elves, Shadow](enemies/elves-shadow.md) - Children of the Twilight Celestials, native to the dying parasitic manifold of Equinox — a world of perpetual twilight with no true day or night; every shadow elf is incompatible with sunlight, and the Shadow Queen leads their invasion of Orden to rewrite its topography into a new home.
- [Elves, Wode](enemies/elves-wode.md) - Forest-dwelling fey elves cloaked in natural glamour, who fight from cover and climb to height in the dense wilds they call home; wodeworkers sing moose-like wodenelg mounts into existence from living wood.
- [Fossil Cryptic](enemies/fossil-cryptic.md) - Primal elemental spirit inhabiting a cluster of ancient fossils atop a churning column of rock and metal — guardian of places connected to Quintessence.
- [Giants](enemies/giants.md) - Size-4 humanoids adapted to extreme habitats — fire giants from volcanic calderas, frost giants from arctic tundra, hill giants of the plains, and stone giants of mountain peaks, each embodying the emotion of their terrain.
- [Gnolls](enemies/gnolls.md) - Hyena-faced fiends born from abyssal hyenas that lapped the ichor of a dying demon prince; they hunt in war bands fueled by chained Cackletongue maneuvers and a leader (the carnage) that respawns from any living gnoll on the map.
- [Goblins](enemies/goblins.md) - Size-1 climbing humanoids (*rogabrin*, "more of us") who fight as overwhelming mobile swarms; nearly every goblin has the Crafty trait — no opportunity attacks from movement — and they ride skitterlings, worgs, and blade-legged war spiders into battle.
- [Griffons](enemies/griffons.md) - Eagle-headed, lion-bodied apex flying predators that nest in unreachable aeries and hunt the skies over hundreds of leagues; hand-raised from eggs they become loyal mounts, but wild griffons kill trespassers on sight.
- [Hags](enemies/hags.md) - Former mortal spellcasters who bargained with evil archfey and were twisted into clawed, ageless crones who hide behind doting grandmotherly disguises; they collect curses, explosive pastries, and whimsical trinkets in hidden huts deep in the wode.
- [Hobgoblins](enemies/hobgoblins.md) - Tusked, horned infernal goblinoids descended from an ancient pact with a fiendish power; they settle hot climes, honor their word, and fight as drilled synergized war-bands of fire- and corruption-wielding soldiers, mages, and the occasional summoned slaughter demon.
- [Humans](enemies/humans.md) - The most widespread humanoid species, found as everything from city guards and mercenaries to cultists, bandit chiefs, and armored blackguards; short-lived and ambitious, they take on every role a villain or faction might need.
- [Kingfissure Worm](enemies/kingfissure-worm.md) - Mountain-sized burrowing ambush beasts that terraform the land into tunnels and crevasses, lying in wait underground to sense surface vibrations and dragging prey down through cracks with long serpentine tongues into a gut spacious enough that swallowed communities have been known to live there.
- [Kobolds](enemies/kobolds.md) - Size-1 draconic humanoids organized into disciplined Roman-style legions of shielded infantry, trap-rigging engineers, and standard-bearers; legions that settle in a dragon's domain gradually take on the dragon's color and affinity, and every kobold fights as part of an interlocking shield formation rather than alone.
- [Lich](enemies/lich.md) - Mortal mages who tore out their own hearts (and the hearts of those they loved) to lock their souls into an external *soulstone*, becoming skeletal undead schemers who cannot truly die as long as the stone survives; they build armies of undead, cultists, and mercenaries, and usually scatter their lairs across entire worlds to keep the soulstone hidden.
- [Lightbenders](enemies/lightbenders.md) - Lion-like daylight predators with iridescent manes and crystal-tipped tails whose fur refracts the light around them; they prowl sunlit wildernesses, leaving visual afterimages and muted sound trails as they teleport between shadows to stalk their prey.
- [Lizardfolk](enemies/lizardfolk.md) - Reptilian humanoids of wetlands and marsh — tropical coasts, bogs, and any humid ruin will do — who tend toward honorable trade and craftsmanship until an outsider takes more than they were offered, at which point the tribe fights as a coordinated amphibious war party that drops its tails when cornered and regrows them in hidden water pits.
- [Manticore](enemies/manticore.md) - Lion-bodied, dragon-winged, scorpion-tailed flying predators with an unsettling humanoid face borrowed from whichever ancestry dominates the region of their birth; they mimic regional voices to lure travelers into ambush and drag their kills back to cave lairs strewn with the belongings of earlier victims.
- [Medusa](enemies/medusa.md) - Mortals cursed by a god into reptilian-skinned, serpent-haired accursed humanoids with a petrifying gaze, often driven into reclusive lairs by the myths their cursing deity spreads about them; their homes tend to be grotesque stone gardens strewn with the statues of previous would-be slayers.
- [Minotaurs](enemies/minotaurs.md) - Size-2 bull-headed accursed humanoids descended from a human rebel cursed by the Beast Lords of Kham, prone to fits of charging rage but equally capable of deep loyalty; they prefer labyrinthine terrain — canyons, ruins, twisting dungeons — where their innate navigation sense makes them impossible to ambush or lose.
- [Ogres](enemies/ogres.md) - Massive bull-necked giant-kin who feel every emotion at its extreme and indulge every impulse — easily bullied into service by wealthy kin or evil powers, occasionally the devoted protectors of a chosen people, and descended from the one-eyed cyclopes who still scavenge remote canyons and valleys.
- [Olothec](enemies/olothec.md) - Ancient alien horrors older than almost anything in the timescape — writhing shrouds of tooth-and-eye-tipped tentacles coated in psionic slime, who hate the mundane world and reshape their victims' flesh into primordial oozing thralls; they lair in drowned sea-tunnels and ruin undercrofts and war eternally against the voiceless talkers.
- [Orcs](enemies/orcs.md) - Tusked, relentlessly tough humanoids whose veins glow with "blood fire" when pushed to their limit; they live across every climate and culture of the world and field warbands with burrowing pig-mohler companions and giant bipedal scyza war-lizards mounted by orc platoons.
- [Radenwights](enemies/radenwights.md) - Small, boldly acrobatic ratfolk who live in tight-knit underground warrens called *meddles* beneath cities, raising their children on bravery, fair play, and unbreakable comradery; their magic expresses through flutes and panpipes, and their most famous maestro leads city-wide heists he treats like musical compositions.
- [Shambling Mound](enemies/shambling-mound.md) - Wandering mass of rotten vegetation, writhing vines, and rich soil that oozes out of swamps, marshes, and rainforests where evil or green-magic mages have been working; hungers for any organic matter it can drag into its sack-like body, secretes region-specific toxins, and hides a small magic crux at its core.
- [Time Raiders](enemies/time-raiders.md) - Four-armed, six-limbed space-punk pirates (kuran'zoi in their own language) who ply the Sea of Stars raiding empire frigates and leaving just enough of the crew alive to bring the ship back next year; rebelled from their synlirii creators thousands of years ago and don't age while in the Sea of Stars, which leaves their speech peppered with misplaced tenses and references to events from before or yet to come.
- [Trolls](enemies/trolls.md) - Tall, gangly, mottled-green predators driven by a fathomless hunger that leaves only destruction in their wake; they hunt in violent rampages, rest in torpor between sprees, and regrow from torn-off pieces so readily that the detached limbs sprout their own arms and legs and tumble after them.
- [Undead](enemies/undead.md) - Living things who now walk the land after death in defiance of the natural order — from mindless zombie and skeleton soldiers under a necromancer's control, through ghouls, wraiths, mummies, and vampires, up to liches; some were raised by dark rituals, some rose from bitter death on cursed ground, and a few chose to become something beyond mortal.
- [Valok](enemies/valok.md) - Autonomous truemetal-and-prismacore constructs built by the long-vanished steel dwarves, still running the commands they were given millennia ago in the ruins their makers left behind; humaniform multivok supervisors and bodyguards coordinate wagon-sized servok laborers, miners, and war engines, all nearly indestructible and powered without magic.
- [Voiceless Talkers](enemies/voiceless-talkers.md) - Cephalopod-descended psionic horrors (the synlirii) who dwell in the World Below and style themselves the future masters of the timescape; they engineer their servants from stolen flesh — four-armed hulking brains, body-jacking mindkillers, drained-of-will graywarpers — and hate the "barking ones" (humanoids) second only to the olothec they fear gave them their powers.
- [War Dogs](enemies/war-dogs.md) - Ajax the Iron Saint's patchwork soldiers — stitched and welded from humanoid body parts harvested by the Body Banks, kept in line by explosive loyalty collars around their necks, and fielded as disposable shock troops behind shrikegun artillery, fuse-iron specialists, and legendary named commanders; they aren't undead, but their souls were destroyed in the making, and they fight fanatically for a tyrant who can rebuild them the next morning.
- [Werewolf](enemies/werewolf.md) - Ordinary humanoids twisted by a Primordial Chaos curse into red-eyed shapeshifters driven to rend and tear; a single bite passes the curse on, and the worst among them are brokered nobles and rulers who sought the change out through devil-pacts to terrorize their own people.
- [Wyverns](enemies/wyverns.md) - Leathery-hided flying predators superficially resembling dragons but actually distant relatives of the terror lizards of Ix; solitary perch-hunters who track prey by the scent of fear and strike with long acid-tipped tail stingers from rotting trees and jagged mountain peaks, and become most dangerous of all during hatching season when mated pairs aggressively hunt anything that moves to feed their young.
