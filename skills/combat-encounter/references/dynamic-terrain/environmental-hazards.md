# Environmental Hazards

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 332–335. Reference only — full stat blocks live in the book.*

Environmental hazards are natural elements that creatures — typically those defending a location — have channeled, cultivated, or otherwise placed to gain an edge in an encounter. Most have a per-square Stamina and occupy one or more squares of difficult terrain; most activate when a creature enters without shifting, or starts their turn in the area. They scale by occupying more squares and can be upgraded for extra EV.

See `dynamic-terrain.md` for the shared stat block format (EV, Stamina, Size, Direction, Deactivate, Activate, Effect, Upgrades, Hidden, Allied Awareness).

## Angry Beehive

**Tags:** Level 2 Hazard Hexer, EV 2
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 332

**Flavor:** A bee colony whose residents swarm on the slightest provocation. Small (1S), fragile, and impossible to deactivate without unleashing it.

**Tactics:** Damage it *or* walk adjacent and it releases a bee swarm that grows a square larger each round and preferentially chases creatures. Use as a punisher for melee approach lanes and as a moving area hazard that forces reposition. Upgrades: **Concealed Hive** hides the hive until triggered; **Killer Bees** makes the swarm more aggressive and damaging.

## Brambles

**Tags:** Level 1 Hazard Defender, EV 1 per 10 × 10 thicket
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 332

**Flavor:** A thorn-tipped vine thicket, close-growing and hostile to passage. Difficult terrain per square; each square destroyed individually.

**Tactics:** Passive damage for any creature that enters without shifting — punishes careless movement, not teleporters. Good for shaping lanes around a defender or funneling heroes into kill zones. Upgrade: **Poisonous Thorns** adds bleeding (save ends) on any damage taken.

## Corrosive Pool

**Tags:** Level 2 Hazard Hexer, EV 3 per 10 × 10 pool
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 333

**Flavor:** A shallow pool bubbling with acid or some other caustic liquid. Difficult terrain, high Stamina, mostly immune to everything except cold and fire.

**Tactics:** Acid damage on entry or turn-start, but the real use is **Explosive Reaction** — any fire damage to the pool detonates it in a 3-burst for heavy fire damage and burning, consuming the liquid. Pairs with allies throwing torches (see Allied Awareness) or a hero's own fire attacks, creating a terrain trap where forced-movement into the pool plus a fire trigger becomes a finisher. Creatures with acid weakness suffer extra.

## Frozen Pond

**Tags:** Level 1 Hazard Hexer, EV 1 per 10 × 10 pond
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 333

**Flavor:** A shallow, frozen patch of water — ice thick enough not to break, but slick enough to send charging creatures sprawling.

**Tactics:** **Slippery Surface** pushes the triggering creature in the direction they were moving and can leave them prone — excellent for amplifying ally forced movement, since remaining push distance stacks into the pond's own shove. Destroyed squares turn to shallow icy water. Upgrade: **Thin Ice** replaces the effect with **Icy Water** — entering or falling prone destroys the square and subjects the creature to cold damage and slowed/restrained, with the possibility of suffocation.

## Lava

**Tags:** Level 3 Hazard Hexer, EV 4 per 10 × 10 patch
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 334

**Flavor:** A blistering patch of molten rock welling up from the ground. Threatens anyone who gets close — not just those who step in.

**Tactics:** **Liquid Hot Magma** triggers on entering, starting the turn in it, *or starting the turn adjacent* — a rare "zone of control" hazard. Damage scales high at higher tiers; burning lingers. Use to gate approaches, punish melee clustering, and threaten fliers forced low. Upgrade: **Magma Flow** adds one adjacent lava square per round, closing paths over time.

## Quicksand

**Tags:** Level 3 Hazard Hexer, EV 3 per 10 × 10 patch
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 334

**Flavor:** A patch of sand that turns out to be water-saturated slurry, waiting to draw creatures down to their doom. No Stamina — it can't be destroyed, only avoided.

**Tactics:** **Grasping Depths** slows or restrains — a creature restrained this way while starting their turn in the sand is **suffocating**, making quicksand one of the few hazards that can kill on its own timer. Shifting in makes the ability weaker (bane); shoving a creature in is the nasty play. Inherently **Hidden** until triggered or detected.

## Toxic Plants

**Tags:** Level 2 Hazard Hexer, EV 2 per 10 × 10 field
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 335

**Flavor:** Colorful mushrooms or lovely flowering plants that release a cloud of sleep spores when disturbed. Difficult terrain; each square destroyed individually.

**Tactics:** **Sleep Spores** dazes creatures who start their turn in the field or enter a square without shifting; while dazed this way, ending their turn in the field drops them prone and they can't stand — a soft lock that stacks with ally ranged pressure. Upgrades: **Poisonous Spores** adds ongoing poison on any dazed target; **Carnivorous Plants** deals acid to creatures who start prone in the area, turning the soft lock into a kill box.
