# Mechanisms

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 339–342. Reference only — full stat blocks live in the book.*

Mechanisms represent any number of intricate devices that can complicate a battle. Unlike more straightforward traps, a mechanism is often **linked** to another triggering mechanism — the trigger (pressure plate, switch) activates the effect (ram, portcullis, dart trap), letting the GM compose layered contraptions. Triggers are Support roles; effects are typically Ambushers or Defenders.

See `dynamic-terrain.md` for the shared stat block format (EV, Stamina, Size, Direction, Deactivate, Activate, Effect, Upgrades, Hidden, Allied Awareness).

## Column of Blades

**Tags:** Level 3 Fortification Defender, EV 3
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 339

**Flavor:** A spinning wooden column studded with blades, 1L. No test-based deactivation — it must be destroyed outright (Stamina 5).

**Tactics:** Triggers on any creature moving *adjacent* (Melee 1 reach), dealing heavy damage + bleeding (save ends). Fire-and-forget area denial that punishes melee heroes who want to close. **Allied Awareness** lets allies shift past untriggered and invites an **Intuition test** for observers to imitate — succeed and you slip by, fail and the column swings with an edge. Upgrades: **Stone/Metal Column** add Stamina; **Concealed** hides the blades until triggered; **Spiked Flails (+4 EV)** swaps Spinning Blades for **Whirling Flails** — Melee 2 reach, heavier damage, dazed instead of bleeding. A premier "don't stand near me" guardian in tight halls.

## Dart Trap

**Tags:** Level 1 Trap Ambusher, EV 1
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 339

**Flavor:** A concealed dart thrower aimed in a **fixed direction**, Ranged 5. Hidden until triggered or detected.

**Tactics:** Needs a **linked trigger** (pressure plate, switch, tripwire) to fire. Low single-target damage on its own, but cheap enough to paper a corridor with them and punish bunched heroes. Upgrades transform it: **Poison Darts (+2 EV)** add ongoing poison; **Large Darts (+1 EV)** add forced push (1/2/3 squares by tier) — pair with a pit or hazard behind the hero's line; **Gatling Darts (+4 EV)** converts the ability to a 5×1 line Area attack with bonus damage, becoming a serious threat on its own.

## Pillar

**Tags:** Level 2 Hazard Hexer, EV 3
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 340

**Flavor:** A stone column, one square that can't be moved through, rigged to topple in a preset **direction**. No Agility-test deactivation — kill the trigger or destroy the pillar (Stamina 6).

**Tactics:** On trigger, sweeps a 4×1 line with heavy damage and restrains (save ends); the toppled area becomes difficult terrain. Best positioned along a natural approach lane, with its fall angle pre-planned to catch a cluster. Upgrades: **Metal Pillar (+1 EV)** adds Stamina and +1d6 damage; **Multiple Pillars (+3 EV each)** represent a larger falling structure (wall, tower) — all must be destroyed to stop the topple if destruction is the trigger. Combine with a pressure plate or switch to time the fall on the party's entry.

## Portcullis

**Tags:** Level 3 Trap Ambusher, EV 4
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 340

**Flavor:** A heavy gate hidden in a ceiling, dropping to seal a corridor. Stamina 9 per square, filling 2×1 up to 4×2. Hidden until triggered or detected.

**Tactics:** On trigger, drops into its footprint — the area beneath the gate — dealing damage, sliding, and restraining targets caught in the fall; a slid target ends up on one side or the other (choose randomly). Primary use is **splitting the party** — drop it between the front line and the squishies, then have the monsters converge on the isolated half. Manual reset, so it's a one-shot per fight unless the encounter has minions to reset it. Deactivation is an Agility test that can drop the gate on a bad roll.

## Pressure Plate

**Tags:** Level 1 Trigger Support, EV 2
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 341

**Flavor:** A hidden floor plate calibrated to a size threshold. No Stamina, any area up to 4×4. Auto-resets — triggers repeatedly.

**Tactics:** The workhorse trigger. Links to another mechanism (dart trap, ram, portcullis, pillar) and fires it when a qualifying creature enters its area. Because it auto-resets, every creature that crosses eats the effect — devastating against marching order. Deactivation is an Agility test; failure still springs the trap. Upgrade: **Tripwire (−1 EV)** swaps the plate for a tripwire that triggers once and requires manual reset; easy Intuition test to spot. Hidden by default.

## Pulley

**Tags:** Level 1 Trigger Support, EV 1
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 341

**Flavor:** A counterweighted rope-and-pulley rig that ferries a single creature to the top of a wall, scaffold, or tower. 1S, Stamina 1 — trivially destroyed.

**Tactics:** Not a damage mechanism — it's vertical movement. Defenders can yo-yo between battlements and ground, or drop reinforcements from upper floors mid-fight. **Climbable** lets a creature skip the ride via an easy Agility test. Upgrade: **Looped Chain (+1 EV)** auto-resets, enabling repeated use and fast rotation of defenders up and down the structure. Use to turn a single encounter space into a vertical fight with real tempo shifts.

## Ram

**Tags:** Level 2 Trap Ambusher, EV 3
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 342

**Flavor:** A heavy wooden ram that drops or swings through a defined path; area can't be moved through. Typical footprint 1×3 or 2×2, with a defined **front**. Stamina 3 per square.

**Tactics:** On trigger, sweeps everything in its path — damage scales from 3/slide 1 to 9/push 5 by tier, and a slid target lands on one side or the other (choose randomly), splitting formations. Manual reset out of the box, but **Repeating (+1 EV)** and **Rapid Repeating (+3 EV)** make it fire every round or every turn — the difference between a one-shot and a persistent hazard that reshapes the battlefield each initiative. **Stone (+1 EV)** / **Metal (+2 EV)** upgrade material and damage; **Multiple Rams (+3 EV each)** chain them into a tumbling-log gauntlet. Pair with a pressure plate at the entry square for a devastating opener. Hidden until triggered or detected.

## Switch

**Tags:** Level 1 Trigger Support, EV 1
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 342

**Flavor:** A lever, button, or dial in any surface. 1T, Stamina 3. Auto-resets.

**Tactics:** Manual trigger — a creature adjacent uses a maneuver to activate whatever it's linked to. Cheapest way to hand a defender a big red button: pull the switch to drop the portcullis, swing the ram, or fire the dart wall on the party's position. Deactivation is an Agility test; failure pulls the switch. Upgrade: **Concealed (+1 EV)** hides the switch, letting an ambusher flip it from a hidey-hole or behind a pillar without the party knowing the trigger exists.
