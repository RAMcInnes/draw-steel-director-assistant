# Fieldworks

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 335–338. Reference only — full stat blocks live in the book.*

Fieldworks represent temporary military fortifications meant to give defenders an edge in an encounter. Most are Fortifications (Defender or Ambusher roles) or Traps (Ambusher role); they shape lanes, punish approach, and reward pre-positioned allies via **Allied Awareness**.

See `dynamic-terrain.md` for the shared stat block format (EV, Stamina, Size, Direction, Deactivate, Activate, Effect, Upgrades, Hidden, Allied Awareness).

---

## Archer's Stakes

**Tags:** Level 1 Fortification Defender, EV 2
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 335

**Flavor:** Sharpened wooden stakes driven point-out in a 4×1 line to break charges. Difficult terrain with a defined **front**; each square destroyed individually.

**Tactics:** Flat 2 damage per square entered from the front, +3 more if force-moved in — pairs perfectly with a shover behind the line. **Allied Awareness** lets defenders ignore the difficult terrain and gain cover while standing in it, so park archers inside the stakes and dare the heroes to close. Upgrades: **Poison** tacks on recurring 1d6 poison; **Sticky Stakes** turns the line into a slow/restrain zone that holds targets in the damage field.

## Bear Trap

**Tags:** Level 1 Trap Ambusher, EV 2
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 336

**Flavor:** Spring-loaded steel jaws, single-square (1S), calibrated to a size threshold. Hidden until triggered or detected.

**Tactics:** Ends the triggering creature's movement and slows them (save ends), stranding them mid-approach where allies can converge. Deactivation is an Agility test — a bad roll springs the trap on the would-be defuser. Upgrade: **Chain** upgrades slowed into restrained, turning the trap into an anchor. Best placed in chokepoints and on obvious approach lanes; combine with ranged attackers for a kill pocket.

## Flammable Oil

**Tags:** Level 1 Trap Ambusher, EV 2 per 10 × 10 patch
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 336

**Flavor:** A patch of oil or pitch waiting for a spark. No Stamina — it can't be destroyed, only ignited, avoided, or rendered safe.

**Tactics:** Inert until a heat source arrives: fire damage in a square, or a creature entering/starting in burning oil. Deals 3 fire + burning (save ends), with 1d6 on each burning turn. **Allied Awareness** arms allies with torches they can hurl up to 5 squares — the real play is baiting heroes onto the patch, then lighting it on an ally's turn. Upgrade: **Concealed Oil** hides the patch until ignition.

## Hidey-Hole

**Tags:** Level 1 Fortification Ambusher, EV 1
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 337

**Flavor:** A cavity in floor, wall, or ceiling — just big enough to conceal a threat. No Stamina; defeated only via Might test.

**Tactics:** Lets an ambusher start the encounter hidden or re-hide at turn's end, free triggered. The Might-test deactivation can restrain the tester on a poor roll, so heroes can't safely collapse them mid-fight. Upgrade: **Network (+1 EV per hole)** chains multiple hidey-holes into a tunnel system — familiar creatures teleport hole-to-hole using straight-line distance, making for pop-up attackers who can't be pinned down.

## Pavise Shield

**Tags:** Level 1 Fortification Defender, EV 1
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 337

**Flavor:** A reinforced metal shield planted in the ground; mobile once grabbed. Stamina 9, Size 1M.

**Tactics:** Grants cover and halves damage from any ability passing through it (the shield absorbs the other half). The controlling creature can reposition it, but at half speed — trading mobility for defense. A hero can wrestle it free via a Might test, but a bad roll eats an opportunity attack. Use as a pocket of hard cover for ranged defenders; plan reposition windows to deny heroes a clean angle.

## Snare Trap

**Tags:** Level 1 Trap Ambusher, EV 1
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 338

**Flavor:** A rope noose rigged to hoist its target into the air. 1S, single-use, Hidden until triggered or detected.

**Tactics:** Ends movement and restrains (save ends); on a hit, the target is vertical-pulled 2 squares and left dangling — isolated from allies, trivial to shoot. Low damage but high control. Deactivation is an Agility test with a springing failure. Upgrade: **Net Trap (+1 EV)** swaps the snare for a 3×3 net that catches multiple targets at once; any one creature's save frees everyone, so time the spring for a cluster.

## Spike Trap

**Tags:** Level 2 Trap Ambusher, EV 3
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 338

**Flavor:** A camouflaged pit lined with spikes, typical footprint 2×2, 2 squares deep. Hidden until triggered or detected.

**Tactics:** Area trap — the first creature through catches damage, falls prone at the bottom of the pit, and at tier 3 is also restrained (save ends). Ends movement on entry, wasting the hero's action economy and dropping them below the fight line where their reach and LoS collapse. Deactivation is an Agility test that can drop the would-be defuser into the pit. Great for funnels and doorways where heroes can't easily route around.
