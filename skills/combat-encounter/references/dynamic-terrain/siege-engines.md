# Siege Engines

*Source: Draw Steel Monsters, pp. 344–347. Reference only — full stat blocks live in the book.*

Siege engines are powerful weapons that require a **team** of creatures to operate and move. Most don't act on their own — a creature **adjacent** spends a main action to fire, reload, aim (**Spot**), or push the engine into a new firing position (**Move**). Multiple minions in a squad can share the load, with different crew members firing, reloading, and spotting in the same round. Heavy Stamina pools mean siege engines absorb punishment; the real counterplay is killing the crew.

See `dynamic-terrain.md` for the shared stat block format (EV, Stamina, Size, Direction, Deactivate, Activate, Effect, Upgrades, Hidden, Allied Awareness).

## Arrow Launcher

**Tags:** Level 2 Siege Engine Artillery, EV 8
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 344

**Flavor:** A wooden cart rigged with alchemical rockets, firing up to a hundred arrows in one volley. Stamina 30, Size 1L.

**Tactics:** **Arrow Storm** fires a 5-cube within 20 — a massive area wipe aimed at tight formations; reload-gated, so the crew rotates shooter/reloader/spotter. **Spot** gives the next volley an edge and +10 range, turning a single minion's turn into a force multiplier. Upgrades: **Flaming Arrows (+1 EV)** add fire and ignite flammable terrain; **Screamers (+3 EV)** swaps the ability for one that dazes (or frightens at tier 3) instead of pure damage — better against morale-driven parties and lone bosses.

## Boiling Oil Cauldron

**Tags:** Level 3 Fortification Defender, EV 10
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 345

**Flavor:** A cauldron of oil perched at a high vantage, waiting to be tipped onto whoever's climbing the wall. Stamina 50, Size 1L.

**Tactics:** **Boiling Oil** is a 3-cube within 1 dealing heavy fire + burning (save ends); poured **from above**, it gains high ground and an edge on the power roll — so its intended use is from the battlements, not ground level. Reload-gated and sluggish (one use per reload), but defensive positioning does the work. Deactivation is an Agility test; a failure pours the oil on the would-be tamperer. Common **Watchtower** upgrade (+17 EV), turning a tower into a lethal kill-funnel.

## Catapult

**Tags:** Level 3 Siege Engine Artillery, EV 10
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 345

**Flavor:** A counterweighted engine hurling heavy projectiles. Stamina 50, Size 2.

**Tactics:** **Arcing Shot** is a 3-cube within 20 whose line of effect traces an **arc over obstacles** — bypasses walls, cover, and anything short of overhead cover. Tiers 2 and 3 add push 1/2 to reposition survivors. Reload-gated with Spot to sharpen the next shot, and **Move** lets the crew creep it forward at 2 squares a turn. Upgrades: **Air Assault (+2 EV)** lets the crew vertical-push an ally up to size 1L across the battlefield as a main action — a gimmick with devastating flanking potential; **Flammable (+2 EV)** adds fire damage and leaves the target area burning for the rest of the encounter.

## Exploding Mill Wheel

**Tags:** Level 3 Siege Engine Artillery, EV 10
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 346

**Flavor:** A massive wheel packed with explosives, rolled toward the enemy and ready to detonate. Stamina 25, Size 2.

**Tactics:** Unique engine — **Roll the Wheel** sets it moving 2 squares straight every turn thereafter, crushing creatures and objects size 2 or smaller in its path (push, heavy damage). Once rolling, it can't be deactivated; stopping it means destroying it or blocking with a size-3+ object — and blocking makes it **explode in a 5-burst** for massive damage, push, and burning. A ticking bomb the players have to actively manage. Upgrade: **Piloted (+4 EV)** adds a seat for a size 1M or smaller pilot, who can steer the rolling wheel and bail out mid-roll; untrained pilots roll Reason or detonate the wheel on themselves. A natural 19–20 lets the pilot both steer *and* disarm the explosives as a maneuver — a late-fight reveal worth setting up.

## Field Ballista

**Tags:** Level 2 Siege Engine Artillery, EV 8
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 346

**Flavor:** A massive crossbow firing thick metal bolts. Stamina 40, Size 2.

**Tactics:** **Release Bolt** is Ranged 20 single-target — heavy damage with push at higher tiers. Reload-gated with Spot and Move. Upgrades: **Penetrating Bolt (+2 EV)** hits the nearest two additional targets in a straight line past the initial target — wreck formations; **Chain Bolt (+2 EV)** swaps the ability for one that slows, and adds **Crank the Chain** — a pull (1/3/5 by tier) on a slowed target that **triggers opportunity attacks** along the path. The chain combo is the centerpiece: slow a front-liner, then yank them through the monster line for OAs.

## Iron Dragon

**Tags:** Level 4 Siege Engine Artillery, EV 12
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 347

**Flavor:** A massive metal bellows-and-fuel rig that breathes fire. Stamina 60, Size 2.

**Tactics:** **Gout of Flame** is an 8×2 line within 1 — heavy damage and burning (save ends) at every tier. Short reach means the crew has to advance it (Move 2/turn) toward the front, but a full line-breath through a tight corridor is punishing even for high-tier parties. Reload-gated with Spot; no flashy upgrades, just a brutally reliable damage engine for set-piece fights. Frame as a boss-fight hazard that the party must flank or disable, not trade shots with.

## Watchtower

**Tags:** Level 2 Fortification Defender, EV 8
**Source:** Draw Steel Monsters, p. 347

**Flavor:** A sturdy wooden tower with interior ladders or stairs. Stamina 50, Size 3. No Agility-test deactivation — it must be destroyed outright.

**Tactics:** Not a weapon — a **position**. Any creature inside has **high ground and cover** vs. anyone outside. Heroes breaching have to climb (Agility test) or smash through (Might test); on the lowest tier they take 1d6 and stay outside. Park ranged defenders inside and make the party choose between slow climbs, patient chopping, or eating arrows. Upgrades stack siege capability: **Ballista Emplacement (+12 EV)** bolts in a field ballista; **Boiling Oil Cauldron (+17 EV)** turns it into a kill funnel; **Spyglass (+2 EV)** extends perception to 15 squares with an edge; **Stone (+2 EV)** / **Iron Tower (+4 EV)** raise Stamina to 75 / 100 for a siege-proof pillar of the encounter.
